All (H)ours Rise Up tour - First World Tour
HEAVEN
Under The Arches, Villiers Street, London, GB, WC2N 6NG

ALL(H)OURS is a South Korean K-Pop boy group under EDEN Entertainment, who debuted on January 10, 2024 with their first mini album “ALL OURS”. The seven-member group consists of Kunho, Youmin, Xayden, Minje, Masami, Hyunbin, and ON:N.

The group name ALL(H)OURS combines the meanings of “all ours” and “all hours”, representing the group’s message of giving their all at every moment and sharing their energy with their fans. Their official fandom name MIN(UT)E also follows the theme of time, symbolizing the special moments created together between ALL(H)OURS and their fans.

All ticket categories already include admission to the concert. This means that if you purchase an Early Entrance, VIP or VVIP ticket, you do not need to purchase an additional GA ticket.
