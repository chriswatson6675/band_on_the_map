3.9. THURSDAY Lofi, Jazz und Indie-Musik

Donkeychote

Berlin based multi-instrumentalist „Donkeychote“ bribes his audience with his distinctive playing and producing. Genuine passion shaped his musical expression over the years, merging his styles to a juicy yet jazzy indie vibe. Organic saturated drums pumping throughout his music, laying down the foundation for his sticky bass and fuid lyrical guitar lines.
Since “Donkeychote” started his career in 2021 he generated millions of streams and listeners on digital streaming platforms and released more than 50 songs. Moreover he is frequently being featured on the biggest Spotify playlists like: lof beats or Jazz Vibes, Jazz Rap, Lof Cafe
Now it's time for him to play his music live on stage. His 3 piece band includes virtuoso instrumentalists and enhances his music magnifcently. Occasionally joined by vocalists & rappers the entire performance gets complimented perfectly.

https://linktr.ee/Donkeychote

4.9. FRIDAY  soul  

SOUL SYNDICATE

Soul Syndicate bringen mit ihrer modernen Mischung aus Funk und Northern Soul Schwung in die Berliner Club-Szene, aber auch auf die großen und kleinen Bühnen der Republik.
Das umfangreiche Programm aus Klassikern und Rariäten wird aufgefrischt durch Songs, die man nur von Soul Syndicate hören kann - bis andere Bands sie eines Tages covern! Diese Eigenkompositionen fügen sich geschmeidig in das bestehende Soul-Repertoire ein und bringen doch einen modernen, einen eigenen Touch - den Berlin City Soul!

https://linktr.ee/soulsyndicate
