Leading the fourth edition of KEYI Magazine's carefully curated club night at Säule is a live set by Oppenheimer MkII, a new project stemming from the legendary Oppenheimer Analysis and fresh off a new album created with Codex Empire. Also playing live is L.F.T., whose dynamic electro selections and masterful energy shifts have made him one of the most exciting DJ/producers on today's circuit. Joining them are Samantha Togni, founder of Boudica, bringing her uncompromising approach to the dancefloor, and Bezier, whose latest release on Dark Entries push the boundaries between electro, wave, EBM and experimental electronics. As with their magazine, in their DJ sets, KEYI founders Berlin Bunny and Eyes Dice connect cultures, communities and forward-thinking electronic music.
