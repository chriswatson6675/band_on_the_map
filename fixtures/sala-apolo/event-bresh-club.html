BRESH CLUB

Bresh in Barcelona - 26th August, 2026

After four years of hosting the world’s best party at Sala Apolo, Bresh decided to reinvent itself. It’s not just a facelift, it’s a statement: the DJ booth has moved down onto the dance floor, the décor has been transformed, and the set-up feels more intimate. Less visible production, more authentic atmosphere.

Bresh Club isn’t just a venue with a programme. It’s an event with its own identity that always takes place in the same spot. Four years of building a community in Barcelona, without needing to convince anyone every week. The venue fills up because people keep coming back.

You don’t come to watch. You come to feel. The music selection isn’t just a playlist: reggaeton, pop, dancehall, rock, electro and disco, carefully curated, one smash hit after hit, in mixes that leave you thinking about the next session. It’s not hype. It’s what happens inside. Every Wednesday is different, not because the concept changes, but because it’s alive.

Bresh Club is the evolution of Bresh in Barcelona: a closer, more immersive and authentic experience that preserves the essence of the most beautiful party in the world. Every Wednesday at Sala Apolo, the dance floor becomes the center of everything, creating a vibrant atmosphere and bringing together a loyal community that returns week after week. Its musical selection blends reggaeton, pop, dancehall, rock, electro and disco into sessions packed with hit after hit—music to dance to, feel and remember. More than just a party, Bresh Club is a gathering place with its own identity, where every night is unique.
