Event information

The Sardelli brothers closed out 2024 with an international tour that took them to more than 40 stages across Latin America and Spain, culminating in three sold-out shows at Vélez Sarsfield Stadium before more than 120,000 people. They kicked off 2025 at Cosquín Rock, headlining the festival's anniversary edition with a electrifying performance that reaffirmed their place in the history of Argentine rock.

With a steadily rising career, they released El Club de la Pelea I, the first part of their eighth studio album: ten songs where chaos, rebellion, and introspection fused with the pulse of 90s alternative rock, British influences, and road trip vibes. Produced entirely by AIRBAG, the album combined unreleased tracks with previously released songs like "Anarquía en Buenos Aires" and "Nunca Lo Olvides," creating an intense and cinematic experience that has already surpassed 61 million streams on digital platforms.

The launch culminated in an unprecedented milestone: two completely sold-out shows at the Monumental Stadium on May 31st and June 1st. Before 150,000 people, AIRBAG debuted at River Plate Stadium with a devastating performance, complete with lights, fire, anthems, and mosh pits. For almost three hours, the show spanned the fury of their new songs and the emotion of classics like "Cae el Sol" and "Por Mil Noches," closing with tangos and a total connection with the audience.

 

*Children under 16 years of age must access with the authorization of their mother, father or legal guardian (Article 53 of Decree 112/2010 of the Government of Catalonia) and accompanied by a responsible adult. More information in the minor’s section.
