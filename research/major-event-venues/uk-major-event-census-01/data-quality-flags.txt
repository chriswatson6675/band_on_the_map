--------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Calendar sources not gathered, and why
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The football calendar pass did not patch venues that ALREADY had calendar
sources, because it was unsure whether the patch merge would append or
overwrite. The merge in run-census.mjs `loadWorkstreams()` is genuinely
additive — it matches on name+city, skips any source_url already present,
and can only ADD — so that caution was unnecessary but harmless.

Consequence: requested non-football CONCERTS calendars for Wembley,
Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, Anfield, Etihad Stadium, Principality Stadium
and Hampden Park were not added. Anfield and Etihad were additionally
reported as having no distinct non-football events page. These remain
genuine gaps, not failures.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Hosts that would not fingerprint, and the actual reason for each
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

These carry acquisition_readiness SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED — attempted and
failed, NOT unattempted.

They were originally all recorded as the single opaque string "fetch
failed", which is what Node's fetch reports for almost every transport
problem. That flattened a cookie-consent redirect loop, an expired
certificate and a domain that does not resolve into one indistinguishable
bucket, and made live venues look dead. fingerprint-sources.mjs now
records error.cause as well, and a retry produced real diagnoses:

  https://www.boltonarena.co.uk/
    TLS alert internal error (SSL alert 80) — server-side TLS fault.

  https://www.thelowry.com/whats-on
  https://www.trch.co.uk/whats-on/
    Redirect loop against a cookie-less fetch. These sites bounce through
    a cookie/consent redirect that a plain fetch cannot satisfy. The
    venues are live and DO publish programmes — this is a limitation of
    our fetcher, not an absence of data. A cookie jar would resolve them.

  https://ffoslasracecourse.com/
    Certificate has expired.

  https://www.swanseaarena.co.uk/whats-on
    getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND — the domain does not resolve. This CORROBORATES
    the identity_review flag already on Swansea Building Society Arena:
    two researchers recorded two different official sites for it, and this
    confirms the un-hyphenated spelling is the dead one. The live site is
    https://www.swansea-arena.co.uk/.

  https://www.cooplive.com/events
    Still a bare "fetch failed" with no cause exposed. Co-op Live is
    plainly a live 23,500-capacity arena, so this is unexplained rather
    than evidence of anything.

  http://www.olympia.co.uk/whats-on
    Succeeded on retry and is no longer in this list — it WAS transient.

Nothing in this section should be read as "the venue publishes no
calendar". Not one of these was shown to lack a programme.
