--------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Calendar sources not gathered, and why
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The football calendar pass did not patch venues that ALREADY had calendar
sources, because it was unsure whether the patch merge would append or
overwrite. The merge in run-census.mjs `loadWorkstreams()` is genuinely
additive — it matches on name+city, skips any source_url already present,
and can only ADD — so that caution was unnecessary but harmless.

Consequence: requested non-football CONCERTS calendars for Wembley,
Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, Anfield, Etihad Stadium, Principality Stadium
and Hampden Park were not added. Anfield and Etihad were additionally
reported as having no distinct non-football events page. These remain
genuine gaps, not failures.

C. Hosts that would not fingerprint, and the actual reason for each
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

These carry acquisition_readiness SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED — attempted and
failed, NOT unattempted.

They were originally all recorded as the single opaque string "fetch
failed", which is what Node's fetch reports for almost every transport
problem. That flattened a cookie-consent redirect loop, an expired
certificate and a domain that does not resolve into one indistinguishable
bucket, and made live venues look dead. fingerprint-sources.mjs now
records error.cause as well, and a retry produced real diagnoses:

  https://www.boltonarena.co.uk/
    TLS alert internal error (SSL alert 80) — server-side TLS fault.

  https://www.thelowry.com/whats-on
  https://www.trch.co.uk/whats-on/
    Redirect loop against a cookie-less fetch. These sites bounce through
    a cookie/consent redirect that a plain fetch cannot satisfy. The
    venues are live and DO publish programmes — this is a limitation of
    our fetcher, not an absence of data. A cookie jar would resolve them.

  https://ffoslasracecourse.com/
    Certificate has expired.

  https://www.swanseaarena.co.uk/whats-on
    getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND — the domain does not resolve. This CORROBORATES
    the identity_review flag already on Swansea Building Society Arena:
    two researchers recorded two different official sites for it, and this
    confirms the un-hyphenated spelling is the dead one. The live site is
    https://www.swansea-arena.co.uk/.

  https://www.cooplive.com/events
    Still a bare "fetch failed" with no cause exposed. Co-op Live is
    plainly a live 23,500-capacity arena, so this is unexplained rather
    than evidence of anything.

  http://www.olympia.co.uk/whats-on
    Succeeded on retry and is no longer in this list — it WAS transient.

Nothing in this section should be read as "the venue publishes no
calendar". Not one of these was shown to lack a programme.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. Venue identity defects found and FIXED in compilation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DUPLICATE VENUES UNDER DIFFERENT HEADLINE NAMES. Exact-name matching
   missed records that two workstreams filed under different names, e.g.
   "Aviva Arena" vs "Aviva Arena Bristol" (both listing the aliases "YTL
   Arena Bristol" and "Bristol Arena") and "ICC Belfast" vs "ICC Belfast /
   Belfast Waterfront" (each naming the other). Reconciliation now also
   merges on a SHARED ALTERNATIVE NAME within the same city+nation. 13
   duplicate pairs were merged, including the Keepmoat / Club Doncaster
   Sports Village pair independently reported by the sport audit, and six
   stale-sponsor-name duplicates.

   A declared parent/sub-venue pair is explicitly protected: "Indoor Arena,
   Coventry Building Society Arena" correctly survives as its own record
   alongside its parent. Alias merges are flagged `identity_review` because
   the evidence is weaker than an exact match.

2. VENUE CLASS DECIDED BY FILENAME ORDER. A merged record took its
   `venue_type` from whichever workstream file sorted first. Coventry
   Building Society Arena — a 32,609-capacity Premier League stadium —
   was therefore presented as an EXHIBITION_CENTRE, because
   conference-exhibition.json sorts before football-stadiums.json.

   Class is now ELECTED: researchers disagreeing across activity families
   means the venue genuinely does several things (now
   MULTI_PURPOSE_EVENT_COMPLEX, 34 venues); disagreeing within one family
   elects the best-evidenced largest configuration. Every claim is retained
   in `venue_type_claims`, so a dual-code football/rugby ground never loses
   the fact that it is one. 40 venues had their class elected.

future-tense and the last direct physical statement is that the stands

can never evidence PRESENT operation. Three new operational statuses
(DERELICT, NO_LONGER_EVENT_VENUE, REDEVELOPED) exist so that distinction
can be recorded rather than flattened into CLOSED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
K. A LIMITATION OF THE SWEEP, STATED PLAINLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SWEEP CANNOT DETECT WRONG_ENTITY. Separating "the venue's own official
site" from "a page that legitimately mentions the venue" is beyond a text
check.

Worked example, found and fixed by hand: Gander Green Lane was recorded
with https://www.cpfc.co.uk/palace-women/ — a page titled "Crystal Palace
Women FC" which never names Gander Green Lane. Crystal Palace Women play
there as tenants; the ground is Sutton United's. It is quarantined, and
left OFFICIAL_URL_REVIEW_REQUIRED because suttonunited.net returns a
Cloudflare 403 and a domain resembling a club's name is not evidence.

An attempt to automate this detection — by requiring the page to match the
venue's OWN name rather than its city or operator — produced 51
WRONG_ENTITY verdicts, nearly all false: a ground recorded against its
RESIDENT club's official site is legitimate (that is what
VALID_OPERATOR_OR_CLUB_OFFICIAL means), and venues such as "The Den" and
"The Old Vic" leave no usable token once generic words are stripped. A
rule that condemns 51 valid records to catch 1 invalid one is a worse
instrument than admitting the limit. WRONG_ENTITY is therefore only ever
assigned from manually evidenced findings.
