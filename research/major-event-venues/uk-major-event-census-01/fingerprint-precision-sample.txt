FINGERPRINT PRECISION SPOT-AUDIT — OTHER_EMBEDDED_APP_STATE
BEATMAPPED-UK-MAJOR-EVENT-VENUE-CENSUS-01

WHY: OTHER_EMBEDDED_APP_STATE was the largest single source family in the
census fingerprint pass (214 of 586 sources), which would normally imply a
very large TIER2_REUSABLE_FAMILY engineering opportunity. Before reporting
that as fact, its precision was tested rather than assumed.

METHOD: a deterministic even spread (every Nth record, not the head of the
list) of 14 of the 214 sources was re-fetched, and each page checked for
whether it actually carries a real <script type="application/json"> data
island, or whether the fingerprint fired on something else.

The detector on this branch (fresh origin/main) fires when the literal text
"application/json" appears anywhere in the body AND any <script> tag exists.
Two false-positive shapes follow from that:
  1. WordPress's near-universal <script id="wp-emoji-settings"
     type="application/json"> default, present regardless of content.
  2. Pages with no application/json script at all, where the string merely
     occurs in inline JS (e.g. a fetch() Content-Type header).

RESULT (see raw output below): 3 of 14 were wp-emoji-only, and a further 5
had no application/json script element at all — so 8 of 14 (57%) of this
sample are misclassified. Only 6 of 14 carry a genuine embedded JSON island.

CONSEQUENCE: the TIER2_REUSABLE_FAMILY total (314) and the
OTHER_EMBEDDED_APP_STATE family total (214) should be read as UPPER BOUNDS,
not settled figures. Both false-positive shapes are already fixed on the
parked Manchester calibration branch (PR #41), which tightened this exact
detector to require a real application/json script element and to ignore
wp-emoji-settings. Landing those fixes would materially re-grade this
census; this census is, independently, national-scale evidence that they
matter beyond Manchester.

Sample size is 14 — small. Treat the 57% as directional, not precise.

ADDENDUM (2026-09-20, after the calendar-completion passes)

The audit above was performed against the fingerprint snapshot of 586
calendar sources, in which OTHER_EMBEDDED_APP_STATE held 214 and
TIER2_REUSABLE_FAMILY held 314.

Three calendar-completion passes subsequently added sources, and the final
census population is LARGER:

  calendar sources        586 -> 668
  OTHER_EMBEDDED_APP_STATE 214 -> 245
  TIER2_REUSABLE_FAMILY    314 -> 366

The 14-source sample was NOT re-drawn against the larger population. The
misclassification rate measured above (roughly 57% of the sampled
OTHER_EMBEDDED_APP_STATE sources were not in fact reusable embedded app
state) is therefore a measurement of the 214-source population, not of the
245-source one. It is carried forward as the best available estimate
because the added sources are of the same kinds and were fingerprinted by
the same unchanged engine — but that is an expectation, not a measurement.

The conclusion is unchanged and applies to the final numbers: the
TIER2_REUSABLE_FAMILY total of 366 is an UPPER BOUND on genuinely
Tier-2-reusable sources, not a count of them.
