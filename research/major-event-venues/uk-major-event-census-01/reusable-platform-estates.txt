--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHY THIS MATTERS FOR TIER-2 COUNTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

These 47 sources sit inside TIER2_REUSABLE_FAMILY, which the Census-01
report correctly flagged as an UPPER BOUND rather than a count. Phase 15
of this package re-checks every TIER2-contributing source against its
family's own structural claim and conservatively downgrades the ones the
live page does not support — see `source-family-audit.json`.

The EMBEDDED_NUXT_STATE family is verified by the presence of a
`window.__NUXT__` / `__NUXT_DATA__` payload, which all four sampled
members demonstrably have, so this particular estate is expected to
survive that audit. The audit result, not this expectation, is the
authority.

READ THIS CAREFULLY BEFORE PLANNING ACQUISITION:

- UNIFORM family means one implementation plausibly covers the estate.
  That applies to gc (47), ATG (28), AMG (18), Trafalgar (9) and Delfont
  Mackintosh (5) — 107 venues across five implementations.

- The Jockey Club estate is NOT uniform: 14 venues across 20 sources
  spanning three different families. One adapter will not cover it.

- champrugby.com (10) and lwtheatres.co.uk (5) fingerprint ACCESS_BLOCKED.
  They are blocked to a plain fetch, not absent. A previous pass reported
  both render normally in a browser, so these are a browser-capability
  question, not a data-availability one.

- The three shared league platforms (Highland League, Scottish Rugby, WRU)
  total 33 venues but all fingerprint CLIENT_RENDERED_UNKNOWN, and a
  previous pass noted the WRU and Scottish Rugby list views show no venue
  name. Venue attribution, not fetching, is the hard part there.

ARC racecourses do NOT appear in this host table because the ARC platform
uses per-course domains (<course>-racecourse.co.uk) rather than one shared
host. The shared-pattern claim stands, but it is a URL-pattern estate
rather than a single-host estate, and any collector must treat it as such.
