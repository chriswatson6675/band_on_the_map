IMPLAUSIBLE GAPS — stated, not smoothed over:

1. NORTHERN IRELAND's non-sporting estate is 3 theatre/concert venues and
   1 conference/exhibition venue for the entire nation. That is not
   credible as a description of NI; it is a description of how little was
   reachable. Visit Belfast's venue directory is client-rendered and its
   sitemap contains no venue pages; Tourism NI's business-events paths
   404. NI is effectively UNSWEPT outside sport.

2. NI calendar coverage (60%) is 30 points below England's. Same cause.

3. SCOTLAND's 17 theatre/concert venues and WALES's 8 are thin. Both
   research passes judged the genuine >=1,000 estate in those nations to
   be small and mostly already held, but neither established that — both
   exhausted their search budget first.

4. CONFERENCE_CENTRE calendar coverage is 38% and CONVENTION_CENTRE 50%.
   Business-event venues frequently publish venue-hire pages rather than
   public event calendars, and a hire page is explicitly NOT accepted as a
   calendar. Some of this gap is real absence rather than failure to find.

5. OTHER_SPORTS_VENUE coverage is 43% — the lowest of any class. It is a
   residual bucket, so this is expected rather than alarming, but it means
   14 venues are poorly characterised.

NO QUOTAS WERE IMPOSED. Nation totals reflect what was evidenced. England
genuinely holds most of the UK's major event infrastructure; the gaps
above are about SWEEP COVERAGE, not about forcing nations to be equal.
