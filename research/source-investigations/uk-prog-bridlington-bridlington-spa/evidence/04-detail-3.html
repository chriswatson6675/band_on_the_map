Ticket Insurance

Protect your tickets

Tickets for shows at Bridlington Spa are non-refundable, protect your order from the unexpected for just £2 per ticket with Secure My Booking. Our ticket refund protection allows you to recover 100% of the paid ticket price (including all booking fees, evidence will be required).

For just £2 per ticket you can apply for a refund in the event of:

  • Accident, injury, or illness (including COVID-19)
  • Severe weather preventing travel to the venue
  • Travel delays due to breakdown or public transit failures
  • Plus much more!

For full terms and conditions please visit the Secure My Booking website.

You will be prompted to add ticket insurance when buying your tickets. It can only be bought in the same transaction as the tickets. For a worry-free purchase, protect your tickets today as you cannot add to your order after you have paid.

Please note: Only tickets for events at Bridlington Spa are covered by ticket protection.

Secure My Booking ticket protection is non-refundable through Bridington Spa.
