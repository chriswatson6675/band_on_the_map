Theatrix – after school and Saturday mornings at the Abbey Theatre

The Abbey Theatre hosts a number of regular events for local arts organisations. If you’d like to find out more about these, please click on the links provided. And if you’d like to talk to us about the possibility of using our spaces for your events, please feel free to drop us a line.

Theatrix offers weekly classes in drama, acting, speech and musical theatre for groups, individual and pairs of young students, aged 5–19, after school and on Saturday mornings. In Theatrix classes you gain self-confidence, assertiveness, poise, trust and respect – not to mention nationally recognised qualifications and many, many friends. Visit Theatrix for more information and details of classes.

The Arts Society, St Albans, brings people together through a shared curiosity for the arts. Its monthly events provide welcoming places to hear expert lecturers share their specialist knowledge about the arts. Members contribute to and preserve our artistic heritage through volunteering and grants. Ultimately, The Arts Society work creates a better, healthier and more connected society. Visit The Arts Society for more information.

Mini Mozart offers fun and interactive live music classes for toddlers and babies. The classes feature two or more live instruments; a clarinet, violin, flute, French horn, saxophone or trumpet with a piano accompanist. The idea of Mini Mozart is to get children actually listening, not just hearing. Children interact with  live musicians in a way that is impossible with recorded music. Visit Mini Mozart for more information and details of classes.

Noise Makers runs award-winning fun, interactive singing, music and movement classes for babies and toddlers. Our classes include singing, movement, instruments, scarves, a parachute, bubbles and fun. Run by Nicola and her side-kick Norman, the noisiest of all the Noise Makers, classes are designed to be engaging, interactive and fun! Visit Noise Makers for more information and details of classes.

Babyballet’s preschool dance syllabus has been carefully designed to ensure each child is taught the principles of ballet and tap at the right developmental level while boosting confidence, and of course having lots of fun.

Our programme fits well within the UK Government’s Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework, with our classes providing the opportunity for babies and young children to learn and develop key life skills in a healthy and safe environment. Visit Babyballet for more information and details of classes.
