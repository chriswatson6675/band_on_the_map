Welcome to LIVEHOUSE – The New Pulse of Dundee

Rising from the legendary walls of the former Green’s Playhouse on Nethergate, LIVEHOUSE is Dundee’s bold new home for live music, entertainment, conferencing, and the arts.

Launching in phases from May 2025, this iconic transformation is more than just a venue—it’s a statement. Once fully open, LIVEHOUSE will stand among the largest and most versatile event spaces in Scotland, hosting world-class artists, electrifying club nights, powerhouse conferences, and unforgettable performances under one roof.

As Dundee surges forward in its city centre renaissance, LIVEHOUSE leads the charge—breathing fresh energy into a landmark location and creating a cultural powerhouse at the heart of the action.

With cutting-edge production, adaptable space design, and an unapologetic commitment to excellence, LIVEHOUSE isn’t just built for events—it’s built for moments that move people.

Get ready, Dundee. The future of live entertainment has a new home.

Venue Information & FAQs

Parking

We have an on-site car park that sits underneath the venue which is available to use during live shows. Whilst events are on at the venue, our car park will remain open for 90 minutes after the event finishes, but vehicles can be left overnight also. The car park is managed by Ring Go and payment is made via the app. Access to the car park is from Yeaman Shore.

Accessibility

LIVEHOUSE is fully accessible and offers the following:

  • Accessible seating on the balcony
  • Carer Ticket – email info@livehousedundee.co.uk

Please note, while every effort will be made to ensure a fantastic and inclusive experience for all of our guests, like all ticket types, we are limited by available space therefore we have a capacity to all accessible spaces, wheelchair sections, and carers slots. To enquire please get in touch directly by e-mailing info@livehousedundee.co.uk

Lost Property

If you’ve lost an item at the venue, please email info@livehousedundee.co.uk with:

  • a description of the missing item
  • the date of the event
  • your contact details
  • any other relevant information

If the item can be located, a member of the Lost Property team will be in touch to arrange for you to collect the item. The venue keeps items for a maximum of four weeks from the date of the event. After this period, items such as passports, ID, keys, jewellery and mobile phones are handed to the police. All other items are donated to charity after this period. Our Lost Property team aim to respond within 48 hours.
