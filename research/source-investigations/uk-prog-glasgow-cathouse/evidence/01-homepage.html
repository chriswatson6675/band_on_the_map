WELCOME TO CATHOUSE ROCK CLUB

SCOTLAND’S FOREMOST AND ORIGINAL ROCK CLUB SINCE 1990

Cathouse Rock Club is Scotland’s foremost and original rock club since 1990, distinguished for leading the way in power age rock and metal for 3 decades. We are not afraid to embrace some genres you might not to expect in a traditional rock club. Times change, tastes change and we like to make sure we play a mix of tunes throughout our 3 rooms: Level 1, Level 2 & Jäger Bar. Open Wednesday – Sunday, there is most definitely a night for everyone!

 

Over the years, we have hosted both huge names in rock as well as smaller, more local bands. Our favourite Cathouse Rock Club performances include Anthrax; Black Stone Cherry; Frank Carter and the Rattlesnakes; Pearl Jam; Bring Me the Horizon; Queens of the Stone Age; Fall Out Boy; Halestorm; Beartooth; Static X; Soil; and Shangela. Find out what gigs are on next!

 

As an alternative club and venue, we love to host regular alternative shows and events.
