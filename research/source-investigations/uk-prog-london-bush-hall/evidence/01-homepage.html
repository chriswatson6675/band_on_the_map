Originally built by a publisher in 1904, one of a trio of London dance halls he built for each of his daughters, Bush Hall is the only surviving of the three.

Since 2001, the building has been returned to its former musical glory.

Based in the vibrant heart of West London, we are a fully independent, grassroots music venue having hosted some of the biggest names in music.

Membership

Forget one-off gig tickets, standard subscriptions or dust- gathering gift-cards – Bush Hall Group members can expect unparalleled access to a raft of eclectic live music, modern dining and unique entertainment.

Culture vultures can enjoy free comedy tickets. For the person who always wants to be on the guest list there’s access to exclusive gigs and secret shows. There’s also discounted private hire of Bush Hall or The Music House for Children all year-round.

Memberships start from £75 for AAA or £200 for VIP – for full membership benefits and details of how to purchase, contact us.
