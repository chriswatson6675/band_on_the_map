Right on Brighton’s iconic seafront, Tide is a vibrant coastal spot where relaxed beach days naturally roll into unforgettable nights.

With panoramic views across the beach, it’s the perfect place to meet friends, enjoy great food and drinks, dance to great music and soak up the atmosphere from sunny afternoons through to late night.

 

Beach club events take place every Friday and Saturday from 7pm until late, with DJs, sunset terrace vibes and the perfect soundtrack to Brighton evenings. Tables are free to reserve, with VIP cabana options available for those looking to elevate their night.

 

Alongside the beach club sessions, Tide also hosts a fully programmed calendar of nightclub and special events throughout the year.
