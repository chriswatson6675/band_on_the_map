White Christmas – Maghull Musical Theatre Company

November 19 – November 22
WHITE CHRISTMAS
19th – 22nd November at Little Theatre Southport .
 
This November, Maghull Musical Theatre Company brings the festive charm of White Christmas to life with a dazzling production full of snow, songs, and sparkle. Based on the beloved 1954 film, the story follows entertainers Bob Wallace and Phil Davis as they team up with the Haynes sisters and head to Vermont to save a struggling inn—rekindling love, friendship, and holiday cheer along the way. With classic Irving Berlin tunes like “White Christmas,” “I Love a Piano,” and “Count Your Blessings,” plus vibrant choreography and a cast of colourful characters, it’s set to be a heartwarming seasonal treat.
