CROMARTY CHRISTMAS CRAFT MARKET 2026

HomeEventsChristmas Craft MarketCROMARTY CHRISTMAS CRAFT MARKET 2026

Fri, 13th – Sun, 15th Nov

The Old Brewery, Burnside Place Cromarty IV11 8XQ

Join us for this year’s Cromarty Craft Market and enjoy a carefully selected range of handmade work from local and regional makers.

The market brings together artists, designers and craftspeople working across textiles, ceramics, jewellery, print, wood, glass, homeware, gifts and more. It is a great opportunity to buy directly from makers, find something special and support creative businesses from Cromarty, the Black Isle and beyond.

Set within the welcoming surroundings of Cromarty Arts Trust, the Craft Market is always a popular day out for visitors and local residents alike. Come along, browse at your own pace, meet the makers and enjoy the atmosphere.

Whether you are looking for a unique gift, something beautiful for your home, or simply a chance to enjoy high quality local craft, we would love to see you there.

Friday 6pm – 8pm – Free Mulled wine and Mince Pies

Saturday & Sunday 10am – 4pm

Free entry.
