The Venue

Silk is a stylish modern venue located in Wellingborough town center with full disabled access. We have a fully licensed bar with a maximum capacity of up to 350 people standing and 150 people seated. Silk’s the prefect venue for any birthday party, wedding, conference or any other type of function.

We offer the best venue in town at great prices.
