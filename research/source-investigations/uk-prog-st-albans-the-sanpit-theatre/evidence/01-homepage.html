All About Us

We are a highly-experienced and enthusiastic team based here at the SandPit Theatre. We work together to ensure the smooth running of shows, events, training sessions for artists and schools and delivering inclusive theatre for schools, theatres and families. We are passionate about what we do and want to share the SandPit magic as widely as possible.
