Monomyth & LBU School of Arts Presents – Freshers Showcase 26

Friday 25 September 2026

Doors - 19:30

Buy tickets →
!Age Policy - 14+ (Under 14s must be accompanied by an adult)

Monomyth Records and Leeds Beckett University School of Arts present Freshers Showcase 26, a night celebrating some of the most exciting emerging music from Leeds. Taking over The Wardrobe on Friday 25 September from 7.30pm, the showcase features live performances from Señor Pink, Hitlist, Twinsize and Porcelain. Bringing together new talent, students and the wider Leeds music community, the event offers a first taste of the city’s thriving grassroots scene. Free entry for students.
