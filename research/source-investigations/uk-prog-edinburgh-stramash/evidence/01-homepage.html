About us

Stramash live music bar is located in The Cowgate in Edinburgh’s Old Town, a 700-capacity live music venue with live bands performing every day of the week until 3am. Free entry every night with a traditional ceilidh dance on a Wednesday, party bands on ‘til the wee hours, touring bands, tribute acts and everything in between. There really is no other venue like it!
