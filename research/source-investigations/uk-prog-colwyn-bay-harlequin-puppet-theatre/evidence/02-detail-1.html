Peter and the Wolf

This Christmas, the Harlequin Puppet Theatre in Rhos‑on‑Sea invites families to enjoy a magical new marionette production of Peter and the Wolf. Performed with beautifully crafted string puppets and the theatre’s trademark storytelling charm, this festive staging brings Prokofiev’s classic tale to life with warmth, humour and plenty of seasonal sparkle.

Join Peter, the daring bird, the grumbling duck and the mischievous cat as they venture into the wintry woods on Saturday 19 December and Sunday 20 December 2026. A perfect Christmas treat at Britain’s first purpose‑built puppet theatre, it promises an enchanting experience for audiences of all ages.
