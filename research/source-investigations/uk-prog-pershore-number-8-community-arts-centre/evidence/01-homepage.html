Our Venue

Number 8 is a fantastic purpose built venue with a 250 seat auditorium, dance studio, meeting room and coffee bar.

Find out more

Volunteering

Number 8 is managed and staffed by over 230 volunteers working alongside a small team of paid staff. Volunteers lead the management team and work in a variety of roles across the organisation – from customer facing roles like coffee bar assistants and stewards to work behind the scenes as admin assistants, or as part of the maintenance team.

Read more >

Venue Hire

Number 8 offers high quality facilities for hire in the unique atmosphere of a local community arts centre. Whether you’re looking for a conference venue, a room in which to hold a training course, workshop, meeting or party, or wish to stage a live event we have the space for you!

Read more >

Donations

As a registered charity Number 8 welcomes donations to support our mission to provide arts and leisure activities, education, and opportunities. Any donation you can make, whether large or small, will help us to continue the work we do to enhance the cultural life and wellbeing of the community.
