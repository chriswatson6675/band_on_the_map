Welcome to the Newbiggin Arts Centre
… the place for all things art…where arts are for all.
Situated in the heart of Newbiggin, here you’ll find a vibrant, welcoming space where people of all ages and abilities can explore their creativity, connect with others, and express themselves through art.
Whether you are picking up a paintbrush for the first time, looking to reconnect with your artistic side, or just want to meet new people and make friends, you’re in the right place.

Our modern and bright studio also hosts art exhibitions from a variety of visiting artists as well as our own.  We also have a well-stocked shop for anyone needing some new art materials.  In addition, we also host speakers, hold community events, and offer our space to other groups.

Our aim is to make art accessible for everyone, so why not drop by and see us in action—we love visitors!
