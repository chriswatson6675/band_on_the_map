About The Hive Skegness

The Hive Skegness is Lincolnshire's premier nightlife destination, bringing together 6 unique venues across 5 incredible floors of non-stop entertainment. Whether you're planning a big night out in Skegness, celebrating a birthday, or looking for live music on the East Coast - The Hive has something for everyone.

​

Located on Grand Parade in the heart of Skegness, we're home to some of the best bars, nightclubs, and live music venues in Lincolnshire. From the 80s Fun Pub of Busters to the Karaoke vibes of Gilly's Tiki Tavern, The cocktails of Tantra to the pounding beats of Kush Nightclub - every floor offers a completely different experience all under one roof.
