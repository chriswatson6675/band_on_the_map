Welcome to Westpoint

Westpoint is home to the South West’s largest and most versatile arena and showground, providing the ultimate destination for a wide range of exhibitions, concerts, shows and industry events.

Set within 150 acres of rolling countryside, we are ideally situated, just off the M5 close to the vibrant cathedral city of Exeter. We offer ample, free car parking in a safe and secure environment.

Indoor arena

Home to the largest indoor arena in the South West

Our arena is a blank canvas for Event Organisers. We host an enviable selection of events that have given us the reputation as a leader in our field. From Disney on Ice to Sky Sports Darts and business expo’s to wedding extravaganza’s, we really do enjoy hosting events and exhibitions from every genre.

Conference Suite Hire

Our Conference Suite provides a modern, spacious venue for a range of events

Perfect for business meetings, small exhibitions and conferences  with a light, open space seating up to 300. It’s a blank canvas for any event, from a regional business seminar or exhibition to a conference or staff awayday.
