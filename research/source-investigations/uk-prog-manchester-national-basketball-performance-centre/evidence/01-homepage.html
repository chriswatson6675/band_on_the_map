our venue

The National Basketball Performance Centre
Get DirectionsGet Tickets

The National Basketball Performance Centre, home of Manchester Basketball has a 2000 spectator capacity. The venue is fully accessible with onsite parking and a range of other gameday facilities.

The centre is part of the Belle Vue Sports Village, part of a unique collection of community and elite facilities governed by MCRactive, a not for profit organisation established and overseen by Manchester City Council.

Accessibility

The venue has been adapted for wheelchairs, has disabled toilet facilities, accessible parking, changing and a lift (although use of it will not be required to attend a game).

If you have any questions then please get in touch before booking tickets.

PARKING

NBPC has a secure car pack at the back of the venue, you can only pay on arrival and please note that there are often queues to get into the car park on game day. We would advise arriving with plenty of time to spare.

Payment for parking can be made by visiting parkonomy.com, downloading the app or scanning the QR code available on the car park signage. The car park location is 1341.

For any enquiries regarding parking at the National Basketball Performance Centre please contact Belle Vue Sports Village by email at bellevuesportsvillage@gll.org

public transport

You can get to the NBPC on bus number 201 or 203, you can plan your route from anywhere in Greater Manchester here.

bike storage

There is storage for 11 bikes onsite, available on a first come first served basis.
