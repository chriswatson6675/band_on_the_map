House of Lunacy Halloween – The Danse Macabre
CLUB

House of Lunacy is an Adult playground for the senses, a sex positive space with consent and comfort at it's principle core. A lovers Labyrinth of Art, Theatre and Performances from Berlins most creative souls.

Dearest Darlings,
Welcome to our final chapter of the year. Chapter IV: Decay.

This year, we pass through the veil to a ballroom of magnificent revelry and rot! Step across the threshold into a twilight realm where 1920s Weimar decadence bleeds into the shadowed, velvety boudoirs of turn of the century Montmartre. We collide the dark, avant garde expressionism of Anita Berber’s Dances of Vice, Horror, and Ecstasy with the sultry, cigarette stained hedonism of a belle époque Parisian brothel.

We have reached the absolute end of this cycle. Since our rebirth, we now prepare to decay once more, letting our world settle before the wheel turns for 2027.

Join hands with the damned as we dance into the night with the forgotten souls of the past. A ballroom of raw sensuality, scandalous erotica, and total liberation.

Channel the provocative spirit of Berber: androgynous tuxedos, stark Expressionist dramatics, and radical, unfiltered expression, entwined with the lace, powdered wigs, corsetry, and silk petticoats of Montmartre’s most infamous opium dens and pleasure houses.

The Damned have returned to this world for one single night, leaving their beauty to rot and decay in their once magnificent splendour as they swirl across the floor in pure rapture and lust.

DRESS CODE & INSPIRATION

We invite you to manifest your darkest, most decadent interpretations of the theme:

Weimar Cabaret & Expressionist Vice:

Anita Berber, Sebastian Droste, sharp suits, monocles, sharp tailoring, smudged rouge, theatrical macabre, and avant garde nudity. 

Montmartre Boudoir:

Silk robes, stained velvet, corsets, ripped lace, powdered faces, disheveled aristocrats, salon poets, and courtesan glam. 

Rot, Decay & The Undead: Faded grandeur, tattered ballgowns, skeletal elegance, and opulent grotesque beauty. 

Your creativity always brings us so much joy and amazement, we cannot wait to see what sublime madness you will manifest.

GUIDELINES ✵ HOUSE RULES✵

Unfortunately nobody can ever guarantee a space is 100% safe, but we are striving for perfection.

No means no. Yes can always become No.

Never take what doesn't want to be taken and don't give what is not wanted.

Consent is not negotiable. We take this very seriously.

We DO NOT tolerate any form of discrimination, Racism, homophobia, anti trans or hate speech.

Respect. Respect. Respect.

Anyone who is found to be disobeying these rules, you will be leaving immediately and dealt with accordingly.

We will have our care team and Awareness team walking around The House at all times.

A hand-picked selection of caring and loving souls and party veterans who know how to look out for you and are there to help with whatever the situation.

Any member of staff (incl bar staff) can be spoken to at any time, they are all connected via walkie talkie to security and the door.

Never feel embarrassed about asking and talking to them.

Take care of one and other and always reach out if needed.
