Sunday Night Jazz

Ease your way back into the week with our Jazz Takeover.

Whether you're bringing your dancing shoes for a boogie or grabbing a table and ordering drinks via our app, you're sure to have a great time.

Can you play jazz? Don't be afraid to bring along your instruments and get involved!

This is a FREE event. See you at The Pleasance!

Venue: Cabaret Bar, 60 Pleasance, Edinburgh, EH8 9TJ

Get information on how to attend your event/s safely and the terms and conditions of your purchase: https://www.eusa.ed.ac.uk/about/ticketterms
