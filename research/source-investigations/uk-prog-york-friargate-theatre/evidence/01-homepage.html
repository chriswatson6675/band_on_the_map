    THE SELFISH GIANT

    08 SEPT - 30 OCT, UK TOUR

    FIND OUT MORE

    Step into the magical world of the Giant's garden.

    It's full of music, beauty, bird song and stories!​

    ​

    Through storytelling, interactive games, live music, and puppetry, Riding Lights Theatre Company brings you The Selfish Giant - a wonderful play for family audiences (perfect for those 3+) and inspirational tale of love and generosity.

    More Info
    Book Tickets

    Based in Friargate Theatre in York, Riding Lights Theatre Company is one of the UK’s most productive and long-established independent theatre companies. Founded in York in 1977, partly through the initiative of a city-centre church, the company continues to take innovative, accessible theatre into all kinds of communities far and wide.
