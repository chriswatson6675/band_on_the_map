Multi Use Arena with Activities for All

Dundee Ice Arena is a versatile sports and events venue in Dundee, Scotland, built in 2000 and located at the Camperdown Leisure Complex. It features an Olympic-size rink with seating for 2,300 spectators and hosts public skating, ice hockey and curling. The facility also includes hospitality rooms, a cafe, a bar, and a shop.
