About Us

Opened in 2008, Lightbox is a 3 roomed club venue located in Lambeth, South London. The 600 capacity space takes its name from the LED walls that cover the club’s main room. Lightbox regularly plays host to techno, house, UK garage and drum & bass. The club regularly runs Thursday night events and after-hours parties in addition to regular weekend club nights. With a recently installed, brand new soundsystem supplied by Danley Sound Labs expect state of the art sound & lighting.

FAQs  

We do not allow any unauthorised filming or photograph. You will not be allowed to bring in professional cameras. If you would like permission to film or take photos please email info@lightboxlondon.com

Lightbox London is always looking for staff to join our team. If you are looking for work please send your CV to info@lightboxlondon.com

Yes you can. However, all medication and equipment will need to be registered with our on-site medics provided it is in the manufacturer's original packaging. We strongly encourage that all medication and medical equipment is stored with our on-site medics wherever possible, and that you use our medic room when administering any medication. The medics will show you the area we use exclusively for our medics and any member of staff or security can escort you to the medic room should you need to use the area.

You can buy tickets for Lightbox London through our website – please click on the What's On tab.

It couldn’t be easier to find Lightbox London – we are in the transport hub at Vauxhall. We are less than 100 metres from Vauxhall Underground Station.

Underground – Victoria Line

Overground – various train services

You can use Transport for London’s website to plan your journey www.tfl.gov.uk

There is no local parking and Lightbox London recommends using public transport to get to us.

Lightbox London has a strict over 18 policy.

We also operate a No ID No Entry policy – this means no matter how old you are you must present valid ID to gain admission to the venue.

Valid ID must be in date. We accept the following forms of identification;

  • Valid Passport
  • Valid Driving Licence
  • Valid National ID card
  • Valid ID with PASS hologram (find our more information at pass-scheme.org.uk)

Please note that we do not accept photos of ID or photocopies.

Yes Lightbox London is an accessible venue, although there are some parts of the venue which are not accessible via wheelchair due to steps.

Please feel free to email us at info@lightboxlondon.com if you have an specific access needs.

Lightbox London has various events with various music policies. The best thing to do is check our What's on section.

The dress code at Lightbox London is casual. There are different rules for certain events however, so best to call or email us to find out more. 

We do not allow people to wear hoods in the queue or in the venue.

Lightbox London operates various events with various opening times. The best thing to do is check out the event listings in the What's on section.

If you think you have lost something at Lightbox London you can click on Contact Us and complete the lost property form. Please make your description as detailed as possible to help us.

Please note we hold property for 3 months and then it is donated to charity.

All ID documents and mobile phones are handed to the local Police.

Venue Hire

Vauxhall Arches is a collection of 10 railway arches close to the River Thames. Only 20 yards from Vauxhall Underground, Rail and 24-hour Bus Station, the venue is well known and easily accessible.

Close to the banks of the River Thames under Vauxhalls railway arches possibility can flow freely and spontaneity has more than enough room to dance.

A versatile space with multiple rooms, outdoor areas and VIP spaces, catering for events of varying capacity, our team are happy to accommodate your individual requirements.

Vauxhall Arches are available for external hire throughout the year. Now regarded as one of London's leading clubbing destinations, we offer a multi-functional and flexible interlocking space for your event or production.
