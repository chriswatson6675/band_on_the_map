Hope and Ruin

The Hope & Ruin is a grassroots music venue and pub that strives to support the independent live music scene here in Brighton. All of our bar products are plant based to compliment the offerings from Beelzebab who provide fast food from a caravan in the corner of the pub. There’s always something going on most nights including DJs, art happenings, quizzes and more. We also love dogs who bring their humans to say hello.
