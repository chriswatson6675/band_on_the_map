Our Mission Statement
"To create an environment that fosters an inclusive and welcoming community which encourages creative excellence, celebrating the shared experience of live entertainment.”

BOX OFFICE and ART GALLERY OPENING TIMES:

Our team of volunteers staff the Box Office during the following times:
Monday to Saturday: 10:30am - 1pm ~ Sunday: Closed ~ Show Nights: 6pm – 7:30pm.

We will also be open 1hr before matinee performances. Closed on all Bank Holidays.

Telephone Bookings can be made during these times on 01424-423221
