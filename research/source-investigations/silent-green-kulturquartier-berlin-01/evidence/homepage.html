Film Feld Forschung

The silent green project Film Feld Forschung (Film Field Research) offers a centre for art film and is dedicated to the various fields of application of the audiovisual through exhibition projects, film screenings and discourse formats.

Guided Tours

The former crematorium of Berlin-Wedding is listed in Berlin as a historically protected building. If you would like to take a look behind the scenes of this unique historical monument, please register for one of our guided tours (in German).

MARS | Café & Bar

Our in-house restaurant, MARS, is a little hidden in the side wing of the building and offers peace from everyday life with lunch, café, evening snacks & weekend brunch.

Renting

In addition to running our own projects and event formats, we at silent green also rent out our venues to third parties. Find out more about the available spaces and the conditions here.
