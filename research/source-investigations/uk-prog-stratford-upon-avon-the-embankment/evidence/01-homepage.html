Our menus

The Embankment is more than just a cocktail bar located in Stratford-upon-Avon. We care about our food as much as our drinks. Our kitchen team gets ideas from around the world.

We serve fresh, tasty dishes made with local ingredients. This way, every bite is as satisfying as the cocktails we make. Whether you’re here for dinner or weekend brunch, our diverse menu is sure to impress.

The Embankment has a stylish, open-plan space. It covers two glass-fronted floors near the River Avon.

This venue is great for many events. Our modern spaces can host everything from small weddings to large-scale corporate events. This makes The Embankment a great choice for those looking for nightlife in Stratford-upon-Avon that offer more than the usual options.
