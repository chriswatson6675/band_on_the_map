Discover our venue

Ideal for events, gatherings, or simply a peaceful retreat, the embankment provides various spaces that cater to diverse needs, making it a sought-after destination in the heart of Stratford-upon-Avon.

Perfect venue for any occasion

The Embankment’s Stratford venue gorgeous spaces are spread across two glass-fronted storeys close to River Avon. The open-plan design makes all sorts of events possible, from breakfast meetings onwards, intimate weddings to large corporate canapé events and everything in between, our restaurant and bar can accommodate your reservation large or small gatherings there’s a dedicated semi-private area for more secluded dinners. Menus are hearty and casual; cocktails are a house speciality.
