FUSEBOX

A multi-arts space for young people where ideas and imaginations are sparked and dreams realised.

FUSEBOX is a creative playground in Kingston, a place for young people to produce work, try new things, learn, share and develop their talents.

The venue is available for hire and offers a flexible, open-plan space steeped in local heritage. It houses the footings of the original Kingston Bridge and a medieval Undercroft.

ABOUT US

FUSEBOX IS BACK

After more than a year of closure following the devastating floods of 2025, we are delighted to announce that FUSEBOX is reopening.

Over the past year, we’ve worked closely with our venue partners to recover, rebuild and prepare the space for its next chapter. This milestone would not have been possible without the support of our artists, hirers, audiences, partners and wider community. Thank you to everyone who has stood with us throughout this journey.

FUSEBOX has always been a place for creativity, connection and community, and we can’t wait to welcome you back through our doors.

Explore our upcoming events and join us as we begin this new chapter.

ABOUT FUSEBOX

FUSEBOX is a place for young people, emerging artists, key academic partners, and community organisations locally, nationally and internationally to come together. With innovation, diversity and inclusion at its heart, it is a multi-functional space, with areas for performance, exhibitions, creative practice, socialising, co-creation and more.

Venue hire

FUSEBOX is available for hire in its entirety as an open-plan venue, or has two exhibition spaces surrounded by the historic Kingston Bridge. The space also hosts BOX Bar & Café.
