About us

We are an independent, heritage theatre, situated at the heart of the English Riviera, in the beautiful, vibrant, historic fishing port of Brixham. We offer a year-round programme of events to cater for all tastes these include a wide variety of live music performances, stage plays, musicals and one-man shows by local performers and touring theatrical groups.
