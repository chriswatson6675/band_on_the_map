Welcome to The

OCULIST

TL;DR Oculist is a cocktail bar, rooftop terrace and late-night DJ venue in central Brighton.

Brighton’s bar

for electronic

music lovers.

We back the beats that matter: UK garage, house, disco and techno. Every weekend, Oculist brings together local favourites, rising selectors, headline guests, underground collectives and queer nightlife talent in the heart of Brighton.

If you’re chasing late-night energy, raw dancefloor moments and carefully curated electronic sets, this is your spot.

DRINK. DANCE. DISCONNECT.


Check our UPCOMING EVENTS to scope the line-ups and plan your next night out on Brighton’s sunniest rooftop.

Why come here?

ELECTRONIC MUSIC EVERY WEEKEND
» UK garage, house, disco, techno and hybrid sets built for late nights.

DJs, GUESTS & COLLECTIVES
» Local favourites, STUDENT NIGHTS, headline selectors and underground collectives rotating through the calendar.

QUEER-INCLUSIVE NIGHTLIFE
» A safe space for LGBTQIA+ crew and allies, with queer-led nights that recentre the girls, the gays, the bis and theys.

TWO-TIER ROOF TERRACE
» A heated ROOFTOP TERRACE with suntrap seating, open-air space and somewhere to reset between sets.

COCKTAILS, BEERS & SPIRITS
» Late-night COCKTAILS, cold beers, house pours and back-bar favourites to keep the night moving.

CENTRAL BRIGHTON LOCATION
» Tucked into Boyce’s Street, close to the Lanes, the seafront, student routes and the city’s nightlife circuit.

What to expect:

Late doors, weekend DJs and sets running into the early hours.

A sound-focused dance floor downstairs and a rooftop terrace made for dancing, smoking, chatting and catching air between tracks.

Inclusive, student-friendly nights with DRINK DEALS, familiar hooks and underground heat.

Fresh event listings, guest DJs and queer collectives announced regularly.
