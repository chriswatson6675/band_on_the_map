Photoworks Festival x Queer Heritage South present: Queer History Clubbing: framing the night, making a scene

  • Fri, 2 Oct 2026 20:00 Sat, 3 Oct 2026 01:00
  • Oculist (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

Join Queer Heritage South and Photoworks for a one-off immersive, queer club night , as we launch Photoworks Festival.

Discover the queer clubbing scenes of the past, expect a kaleidoscope of images; queer ravers, daring outfits, cubical carnage, secret snogs and memories of steamy nights.

We’ll be joined by four legendary club photographers, and a line-up of DJs to create a clubnight transporting us through decades of sound and vision, from queer clubbing in Brighton & Hove across the last forty years.

Dance the night away with Xan (Gal Pals) and Dykes on Decks. Visuals from LadyPat and Boogaloo Stu, Alonglines Photography, and Real Brighton.

Our last QHS party marked the launch of the Gender Stories exhibition at Brighton & Hove Museum in February 2026. One sold-out night, over 500 queers, and a plethora of special guests ensured a night for the history books! Don’t miss our next party as Queer History Clubbing:  framing the night, making a scene is coming.
Book your ticket today to avoid missing out!

Queer Heritage South is a project powered by Marlborough Productions. Made possible by The National Lottery Heritage Fund.

Photoworks is the UK’s leading development organisation for photography that champions photography for everyone.  An international platform, global in reach, Photoworks has provided opportunities for artists and audiences since 1995. Photoworks is a registered charity and our work is supported by public funding through Arts Council England’s National Portfolio.

Tickets: https://www.ticketsource.com/marlborough-theatre-productions-ltd/queer-history-clubbing-framing-the-night-making-a-scene/e-vgmear
