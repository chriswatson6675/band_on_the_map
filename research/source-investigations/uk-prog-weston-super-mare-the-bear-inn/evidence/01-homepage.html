    The Bear Inn was opened in 2012 and has since undergone much refurbishment, making it a pleasant & comfortable place to be . On top of a friendly Pub ,The Bear Inn has en-suite rooms all tastefully decorated.

    The Bear has two Bars, a Skittle Alley, and front and rear covered smoking areas. A walkable distance from the Town centre and Pier and just two minutes from the Sea front .

    Our bar is dog friendly and your dog will be made more than welcome .

    The Bear Inn accomdation is ideal for both holiday makers and contractors working away from home. We also have a selection of dog friendly rooms enabling you to take a break with your dog at the Seaside
