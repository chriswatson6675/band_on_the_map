A people’s palace

Welcome to one of the UK’s oldest concert halls. Our stunning Victorian Concert Hall was built as a place where the communities of Bradford could gather to be inspired by the world’s best music and entertainment.

Audiences continue to be treated to a wide variety of events, from top comedians to wonderful family shows, fascinating talks and of course great music concerts. With world class acoustics, St George’s Hall hosts a classical music season every year welcoming world-renowned orchestras, including the Hallé to the stage.

Getting Here

St George's Hall is located in the heart of the city centre, and is easily accessible via car or public transport.

For information about accessible travel, visit our access page.

For driving directions, use the postcode BD1 1JT. You can find live information about congestion and road closures via the Causeway website.

There are several car parks in the city centre within close walking distance. These include The Broadway Shopping Centre; Crown Court; Jacob's Well; Kirkgate Shopping Centre (daytime only); Leisure Exchange; Ocean Parking Southgate; Oastler Centre; Sharpe Street (behind the National Science & Media Museum).

The car parks in the city centre can be very busy during the daytime – please allow a little extra time to make your journey to the venue.

Disabled parking for Blue Badge holders is available in front of the main entrance of The Queen's Hotel on Bridge Street.

St George's Hall is a short walk from the Interchange and Forster Square train station, and various bus stops.

To find out about train times, please go to West Yorkshire Metro or Trainline.

Belongings & Security

Your tickets will be checked when you enter the venue. We conduct bag searches before shows, for the safety and security of customers and staff. Please avoid bringing rucksacks and suitcases to the venue and please allow enough time before shows. We do not have any onsite cloakrooms.

Seating and Access

Bradford Theatres and Bradford Council are committed to ensuring all customers can participate and enjoy Bradford Theatres events equally. For customers who need someone to be with them to access the service or facility at Bradford Theatres, there is an Access Scheme in operation. Membership is free.

At Bradford Theatres we aim to make our venues fully accessible, and we offer an extensive range of access facilities to ensure an easy and enjoyable experience for all our customers.

Find out more about the type of assisted performances we offer on our access page.

There are 12 wheelchair spaces at St George's Hall. There is a drop off point in the layby outside the venue with easy access to the ramp to Stalls level. Unfortunately, due to the layout of our wheelchair accessible spaces, we are not able to accommodate scooters and other vehicles designed for outdoor use. Please let us know your requirements at the time of booking. Wheelchair accessible toilets are available.

For customers with physical access needs coming to St George’s Hall please note there is no lift access to the Dress Circle and Grand Tier. The lift only operates to the Stalls Level.

Our printed brochures are available in large print, Braille and on audio CD. Programmes for some shows can be made available in large print, Braille and on audio CD but you will need to ask us for them at least two weeks before the performance you want to come to.

Get in touch with us to request printed materials via our online enquiry form or call us on 01274 432375. 

Please note, for some shows rows A to E in the stalls will be removed.

Access Scheme

Access Scheme members are entitled to a free Essential Companion ticket, and are eligible to receive access to best position seats for our Access performances, including wheelchair spaces.

Our Sister Venues

Alhambra Theatre

Often said to be the jewel in the crown of the city of Bradford, the Alhambra Theatre is regarded as the North's premier receiving venue, and has been delighting audiences for over 100 years with an exciting mix of musicals, drama and dance.

King's Hall & Winter Garden

Located in the heart of the picturesque town of Ilkley, this venue hosts a wide range of theatre events including family shows, music concerts and talks.

The Studio

The Studio is a flexible small theatre and rehearsal space adjacent to the Alhambra Theatre. This intimate setting is popular with both established and up-and-coming comedians.
