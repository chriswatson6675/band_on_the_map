        Make Your Night Special

        Every show here is a night out. Some can be something more — the best seats in the house, a private box above the stalls, and the extras arranged before you even arrive.

        VIP Packages

        Available on many of the shows in our programme, a VIP package turns a ticket into an occasion — premium seats and extras arranged in advance, so there’s nothing to organise on the night.

        Look for the VIP star on any show

        The Royal Box

        A private box with its own view over the fourth-largest stage in Scotland. Ideal for anniversaries, birthdays and small groups who’d rather have the evening to themselves.

        Look for the Royal crown on any show
