Workshop

Yin Sound Plant

Wed 7th Oct

A unique yin yoga, sound and plant experience rooted in presence, ritual and rest.This immersive offering combines yin yoga with therapeutic sound and plant energetics to honour the season — a moment of fullness, light and reflection, as the annual Draconids meteor shower reaches its peak. Together, we’ll surrender and soften into stillness, creating space to restore, reset and connect our inner and outer landscapes, supporting a state of internal coherence.

Singing Workshop with Katy Rose Bennet

Sat 10th Oct

Join Katy Rose Bennett for a day of glorious a-cappella harmony singing in the lovely Rook Lane Chapel, Frome Katy will be bringing a load of her own a cappella songs and arrangements which she'll lead in her unique, energetic and infectious teaching style! Katys songs are very popular in community choirs across the UK and beyond.  It'll be fun, relaxed and engaging. You'll leave with songs and harmonies stuck in your heads for a good few weeks!

Music

Frome Concerts Group present: CarmenCo

Sun 11th Oct

CarmenCo’s new show, "My My My Delilah!" will be ready in Autumn 2026, and already has promoters queueing up to book it after the success of their “A Pocket Opera” which was hugely popular when it came to Rook Lane Chapel in 2024. 70% music and 30% spoken word, My My My Delilah! is a riotous concert-play in which the Bible's most notorious femme fatale finally gets to tell her side of the story. What really motivated her betrayal of Samson? Was she chasing fame, settling scores, or simply fed up with all that hair?
