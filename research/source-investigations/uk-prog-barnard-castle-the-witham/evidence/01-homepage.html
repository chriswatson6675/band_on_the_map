All what's on

Something for everyone in Barnard Castle

For more than 150 years, The Witham has been at the heart of Barnard Castle and Teesdale's cultural and community life. We offer all sorts of events – theatre, music, art, dance, film, comedy and classes, in our beautifully refurbished home, that began life as an educational institute, a dispensary, a library and a music hall.

Our café bar and garden are pleasant spaces in which to socialise and meet people. Or you could come in and browse in our box office and shop. And, if you want to hire a venue for a celebration or a party, a market, a memorial, or a meeting – The Witham has lots of great spaces to offer you. Please contact our friendly team to discuss your requirements.

  • Shop & Box Office

    Whether you’re choosing a gift, buying a ticket for a Witham event or wanting information on local attractions you can be sure of a warm welcome in The Witham shop.

  • The Witham Café Bar

    The Witham Café Bar is a warm, friendly place in the award-winning modern atrium that bridges our two older buildings. Licensed day and evening, we serve locally sourced, seasonal lunches and suppers, full afternoon tea, home-baked cakes and scones, as well as evening drinks, snacks and ice cream.

  • Volunteer with us

    Come and join our team of wonderful volunteers to help us sell tickets, usher at events and generally keep the cogs at The Witham well oiled.
