Welcome to Blanco's Hotel & Restaurant

Luxury Hotel & Restaurant in Port Talbot

Experience the Style, Comfort, and Charm of Blanco’s Hotel & Restaurant

Step into Blanco’s Hotel & Restaurant in Port Talbot – where warm hospitality meets modern luxury. From the moment you arrive, you’ll feel the difference. Whether you're here for business, pleasure, or a bit of both, we’ve created a space that goes beyond just a place to stay — it’s a place to relax, recharge, and feel right at home.

Our 63 spacious rooms are designed with comfort in mind — calm tones, soft lighting, and plush furnishings that invite you to switch off and unwind. Think crisp white linens, cloud-like beds, and sleek bathrooms stocked with fluffy towels and quality touches that make all the difference.

Looking to treat yourself? Our five deluxe suites take it up a notch. Each one is individually styled to offer something a little special — more space, more comfort, more indulgence. It’s your own private escape, with thoughtful extras and the kind of personal service that makes you feel looked after from check-in to check-out.

Located right in the heart of Port Talbot, Blanco’s is perfectly placed for exploring South Wales. We’re just two minutes from Junction 41 of the M4, five minutes from Junction 40, and only 15 minutes from Swansea city centre. Whether you're visiting for a weekend getaway, a special event, or a business trip, everything you need is close by — including our on-site restaurant and bar, which serves up great food and drinks in a relaxed, stylish setting.

At Blanco’s Hotel & Restaurant, we don’t just offer a stay — we offer an experience. Comfortable. Sophisticated. Effortlessly welcoming. Come and see for yourself.
