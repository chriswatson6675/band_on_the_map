Fakermaker

October 16 @ 8:00 pm - 10:00 pm
£10.00 – £15.00
  • « Goole Connect | Growing Visibility: Marketing Your Business for Success

Venue: Goole Market Hall, 25 Stanley St, Goole DN14 5AB

Back in 1991, somewhere over the Pennines in deepest, darkest Lancashire, the band known as “Rain” evaporated into the ether and out of that residue, Oasis formed. Seven no.1 albums, 8 no.1 singles and 18 years later, the whirlwind that was Oasis, finally dwindled and died.

During this time, a silver cloud had formed and had transgressed the arduous journey over the Pennines, making its way to the holy ground of West Yorkshire. Here, the resulting cloudburst nourished the seeds of Rock ‘n’ Roll and as Spring, sprung, in early 2018, these precocious white roses began to blossom and formed West Yorkshire’s most successful Oasis Tribute band.

Fakermaker recreate the sights, sounds, atmosphere, and to some extent, the smells of 1995 to give the crowd what they want, which is a killer Oasis tribute, with enough decibels to leave your head ringing like a ghost train!
Despite Liam’s twitter rants, we head into the ‘20s with no sign of an Oasis reunion on the horizon, so until then, Fakermaker are the closest thing you can get to seeing them live.

Early bird tickets are available up to 18th August at a discounted rate of £10, standard price is £15.
14+ gig only.
