Springfields Events Centre

Springfields Events Centre is ideally situated on the A16 on the outskirts of Spalding, Lincolnshire, adjacent to Springfields Designer Outlet.

The Centre features three multi-functional exhibition spaces and seminar rooms, with a five-acre outdoor show ground and parking on site. It is available for hire throughout the year with a choice of catering to suit individual needs.

A list of events take place at the Exhibition Centre can be found on their dedicated website.
