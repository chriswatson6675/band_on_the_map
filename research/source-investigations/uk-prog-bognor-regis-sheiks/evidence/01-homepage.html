Welcome to Bognor Pier

The Pier Bognor is one of West Sussex’s best-loved entertainment venues. Come and join us to eat, drink, play, celebrate and make some wonderful memories!

Bring your friends, bring your family, bring your kids - there’s something for everyone at the Pier Bognor.

Five outlets under one roof

Grab a coffee at the Café at the Pier or a cocktail at the Beachside Brew, enjoy live entertainment at Venue 24 or try your luck in the arcade – there’s always something happening at the Pier.

 

Things to do in Bognor when it’s raining

All our attractions are indoors, making the Pier one of the top things to do in Bognor when it’s raining. Stay safe and dry indoors with a warming coffee or a pick-me-up cocktail. Beat your high score at the arcade or get yourself a ticket for Venue 24’s next event. And when the skies are clear again, take a stroll down the historic Pier and enjoy a fabulous view of the sea.

Whether you join us for just an hour or spend the whole day here, you can depend on a warm welcome and loads of fun.
