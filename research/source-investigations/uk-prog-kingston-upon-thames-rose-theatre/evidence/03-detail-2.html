Tickets for Schools

Inspire your students with an unforgettable trip to Rose Theatre. Our exciting programme offers something for every age group, from family-friendly favourites and curriculum-linked productions to the enchantment of our much-loved annual Christmas show. Come and share the joy of live performance!

 

Ticket offers for schools

Groups of 10 or more enjoy generous discounts on selected shows, call the Box Office for the full details on 020 8174 0090.

Schools, colleges and universities can get tickets from £15 on selected performances for groups of 10 or more and an 11th teacher ticket is free for every 10 purchased.

 

How to book

Use our booking form below to request your tickets:

School booking form

If you need any assistance, our friendly box office team will be happy to help! You can contact us directly on roserequests@rosetheatre.org or 020 8174 0090.

Once your tickets have been booked, you will receive an invoice with the full details and a payment deadline. Any amends to your booking can be made before this date.

 

Rose Routes Scheme

Free Theatre Tickets & Workshops for Students! Do you work with young people who would benefit from theatre experiences or creative workshops, but face financial barriers? The Rose Routes Scheme is here to help. Find out more here.

 

FAQs

Is parking available?

There are coach drop off points opposite the theatre and plenty of car parks nearby. Please refer to our Getting Here page for more information about transport links and parking.

Can our students eat their packed lunch at the theatre?

Unfortunately, we don’t have a designated space for students to eat lunch at the theatre and we don’t allow food in the auditorium. We’re very lucky to be close to lots of lovely outdoor space, including the Kingston riverside. If it’s a sunny day, you will be able to find somewhere to eat with a great view!

Can we order ice-creams in advance?

Absolutely! Pre-order ice-cream here: Ice Cream Pre-Order Form

All ice-creams are gluten free and come in a range of flavours. Please place your order 7 working days before your visit.

What happens if we’re running late?

Please call our box office team on 020 8174 0090 to let them know. Each show is different, so admittance of latecomers is at the discretion of venue. We advise schools parties to aim to arrive 20 minutes before the start of the performance.

Do you offer accessible performances?

Relaxed, audio described and captioned performances are available for selected performances. Please contact the box office team to find out what’s available. You can find out more information about our access provision, including a visual story, here.
