Venue Policies

Safe. Legal. Responsible.

01 — Age & Entry

Strictly 18+ Only

The Palace operates a strict 18+ entry policy. Valid photographic ID is required at all times.

  • Passport
  • UK Driving Licence
  • PASS-approved ID card

We operate a Challenge 25 policy. Entry may be refused at management's discretion.

02 — Alcohol

Venue & Alcohol Policy

The Palace is a licensed premises operating in full accordance with UK licensing laws.

  • Alcohol served to 18+ only
  • We promote responsible drinking at all times
  • Service may be refused to anyone intoxicated
  • Anti-social behaviour may result in removal

Our team reserves the right to refuse service in line with responsible licensing and customer safety.

03 — Drugs

Zero Tolerance Policy

The Palace operates a zero-tolerance policy towards drugs of any kind.

  • Possession, use or supply results in immediate removal
  • Relevant authorities notified where appropriate
  • Searches conducted as a condition of entry

Our priority is a safe environment for every guest.
