OUR SPACES

The Globe Theatre

Throughout the summer season, the Globe is home to multiple productions and performances with audiences up to 1,500. The most recognisable and largest of our spaces, the theatre may be hired for filming and photography as well as private hires and celebrations.

Sam Wanamaker Playhouse

One of the only candlelit theatres in Europe, the Sam Wanamaker Playhouse is the perfect setting to host an unforgettable event. The space can host 200 guests for lectures, presentations, and corporate events.

The Education Studios

The Education Studios are located on 1-2 Bear Gardens, SE1 9ED, perfectly situated just around the corner from our theatres. The studios have a range of spaces suitable for smaller events of up to 35 people and can accommodate meetings, workshops and away days. The Perry Studio is the largest of the spaces and can hold 80 people.

Upper Foyer

This semi-private space is perfect for a relaxed drinks reception of up to 60 people. The space can be yours to use exclusively for events or for group visits to performances.

Main Foyer pictured

Wolfson Lecture Theatre and Weston Foyer

Our newly renovated Wolfson Lecture Theatre can seat up to 120 people and is perfect for smaller, private presentations and lectures.

The Weston Foyer, situated just outside, is available to hire alongside for receptions, as a breakout space or for welcoming guests. Both rooms have step-free access.

BESPOKE EVENTS

Tours

Treat your attendees to a private tour of Shakespeare’s Globe, where they will discover the stories and secrets of our open-air theatre – how the Globe battled to survive plague, fire and political oppression, and how one man’s vision saw it rise again after 400 years.

Historical Demonstrations

Enjoy dynamic demonstrations on costume design and sword fighting from our specialists. Whether as part of a schedule of activities or a one-off experience, our in-house experts can organise demonstrations specially tailored for your group.

Presentations and Panel Events

Whether hiring our spaces to deliver your own talk or presentation or arranging for your guests to hear from the Globe’s world-renowned experts, a panel event is a great way to wow your guests, bringing the magic of the Globe directly to them.

Private Box Hire

Our exclusive boxes are the perfect way to entertain an intimate group of guests for a memorable night at the theatre, including delicious seasonal private dining at Swan London.

Performance

If you’re looking to really impress your guests, why not curate an unforgettable event for your attendees, centred around one of our critically acclaimed productions.

Workshops and Team Building

Whether you want an ice-breaker at the start of an away day or something to get everyone up on their feet after a long meeting, our tailored workshops provide an interactive and thought-provoking link between the world of Shakespeare and your team. Find out more about our Executive Training Programme, Centre Stage.

Spaces at The Swan

The team at Swan at the Globe have a wealth of experience in delivering stylish evening events and functions for any occasion. They have several private dining spaces available for hire, including the beautiful Balcony Room overlooking the Thames, and the Underglobe.
