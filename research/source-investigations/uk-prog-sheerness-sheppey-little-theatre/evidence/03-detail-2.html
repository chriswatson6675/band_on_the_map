Biffa awards supports theatre

Biffa Awards have granted Sheppey Entertainments Association £6,194 to install a new energy efficient boiler in the theatre. The old boiler was over 40 years old. The new boiler is more efficient and helped us to both cut our fuel bills and our carbon footprint.
