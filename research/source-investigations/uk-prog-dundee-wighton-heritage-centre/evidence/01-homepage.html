Looking for sport, leisure, or wellbeing services?

Community sport, leisure, and wellbeing services are delivered by Active Dundee.

Active Dundee

Active Dundee is part of Leisure & Culture Dundee, a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation (SC042421). They manage 21 public facilities on behalf of Dundee City Council, including gyms, swimming pools, wellbeing programmes, and leisure spaces. Their mission is to help people discover the joy of movement, connection, and relaxation.
