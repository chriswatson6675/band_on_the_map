Welcome To Club Veeda

Here at Club Veeda our intention is simple, we aim to bring a new dimension of clubbing to the city and transform nights out for good without making a dent in your pocket!

The venue has undergone a multi-million pound refit to ensure that all 3 floors come complete with a vogue like stylishness offering comfort and elegance throughout.

With 3 floors of music, there really is something for everyone. Club Veeda offers the opportunity for you to grab your friends and dance the night away, celebrate in style in one of our VIP booths or sit down to have a drink and a chat in contemporary surroundings.
