About Us

The Seagull is a small not for profit community theatre in Pakefield, Lowestoft, which plays host to a regular stream of local talent, regional and national touring theatre companies and a vibrant selection of classes and community events.

The Seagull is run by volunteers, supported by  three permanent staff members. Please contact us anytime and our dedicated team of volunteers will endeavour to assist you with any queries and ideas that you may have. The Seagull is a community venue working with and for you, to ensure this vital resource continues to thrive and contribute quality programming, facilities and opportunities to the area..

If you wish to volunteer at the theatre, please contact us using the form on the side of this page or pop in for a chat.  We offer opportunities across the whole business from technical operations and administration through to set building or front of house duties..
