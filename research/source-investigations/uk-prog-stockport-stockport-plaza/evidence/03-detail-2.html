For many years the Plaza has played host to those special occasions which have ensured that she is at the very heart of her local community; from the very best in cinematic and theatrical productions, to fine dining, private parties, wedding celebrations and conferences.

Incorporating our auditorium, stage and Plaza Café all of which have been restored to their 1932 splendour, you can be sure to wow your guests in a unique and awe inspiring venue.

Packages for your event are always bespoke to enhance your ideas so please do not hesitate to contact us to discuss your event.

To find out more contact Ted on 0161 480 3818 or email ted.doan@stockportplaza.co.uk. Alternatively complete the form below and we will get in touch asap.
