Welcome to The Concorde, the South's premier entertainment venue. Our diverse entertainment programme offers a range of artistes and music styles; with restaurant and on-site Hotel accommodation it's the perfect package for an entertainment break.

The Concorde is also home to The Moldy Fig bar and restaurant which is open all day and evening. It boasts a split level terrace overlooking the beautiful Concorde garden with the lower terrace providing tranquil seating by the Monks Brook river.

About The Concorde

PHONEYFEST 2027 TICKETS NOW AVAILABLE

The Concorde is a lively entertainment venue in Eastleigh, near Southampton, with easy access just off junction 5 of the M27. Established in 1957, its heritage includes performances from legendary jazz artists, shaping the welcoming atmosphere it’s known for today.

More than just a music venue, The Concorde offers a full experience. The Moldy Fig bar and restaurant serves delicious home-cooked food all day, while the main club features its own bar and restaurant, perfect for enjoying a meal alongside live entertainment. Guests can also extend their visit with a stay at Ellington Lodge and enjoy a leisurely breakfast in The Moldy Fig the following morning. 

Set within beautiful grounds, the venue includes a large, heated marquee ideal for weddings, parties and corporate events. A versatile destination for entertainment, dining and special occasions, all in one place.

While no longer a members-only club, Concorde Membership offers exclusive benefits, including discounts on dining, entertainment, and accommodation.
