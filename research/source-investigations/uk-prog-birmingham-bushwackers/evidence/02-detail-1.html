Birmingham comedy listings and tickets

Thinking of checking out a comedy night in Birmingham? Fantastic idea! Birmingham and the Midlands in general have spawned a wealth of notoriously funny comedians, from Frank Skinner to Tony Hancock, the second city has a thriving comic circuit that rivals the likes of Manchester, Edinburgh and London. Venues like Glee Club Birmingham Comedy Club And Music Venue and Hamilton House showcase up and coming talent, whilst places like the Civic Hall in nearby Wolverhampton showcase some of the big hitters in the comedy world. 

Whether you’re looking for a free comedy night, improv, the most exciting new names on the circuit or the established comics who know how to entice howls of laughter that leave you clapping like a seal, our Birmingham stand up comedy events can sort you out.

So have a browse through Birmingham’s comedy nights that’ll leave your sides aching like you’ve done an hour at the gym, tears rolling down your cheeks and a big cheesy grin on your face. We know you want to.
