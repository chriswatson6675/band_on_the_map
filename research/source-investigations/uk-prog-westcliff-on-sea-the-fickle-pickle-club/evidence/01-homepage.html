About The Fickle Pickle Club

Located at 228 London Road (next to the Cricketers pub) in Westcliff-on-Sea, Essex, The Fickle Pickle Club is a vibrant entertainment venue steeped in musical history. Once a stage for legends like David Bowie, Status Quo and Thin Lizzy, it now hosts a variety of events, including live music, intimate theatre, and private parties.

With a capacity of 250 and a cozy atmosphere, it's the perfect spot for unforgettable nights out. Whether you're enjoying bands, tribute acts, comedians or celebrating with friends, The Fickle Pickle brings together the best of retro charm and modern entertainment.
