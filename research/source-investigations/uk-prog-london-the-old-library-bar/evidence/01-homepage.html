    The Old Library

    BAR & VENUE

    It’s what we’ve all been waiting for in New Cross Gate – a quality place to meet, drink and listen to live music - we’re open Monday-Saturday 5pm-11pm & Sunday 1pm-9pm.

    ​

    The Old Library Bar, lovingly refurbished to bring our character historic building to life is open all week to the public including our rehearsing bands, and for live music (usually Friday, Saturday and Sunday) offering:

     

    - Unique Edwardian building, wooden tables under our planted dome 

    - Locally brewed craft ales

    - Select wine from Davys, our local wine merchant

    - Quality cocktails & a wide variety of alcohol-free and soft drinks.

    - Live music and events on the weekend

    - Secret planted beer garden festooned under the vines

    Our goal - to offer a refined quality space for socialising, meeting and spending time with friends old and new, enjoying local beers/wine and live music.
