The Barn will no longer be running cafe days on Saturdays. Instead, we will be open for music club and bands on Friday and Saturday evenings. Watch this space for future events and party nights!

The Barn is an historic, uniquely independent venue. An eclectic mix of music, art, free speech and conversation.

Located in the historic town of Ledbury, The Barn sits among other historic buildings – nestled in the back corner of St Katherine’s and part of our new town square. Our style is open, relaxed, friendly and inclusive. The Barn is a Music and arts centre, involving Many genres and generations, of both Music and Art. To enrich your experience, we offer home-made Street food complementing the local Ales and fine wine from our local shop, local ciders and a few choice cocktails.

We have been complimented on the ambience and fun created by our amazing speakers, artists and musicians who frequently visit! Come and enjoy our Farmers Market, which is just starting up again on Saturdays, beginning April 12th 2025. A place for great conversation, great listening, and if you’re lucky, a good dance!
