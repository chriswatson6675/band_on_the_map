About The Henrician

The Henrician (traditionally known as Evesham Arts Centre) is a popular arts theatre based in Evesham.

We host a diverse collection of community and professional events throughout the year. We have two venues, the main auditorium seats 312, and the smaller studio theatre has seating for around 60 persons (depending on seating arrangement). In addition, the theatre foyer also hosts events and exhibitions. We have a brand new hand-made bar serving pre-show and interval drinks and refreshments.
