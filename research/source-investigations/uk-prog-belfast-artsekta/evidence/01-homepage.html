ArtsEkta

20 YEARS OF CREATIVITY & CONNECTION

ArtsEkta is an award winning social enterprise that transforms communities through shared creative experiences and is home to the Belfast Mela – the largest celebration of cultural diversity on the island of Ireland.

Our Projects

Belfast Mela

Belfast Mela, is one of Northern Ireland’s largest arts festivals bringing together music, dance, arts and food from across the world to celebrate our city’s proud cultural diversity. Mela celebrates it’s 20th birthday in 2026 bursting across Belfast from 22 – 30 August.

Mela View all Projects

Earth Child

Earth Child is a new large scale outdoor theatre spectacle co-created in partnership with Fidget Feet Aerial Dance to celebrate 20 years of ArtsEkta and Belfast Mela. Coming August 2026.

View all Projects

Cork Mela

The inaugural Cork Mela brought thousands of people together in Fitzgerald Park’s for a vibrant afternoon of music, dance, food and cultural experiences from around the world.

Cork Mela View all Projects

Silk Road Supper Club

The Silk Road Supper Club experience takes you on a voyage of culinary discovery through the Silk Road – an ancient trading route bridging East to West.

Silk Road Supper Club View all Projects

Cultural Coach

Cultural Coach is a major outreach initiative providing intercultural arts workshops across Northern Ireland in education and community settings.
