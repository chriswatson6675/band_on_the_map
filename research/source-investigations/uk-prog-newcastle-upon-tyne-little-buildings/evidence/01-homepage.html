Bar

Enquire about our range of 'specality cocktails'!

Our Bar is packed with a good range of Beers, Lagers, Ciders and spirits, Buckfast and now with draught Anarchy Craft ales we should have something that will hit the spot. We also offer a selection of non-alcoholic Beers and Soft Drinks, so we have you covered.

We are extremely proud of our unique 'specality cocktails' for more details just ask our cheerful and 'Interesting' bar staff who have been with us since the early days and they will advise.

Stage

Our Live room holds 55-60 people and offers an intimate setting for new and established artists alike.

Local artists love the vibe the Little Buildings is famous for. We regularly attract Touring Artists and Bands, stopping by for a drink or playing our stage.

We can provide in-house Sound Tech and full Backline. While we promote our own gigs, the venue can be hired for external promoters and DIY Gigs. We also have the ability to record live sets to a very high standard.
