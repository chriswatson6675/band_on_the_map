West Hove Studio

We are a light, airy and spacious art Studio in West Hove. A versatile room, with space for up to 25 people. We have 13 floor standing easels and lots of fold away art/craft tables, an Art Sink, cutting table, print presses, projector & screen and a small kitchen space. Hire the space for many types of creative classes, craft workshops, sewing groups, or even photography.
