UK City of Culture 2029

Where the Extraordinary Belongs to Everyone

Blackpool is bidding to be UK City of Culture 2029. This will transform the nation’s favourite playground into a bold, brilliant celebration of creativity, joy and shared wonder: from large-scale anchor events to vibrant neighbourhood talent, uniting communities and visitors in an experience that is bigger, bolder and open to all.
