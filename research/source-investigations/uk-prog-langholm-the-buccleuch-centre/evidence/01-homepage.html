Welcome to The Buccleuch Centre

See What’s Coming in 2026

Autumn/Winter 2026 SEASON is now on sale online and at the box office.

Just visit the What’s On page and click on your favourite shows to purchase tickets through TicketSource.

The box office at the Centre is open for face-to-face and telephone bookings Mon – Fri 10am – 4pm.

Call 013873 81196

The Buccleuch Centre in Langholm is a modern, fully accessible events venue with automatic doors, no steps and wide open spaces for easy access. Our auditorium has tiered seating with one or more rows of seats on the flat, wheelchair users are always welcome to any of our shows if you can contact us in advance we can be ready for your arrival.

We are fully licensed and have a well stocked bar with a wide range of alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks.
