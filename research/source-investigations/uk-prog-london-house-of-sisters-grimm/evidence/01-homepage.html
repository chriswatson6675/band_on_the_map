Three Experiences. One Ticket.

Witness
INALA

Witness the South African musical phenomenon, INALA — a 55-minute journey of music, dance, and storytelling, at your reserved showtime. A global success story reimagined in its most intimate form.

 

Learn more Book now

iGOLI
Wine bar

Indulge in the UK’s first South African fine wine bar, a fully air-conditioned, temperature-controlled environment. Curated by Michael Fridjhon with Spirits & Bubbles SA, it presents site-specific wines shaped by South Africa’s terroirs.

Learn more

Immersive
Art Gallery

Explore Ella Spira MBE’s South African Collection, a multimedia gallery of works painted in situ across the country. Each canvas captures land, people & cultural landscapes at the heart of INALA.
