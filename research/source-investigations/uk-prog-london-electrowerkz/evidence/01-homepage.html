VENUE hire

Electrowerkz is a late 19th-century industrial warehouse event space and club housed with over 30,000 sq.ft of event space. The former steelworks factory consists of seven unique spaces over five levels. The venue is licensed until 6am daily. Highlights include the Courtyard with its glass atrium ceiling, the dramatic day-lit Penthouse and four club spaces with world-class sound and lighting.
