A Pioneering Cultural & Community Venue

Stage @TheDock

A Pioneering Open Air Venue with culture and community at its heart

 
Stage @TheDock is a stunning multi-purpose venue as unique and ambitious as the city in which it resides. Emerging from the bed of the old dry dock, looking onto the River Hull & Humber Estuary, with wonderful views of The Deep and set in the heart of the thriving Fruit Market area of Hull.

Our impressive 350 capacity venue is a versatile space available for a variety of uses. It provides an atmospheric setting for audiences and an inspirational environment for producers, performers and events organisers.

We attract a diverse range of performances and events. From site specific shows and touring productions, to corporate and community events.The possibilities are endless in this exciting and unique space.
