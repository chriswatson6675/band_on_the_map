Jazz at the Table

"Jazz à Mesa" is a weekly jazz concert series organized by the Porta-Jazz association. Every Tuesday, we showcase talented emerging jazz musicians in improvisational sessions, as well as introducing new projects and collaborations. This is the perfect opportunity for jazz enthusiasts to discover fresh, innovative sounds.
