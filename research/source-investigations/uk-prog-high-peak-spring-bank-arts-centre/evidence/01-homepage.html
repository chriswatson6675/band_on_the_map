Spring bank arts centre

Music, Arts, Drama For the Community


Spring Bank Arts organises a varied programme of live music events, theatre, poetry, comedy, dance, art exhibitions and international films throughout the year.

 

Home to several local community groups, clubs and organisations running a range of activities seven days a week as well as an outreach venue for the Buxton Festival Fringe and base for the New Mills Community Festival. Bringing the arts to the centre of the community.
