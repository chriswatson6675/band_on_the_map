Yoga Sessions at the Lakeside Centre

Event Series (See All)

Saturday 26th September 2026 , 10:00am to 11:00am

Amanda Eatwell returns this summer to teach her much-loved yoga sessions, located on the stunning Lakeside Centre rooftop.

Join us at the Lakeside Centre rooftop on Saturday mornings for a series of uplifting yoga sessions designed to help you start your weekend with intention, movement, and joy.

What’s included in a session?

You can expect a 60-minute flow that is:

  • Dynamic and energising – to wake up your body and clear your mind.
  • Accessible to all levels – whether you’re new to yoga or have an established practice.
  • Intention-led – helping you reconnect with yourself and the season.

Yoga mats are available on-site, but please feel free to bring your own if that is more comfortable. Please bring:

  • Comfortable clothes
  • A water bottle

Dates & Times

Sessions will run on a Saturday morning:

2nd May- 3rd October

Start time: 10:00am. Please arrive 10-15 minutes before the start of the session. Late entry may not be possible due to the layout of the building.

Please note, there are no classes on August 1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, and September 12th.

About Amanda Eatwell

Outside of yoga, Amanda is a fine art and documentary photographer, based in Thamesmead. She has had two solo exhibitions at The Lakeside Centre, and has played an active role in the Thamesmead Festival for the past three years – creating an art installation one year, and helping with production in 2025. 

Amanda has practiced yoga since her twenties, and qualified as a BoxingYoga coach nine years ago. This is where the dynamic yoga class stems from: a unique yoga-based training system that was originally born to help boxers reach new levels of performance through improved core strength, body awareness, balance and flexibility. Amanda provides a friendly and inclusive environment for her classes. 

Amanda is a keen runner, with several marathons under her belt. She can confirm that stretching and strengthening through yoga support her physically and mentally through every aspect of her life! You can find out more about Amanda’s Yoga sessions on her Instagram account @amandaeatwelldoesyoga
