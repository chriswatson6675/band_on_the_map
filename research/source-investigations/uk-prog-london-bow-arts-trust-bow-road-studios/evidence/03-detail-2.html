Artist Educator CPD: Stop-Motion Animation Techniques with Reza Ben Gajra

Friday 25th September 2026 , 5:30pm to 7:30pm

Join Reza Ben Gajra who has extensive experience delivering animation projects in schools across London for this hands-on animation workshop. You’ll learn tips and tricks for delivering a stop-motion animation project in an education or community setting, including any equipment you may need and where to source these. 

What is the Artist Educators’ CPD programme?

Organised by Bow Arts Learning, the artist educators CPD program is a series of sessions offered to Bow Arts Educators as a form of skill sharing and network-building. Bow Arts artist educators are a freelance pool of educators who work on schools’ projects across London, mostly in east and south-east London. This programme of professional development has been running successfully for the past 10 years.

The programme consists of:

  • Skills-share sessions, where artists share skills from their own practice with each other.
  • The Happening Now programme, where artists facilitate sessions around what is happening nationally and globally in current affairs and how this may be affecting young people, their families and teachers.
  • Training delivered by external providers such as art psychotherapists and organisations that can share insight about working with children who have experienced trauma.
  • Bow Arts’ annual artist educators’ retreat – a two-day event of workshops, talks and hands on activities exploring the boundaries of art teaching in the classroom and beyond.
  • We are delighted to be able to extend this workshop beyond our artists educator community welcoming anyone with an interest or curiosity of how a learning environment can complement your own practice.

This session is ‘pay what you can’ with a suggested donation of £7 towards materials and costs. This event will be held in the Bow Arts Courtyard Room at 181 Bow Road.

More about Reza Ben Gajra

Reza Ben Gajra is a British artist. He was born in 1962 and lives in London. Reza facilitates art & animation workshops for children, families & adults. Reza also leads interactive cine-literacy cinema sessions at the BFI. 

Access information     

The Bow Arts Trust offices have step-free access throughout from street level, including to the accessible toilet, and is service animal friendly. This venue does not have a hearing loop system. Accessible parking is not available on-site. 

If you have any questions regarding accessibility at this venue or event, would like to make us aware of any access requirements that you have in advance of visiting, or would like this information in an alternate format including Easy Read, please email learning@bowarts.com or call 020 8980 7774 (Ext. 3)   

Access requirements could include things like providing equipment, services or support (e.g. information in Easy Read, speech to text software, additional 1:1 support), adjusting workshop timings (e.g. more break times), adjustments to the event space (e.g. making sure you have a seat near the entrance) or anything else you can think of!
