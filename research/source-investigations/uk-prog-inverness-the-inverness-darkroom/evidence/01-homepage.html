The Inverness Darkroom ✶

Registered Scottish Charity No. SCO53487

📷

Inverness Darkroom is a community black and white darkroom based in Wasps Inverness Creative Academy in the centre of Inverness.

As well as being a community resource we offer workshops on photography and other more experimental photography techniques, Please visit the workshop page to see what’s coming up.

Tuesday nights, 7-9pm each week come along to a supervised darkroom session with Matt Sillars. Any level of darkroom skill welcome, including those in need of a refresher, or just starting out. These sessions are for sharing tips and tricks and getting feedback on your photographs and prints. Social and productive! For this session a £2 donation gratefully accepted going towards darkroom chemistry. Paper will be available to purchase – by the sheet!! Note: You must be a current member of the Inverness Darkroom.

If you’d like to join the Inverness Darkroom, please see information below and fill out the form at the bottom of the page 😊

How to join!

Induction – £10 one-off fee 

All new members are required to attend an hour-long induction to health and safety and basic operating procedures for the dakroom before they use the space.

To arrange an induction please email theinvernessdarkroom@gmail.com

(Induction fees will be waived for new members who have taken an Introduction workshop)

Membership fee 

Yearly membership is £120 payable annually (or Monthly instalments of £10 by arrangement)

A month-long pass of £40 can be purchased for visiting photographers

Payments can be made through BACS payment. 

Please email for details or complete the form at the bottom of the page.

No cash payments are possible.

Gift memberships and vouchers are also available – please email us to arrange.

Key Deposit – £10 (refundable)

Members are given a Key pendant to have 24/7 access to the building
