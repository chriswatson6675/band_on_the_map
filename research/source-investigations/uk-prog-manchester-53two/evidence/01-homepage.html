    WHAT'S ON

    More than just a theatre

    53two exists to make the performing arts more accessible, inclusive and possible for people who face barriers to participation and creative careers. We provide opportunities, space, skills, support and platforms that build confidence, develop artists and companies, and enable new voices and stories to reach audiences.

     

    53two’s a unique theatre and event space found in the railway arches of Manchester City Centre.

    We’re a registered charity too, striving to make quality, professional, accessible theatre.

    We host plays, concerts, weddings, corporate events… everything.

    Nestled in the heart of Manchester, the theatre has built a reputation for championing bold new work, emerging artists and diverse voices. Our intimate setting creates a unique connection between performers and audiences, making every show feel personal and immediate. Whether you're stepping onto the stage for the first time or returning with a new production, we offer a welcoming, creative environment that celebrates storytelling, collaboration and the vibrant spirit of independent theatre.
