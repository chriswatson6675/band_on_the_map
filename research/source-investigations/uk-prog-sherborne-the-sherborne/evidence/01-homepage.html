Our charitable mission & vision

At The Sherborne, we bring heritage to life. We protect the stories, architecture, and traditions that make this place unique, while championing bold cultural experiences that inspire, challenge, and connect. Every exhibition, performance, and community programme reflects our commitment to honour the past while embracing the present.

We aspire to be a cultural beacon for the South West, where history is a living dialogue, creativity thrives, and community flourishes. As a registered charity, every ticket purchased, donation made, or coffee enjoyed helps safeguard The Sherborne’s heritage and nurture the culture of tomorrow. Join us and be part of a vibrant, inclusive cultural future.

Eat at The Sherborne

Food and drink are woven into the rhythm of the day at The Sherborne - from morning coffee, freshly baked treats and nourishing lunches to seasonal plates, elegant dinners and unhurried drinks.

The Pavilion Café is our relaxed daytime space, while the Restaurant brings together carefully sourced ingredients, beautifully simple dishes, wines and signature cocktails.

    Terraces, lawns, and sculpture walks invite quiet reflection and unexpected encounters with art and nature.

    Rotating displays bring new perspectives and playful imagination to The Sherborne’s timeless spaces.

  • The Sherborne & Pavilion Café

    Free entry

    The Restaurant

    Dinner Wednesday–Saturday

  • At The Sherborne, we’re committed to making every visit welcoming and accessible. Our galleries, café, and facilities are fully wheelchair accessible, with step-free entry throughout the site. We offer designated disabled parking, accessible toilets, and comfortable seating areas, and assistance dogs are warmly welcomed.

    If you have specific access needs or would like support planning your visit, please contact our team in advance — we’re always happy to help make your experience as comfortable and enjoyable as possible.

  • Families are very welcome at The Sherborne. Our gardens, café, and galleries are designed to be enjoyed by visitors of all ages, and we regularly host exhibitions and events that spark curiosity and creativity in young minds.

    Baby-changing facilities are available, and our team is always happy to assist with any additional needs.
