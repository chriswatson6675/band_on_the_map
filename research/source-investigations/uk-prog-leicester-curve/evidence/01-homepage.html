SYLVIA

Thu 24 Sep - Sat 3 Oct 2026

The untold story of the rebellious middle child of Emmeline Pankhurst takes centre stage in this irresistible hip hop, funk and soul musical that moves your feet and fires your spirit.

Whilst her mother and sister battled for women’s suffrage, Sylvia ignited a revolution for the forgotten: working women, the impoverished, the silenced. Caught between her family and her beliefs, Sylvia risked it all to bring change to millions.

Educating Rita

Sat 26 Sep - Sat 17 Oct 2026

Bright-eyed twenty-something Susan ‘Rita’ White dreams of making more of her life. Armed with an interest in literature and unwavering determination she enrols in the Open University, forming an unlikely friendship with disillusioned and dishevelled Frank – her self-proclaimed “appalling teacher”. Through their weekly tutorials, they challenge each other into a life-changing journey of self-discovery.

EDUCATING RITA is written by Willy Russell (BLOOD BROTHERS, OUR DAY OUT) and directed by Cara Nolan (WHO’S AFRAID OF VIRGINIA WOOLF?, PRIDE & PREJUDICE), with Alexandra Mardell (CORONATION STREET, ITV, SMOGGIE QUEENS, BBC) as our Rita and Dominic Mafham (VIGIL, BBC, KILLING EVE, BBC) as Frank.

Bharatanatyam Beyond Boundaries

Fri 2 Oct 2026

Caste Away: From Tradition to Heresy is a powerful solo Bharatanatyam work by Dr. Prathiba Batley, exploring five shades of womanhood — divine, comic, lover, survivor, and the disruptor. Drawing inspiration from ancient Tamil poetry, the piece reimagines the classical dance form for contemporary audiences. It is both celebration and provocation — a visual and emotional odyssey that speaks across cultures, generations, and identities.

THE LAST LAUGH

Tue 13 - Sat 17 Oct 2026

Three Legendary Comedians … One very unusual dressing room
DYING EASY IS EASY, COMEDY IS HARD
