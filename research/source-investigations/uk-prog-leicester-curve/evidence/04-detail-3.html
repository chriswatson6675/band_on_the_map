Events and Opportunities

Why your support matters

As a registered charity (no. 230708), your support helps us to enrich the lives of everyone who lives, works and learns in Leicester, Leicestershire and beyond.

We want all people to enjoy and participate in theatre, regardless of their background or financial circumstances.

There are many ways to get involved and support Curve. If you would like to fundraise for our theatre, you can get in touch with our Fundraising Team at give@curvetheatre.co.uk.

Curve Run Challenge

Please join us at the Run Leicester Half Marathon and 10km on Sunday 26 October.

You can enter the run for FREE by participating in support of Curve’s education and community work. All we ask is that you pledge to raise £150. We will cover your entry fee, support you with your sponsorship and provide a branded Curve running t-shirt.

To register please click here and for more information please email challenge@curvetheatre.co.uk

By running for Curve, you will help us to:

  • Offer bursaries for our classes for young people, which help to develop their creative, communication and teamwork skills, increase their self-confidence and improve their wellbeing.
  • Provide free theatre tickets to schools and community groups, with a focus on those based in areas of high disadvantage.
  • Deliver free weekly dance, singing and storytelling workshops to elders in care homes and at memory cafes.
  • Run regular, free events such as community days, foyer cabarets and festivals to entertain and welcome new visitors through our doors.
  • Support the next generation of local theatre-makers to develop their artistic practice by providing free advice, small commissions, free space for residencies and free script reading services.
  • Work with partners on projects supporting young people living in care, vulnerable adults and people living with disabilities.
  • And much more…
All Gift Aid collected on donations supports our work as a Registered Charity (no. 230708).
