Share the joy of seeing a show!

We want everyone who lives, works and learns in Leicester and beyond to have the opportunity to experience amazing live theatre.

We know that cost can be a barrier to many and we work hard to welcome as many people as possible through our doors.

Our Gift a Ticket scheme enables you to “pay it forward” by making a donation that will offer someone the opportunity to attend a Made at Curve production.

Every donation made enables us to give tickets to people who are not usually able to come to Curve. This includes children and families, school and community groups, and participants in our Neighbour-Hubs projects where we work to bring creative opportunities to some of the most disadvantaged areas of Leicester. For many people, this might be the first time they have been to Curve – or to any theatre.

Through our Community Ambassador and Access schemes we already welcome lots of new visitors to Curve, but there’s so much more we want to do. With your help, we can share the magic of theatre with even more people. Support our Gift a Ticket scheme today!

Donations of all sizes are very welcome. All Gift Aid collected on donations supports our work as a Registered Charity (no. 230708).
