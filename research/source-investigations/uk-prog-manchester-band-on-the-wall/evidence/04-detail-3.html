TICKETS

Tickets for all of our events can be purchased:

ONLINE

By clicking the ‘Buy Now’ button, on the event page of your choice. To find out more about our upcoming events, visit WHAT’S ON.

IN PERSON

Our Box Office is open daily from 6pm, except on days when no event is scheduled in the main venue. During busy entry periods immediately before and after doors open, ticket sales at the box office may be temporarily suspended while we prioritise admitting customers safely.
Please note that booking fees and the restoration levy apply to all in‑person purchases.

We no longer issue physical tickets. Instead, you will receive a digital ticket by email, and a receipt containing a QR code will be provided at the time of purchase.

For the quickest and most convenient experience, we encourage customers to book online. It’s fast, secure, and lets you access your tickets instantly—plus you’ll avoid waiting times at the venue.

ON THE DOOR 

If an event has not sold out, there will usually be tickets available to buy on the door. OTD tickets are available from the event’s specified Doors time and are subject to availability.

Prices

Prices advertised are face value and subject to booking fees and a venue restoration levy. The restoration levy is £1.50 for tickets to events in the Main Venue and £0.50 for Copper Bar shows. This levy helps us maintain and preserve the historic fabric of Band on the Wall, ensuring the venue remains in great condition for years to come.

Access

For more information, please visit our ACCESS page.

Membership Discount

Our Band on the Wall memberships can save you money on food, drink, tickets and merch. Find out how to join on our MEMBERSHIPS page.

Age Restrictions

Unless otherwise stated, events at Band on the Wall are 10+ but, under 16s must be accompanied by a responsible adult.

All attendees, irrespective of age, must be in possession of a valid ticket.

Band on the Wall cannot vouch for the content and suitability of any 10+ show. It is the responsibility of the parent or guardian to decide whether the event is appropriate for their child.

ID is required for unaccompanied 16+. Accepted forms of ID are a driving license, passport or PASS card.

All club night events are strictly 18+.
