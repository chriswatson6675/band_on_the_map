A Christmas Carol 2026

Saturday 5th December - Thursday 24th December

About the Show

★★★★★ “Gloriously charming and heartwarming.” – StageTalk

In Dickensian London this Christmas, not all is as it seems. The fog descends, the lights flicker, and strange things start happening to Ebenezer Scrooge. Join us at The Albany Theatre for this atmospheric re-telling of Charles Dickens’ enduring tale of redemption.

Watch in awe as objects come to life on the stage, be entranced by singing and movement and let your heart be warmed by the transformation that unfolds in front of you – the magic of Christmas and the magic of theatre combining for an unforgettable experience.

From the ghosts of Christmas past, present and future, to the plucky Cratchits, this is an adaptation that draws you in, that pulls you forward in your seat and holds you mesmerised from the very first “Bah Humbug”.

Written by our artistic director Alan Pollock and performed by a professional cast, this is a magical festive treat you won’t want to miss!

Performance Dates and Times

A Christmas Carol will be performed from 5 December 2026 to 24 December 2026 at Albany Theatre Coventry.

A selection of matinee and evening performances are available throughout the run. Please check the performances below for specific dates, times and availability.

Tickets Prices

Tickets are available from £18, with concessions, family tickets and group booking options available on selected performances.

Please refer to the booking section below for the latest pricing information and availability.

Age Guidance

A Christmas Carol is suitable for family audiences and is recommended for ages 6 and above.

Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

School Group Bookings

We also put on special school showings with discounted prices during the school day. If you are a teacher and would like to know the dates and prices of these, please inquire by emailing [email protected]

Visiting The Albany Theatre

Albany Theatre is located in Coventry and welcomes thousands of visitors each year to a varied programme of theatre, comedy, music and family entertainment.

For information on parking, public transport, venue facilities and directions, please visit our visitor information page before your visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Long is A Christmas Carol

The running time will be confirmed closer to the performance dates.

Is A Christmas Carol suitable for children?

Yes, the production is designed for family audiences and is recommended for ages 6+.

Are school performances available?

Yes. Special school performances of A Christmas Carol are available during the school day, with discounted ticket prices for educational groups. For performance dates, pricing and booking information, please email [email protected].

Is the theatre accessible?

Yes. Albany Theatre offers a range of accessible facilities. Please contact us before your visit if you have specific access requirements.

Where can I park?

Parking is available close to the theatre at Earlsdon Park multi-storey car park and the Coventry Rugby Club car park. Both are within walking distance of the venue.

Please note that parking charges apply. Coventry Rugby Club car park operates on a pay-and-display basis and accepts payment by cash, card or via the Pay4Stay app.
