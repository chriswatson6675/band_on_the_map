SEE YOUR FAVOURITE STARS LIVE AT THE ALBANY THEATRE

Albany Theatre welcomes some of the biggest names in entertainment for exclusive live events, inspiring conversations, and one-of-a-kind experiences. Discover fascinating stories, behind-the-scenes insights, and special appearances from much-loved personalities.

COMMUNITY PRODUCTION

Get ready for a whirlwind of love, laughter and unforgettable Madness hits in Our House – the Olivier Award-winning musical that follows one life-changing decision and the two very different futures that follow. Bursting with energy, heart and iconic songs including It Must Be Love, Baggy Trousers and House of Fun, this feel-good production from Albany Creates Together (ACT) is a celebration of family, friendship and finding your own path.

CHRISTMAS SHOWS

Looking for the best Christmas shows in Coventry? The Albany Theatre is your destination for festive entertainment, family theatre, and seasonal performances. Join us this Christmas for live shows filled with music, storytelling, comedy, and holiday magic.

VISIT OUR CAFE & BAR

Whether you’re joining us for a show, meeting friends, or looking for a relaxed daytime space to work (find out more about co-working), our café and bar is open throughout the week serving drinks, simple snacks (crisps, sweets, nuts) and more.

And it’s not just for theatre-goers – everyone’s welcome, whether you’re here for an event or simply passing by. With a relaxed armosphere and free Wi-Fi, it’s the perfect place to stop for a drink.

Please note: the bar opens one hour before the advertised show start time if you’d like to enjoy a pre-show drink.

Our café is staffed by volunteers Monday to Friday from 11am–1pm, and the foyer remains open until 5:30pm, offering visitors a welcoming space to relax, meet or work throughout the afternoon.

HIRE OUR SPACES

Looking for a venue in Coventry?

Hire The Albany Theatre for conferences, performances, rehearsals, meetings, workshops and special events. With a 600-seat auditorium, versatile studio spaces and a unique Art Deco setting, we can accommodate everything from small gatherings to large-scale events.

Find out more about foyer and studio hire or get in touch with our team to discuss your plans.
