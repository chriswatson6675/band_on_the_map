Tramshed Creators
Networking Event

Join us for a networking event for creatives based in and around Woolwich.

FREE EVENT

Next dates and speaker announcements coming soon!

Join us for the next Tramshed Creators event, part of our new membership programme designed to cultivate creative networking in Woolwich and give members access to subsidised space at short notice for their creative practice.

We want this membership event to be a space for fun, exciting conversations and lasting creative connections. Come along and meet fellow local creatives in a relaxed environment.

What to expect

This month's session will be dedicated to open networking, giving you the chance to meet fellow creatives, share ideas, explore collaborations and connections. Please note that there will be no guest speaker at this event.

Tramshed 51-53 Woolwich New Road, London, SE18 6ES
