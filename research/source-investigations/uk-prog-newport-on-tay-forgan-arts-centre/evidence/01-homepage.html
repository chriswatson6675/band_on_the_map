Forgan Arts Centre is a home for artistic production, experimentation, and learning in North East Fife

Explore our programme of activities

An Arts Centre as a Garden

Forgan Arts Centre’s activities and operations are conceptualised as An Arts Centre as a Garden, a methodology rooted in permaculture principles. This is an experiment: imagining an art centre as a garden where a multisensory and sentient ecology of plants, people, and creatures support one another through mutually beneficial relationships and reciprocity.

Children’s Art Club: Autumn

Children’s Art Club is back for two weeks during the October holidays and open now for booking.

Join artist Jeanne Hamilton on week one to see The Garden as a Visual Story, finding inspiration and building “creature” characters, sets and stories for film.

Week two is lead by artist by Sam Harley. The week’s activities are inspired by transformation, of ourselves, our surroundings and our future.

Studio Spaces Available

We have 3 studio spaces available. They are bright and airy, each with their own sink and views across the garden or river Tay. They are suitable for a variety of purposes and current members include visual artists, ceramicists, makers, and musicians.

Studio members have access to some on site creative facilities, tools, and knowledge. The studios are a blank canvas and we support adapting the spaces to best support your creative practice.

Find out more

Edge School

Edge School is a new peer-to-peer learning programme at Forgan Arts Centre for LGBTQIA+ creatives in Fife. Edge School explores the garden as a metaphor for spaces of cultivation, rooting and unexpected encounters through art, craft, landwork, permaculture, writing and food. The space is queer-led and suitable for people aged 16 years +. All materials are provided, just come along!

Find out more

Connecting Voices

Connecting Voices is back from Saturday 5 September, 2pm-3.30pm.

Connecting Voices is a place to sing together.

It’s a group where people come together to use their voices, singing, making experimental sounds, and playing with vocal exercises. The group meets twice a month on Coorie Saturdays.

Everyone is welcome. Anyone who wants to use their voice, from complete beginners to regular singers and performers. It’s a space to meet others, enjoy time together, build confidence, and develop awareness of others through close listening and vocalising.

Find out more

Gardening for the Future

Every 1st and 3rd Saturday we host an open volunteer meet-up in the gardens at the centre. Everyone is welcome, no experience necessary. The meet-up is led by gardener, grower, and environmentalist Dr Helena Simmons who will support people to get involved.

The activities include building raised beds, learning about ‘no-dig’ growing, planting vegetable and flowers, harvesting, and the never ending weeding and pruning to keep the gardens tidy. All activities relate to the sustainability, improving biodiversity, and tackling the climate emergency.

Find out more

Coorie Saturdays

Coorie Saturdays are a blend of lots of different activities at that you can get involved in. From drop in crafting, singing workshops, learning about gardening and food growing, and workshops and classes. There’s free tea, coffee, juice and snacks available all day, free of charge.
