About The Keys

Right in the heart of Yarm, on the cobbles close to the Victorian viaduct, The Keys is the one venue on the High Street that doesn't make you choose. Start with dinner in the Gastropub, move through to Cabana for handcrafted cocktails, and finish the night dancing until close at one of our many outstanding events, all without leaving the building.

Weekends bring bottomless brunches and jaw-dropping cabaret drag shows. Fridays turn into Funky Fridays and Vibe nights with DJs and a dance floor that fills up fast. And when it's time to slow down, our Pergola and outdoor spaces are made for long summers and golden-hour cocktails.

Whether you're planning a big night with the girls, a celebration that needs more than one "vibe," or just want somewhere that goes from dinner to dancing without a taxi in between — The Keys is where Yarm gathers. Grab your tickets and be part of it.
