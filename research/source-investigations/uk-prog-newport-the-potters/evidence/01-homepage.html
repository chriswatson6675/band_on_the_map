Home of Music, Food & Sport

Welcome to Potters, your go-to destination for great food, music, and live sport in Newport. A true local favourite, we bring people together to eat, drink, and enjoy great times.

From breakfast through to late drinks, there’s always something happening – whether you’re joining us for a meal, a night out, or to catch the big game.

Sunshine, Spritz & Good Times

When the sun’s out, there’s only one thing for it – a spritz in hand at Potters.

From Aperol Spritz and Hugo Spritz to Rhubarb Rosé and alcohol-free options, our summer drinks menu is packed with refreshing serves perfect for sunny afternoons, rooftop catch-ups and weekend celebrations.

Why Choose us?

Known for our quirky yet traditional charm, Potters is a true cornerstone of the Newport community, bringing people together for great food, drinks, and good times.

Join us every Friday and Saturday for bands and DJs, creating one of the best atmospheres for music in the City. Enjoy drinks on our stunning rooftop terrace, complete with its own bar – perfect for relaxing with friends, whatever the weather.

From hearty breakfasts and classic Sunday lunches to Afternoon Tea and late nights, there’s always something happening- plus live sport shown across multiple screens, so you never miss the action.

We are the perfect place to kick back, unwind, and make memories.
