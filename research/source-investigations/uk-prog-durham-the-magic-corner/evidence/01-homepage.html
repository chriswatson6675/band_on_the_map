What Is
The Magic Corner?

The Magic Corner is a unique, immersive experience that brings the art of close-up magic to life in an intimate setting, created by Award Winning Magician Tom Bolton. With every performance unfolding just inches away, it invites audiences into a world of astonishment—where sleight of hand, storytelling and surprise create unforgettable moments.
