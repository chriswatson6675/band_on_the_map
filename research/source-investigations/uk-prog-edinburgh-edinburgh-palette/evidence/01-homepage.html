Advancing Regeneration & Transformation

It is the aim of Scot-Art to stimulate urban regeneration and to breathe new life into under-used spaces, driving economic growth, social cohesion and opportunity. The vision of Scot-Art is centred around fostering a spirit of community, individuality, and creative expression with an aim of providing desperately needed local services to the people of the local community.
