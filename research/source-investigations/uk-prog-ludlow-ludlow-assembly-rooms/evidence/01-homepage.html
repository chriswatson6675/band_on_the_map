A helping hand

Volunteers are the life blood of Ludlow Assembly Rooms

Over 150 people give their time for free to support us in the smooth day-to-day running of our amazing arts centre. From accounting to zapping tickets, serving drinks to designing posters, delivering film guides to checking tickets, there really is a role here for everyone.

Click Here


If you can spare a couple of hours a week and want to join our amazing team then please get in touch with us at: volunteering@ludlowassemblyrooms.co.uk

Did you know we show films every week? Monday and matinee* tickets are just £7.

You can buy a whole range of drinks and snacks to take into the film. So whether you’re a coffee-and-ice-cream kind of cinema goer or a sob-into-your-glass-of-wine sort, you can do it here in the comfort of our recently renovated auditorium. We have a number of captioned screenings each month, these are marked as 'captioned' in our listings. We also offer 'relaxed' screenings. See these accessible film listings here.

*Matinees start at 2pm. Evening showings start 7pm (until end of March) from April they will start at 7.30pm

Accessibility

Ludlow Assembly Rooms welcomes audience members with additional needs.

If you have any queries or requests, please get in touch with our friendly box office team before you visit. Call us on: 01584 878 141

We offer Descriptive Subtitle screenings of some films for those who are hard of hearing, this is not just subtitles but audio description.
Two dedicated wheelchair spaces are available in our auditorium. Please contact box-office if you require a
wheelchair space. 
Service dogs are welcome and our lift provides access to all floors.  
Blue badge parking is available nearby on Mill Street.
