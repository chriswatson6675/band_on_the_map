Welcome to The Florians Theatre
We are an amateur dramatics company based in the city of Inverness, in Scotland. Founded in 1944 with the purpose of entertaining the troops stationed in town in World War 2, we nowadays produce approximately 5 productions a year in our premises.

​Please feel free to browse our website where you will find lots of information on our theatre, our productions, our 75th anniversary, and much, much more. We are always delighted for new people to come along and join our company, so if you would like to be involved, please e-mail us at [email protected]

News and Upcoming Events
The Florians embarked on some theatre improvements this year and in order to facilitate this we went dark between January and March. Our stage floor was sanded and sealed, we replaced our stage lighting system with LEDs, and our seats were re-upholstered. We offered the opportunity for people to sponsor a seat for the sum of £50, and for this a small plaque was placed on the back of the seat with their name, or the name of someone this was dedicated to. We do still have a small number of seats left, so if you would like to sponsor one and secure your place in the Florians' history, please e-mail [email protected] and we will give you all the details you need.

PARKING: For those of you coming to the theatre, please be aware that there are now parking restrictions on the streets around the theatre, so please allow yourself more time to find somewhere to park. We also now have 2 designated disabled bays at the back door of the theatre.
