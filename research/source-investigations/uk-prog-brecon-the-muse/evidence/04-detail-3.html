The Mid Wales R&B Club presents The Achievers

The Mid Wales Rhythm and Blues Club are back at The Muse with a much anticipated return from The Achievers plus special guest Pete Hollingworth The Achievers have a reputation for innovation and originality and prove that soul, groove and song-craft still matter in the blues. A club favourite, the band bring their own brand of modern, ‘dancefloor gospel-blues’ gaining them rave reviews and big-stage appearances across the world. Following the release of their 2019 album ‘the lost arc’ they received radio airplay by cerys matthew (BBC 2 blues show) and reached #1 in the independent blues broadcasters airplay chart and were nominated for three UK blues awards. https://www.theachieversuk.com/
