Michael Mania Live

Tuesday 13 Oct 2026, 7:30pm - Wednesday 14 Oct 2026, 7:30pm at Venue Cymru

Book Tickets

Share

MICHAEL MANIA™ is the sensational stage show that celebrates the King of Pop and his extraordinary musical legacy. More than just a tribute, it’s a spectacular concert experience that captures the energy, creativity, and brilliance of Michael Jackson.

Featuring a world-class cast, breath taking choreography, and stunning visuals, every detail is designed to immerse audiences in the unforgettable spirit of the King of Pop.

From ‘Billie Jean’ to ‘Thriller’, ‘Smooth Criminal’ to ‘Black or White’, this electrifying production brings his iconic music and dance to life like never before, delivering a performance so authentic, it’s the closest you’ll ever come to experiencing Michael Jackson, live. 

Get ready to relive the hits that shaped musical history. This is MICHAEL MANIA™.
