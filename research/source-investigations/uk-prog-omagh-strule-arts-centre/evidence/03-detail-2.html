Tuesday 22 September - Tuesday 27 October (6 weeks)

Join Frances Sweeney for this six week block of pottery classes. The focus will be on slab work and surface decoration. We will be using coloured slips on leather-hard clay followed by the process of sgraffito ( scraping through the layer of slip) to create interesting designs.

Come along and nurture your innate creativity!

Once purchased, tickets are non-transferable and non-refundable
