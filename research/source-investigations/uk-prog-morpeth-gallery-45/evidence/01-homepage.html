Art Gallery, Workshops, Gift Shop and Venue Hire in Felton, Northumberland

Comprising of two gallery and exhibition spaces, a gift shop with a cosy, fireside seating area where cake and coffee are served. Also on site are 6 artists’ studios, currently occupied by a variety of designers, makers and artists. The exhibitions spaces are also available for hire and are perfect for parties, celebrations, corporate meetings and many other types of events.

We do not have a dedicated car park but you can find on street parking at the front of the Gallery. Please do be courteous of our neighbours.

 

Gallery Forty5 also runs a programme of creative activities throughout the year, including painting, sewing and printing workshops and classes. We also hold music concerts in our beautiful Long Room. Many of the resident artists also run workshops from their studios, so if you are interested in art and creativity – there’s plenty to see. Sign up for our newsletter below and we’ll send you a monthly-ish update of what’s on and upcoming. We are open from 10:30 until 4:30 Monday to Saturday and closed on Sundays. Please note, we are closed on Bank Holidays.  If in doubt, please give us a ring before making a special trip. Studio artists keep their own hours so check beforehand to find out if they are in. We look forward to meeting you!
