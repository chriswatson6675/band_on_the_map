Friend Subscription

Become a friend of Penlee Park Open Air Theatre

The Open Air Theatre’s first performance was in 1948.

It’s a special place for so many people.

Please help us to assure its future by becoming a Friend.

You’ll be supporting our work and in return we’ll say thank you by giving you exclusive benefits, discounts, priority booking, an E-Newsletter and access to special events.

  • Becoming a Friend is very easy.
  • Your subscription lasts for twelve months.
  • An annual subscription costs £25.

We Value You

Your Friends benefits include:

  • Priority booking and your choice of front row seats for up to four people (subject to availability).
  • Advance notification and updates of future shows and events.
  • 10% Discount in the Refreshment Bar on presentation of your Friends Card.
  • Circulation of our regular E-Newsletter updates.
  • Occasional opportunities to attend additional Friends-only events.
