FLEXIBLE SPACES AVAILABLE

an independent arts centre where creativity, community, & sustainable local living come together

The Globe is located in the renowned book town of Hay on Wye. Nestled between the Powys and Herefordshire border, Hay on Wye is an area of outstanding natural beauty, and a rich cultural heritage. 

We run a wide range of events, including live music, film screenings, debates, workshops, and much more. Want to run your own event? Our various spaces are highly versatile and available for private hire!

The Globe at Hay is a licensed wedding venue! From al-fresco gatherings to majestic ceremonies, couples have ample choice when staging their happily ever after.

You will find our cosy and welcoming café and bar, run in collaboration with our Globe Kitchen. Our talented Head Chef has specially created a variety of delicious dishes guaranteed to get your taste buds tingling.

Right on the doorstep of the Globe is our Smallbrook and Southwick Cottage. Each have three bedrooms, with room for up to six guests to find a little piece of rustic bliss in one of the UK’s most desirable towns.

HowTheLightGetsIn Festival

Every year Hay-on-Wye hosts the world's largest philosophy and music festival! With 300 events in one weekend, we're confident you'll find something that interests you, whether it’s a late-night party or a talk from one of the world's leading professionals in philosophy, politics, science and much more.

Our Story

The Globe at Hay is a part of the Institute of Art and Ideas, a charitable organisation dedicated to free thinking, creativity, and open debate, The Globe stands as a beacon for those who want to explore, question, discuss, and challenge.

Community comes first at The Globe; we’re committed to championing the people, talent, and spirit of Hay-on-Wye and its surrounding hills. At the heart of who we are is our 100-acre farm in the Welsh mountains, a place that inspires our values and enriches our restaurant with truly local produce.

When you visit us, you support a place that proudly invests back into its roots!
