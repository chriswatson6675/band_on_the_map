Run for Charity

Whether it’s running a Half Marathon or the full 26.1 miles, taking on a challenge event for the London Irish Centre makes a real difference to the lives of those we support.  

All the money you raise helps support our charity’s vital services; alleviating poverty, building social connection, and ensuring that no one has to face hardship alone. 

If you’re new in London or looking to build more community, it’s also a great way to connect with fellow runners. With running socials, WhatsApp groups and one-to-one support from our fundraising team, joining Team LIC is the perfect way to keep moving, have fun and make a real, positive impact.

Discover our upcoming Challenge Events…

Run Hackney Half Marathon 2027

One of the highlights of the running calendar, the Hackney Half is one of the most popular fundraising events in the UK. Taking runners on a scenic route through vibrant East London, including Hackney Downs, Broadway Market, and Victoria Park, this is not one to be missed.

Hackney Half 2027 takes place on Sunday 16 May, 2027.

Register now

Run London Landmarks Half Marathon 2027

This is a unique, closed-road route through the heart of London, offering runners the chance to take in the city’s most iconic landmarks, including St. Paul’s Cathedral, Nelson’s Column, and the Tower London. On top of this, thousands of spectators line the streets, and the route is filled with dancers, DJs, and musical performances.

London Landmarks Half Marathon 2027 takes place on Sunday 4 April, 2027.

Register now

Why run for the London Irish Centre? 

You will be making a real difference! All funds raised go directly to brightening the lives of our most vulnerable community members. Alongside this, you’ll receive…

  • One-to-one fundraising support and training advice 
  • Team LIC Fundraising Pack, including: a London Irish Centre technical running top, support resources with fundraising tips and advice, and promotional materials to help you in your fundraising 
  • Running social to meet your fellow runners and fundraising team 
  • Access to Team LIC Strava group and a WhatsApp group to meet fellow runners 
  • Free access to bespoke training plan on Coopah 
  • A dedicated cheer squad on race day 
  • Post-race celebration in a Central London location 
  • Regular updates from a dedicated point of contact 

Your Impact in Action

In our last complete year, the London Irish Centre… 

  • Welcomed 55,000 people through our doors for live events, social gatherings, educational classes and courses 
  • Supported 2,750 people through our community services 
  • Gained £4.5m in income for our clients through advice and advocacy 
  • Helped over 1,700 people access their Irish passport.
