A SPACE FOR CELEBRATING, RECORDING, DANCING, FILMING,
BROADCASTING, MEETING, DINING, TALKING, SINGING, STRETCHING & THINKING.

Located on Exeter’s historic Stepcote Hill, our lovingly restored Grade II-listed Victorian Sunday School is a beautiful, intimate space with a unique home-from-home atmosphere where our guests can eat, drink, host events and have fun.

WEDDINGS AT THE HALL

Weddings at The Hall are very special. Our gorgeous room is full of relaxed charm and is a magical place for celebrations at any time of year. We have homely seating, vintage tableware, both vintage and cutting edge lighting rigs, and we can arrange and dress The Hall exactly as you wish.

PRIVATE DINING
AT THE HALL

The Hall can cater for up to 80 people seated, or up to 100–120 for buffets / evening parties. Our caterer, Bel’s Kitchen, is well known as one of the region’s best private chefs and can create a wonderful menu tailored to your needs.
