Live Music & Performance Venue Hire

The Hall provides a beautiful setting for live music and performance. With exceptional acoustics, professional sound system, and theatre lighting, we create memorable experiences for both performers and audiences.

We've hosted comedy from Mike Wozniak, Bridget Christie, Josie Long, Mark Watson, Daniel O Docherty, Reg D Hunter, and Marcel Lucont; poets including Hollie McNish; musicians Poco Drom, Old Time Sailors, The Apricity Trio and Cole Stacey, performances and countless local artists; Spoken word and talks from George Monbiot, The Happy Pear, The Annual Exeter Poetry Slam and the wonderful Mothers On The Mic. We have hosted film festivals, the Matrescence Festival, the Exeter International Dance Film Festival and the Two Moors music Festival.

Capacity: 105 seated | 120 standing
Times: 7:00pm - 11:00pm | Setup from 5:00pm or earlier by discussion.

View our seating plans →

Pricing

Sunday to Thursday: £500
Friday & Saturday: £800

You keep 100% of ticket sales. This is flat-rate venue hire - you handle promotion, ticketing, and marketing, and retain all revenue.

What's Included

  • Professional PA system and speakers

  • Theatre lighting rig with colour washes

  • Bar service with professional staff

  • Tables and chairs (configured as needed)

  • Venue staff member throughout

  • Setup access from 5:00pm

What You Provide

  • Mixing console (connects to our PA)

  • Microphones, cables, and performance equipment

  • Qualified sound engineer

  • Public Liability Insurance (£2m minimum)

  • PAT certificates for all equipment

Optional Extras

  • Extend to midnight: £150

  • Additional staff: £100 per person

  • Bar setup: £100 (refundable when bar takings exceed £200)

Booking Details

Payment:

  • 50% deposit secures your booking

  • Balance due 4 weeks before event

Important Information

Sound & Timing:

  • Events must finish by 11:00pm

  • Sound levels must comply with local noise regulations

  • All equipment must be removed by midnight

Licensing:

  • We're a licensed premises - no external alcohol permitted

  • Under 18s require adult supervision after 9:00pm

Insurance:

  • Copy required 2 weeks before event
