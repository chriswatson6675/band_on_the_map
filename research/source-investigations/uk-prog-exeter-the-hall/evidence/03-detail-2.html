Planning a milestone birthday, anniversary, or special celebration?

Our Party Package takes care of all the details - the atmosphere, the entertainment, the bar, and the setup - so you can enjoy the evening with the people who matter.

Our Victorian hall transforms beautifully for parties. Disco lighting, silent disco headphones for up to 120 guests, vintage mirror ball, a well-stocked bar with friendly staff, and our vintage photo booth to capture the memories. It's everything you need in one place, with none of the stress of coordinating multiple suppliers.

Catering

All catering at The Hall is provided by Bel's Kitchen. Isabel Davies is one of the region's most respected private chefs, and works with every client to build a menu around them - from elegant plated dinners to relaxed sharing feasts, with seasonal ingredients at the heart of everything she does.

Bel's Kitchen caters for virtually all dietary requirements, and there's no additional venue charge for using them.

Treating Your Guests

A few ways to treat your guests to drinks on the day — pick whichever works best or combine them.

1 Pre-order — welcome drinks, wine with dinner or a case of real ale, ready and waiting when guests arrive.

2 Run a tab — set a spending limit and specify what it covers. We'll let you know when you're approaching the limit.

3 Drinks tokens — give guests a token or two each, redeemable at the bar. We settle the tab with you at the end of the evening.
