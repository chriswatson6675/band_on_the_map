About 12 Days

This Christmas, five children are chosen for an extraordinary journey to discover the true meaning of love. Travelling through a divided world, they uncover empathy, trust, courage and belonging - learning that love grows when we come together. 

12 Days is a joyful, heart-warming festive adventure for all ages, brought to life in a spectacular Chickenshed production with a large, diverse cast - not to be missed!

Age Recommendation

12 Days is recommended for all ages from 5 and above. 

We understand that childcare can sometimes be a barrier for parents attending the theatre, so if you need to bring a child under the age of five, you should

  • Consider the suitability, content, length of performance (2 hours) and age recommendation.
  • Be mindful and respectful of other customers’ enjoyment of the event before attending with younger children.
  • Try to book a seat near an aisle/exit so you can leave should your child become restless.

All children over the age of 24 months must have their own ticket. Children under 24 months do not need a ticket and should be seated on an adult’s lap.

What you need to know about cookies

When you read this website on your device, we put some small pieces of information on that device. These are called cookies.

Some cookies have to be there, or this website won’t work the way it’s meant to. For other cookies, it’s up to you whether we put them on your device.

This screen lets you pick which cookies you want us to use. If you’re not sure, please ask an adult you trust to help you decide.

  • Strictly necessary cookies

    Without these cookies, the website won’t work the way it’s meant to. You can’t turn these cookies off here, but you can change how your device works to stop us using them. If you do that, you won’t be able to do things like logging into your account or buying tickets.

  • Analytical or performance cookies

    These cookies help us understand who is visiting the website and how they’re using it. They’ll tell us things like whether you’re using a phone, tablet, or computer, and they might tell us what city you live in.

  • Targeting cookies

    These cookies tell us what pages you’ve visited, and might let us match this up with pages you’ve visited on other websites. These cookies might also give your information to another app or website, like Instagram, and change the adverts you see there.
