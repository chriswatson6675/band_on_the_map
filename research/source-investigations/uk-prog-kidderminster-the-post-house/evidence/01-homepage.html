Welcome to The Post House, Kidderminster – A Proud My Local Pub by Davenports Brewery!


At The Post House, we’re passionate about great food, quality drinks, and fantastic deals. Our menu is packed with pub classics, alongside hearty all-day breakfasts, stacked burgers, authentic curries, and much more. Whether you're popping in for a quick bite or a leisurely meal, we’ve got something for everyone.


Weekly Deals at The Post House:

🍽️ Meal & a FREE Drink – Monday to Thursday, 12 -8pm
🍳 Breakfast Deal – Any Large or veggie breakfast with a free hot drink just £7.95

🍔 Burger Wednesday – Buy any burger and get a FREE drink
🐟 Fish Friday – buy one get one FREE, all day!

🧇 Two Waffle cones for just £8!


To complement our mouth-watering menu, we serve award-winning Davenports ales, available at great prices all week long. Plus, don’t miss our Happy Hours for even more unbeatable value!


Join us at The Post House, Kidderminster, for great food, amazing deals, live sport, and a welcoming atmosphere right here in town.
