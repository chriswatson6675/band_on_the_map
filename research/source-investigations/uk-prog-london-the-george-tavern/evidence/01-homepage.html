Long Live The George!

The George Tavern is a legendary venue, public house and constant foundation for a thriving artistic community in the heart of London's east end. Owner, Pauline Forster has spent the last decade transforming The George into an arts, music and performance venue with an international reputation.

Whether to enjoy live music from a range of different artists or simply enjoy the comfort of a classic East-End boozer, The George is the place to be.

We're now serving fresh hand stretched pizza in the garden! Made with love by chef Rupert, it really is a fantastic pizza. The recipe has been expertly crafted and perfected over years, using only the best restaurant grade ingredients, all coming together in this gorgeous timber shack, nestled with a cosy dining area at the back of our patio. Ru designed and built the structure with his son Oscar over the summer to get the restaurant ready for the upcoming pub season, and you can taste this manifestation of passion kneaded directly into every crisp bite of our traditional roman style pizza. The kitchen will be open Wednesday to Sunday (with some flexibility depending on the week) from 6pm til late 🍴
