We are committed to creating a welcoming space in our gallery, studios and workshops where artists from all backgrounds can freely express their concerns about global conflicts and social or political issues

At OVADA we are proud to affirm our commitment to the ongoing work of relationship building and serving the Oxfordshire community

We support efforts to foster honest and respectful conversations, aiming to strengthen peace, friendships and trust

Our hearts go out to innocent civilians affected by conflicts worldwide, and we call for an end to all forms of conflict and for International Law to be respected 

We do not condone racism and any form of discrimination including race, gender, religion or sexual orientation

Our goal is to ensure that everyone feels welcome and valued in our community 

We emphasise the importance of respecting diverse viewpoints and fostering an inclusive environment

We are determined to not let anyone divide us!

We are here for;

Creativity: a space to experience the arts and share skills, supporting the delivery of innovative events, exhibitions, workshops and courses that are welcoming for all. Our warehouse is available for hire to realise creative projects

Collaboration: and offer dynamic development opportunities, such as open calls, in-house studio spaces and residencies that encourage experimental, cross disciplinary and collaborative approaches

Community:  and strive to reflect our community through supporting socially engaging, inclusive work. We nurture and connect artists and creative communities through our Associates offer and network

Kindness underpins everything we do here at OVADA, we value care and mutual respect for everyone

Accessibility

We would like to welcome you in whatever way we can

There are limitations to our building, we do not have a fully wheelchair accessible toilet and our education space is upstairs

The nearest accessible toilet is just around the corner, at The Courtyard by Marriot, 15 Paradise St, Oxford OX1 1LD

Our gallery space is accessible from the car park but the floor is uneven

We are actively seeking solutions and welcome any suggestions to make your visit to OVADA as comfortable as possible

You can find more information in our accessibility policy to give you a better idea

Detailed information about our venue can also be found in this report published on Accessable

Our car park is private, however please get in touch if you have mobility requirements in advance of your visit at info@ovada.org.uk
