Programme Archive 2026

An exhibition with a message: aside from our geo-political position, our social status, or our ethnicity, we share one thing: we are all human.

OVADA Gallery hosted Silence and Screaming from 20th June - 28th June 2026

Silence and Screaming Exhibition by Qu Lei Lei and Caroline Deane

Learn more

Creation Theatre's thrilling adaptation of Robert Louis Stevenson’s Gothic masterpiece The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde was staged here at the OVADA Gallery.

This run of performances delivered a gripping night of theatre, filled with psychological tension, striking visuals, and an immersive encounter with the dark and divided world of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde.

OVADA Gallery hosted Creation Theatre’s production of Jekyll & Hyde from 29th April - 17th May 2026

Creation Theatre presents: Jekyll & Hyde

Learn more

An exhibition celebrating the fresh voices and emerging talent of first-year BA Fine Artists at Oxford Brookes University

OVADA Gallery hosted Internal Perspective on Wednesday 22 April 2026

Internal Perspective Exhibition

Learn more

This beaded bracelet workshop was a hands-on experience inspired by traditional craft practices from Lagos, Nigeria.

Guided by Amy Harris (Oxford Brookes University), participants were invited to slow down, learn a simple beading technique, and enjoy the experience of making together in a welcoming and supportive environment.

OVADA Gallery hosted Skills on Wheels on Saturday 18 April 2026

Skills on Wheels: Beaded bracelet making from Lagos, Nigeria

Learn more

Grow Into It presented a new body of work by artist Alice Hackney, spanning installation, sculpture, video, print, and textiles.

The exhibition brought together themes from ecological and disability research, drawing attention to overlooked or undervalued connections between bodies, landscapes, and systems of care.

OVADA Gallery hosted Grow Into It from the 26 - 29 March 2026

Grow Into It Exhibition

Learn more

Voices of Iran was an evening of food and art that brought people together to share, listen, and support Iranian voices within our community

At a time when many in the Iranian diaspora were holding deep concern for loved ones far away, the event created a gentle and welcoming space to come together

OVADA Gallery hosted Voices of Iran on Saturday 21 March 2026

Voices of Iran

Learn more

Discontinued was presented to you by art collective Evocation, made up of seven students from The Ruskin School of Art; this marked the collective’s debut exhibition.

Evocation emerged through shared moments of reflection and the convergence of distinct artistic practices. The collective formed around a shifting series of encounters, seeking to create provisional spaces in which conversation, alignment, and productive friction could arise.

OVADA Gallery hosted Discontinued from 12-15 March 2026

Discontinued Exhibition

Learn more

Human beings are fascinated by landscape, and the world around us. We find meaning in landscape but also place our own meanings into it.

Landscape and Meaning was an exhibition by Kieran McLean, Anne Griffiths, Emmett Casley and Mark Clay, whose ideas engage with landscapes, in a dynamic variety of interpretations and locations near, far and imagined

OVADA Gallery hosted Landscape and Meaning from 24-28 February 2026

Landscape and Meaning Exhibition

Learn more

We were blown away by how many of you joined us for A Shared Table: Future of Artists + Communities

This was an open, collaborative conversation about how artists and grassroots arts organisations can survive and thrive together

This event took place on Sunday 22 February 2025

A Shared Table: Future of Artists and Communities

This year’s theme for the Ashmolean Museum’s annual One World Family Festival was Nature, and OVADA took part with a workshop titled Weaving the World

Many thanks to everyone who joined us for a hands-on workshop creating God’s Eyes (or Ojo de Dios) using natural yarns and sticks

This event took place on Saturday 15 February 2026
