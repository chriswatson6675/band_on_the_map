Frequently Asked Questions

How many drama productions are there a year?

We currently put on three productions a year, normally in April, July/August and December. These are interspersed with NT Live screenings and other performances.

How can I buy tickets?

1. Tickets can be bought online at Ticket Source – www.ticketsource.co.uk/attic

2. You can also buy tickets for most productions from Fair and Fabulous in Newcastle Emlyn.

3. Unless we are sold out, tickets are on sale at the box office, 30 minutes before each performance.

Can I reserve a seat?

Unfortunately seats can’t be reserved. We operate on a first-come first-served basis. Doors are open 30 minutes before the start of each performance.

Is there wheelchair access?

No – the theatre is on the first floor and the public entrance is at the top of a stone staircase outside the building with no wheeelchair access. We apologise for any inconvenience caused.

About us

Founded in Newcastle Emlyn, Attic Theatre is a beloved local drama group known for its commitment to creativity, community and quality entertainment. With a dedicated team of talented individuals, we strive to bring the magic of theatre, film and performance to audiences of all ages.

Attic Theatre (Wales) is a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO), registered charity number 1218804
