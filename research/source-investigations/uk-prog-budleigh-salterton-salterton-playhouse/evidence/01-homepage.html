Welcome

Honouring the past. Performing the present. Inspiring the future.

Welcome to Salterton Drama Club, a place where community, creativity and connection take centre stage. For over 85 years, we’ve brought the magic of live theatre to Budleigh Salterton—uniting generations through laughter, storytelling, and unforgettable performances.

Whether you’re joining us in the audience, stepping into the spotlight, or lending a hand behind the scenes, you’re part of something truly special: a family of theatre lovers keeping the arts alive for generations to come.

Come and be part of the story.
