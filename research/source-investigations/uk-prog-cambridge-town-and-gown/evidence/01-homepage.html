Town & Gown

I'm part of the High Street Premium family!

Pub, Bar & Private Event Space in Cambridge

The Town and Gown is a unique, must-try experience in the heart of Cambridge, open from early ’til late. We’re a pub, bar, and private event venue home to the Black Box Theatre, Cambridge’s intimate fringe theatre and one of the city’s most versatile spaces for private hire.

Private Hire & Function Rooms in Cambridge
Looking for a private event space, function room, or venue hire in Cambridge? We have multiple spaces available for private hire, perfect for family get-togethers, corporate meetings, parties, celebrations and more. Click Private Hire to find more information about our options and how to get in touch.

The Black Box Theatre
Located on the top floor, our Black Box Theatre is a wonderfully intimate fringe theatre hosting shows, comedy nights, live music and events from the moment we open the doors. A revival of the building’s history as the Arts Cinema (1933), and later, the Cosmopolitan Cinema. The creative spirit lives on!

Food, Drink & Socialising
From brunching with friends to sharing plates, the best roast in the city to dancing the night away, enjoy locally brewed beers, handmade cocktails, Black Box Theatre shows, DJ sets, live bands, and intimate piano dinners. Whatever the occasion, there’s a reason to visit any day of the week.

The Town and Gown
The name Town and Gown reflects Cambridge’s two communities; the local population and the university, and the space we’ve built for both to meet, eat and drink.

Christmas at the Town & Gown

Make this Christmas one to remember at the Town & Gown! Whether you’re planning a festive lunch with family, Christmas drinks with friends or a work Christmas party, join us for great food, seasonal drinks and plenty of festive cheer in the heart of Cambridge.

Our festive menu is packed with seasonal favourites, making us the perfect place for Christmas dining and celebrations throughout November and December. From cosy catch-ups to larger group bookings, we’ll help you celebrate the season in style. Book your Christmas table or festive party today and get ready to celebrate!

Private Hire, Birthdays and Events

Our mezzanine is perfect for dining and can be hired for private use. This area has 36 covers for seated dining or we can accommodate up to 50 people with a buffet. This space is on a separate level but within the main bar so that you still get the atmosphere of the main bar area.

Our theatre bar is the perfect setting for a sociable gathering. Ideal for dining or a more relaxed get-together, we are able to offer relaxed dining or our popular buffet in this space. The theatre bar is available to hire separately or with the theatre space on dates when no performances are programmed. Our theatre terrace is hidden away on the theatre floor and is included when you hire the theatre bar. Perfect for those summer evenings for some alfresco drinks or taking in some air during your function. This area has a canopy so can be used if the weather isn’t desirable.

Located on the top floor our intimate theatre is available for private hire on days when we do not have a performance playing. The entire top floor can be separated from the rest of the venue so it’s perfect for keeping your function all in one place. Wheelchair accessible with a maximum capacity of 100 and the potential to also hire the Theatre Bar with a maximum capacity of 65 this space is great for parties, celebrations, conferences or a meeting room with a difference. As a professional theatre, the space is well equipped with theatre lighting, sound and presentation facilities. Hire prices for the theatre space are based on the individual requirements of each booking. The theatre floor also features a roof terrace which is the perfect place to take in some fresh air during your event.

Whether you are having a business meeting, baby shower, celebration party, or wake, get in touch so we can help plan your event at the Town & Gown.
