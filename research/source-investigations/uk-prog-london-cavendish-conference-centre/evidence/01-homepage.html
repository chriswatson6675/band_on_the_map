Conferences

Conferences at 22 Duchess Mews

22 Duchess Mews — the Cavendish Conference Centre — is one of our two conference venues in the West End, a short walk from Oxford Circus, Regent's Park and Great Portland Street. Its tiered auditorium seats up to 250 delegates and is built for AGMs, financial results presentations, product launches and conferences, with an exhibition and catering area alongside and exclusive hire available.

Our other West End venue is 44 Hallam Street, five minutes away. For the Square Mile, see our conference venues in the City of London, or all our London conference venues in one place.
