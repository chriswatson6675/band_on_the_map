Frequently Asked Questions

How many event venues does Cavendish Venues have in London?

Cavendish Venues operates five London event venues: three in the City of London (1 America Square, The Minster Building and 1 Basinghall Avenue) and two in the West End (44 Hallam Street and 22 Duchess Mews). All five are within a short walk of at least two Underground stations, and all are owned and operated by us, so one enquiry gets you access to the whole portfolio.

What is the largest event Cavendish Venues can host?

Our largest single-room capacity is around 400 delegates theatre-style at 1 America Square, which houses our main conference hall. For events larger than 400 we split delegates across adjacent rooms with a live video link, or use the whole America Square building end-to-end. Smaller board-level meetings can be as intimate as two people around a private table — the range across the group is 2 to 450 delegates.

Do you provide in-house catering?

Yes. All catering across the five venues is prepared in-house by our own team, Mayfair Catering Services (MCAT). Halal, kosher, vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and full allergen protocols are covered as standard, not as a paid extra. Menus are seasonal and centred on British produce from named suppliers. Coffee is Fairtrade.

Are Cavendish Venues sustainable?

Cavendish Venues is a certified B Corporation and was recognised for sustainability at the 2026 Conference & Events Awards. All catering is prepared in-house from named suppliers, single-use plastics have been removed from delegate service, food waste goes to anaerobic digestion, and every venue is within walking distance of at least two Underground stations so delegates rarely need to drive.

How quickly can I get a quote?

We aim to come back with a first-pass quote within one working hour of receiving an enquiry with date, delegate number and event format. If you need firm availability held while you decide, we can put a soft hold on the room for 24 to 48 hours in most cases.

Browsing by format is one way in. You can also browse by place: all five London conference venues, the three conference venues in the City of London, or our West End venues.

More about Cavendish Venues in London

Our London Meeting, Conference and Event venues offer both traditional and contemporary conference rooms, from Grade II listed buildings to unique event spaces, with a variety of capacities. With venues located across Central London, we pride ourselves on the fact that our conference venues are superbly accessible by public transport.

Read more

All our London Meeting, Conference and Event venues are located within a 5-minute walk of an underground tube or mainline station. Areas served are the West End, Midtown and City of London. We have conference venues to hire near Oxford Street and Bond Street, Tottenham Court Road and Goodge Street, Holborn and Chancery Lane, Russell Square and Kings Cross, Fenchurch Street and Aldgate. There are two London conference venues next to the world-renowned BBC New Broadcasting House, each of these conference venues have capacities for up to 250 people. Another conference centre, with capacity of up to 270 guests, is available right next to the Tower of London, boasting a unique and historic feature: the Roman ruins of the London Wall, dating back to the 2nd or early 3rd century. For guests looking to stay in town overnight, there are plenty of choices – all our conference centres are surrounded by hotels.

Sustainability and environmental responsibility have always been central to the way we run our London Meeting, Conference and Event venues. We are now able to offer you completely Carbon Neutral events by offsetting the carbon generated by your conference and delegates’ travel. At each of our London conference venues we really want to improve all aspects of our operation. Therefore, we are running a scheme whereby we will plant a tree for any suggestion as to how we can improve; be it our website, our venue services, our meeting rooms, our conference rooms etc. If the suggestion is practicable and we adopt it, we will plant 10 trees.

We can help you create the perfect meeting, conference, training or event room layout for your event at one of our London conference venues. Experiment with different virtual room configurations ahead of your conference so that everything runs smoothly on the day. We have created various diagrams that can be shared with everyone involved, from catering to tech support to ensure that conference rooms are setup to your precise specification. We are proud of our continually improved London conference venues: 44 Hallam Street W1, 22 Duchess Mews W1, 1 America Square, EC3 and The Minster Building EC3 – visit our award-winning conference venues for their continuous achievement in exceptional service.
