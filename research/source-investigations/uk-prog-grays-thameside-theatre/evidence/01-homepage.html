Thameside Theatre tickets

You can buy tickets online at any time, or phone our box office on 0345 300 5264 from Tuesday to Friday between 10am - 1pm, and the last Saturday of each month between 10am - 1pm. Our box office counter on the ground floor is open at the same times.

About the theatre

Thameside Theatre is part of the Thameside Complex at the heart of Grays – read where to find us.

Thameside Theatre Technical Specification (PDF 3.63MB)

How to buy tickets

The easiest way to buy Thameside Theatre tickets is online:

Buy Thameside Theatre tickets

You can also buy tickets by phoning our box office. You can pay with any of these credit or debit card types: Mastercard, Visa, Maestro, Visa Electron.

Please read our conditions of ticket sales.

You don't need a ticket for children under 1 year-old – tell us if your booking includes a child of this age.

We welcome parties and will work to make your visit a success. You can contact our box office for details of discounts and other services.

If you need to book tickets for accessible seating or for a group, use this link: 

Group booking or accessible seating

Contact us

Thameside Theatre
Thameside Complex, Orsett Road, Grays, RM17 5DX
: 0345 300 5264
: thameside.theatre@thurrock.gov.uk

Follow us at facebook.com/thameside and twitter.com/TheThameside

If you have a ticket for a show that is postponed or cancelled, we will contact you – please do not contact us.

Where possible, when a show has to be postponed or cancelled we'll move it to a later date and transfer tickets automatically. If you can't attend the new date, we'll offer you a refund. If we cannot move a show to a new date, we'll issue refunds automatically and will let you know.
