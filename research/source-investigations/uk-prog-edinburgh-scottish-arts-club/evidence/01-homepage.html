The Club

We are a sociable Members Club full of arts enthusiasts and creatives in the heart of Edinburgh.

With a 150 year history – we pride ourselves as a classic club with a contemporary twist.
We host events, workshops, classes, groups, exhibitions and at the heart of it all, is our members.

Whether you’re popping in for a creative session, a meeting, lunch, an event or a quiet moment ….
we’re here to welcome you.

With a refresh of the club interior underway we are buzzing with renewed energy and creativity – promoting and supporting the arts community with passion and care. The Arts are a vital part of our Scottish way of life and history.
