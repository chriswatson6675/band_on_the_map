Help Make our St. Paul’s Church Home Fit For The Future

Can you help us raise £10,000 to make Circomedia warmer, more sustainable, and fit for an exciting future?

For more than 21 years, Circomedia’s home at St. Paul’s Church has been a place where people of all ages have discovered confidence, creativity and extraordinary physical skills through circus. Every day, hundreds of children, young people, students, and professional artists come through our doors to train, rehearse and perform.

But now we need your help to keep those doors open. Every winter, keeping our historic building warm enough for safe training and performance becomes more difficult. That’s why we need to raise £10,000 before the colder months arrive.

About us

Founded in 1984, Circomedia is a world-renowned centre for contemporary circus and physical theatre training in the UK. We believe in promoting circus in Bristol and beyond.

Our BTEC, Degree and Diploma courses combine technical and physical training with the development of creative thinking, performance skills and professional business knowledge. As a result, our graduates are confident artists who challenge perceptions of contemporary circus and physical performance.

We curate a fascinating and innovative performance programme from our venue in the heart of Bristol. In the grand Georgian surroundings of St Paul’s Church, you can discover some of the most exciting circus, physical theatre, and dance companies from the UK and beyond.

Our adult classes cover a range of aerial and acrobatic disciplines and are designed to cater to all levels. We are home to one of the largest Youth Circus in the UK with over 500 students.

Introducing Our Academy

Circomedia Academy is a place to train, create and grow as a contemporary circus and physical theatre performer. From age 16, you can begin your journey on our BTEC course and progress through to a full degree in Circus and Physical Theatre. Alongside this, we offer flexible diploma programmes ranging from 3 to 36 months, allowing you to continue developing and specialising at every stage of your career. Delivered in partnership with City of Bristol College and Bath Spa University, our training takes place in Bristol, a city renowned for its vibrant performance culture and strong links to the wider circus and theatre world.

Hire Our Venue

Our dramatic Georgian church in the centre of Bristol offers a beautiful venue for your wedding, Christmas party, conference, or another special occasion. Youth Circus

Circomedia Youth Circus is one of the biggest in the UK with over 500 students. It offers children aged 2 – 21 the chance to enter the extraordinary world of contemporary circus and physical theatre through weekly classes and a series of circus camps held throughout the year. Our groups cater to all different ages and abilities and you don’t have to have previous experience!

Led by our professional circus tutors, our after-school sessions offer a varied programme of acrobatics, aerial skills, manipulation and equilibristics, and performance skills. Children and young people will try everything from diabolo to trapeze over the course of the year,  and develop their coordination, build physical and mental resilience and make friends in a relaxed, creative environment.
