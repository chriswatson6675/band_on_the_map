An update on racing at Chelmsford City Racecourse

Chelmsford City Racecourse remains without a licensed racing operator at the venue at present. This means racing at Chelmsford is currently unable to resume.

We are committed to finding a long-term solution to bring racing back to Chelmsford and are reviewing the most suitable options; however, in the immediate future, the delivery of race fixtures remains on hold.

As a result, bookings for all upcoming August and September events have cancelled. Customers will be contacted directly with refund details. We are also pausing ticket sales for all other events while we work to establish a long-term solution.

We apologise for the cancellations and refund delays customers have experienced. This unprecedented situation has removed the racecourse’s primary source of revenue while we process a very large number of refunds. While a significant proportion have been completed, not all have been processed as quickly as we would like. We understand your frustration and remain committed to fulfilling all refunds as promptly as possible.

Thank you for your support.

Best Racecourse in Essex

Horse racing is the second largest spectator sport in the UK and is intertwined with British culture and identity. Chelmsford City Racecourse, which opened in 2015, quickly has become one of the busiest racecourses in the UK and continues to be a number one choice among racegoers.

The racecourse is set in the stunning Essex countryside, nearby to London. If you’re looking for a racecourse to visit whilst you’re in London, Chelmsford City Racecourse is one of the best. Chelmsford City Racecourse offers horseracing all year round, has some of the best restaurants in Essex, is an event venue for many occasions and hosts exciting concerts and festivals. View the range of events we have coming up and plan your visit to one of the UK’s finest racecourses set in Essex now.
