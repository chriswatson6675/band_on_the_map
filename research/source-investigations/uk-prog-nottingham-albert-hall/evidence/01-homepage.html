A Unique and Inspiring Conference Venue...

Nestled in Nottingham’s historical Castle Quarter, independently and family run since 1990, the Albert Hall is highly reputed for its unique conference and event facilities.

Event venue location info and gallery

Albert Hall Conference Centre

Nottingham, East Midlands, UK


Built at the turn of the century and refurbished to give versatility and choice we are able to host the needs of the modern business world with large global conferences, AGM's, small meetings, exhibitions, fashion shows and corporate dinners. The more traditional architecture within the venue offers the perfect stage to host concerts, grand banquets, sporting events and examinations followed by the presentation and awards ceremony; the range of events we entertain are limitless...
