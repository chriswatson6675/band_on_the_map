More than just a space

The Sustainable Studio is a unique creative Event and workspace for the Community, makers, artists, and creative businesses. It is designed to nurture a positive community of like-minded people through an open and supportive environment.

The Studio is designed around the needs of all the creative people and businesses who work within the space. A communal kitchen and seating area create a flexible approach to working, meaning you can stick to your private studio for some quiet time or hang out and work more casually at the kitchen table. We encourage you to work in a way that best suits you. Pop the kettle on, brew a coffee and make time to connect, collaborate, create and learn.  Ultimately, our mission is to see you thrive.

We are a collective of makers, emerging artists and creative startups working together to promote the creative industries in Wales.
