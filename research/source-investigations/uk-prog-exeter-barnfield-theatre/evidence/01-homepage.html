Welcome! Why you’re here…

Hello and welcome! You’ve landed here because Exeter Northcott Theatre has recently taken over the management of the Barnfield Theatre.

You’ll be able to book tickets for events at the Barnfield on this website and through the Exeter Northcott Box Office, via phone on 01392 72 63 63 or email boxoffice@exeternorthcott.co.uk. The Barnfield box office phone number is no longer in use.

You can use the links below to find out more about our plans for the Barnfield, or go straight to our What’s On pages to find out about upcoming events.
