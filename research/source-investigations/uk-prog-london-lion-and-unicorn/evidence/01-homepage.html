OPENS TONIGHT DO NOT ATTEMPT THIS CONVERSATION 2-6 June | 7.30pm Grief, pain, frustration and… football? Welcome to the NHS. Come on in. Nneka is a resident doctor finishing another exhausting shift in an overstretched hospital. Just as she’s about to leave, she’s asked to lead a DNAR conversation with the relative of a patient she has never met, about a patient she doesn’t know, under impossible time pressure. Set entirely within a single encounter, Do Not Attempt This Conversation is a sharp, funny and deeply human new play that explores the reality of end-of-life decision making in a healthcare system stretched to breaking point. As grief collides with bureaucracy, compassion and exhaustion, this intimate drama asks what it really means to remain humane within an inhumane system. ⚠️ Content Advisory: Strong language; themes of a sexual nature; serious illness; end-of-life decision-making (DNAR); grief; death 🎟 Tickets £15 | Concessions £13 📍 Lion & Unicorn Theatre, Kentish Town 🔗 Tickets via link in bio #OffWestEnd #LondonTheatre #NewWriting #FringeTheatre #NHS DoNotAttemptThisConversation LionAndUnicornTheatre

OPENING NIGHT. What happens when you stare into the abyss… and it offers you a seat? Five opens tonight at the Lion & Unicorn Theatre, running until Sunday. A darkly funny psychological thriller about true crime, obsession, and the monsters we create. A true-crime podcaster walks into a prison expecting a story. He doesn’t expect to become part of one. Over a series of recorded interviews, a charming and deeply unsettling serial killer draws podcast host Morris further into his world. 🎭 WE ARE HIRING | DUTY MANAGERS 🎭 The Lion & Unicorn Theatre in Kentish Town is looking for new Duty Managers to join our team for an immediate start as we gear up for a busy summer. The role: Duty Managers are the front-facing representatives of the theatre and are responsible for Front of House, Box Office, Health & Safety, and supporting visiting companies during performances. Ideal commitment: • Around one shift per week AND • One weekend shift per month We are looking for people who: • Have experience in theatre, live events, FOH or customer-facing roles • Are calm, organised and confident working independently • Enjoy supporting artists and audiences • Are reliable and good with people The details • Casual freelance shifts • Typical shifts are evenings • Paid at £14 per hour (minimum £42 per shift)  • Friendly, supportive fringe theatre environment with support and some free rehearsal space for you as an additional perk if you are a theatre artist looking to make work for yourself. If you are interested, please email lionandunicorn@proforca.co.uk with a short note about your experience and availability. 📍 Lion & Unicorn Theatre, Kentish Town 🎟️ Fringe theatre, new writing, bold work To Apply: send an email to lionandunicorn@proforca.co.uk with a short covering letter and a copy of your CV. 🎭 OPENING NIGHT – DRAGONFLY GIRLS A bittersweet and deeply moving new play exploring family, care, and what happens when the role that defines you begins to slip away.
