London’s eclectic nightlife scene attracts not only the local area skaters, but also visitors from across the globe.

The Tottenham area of London is, and always has been, well known for its history of entertainment and “the place to be seen” for a night out in London. Over the decades large groups of people would visit the world renowned gardens in this area for music, company and refreshments, Roller Nation continues this trend with visitors from across the world coming to the Tottenham based venue to enjoy a night out on wheels.

Join us with your group of friends and enjoy access to our Indoor Licensed Bar and Diner, outdoor seating area and full on disco, DJ and roller skating fun.

Roller Nation, The Home of The Roller Disco’s venue has transport links across the city of London. With easy access to the London underground transport lines and a 24/7 local bus route that runs throughout the city.
