OPENING HOURS

You can book for Byre performances, screenings, and events any time at byretheatre.com, by phone on 01334 475 000, or in person at the box office.

Box Office is open 10.00am-4.30pm Monday to Saturday, except when there is an evening event, when it remains open until after the performance starts. It is closed on Sundays, unless there is a performance, when box office opens two hours before the start time.

Refreshments at the Byre

Preshow and interval drinks, snacks, and, of course, delicious ice cream are available at the Byre. We’re continuing to develop our food and drink offer, and we’d welcome your thoughts as we do so. Email us at byreboxoffice@st-andrews.ac.uk if you’d like to share your experience or suggestions.

Social Study Space, level 2

A popular, informal study space, available to anyone looking for an alternative to home or the library. Open Monday – Saturday 10.00am – 4.30pm.
