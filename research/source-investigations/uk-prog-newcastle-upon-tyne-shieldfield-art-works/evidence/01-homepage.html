    What is Shieldfield Art Works?

    Shieldfield Art Works (SAW) is a project of the Methodist Church in Newcastle upon Tyne which is seeking the good of the city through creative practice.

    We run a programme of art exhibitions, events and regular creative groups from our gallery and workshop space in Shieldfield.
