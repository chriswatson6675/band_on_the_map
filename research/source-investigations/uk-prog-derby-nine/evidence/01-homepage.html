Before You Arrive

Condition of Entry

  • 01 No sportswear including sports shorts.
  • 02 No baseball caps / beanie hats.
  • 03 No work clothes including hi-vis.
  • 04 No fancy dress that others may perceive to be offensive.
  • 05 No inflatables.
  • 06 Helium balloons must be left at the main entrance and collected on departure.
  • 07 No hooded clothing to be worn in the venue. Cloak room available.
  • 08 When requested, we may search personal bags as a routine precaution.
  • 09 On entrance to the venue, we may ask to perform a body search. This will always be carried out in full view of our CCTV.
  • 10 Metal detectors may be used on guests on entry as a precaution.
  • 11 Personal belongings are guest's responsibility. Any lost, stolen or damaged property cannot be held responsible.

We reserve the right to refuse entry to anyone we believe may be intoxicated, under the influence or doesn't comply with the above conditions of entrance.

This is to safeguard all of our guests so that everyone can enjoy NINE safely.
