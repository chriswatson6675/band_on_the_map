Step Into Night Tales

A cornerstone of East London nightlife for over 12 years, our space features three unique spaces designed to host revellers from open to close.

Our lively, covered and heated terrace is perfect for enjoying crafted cocktails and premium beers from early evening through late night. Open from 6pm every weekend, the terrace seats up to 200 guests and is home to Sons of Slice Pizza Restaurant.

Arch 14 houses our iconic Palm Bar and is ideal for group bookings of all sizes, from intimate celebrations to full private hire.

Finally, our world-class club space lays the foundations for an unforgettable party. Featuring a Funktion-One sound system, an embracing dance floor, and finely tuned line-ups of the world’s best DJs.
