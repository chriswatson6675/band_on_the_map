Venue Hire

DanceEast’s home is the buzzy Jerwood DanceHouse, a purpose-built building on Ipswich’s waterfront. Following a successful capital campaign, the DanceHouse opened its doors to the public in October 2009.

This beautiful facility houses classes, performances and other activities for people to enjoy. We are proud of our amazing building and want to share it with everyone. With easy transport links to London, you will find:

  • A 200-seat studio theatre
  • Four world-class dance studios
  • A high-spec digital suite
  • Our family friendly café
  • And more!

Ideally suited to a wide range of events including performances, AGMs, meetings, product launches, award ceremonies, corporate hospitality and parties, the Jerwood DanceHouse is an inspiring, adaptable and accessible building.

Our three studios have wall barres, mirrors and cubby-holes for bags. Each enjoys natural ventilation with operable windows and mechanical comfort cooling when required. The James Hehir studio theatre, our largest space, is ideal for performances and group activities as well as conferences and seminars. Our digital suite is a state-of-the-art facility suited to both recording and live broadcast.

Contact us to hire our venue for your own event.

Price is on application (based on hourly rate) and various rates apply for artists, commercial, artistic, charitable and government funded organisations. Please note that as we run a regular programme of classes, studio space is subject to availability.

 

Take a look inside the Jerwood DanceHouse through our Virtual Tour.

 

ASHTON STUDIO

Space: 12m x 12m
Max. capacity: 60

This double height studio offers the perfect space to host meetings, presentations, breakout sessions and training courses.

The space is equipped with a full sound system and video viewing facilities.

DIGITAL SUITE

The Ashton Studio is home to our state-of-the-art Digital Suite and Green Screen, ideal for chroma keyed recordings, live broadcasts, videography and photography. The digital studio can accommodate up to 25 people and the space is fully equipped with a green screen, lighting, Bluetooth audio and WiFi. The screen can be easily set for basic horizon work and 360 degree for a single continous shot.

JAMES HEHIR THEATRE

Space: 16m x 20m
Max. capacity: 200

The 15m x 12m stage provides ample space for presentations, panel discussions, AGM’s and conferences. The seating can be folded back to allow the space to be set up cabaret style.

 

RED SHOE STUDIO A/B

Space: 20m x 12m
Max capacity: full studio 120 people and split studio 60 people

Long and rectangular, this double height studio can be divided into two spaces by a sliding acoustic partition. Ideal for networking, exhibitions and training sessions.
The space is equipped with a full sound system and video viewing facilities.

 

THE BARRE

Max. capacity: 75 seated / 150 standing

Ideal for a drinks reception, Christmas parties or simply to catch up with a colleague for after work drinks.

 

THE CAFÉ MEZZANINE

Max. capacity: 60

Complete with a fully licenced bar, the Café Mezzanine offers the ideal space for small meetings, get togethers and corporate receptions.

 

THE RETREAT

Space: 4.5m x 5.4m
Max. capacity: 6

This bright and airy double-height studio is filled with natural sunlight complete with our smaller green screen ideal for photoshoots and face to camera work. The Retreat also has a full sound system and video viewing facilities with Bluetooth, WiFi, Zoom and presentation facilities.

 

THE WHISTLER GALLERY

Space: 20m long x 2.5m deep
Max. capacity: 35-50

Ideal for drinks receptions and networking events. Guests can admire the local artwork displayed in the gallery.

 

TREATMENT ROOM

Our Treatment Room is a private room and includes a therapy table.

WELLBEING STUDIO

This studio is perfect for Yoga, Pilates and fitness classes. It comes equipped with a full sound system and video viewing facilities with Bluetooth, WiFi, Zoom and presentation facilities.
