Seathwaite

Duddon Valley


The Newfield is a lovely cosy country pub with lots to offer from real ale, good country pub grub and not to mention we are surrounded with the beautiful fells; Dow Crag, Caw, Harter fell, Wallabarra Crag. 



It really is an amazing part of the world with plenty of exploring to be done.
