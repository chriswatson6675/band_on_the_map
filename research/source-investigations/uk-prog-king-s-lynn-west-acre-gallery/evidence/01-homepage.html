Welcome to West Acre Gallery: a Crowdfunded community art gallery and exhibition space for hire, nestled in the ruins of 900-year-old West Acre priory, Norfolk.

Our summer exhibition season 2024 has now ended. Thank you to everyone who’s visited West Acre Gallery this year, and supported our rural community venue. We are now transitioning to new ownership. Watch this space for our autumn programme of events, coming soon.

Inspired by the wealth of talented artists and artisans living in west Norfolk, West Acre Gallery has evolved in response to the need for a multifaceted space dedicated to art, with grassroots, participatory work at its heart. READ MORE
