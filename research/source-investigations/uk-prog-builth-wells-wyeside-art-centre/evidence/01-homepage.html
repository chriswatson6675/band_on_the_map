All Events At Wyeside

Practical Magic 2 (12A)

On UK Release! 11 September - 4 October

The Owens sisters are back!

A multi generational family of witches, cursed to be loveless for centuries, attempts to break the spell by confronting dark secrets and sacrificing for each other.

Book Now

Shaun The Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom (U)

18 September - 29 October

On Halloween Eve at Mossy Bottom Farm, excitement turns to dismay when the clumsy Farmer ruins the pumpkin patch.

Shaun tries fixing it with science experiments, but things go wrong when a wild beast appears in the woods.

Book Now

One Night Only (15)

26 September - 1 October

In a world in which premarital sex is only legal for one night a year, a frantic night-long quest to find a match leads to a chance encounter between two strangers.

Book Now

NT Live: The Misanthrope (15)

Tuesday 22 September | 7pm

Telling the truth isn’t always that simple. Alice, a brilliant novelist, despises the carefully constructed lies of modern society.

But the more she challenges those around her, the fiercer the backlash becomes.

Soon, she must confront the price of speaking her truth in a world that would rather silence her.

Book Now

Wyeside Arts Centre

Situated in the heart of Builth Wells, on Castle Street, along the main High Street, within easy walking distance from the bus station. Wyeside is situated on the bank of the River Wye, an iconic Grade II listed building.

ADDRESS - Castle Street, Builth Wells, LD2 3BN

PHONE - 01982 552 555

EMAIL - boxoffice@wyeside.co.uk
