Europe's #1 For Ice Leisure

Here's a glimpse of the icy escapades you can indulge in, 365 days a year:

Public Ice Skating: Glide across our glistening ice rinks during our public skating sessions. For those looking to challenge themselves, the Ice Card Membership offers exceptional perks and discounts.

Ice Skating Lessons: Whether you're a novice or a seasoned skater, our range of ice skating lessons cater to all ages and skills. From the fundamentals to figure-skating artistry, there's a class for every enthusiast.

Ice Hockey: Immerse yourself in the fast-paced action of ice hockey. Join our lessons to master the game, or experience the thrill of a match during one of our exciting events.

Celebrate with our ‘Out of this World’ Birthday Parties: Celebrate your special day with a twist! Our party package includes an ice skating session & food to suit everyone's needs. In addition, you will have a dedicated party host who will take care of everything, leaving you free to enjoy the ice skating fun.

Patch Ice Sessions: For figure skaters seeking focused practice time, Patch sessions provide a serene environment to hone your skills and routines.

Fuel the Fun with Food & Drink: Available at every rink, we offer a variety of hot food and snacks, whether that's from our vending machines or the café. Keep your energy up while enjoying your time on the ice!

Embark on a frosty adventure at Planet Ice and craft memories that will last a lifetime. Ready to take to the ice?
