Who we are

The Beaufort Theatre & Café Bar is the premier destination for entertainment in Blaenau Gwent. Situated at the top of the Ebbw Valley, The Beaufort Theatre & Café Bar looks out across the community that it serves.

The Beaufort Theatre & Café Bar

The Beaufort Theatre & Café Bar is a volunteer-led community arts centre in Ebbw Vale, South Wales, that provides opportunities for performance, participation and coming together through an expanding programme of events and activities. Click support to help us keep the theatre alive!
