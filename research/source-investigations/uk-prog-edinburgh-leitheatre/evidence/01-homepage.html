    Church Hill Theatre, 25th - 28th November 2026

Welcome to Leitheatre

Our next full length production is The Tale of Eliza Scrooge, Church Hill Theatre, 25th - 28th November 2026. This is a whole new take on Charles Dickens A Christmas Carol, written for us by Lewis C. Baird who will also direct. Lewis previously directed Quiz for us in May 2025 so we are excited to have him on board again! He also braves taking this show on in collaboration with Forth Children's Theatre - we are so excited to work on this as a big theatre family!

Leitheatre is an amateur theatre group based in Edinburgh. We usually produce three full length productions per year, including one in the Edinburgh Festival Fringe. We also take part in the Scottish Community Drama Association (SCDA) One Act Play Festival. Our productions take place in the Church Hill Theatre, the Studio Theatre and Inverleith St Serf's Church Centre. We also produce a Burns Show in January to celebrate the birth of Scotland's Bard, Robert Burns. This show is performed for other organisations such as Golf Clubs, Care Homes and other social clubs. If you are interested in booking the Burns show, please get in touch by emailing info@leitheatre.com. 

On the Our History page, you will read how our roots are in Leith but we perform in venues and theatres all over Edinburgh. We are not connected to Leith Theatre, in Ferry Road.  You can find information about that organisation at the Leith Theatre Trust .
