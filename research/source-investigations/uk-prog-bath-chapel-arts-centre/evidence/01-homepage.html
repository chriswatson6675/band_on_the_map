All our gigs are seated Cabaret (around small cafe tables), unless it is stated otherwise on the event as ‘STANDING‘ with seats around both sides of the Auditorium.

​Whenever possible, tables will be automatically reserved for parties of ‘4 or more’ if they are purchased under one name.  However, if they are purchased separately, please email me with the ‘full names’ of the persons who have ‘booked the ticket/s’ and I will do my utmost to accommodate you.

We will continuing with:

​​​One way system to the toilets downstairs with a mirror to see who is coming up or down.

​​Thorough cleaning of the venue prior to each event with our Anti-bacterial & Anti-viral solutions.

Jan Cottington – Centre Manager & Chapel Arts Team
