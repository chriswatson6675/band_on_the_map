Museum Late: Good harmonies, misbegotten words and pre-ripped jeans

Guest curated by Alice Theobald, this Museum Late event brings together artists whose work explores the roles we inherit, construct and perform — on screen, on stage and in everyday life.

24 September - 24 September 2026

Book now

Living by the Rule: Contemporary meets Medieval

Exploring the idea of routine and regulation, this exhibition reflects on the ‘rules’ that we live by today, through a dialogue between medieval experiments in a different way to live and modern reflections upon how life is organised.

16 May - 4 October 2026

Universal Ticket

Play Power

Exploring the concept of play in human cultures, Play Power reflects on the broader significance of play and how this is integrated into our daily lives. Would we live a more meaningful life if play were a central focus of day-to-day living?

16 May - 4 October 2026

Universal Ticket

Sculpture Park

Free

Joy Like Time

Joy Like Time explores how memory, ritual and renewal intersect through the work of internationally renowned artists Marina Abramović, Kalliopi Lemos and Gillian Wearing.

20 June - 15 November 2026

14 books exploring nature, connection and care

'Tree of Life' is a tactile and immersive installation, emerging as a universal symbol of the interconnectedness of all life. Artist Seyi Adelekun's reading list explores the symbolism and meanings of the Tree of Life and brings together plant folklore, herbalism, foraging, ecology, spirituality and decolonial thought.

SEE MORE

Interpretations

In this podcast series, second-year UEA Writing students respond to an artwork or aspect of the Sainsbury Centre Collection, exploring the personal connections, questions and ideas it raises for them.

SEE MORE

Worship, learning and hospitality: Norwich Cathedral and the Benedictine Rule in the 21st century

For its first 450 years, Norwich Cathedral was run by monks who followed the Rule of St. Benedict, before the monastic community was officially dissolved in 1538, during the Reformation. St. Benedict's Rule continues to influence the life of Norwich Cathedral and its community. We hear from The Very Revd Dr Andrew Braddock, Dean of Norwich.

SEE MORE

Explained: The history of Monopoly

Listen to our short history of the Monopoly game, as we learn that the game originated long before the board we know today, with The Landlord’s Game, the long-forgotten precursor to Monopoly, patented by Lizzie Magie in 1904.
