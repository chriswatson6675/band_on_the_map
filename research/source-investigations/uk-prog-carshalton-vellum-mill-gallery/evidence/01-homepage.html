Welcome to Vellum Mill Gallery

Vellum Mill Gallery is situated in Mill Lane right on the River Wandle in the historic town of Carshalton, only a stones throw from Central London by train. Surrounded by some fantastic places to eat and drink, and with easy access by bus, train or Car.

Our building was originally built as a snuff mill in the mid to late 1700’s and later in life turned to Vellum parchment making, hence the name Vellum Mill.

We are a family run gallery specialising in local Art and Crafts, We often hold special events and exhibitions throughout the year and run workshops along with our artists.

The gallery is available for hire to artists as well as stocking work on a commission basis.

Open Monday to Friday 9:00am – 4:00pm, for weekend opening or occasional differing times to that above please see our social media.
