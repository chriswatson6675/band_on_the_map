The iconic West End and Broadway smash-hit, Legally Blonde The Musical, is back in fashion with a huge new production on London’s biggest stage.

What? Like it’s hard?

Starring Amber Davies* (Strictly Come Dancing, The Great Gatsby and Pretty Woman) as Elle Woods, Legally Blonde The Musical is the ultimate feel-good rom-com, based on the beloved, award-winning film starring Reese Witherspoon and Jennifer Coolidge.

Follow Elle as she swaps the Californian coast for the halls of Harvard Law, only to discover she’s seriously underestimated. Packed with hit songs, iconic choreography and plenty of pink, this hilarious musical proves that ‘being true to yourself never goes out of style’.

From the team behind the record-breaking revival of Kinky Boots, with award-winning director Nikolai Foster (Grease, The Wizard of Oz) Legally Blonde The Musical arrives at the London Coliseum for just 9 weeks from 22 March 2027.
