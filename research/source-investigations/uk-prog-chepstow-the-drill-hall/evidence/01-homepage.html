Welcoming You To

The Drill Hall Chepstow

The Drill Hall stands as a vibrant, volunteer-driven hub for our local community, combining both a centre for the arts and a focal point for communal engagement. Nestled in the heart of lower Chepstow, this cultural gem is conveniently located in close proximity to a treasure trove of attractions, including the historic Chepstow Castle, the informative Chepstow Museum, and the serene Riverside Park adorned with its charming gardens and Bandstand.
