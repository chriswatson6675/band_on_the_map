The Angels Experience

Welcome to Angels Behind the Mask, where refined glamour meets discreet indulgence, offering an elevated world of captivating performers, bespoke VIP experiences, and an atmosphere defined by sophistication and exclusivity.

Girls

At Angels, our private dance experience is the pinnacle of refined indulgence. Our captivating performers offer a bespoke, one-to-one performance tailored to each guest, delivered with elegance, confidence and irresistible allure. Relax in the comfort and discretion of our exclusive VIP suites as you immerse yourself in an intimate and unforgettable experience designed for true connoisseurs of glamour.

Drinks

From timeless cocktails to the finest champagnes, rare spirits and curated wines, our bar caters to the most discerning tastes. Our skilled mixologists prepare each drink with finesse and attention to detail, ensuring every sip is smooth and memorable. Unwind in refined surroundings, savour your chosen pour, and enjoy the captivating ambience that defines an evening at Angels.

Pole Shows

At Angels, our signature pole showcases are an experience not to be overlooked. Our accomplished performers combine strength, poise and refined allure, delivering a breathtaking display of athletic grace and captivating stage presence. Whether you are a seasoned admirer of the art or discovering it for the first time, the performance promises to leave a lasting impression.

Venue

At Angels, our surroundings reflect the same refined elegance as our performances. The venue is styled with luxurious contemporary décor, plush seating, and an atmosphere of understated opulence. Designed to complement our elevated entertainment, the space features intimate lounges, exclusive VIP suites, and immersive sound and lighting that create a truly distinguished after-dark experience.
