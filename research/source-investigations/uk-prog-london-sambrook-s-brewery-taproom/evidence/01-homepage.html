Sambrook’s Brewery Taproom

Our brewery taproom, is the embodiment of everything we think great hospitality should be.  Set in a unique converted brewery which spans two floors and a secluded outdoor terrace, it is an amazing place to meet, socialise and have fun. Serving our own beers, brewed on-site, alongside great Neapolitan style pizza, we invite you to come down and enjoy one of the best* bars in London.

We have converted a Victorian brewery into a unique space, which blends the charm of our modern working brewery with the heritage of our historic site. 

Set under the chimney which dominates the Wandsworth skyline, our downstairs bar is warm and inviting.  Our beautiful mezzanine, with its own bar, is perfect for sporting events, birthday parties or large social gatherings. And we think that our outdoor terrace is the best in Wandsworth.

*Evening Standard top 10 bars and restaurants in 2021
