The HiFi Club is a nightclub and live music venue in Leeds, England

Three times winner of both the 'Best Nightclub' & 'Best Live Music Venue' at the annual Leeds Bar & Club Awards.

We are a friendly & welcoming spot with an emphasis on quality music & drinks. We have a range of DJ led & live music nights based around a variety of musical genres including Soul, Funk, Hip Hop, Reggae & Disco, sitting alongside our ever popular Saturday evening Comedy Club.
