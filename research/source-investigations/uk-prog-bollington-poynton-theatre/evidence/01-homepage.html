Future Production

A Bedful of Foreigners - Mon 23rd to Sat 28th November 2026

Mistaken identity, a monk, a nun and a deadly radiator all provide the off the wall happenings of a crazy holiday spot.

More details on the productions page.

Big Big Sky - Mon 8th to Sat 13th February 2027

A beautifully tender play which explores nature’s influence on love, friendship and family.

More details on the productions page.

The Unfriend - Mon 19th to Sat 24th April 2027

An hilarious dark comedy by Steven Moffat (Doctor Who, Sherlock) that takes a satirical look at middle-class England’s disastrous instinct to always appear nice.

More details on the productions page.

A Midsummer Night's Dream - Mon 21st to 26th June 2027

A comedy where four Athenian lovers, a troupe of amateur actors, and royalty get lost in an enchanted forest. Chaos ensues as mischievous fairies cast magical love spells.

More details on the productions page.
