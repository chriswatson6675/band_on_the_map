We’re delighted to announce that Iain Irving is bringing his words and work into the space for this summer showing. His lifelong connections to art and love are expansive and alive.

“In life, I like to do DIY (do it yourself) but I enjoy it more when I DIT (do it together). Life being work, play and being with others. These others can be lovers, friends, family or just people you’ve met. Sharing life’s tasks make for a more joyous personal feeling and outcome… mostly.

My work draws from, and visualises, particular relationships, narratives and contexts from my life and background. There is a through-line of some sort of self-portraiture.”

Iain was born in Edinburgh in 1961. He studied product design and later on achieved an MPhil. Mostly, he has worked in teams in his roles in galleries and academia. He has independently curated a number of exhibitions which always required collaboration and compromise. He lived and worked, and raised his family, in Aberdeenshire for many years, returning to Edinburgh in 2021, where he started to make his own work.

Born in 1999, and based in Scotland, McGuire has an interdisciplinary approach, dancing between performance, happenings, spoken word and drawing. Their work is transportable, alternative and ecological. McGuire has a background in dance, radio and community-building.
