Beauty & The Beast: Panto 2026

A tale as old as Fife in the heart of Kirkcaldy

Beauty and the Beast is coming to the Adam Smith Theatre in 2026. Get ready for a magical Christmas pantomime packed with laughs, dazzling sets and show-stopping performances the whole family will love.

Book early to get the best choice of seats and prices, and don’t miss our fantastic preview performances on 4 and 5 December – a brilliant way to enjoy the show for less.

Whether it’s your first visit or a festive tradition, don’t miss one of Fife’s most popular Christmas shows.

Make your visit even more magical

🍹 Drinks, please!

Why not skip the queue and pre-order your drinks before the show? You’re more than welcome to bring them into the auditorium and sip away while you enjoy the panto fun.

🥪 Feeling peckish?

Our café has you covered!

  • Tues–Thurs: open 10am–3pm
  • Fri & Sat: open 10am–5pm

Perfect for a pre-show bite or a cuppa with friends. Download the menu here.

👜 Got a buggy or pram?

No worries – while storage is a bit tight, we’ve got space for buggies and prams in the Beveridge Suite. Just ask one of our friendly team and we’ll help you out.

🪑 Booster seats

We have a small number of booster seats (first come, first served), or feel free to bring your own.

🎉 Birthday or special occasion?

We love a good celebration! If someone in your group has a birthday or special day, let our Box Office team know before the show – and the cast might just give them a special shout-out!

Accessible performances

Relaxed

Relaxed performances create a more welcoming space for anyone who prefers a gentler theatre experience. You can come and go as needed, the house lights stay dim, and we tone down or remove any loud noises or flashing lights.

BSL interpreted

Everyone’s welcome at our BSL performances. A British Sign Language interpreter is on stage throughout the show, ensuring Deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences can enjoy every word and moment.

We also have an infrared hearing system available — ask a member of staff at the Box Office to find out more.

Sponsorship

Support our charity panto night

OnFife is a charity, and our Charity Panto Night helps local children, families and community groups experience the joy and magic of live theatre.

We’re looking for local businesses to join us in making a difference. In return for your support, we’ll work with you to create a sponsorship package that offers valuable marketing and PR opportunities while showcasing your commitment to the local community.

Together, we can make Christmas a little more magical for people across Fife. ✨

Thursday 10am – 6pm

Box office opens one hour before performances.

Located on Bennochy Road, Kirkcaldy. Short walk from Kirkcaldy train station and bus stops.

Parking: 3 Blue Badge spaces in front of building. Limited on-site parking down right-hand side. Additional free parking at Kirkcaldy Galleries / Kirkcaldy train station (3 min walk).

Get Directions

Ramped access from main entrance to foyer, box office, Design Suite and café. Lift to upper floor (Auditorium, Beveridge Suite, The Exchange) and stair lift to lower floor (The Advantage).

4 wheelchair spaces in main auditorium. Row J is step-free — all other rows require steps. Infrared hearing system with receivers and neck loops at box office.

Accessible toilets on ground and first floor. Changing Places facility on ground level (radar key).

Guide and assistance dogs welcome.
