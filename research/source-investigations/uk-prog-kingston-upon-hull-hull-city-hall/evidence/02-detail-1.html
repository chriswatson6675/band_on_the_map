    Hull City Hall Venue Tour - 2026

    When
    28 Nov - 23 Jan 2027
    Genre
    Tours
    Audience
    Venue
    Hull City Hall
    Join us for a tour behind the scenes of one of East Yorkshire's premier venues. With a dedicated guide you will experience the rich and varied history of Hull City Hall. See one of the largest working organs in the country, the stunning Mortimer Suite resplendent with a beautiful domed ceiling, take a peek backstage*, and a chance to walk up the red carpet to the Main Hall. *Subject to show times and availability.
