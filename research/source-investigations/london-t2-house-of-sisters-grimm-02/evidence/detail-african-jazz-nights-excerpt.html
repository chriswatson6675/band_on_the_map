ights at iGOLI Bar

Every Friday at 9:15pm, join us at iGOLI Bar & Gallery for an intimate live Afro-jazz session with the cast of INALA.

Fresh from the stage of the Grammy-nominated production INALA, the artists gather after the show for a 45-minute late-night set of African jazz, rich harmonies, powerful voices and spontaneous musical interplay in a close, atmospheric setting.

When

Friday – 9:15pm
iGOLI Bar & Gallery
House of Sisters Grimm, London
