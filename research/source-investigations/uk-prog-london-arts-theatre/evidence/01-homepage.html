Welcome to The Arts

The Arts at Marble Arch: powered by TodayTix is a striking addition to London's Theatreland, set against one of the most iconic architectural structures in England.

It creates a destination for world-class entertainment set between Oxford Street and Park Lane bringing public space to life with an ambitious cultural offering.

The project was conceived by Louis Hartshorn and Brian Hook to embody the spirit and programme of the Arts Theatre during its major refurbishment, and will continue the Arts' creative output into its hundredth year in London's West End.
