British Airways American Express presents Ezra Collective & Friends

Get ready for an unforgettable night of music as Ezra Collective & Friends take over KOKO for a very special live performance celebrating 25 years of the British Airways American Express Cards.​
​
For 25 years, BA Amex  has been providing rich and rewarding experiences for Cardmembers, whether that’s collecting Avios for your next holiday or when you spend £15,000 in a Cardmembership year you can use a Companion Voucher to take a friend with you on your next trip to unlock new shared memories.¹

Formed in the youth clubs of London, Mercury Prize and BRIT Award-winners Ezra Collective have spent more than a decade making jazz joyful, urgent and undeniable. Their latest album, Here Because Of Hope (out 11 September 2026), is their most ambitious and personal record yet, tracing the movement of Black music across continents and generations from Africa, through the Caribbean, and home to the streets of London.​

Expect an electric night of live music as Ezra Collective are joined by a special line-up of friends and collaborators, bringing their unmistakable blend of jazz, hip-hop and global influences to the legendary KOKO stage.​

A one-night-only celebration of music, connection and 25 years of the British Airways American Express® Card.​
