Art V Motion Dance Academy

  • Health & Wellbeing

Art-V-Motion Dance Academy, an established dance academy based in Alton Assembly Rooms, making the most of the amazing sprung dance floor within the iconic main ballroom. With all lessons skilfully led by Vitor Lemos Fernandes, supported by his medal winning students, Art-V-Motion makes dancing accessible to all, no matter what age or ability

Whether you are a beginner to ballroom or latin dance, or a more experienced dancer who wants to improve – contact Vitor to book a session on either Monday, Tuesday or Thursday
