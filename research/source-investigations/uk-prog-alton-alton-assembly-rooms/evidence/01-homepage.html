About Alton Assembly Rooms

The Alton Assembly Rooms in Hampshire is a vibrant venue renowned for hosting live music tribute events that captivate audiences. With a capacity of up to 300, it’s the go-to destination for unforgettable performances celebrating iconic artists and bands.

With a busy programme of events to cater for all tastes all year, if you are looking for somewhere to hold your event that is a bit different, then you won’t be disappointed. Our friendly team is here to help you make your special day or evening a great success and you will be amazed by just how much of their enthusiasm comes with the booking!

FREE wi-fi available for Assembly Rooms users. We look forward to meeting you.
