The Crescent Theatre Company presents

A Christmas Carol

by Charles Dickens

11 - 19 Dec 2026

Book Tickets

Venue: Main House

Duration: 2 hours *

Price: From £17.00

‘I will honour Christmas in my heart, and try to keep it all the year.’

Dickens’ timeless masterpiece returns to the Crescent at Christmas.

Whilst Christmas cheer and goodwill abound across Victorian England, tight-fisted Ebenezer Scrooge goes to bed on Christmas Eve with no compassion or love in his heart. However, that night he is visited by the ghost of his old business partner Jacob Marley. Bound in chains for a lifetime of greed, Marley warns Scrooge it isn’t too late to change his fate, but only if he survives three more ghostly encounters…

This heart-warming tale of redemption is the perfect festive treat.
