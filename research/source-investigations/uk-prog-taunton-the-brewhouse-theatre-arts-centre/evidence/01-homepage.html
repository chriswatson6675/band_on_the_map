CAN YOU HELP US?

In 2027, we celebrate 50 years at the heart of our community. For half a century, our theatre has been a place to come together — to laugh, reflect, learn, and experience something totally unforgettable.

Now, as we look ahead to the next 50 years, we’re launching Fit at 50 — an ambitious campaign to modernise our venue and ensure it continues to serve the people of Somerset for generations to come.

Donate today and help us reach our £20,000 goal.
