FAQs

What days do you open?

We are open every Friday & Saturday.

Doors open at 22:00 each day. We are also open every Bank Holiday Sunday!

Times and dates may change – please check us on socials for more info

Do you have a dress code?

We know this is a difficult rule to set, which is why we try and keep it as simple as we can.
We ask that you arrive smartly dressed and stylish. So get glammed up and leave any scruffy trainers and sportswear at home! We work hard to make sure Labyrinth looks ready for the party and ask you do the same.
Remember dress to impress! No effort, No entry!

What time is last entry?

Last entry is 2.30am.
To avoid any disappointment we always advise to get down early to avoid any queues!

We reserve the right of admission and also to amend entry times.

LOST AN ITEM

Please contact us to arrange a time and date prior to collection without this we may not be available to retrieve your item.

After 14 days items will be discarded or given to charity.
