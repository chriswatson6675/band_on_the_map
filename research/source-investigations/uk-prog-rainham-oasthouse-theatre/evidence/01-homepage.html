The Oasthouse Theatre, located in the heart of Rainham, Kent, serves as a vibrant cultural hub for the arts. Our commitment to showcasing diverse performances, including drama, dance, and music, reflects the rich artistic spirit of our region.

As a venue, we strive to foster creativity and provide a welcoming space for artists and audiences alike. The intimate setting allows for a unique connection between performers and viewers, enhancing the overall experience of each event. We take pride in offering a range of programming that caters to various tastes and age groups, making theatre accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

Whether you're a first-time visitor or a loyal member, The Oasthouse Theatre invites you to join us in celebrating the arts and making lasting memories in Rainham.
