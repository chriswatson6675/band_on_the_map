Ballet Cymru 2. Pre-Professional Programme

Ballet Cymru’s Pre-Professional Programme is for talented, aspiring and highly motivated young dancers with bold ambitions and is designed to facilitate the transition from full-time training into professional company life in a focused, nurturing environment.

This full-time, intensive course provides opportunities to train and perform alongside the award-winning Ballet Cymru company, the exceptional chance to develop choreographic skills with performance opportunities, networking opportunities, as well as workshops in aerial techniques, light/costume design, marketing and writing grant applications to create comprehensive industry professionals.

Ballet Cymru challenges perceptions of ballet.

We value artistry, individuality and personality.

Help us celebrate the diversity and inclusiveness of ballet as an art form.

Ballet Cymru welcomes applications from Disabled people, people identifying as LGBTQI+, Black, Asian and Ethnic minorities.
