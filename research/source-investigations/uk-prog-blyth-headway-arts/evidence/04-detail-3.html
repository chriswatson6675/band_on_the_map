Newcastle Concert Band plus Support

Come along to Headway Arts on Saturday 11th October for the Grand Concert: headlined by Newcastle Concert Band, supported by Beeswing, Blyth Tall Ship Shanty Singers and introducing Brian Kerr.

Tickets are free and include a complementary supper.

Reserve your seat in advance by contacting John at john.focf@gmail.com or leave a message on 01670351962.

Donations invited on the night in support of the Spartans Serenity Garden, a fully accessible working garden in Blyth for those living with mental and physical disability especially dementia.

  • Date: Saturday 11th October 2025
  • Time: 7pm – 9.30pm (doors 6.30pm)
  • Address: Headway Arts, The Old Church, Waterloo Road, Blyth, NE24 1BY
  • This is a special Third Thursdays event.

There is free on-street parking and the venue is a 3 minute walk from Blyth Bus Station.
