Dick Whittington

Starring dancer, choreographer and TV Star, Louie Spence as Peppe Puss, the Cat!

Join Dick Whittington on a whirlwind adventure to London where the streets are (supposedly) paved with gold! With a sneaky villain, a show-stopping cat, toe-tapping songs and laugh-out-loud chaos, this festive panto is packed with sparkle, surprises, and family fun for 2026! It’ll be the purr-fect Christmas treat!

Louie Spence will bring feline fun as Peppe Puss. Returning from last year’s Robin Hood are Will Jennings, Emma Lindars and James Camp, joining fellow West End stars Issac Elder, Lotti Brogan and Sam Amestoy.

Book now to grab the best seats – it’ll be the grand unveiling of our newly refurbished auditorium!

See our Capitol Refurb page for more information on what’s in store for 2026!

  • All tickets £26 on Sunday 29 November!
  • Group 10+ discounts: 2-17 December
  • Family of 4 discounts (max 2 adults) – available from 2 December onwards
  • For Schools bookings: please call 01403 750220
