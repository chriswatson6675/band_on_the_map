About Us

When it comes to a great pub you’ll be hard pushed to beat The Royal Park in Leeds. We’re a fun, friendly and sociable local which also welcomes customers from nearby Royal Park Road, Hyde Park Road, Brudenell Road and indeed across Yorkshire. Maybe it’s because we offer quality, choice and, above all, value, that we are second to none. Check out our drinks offers!
