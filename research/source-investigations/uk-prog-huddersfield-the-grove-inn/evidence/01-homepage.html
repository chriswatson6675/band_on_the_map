Welcome to The Grove

Located in the heart of Huddersfield, a short stroll from the train station and a firm favourite for real ale and craft beer drinkers, the Grove has a phenomenal list of over 30 cask and craft keg ales.

New breweries feature regularly, along with stouts, strong ales and real cider. In addition there is a superb list of over 100 + bottled beers. With a huge array of speciality whiskies, gins and rums and spirits from all over the world. We can offer something for any discerning palate.

We also have occasional live entertainment so be sure to check out our social media for our events.

With a penchant for the unusual The Grove challenges more than your taste buds. Take a look at our ever changing artwork throughout both of our bars. All the artwork is as individual as our beer and is hand picked to challenge even the most hardened arty types.
