AFTERNOON TEA WEEKEND

Sat 26 September @ 12:00 pm – Sun 27 September @ 5:00 pm

Traditional Afternoon Tea

Saturday 26 – Sunday 27 September 2026
Between 2-5pm in the pub

Join us for a special Afternoon Tea during our dedicated weekend, celebrating local food, good company and the simple pleasure of treating yourself. Gather your friends and family, settle in and enjoy a relaxed afternoon at the Black Swan with a traditional afternoon tea.

Treat yourself to a Black Swan Afternoon Tea. Perfect for catching up with friends, celebrating a special occasion or simply enjoying an afternoon treat.
We’ll be serving up afternoon teas all weekend – book early!

£19.50 per person

Enjoy a delicious selection of:

  • Finger sandwiches
  • Fruit scone with jam, butter & Cornish clotted cream
  • Savoury pastry
  • Selection of cakes & sweet treats
  • Unlimited tea or coffee

Vegan and gluten-free options are available – please let us know about any dietary requirements when booking.

Afternoon Teas are being served on Saturday 26 September and also on Sunday 27 September (instead or our Sunday Roasts).

Pre-booking is essential

Afternoon Tea is served between 2pm and 5pm. Book online using the button below and we’ll contact you to confirm the time for your table.

• £19.50 per person • Pre-booking essential
• To secure your booking, you must pay in advance. This is in full if booking online, or £10 per person if booking when you visit the the pub. There are no transaction fees if you book in the pub.
• Add a glass of Prosecco to your Afternoon Tea on the day for + £6.50
