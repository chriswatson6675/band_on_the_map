Brighton and Hove Arts Council

We are proud to be a member of BHAC, a group which supports not-for-profit arts organisations in and around Brighton. The council is diverse and serves all creative endeavors: visual arts, drama, music, opera, dance, and the spoken and written word.

It Takes a Village...

The Players extend a special thank-you to the many organisations and individuals who gave extremely generous contributions to our refurbishment fund. In the decade upto 2019 we raised over £100,000 to fund the modernisation of your local village theatre.

We are a charity, and rely heavily on community support to maintain and improve our local theatre for the enjoyment of all. You can help us through a direct donation, or simply by coming along to our shows.
