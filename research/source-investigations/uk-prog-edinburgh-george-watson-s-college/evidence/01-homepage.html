George Watson’s College

George Watson’s College offers a unique Edinburgh venue for a wide range of events, including conferences, exhibitions, theatre productions, music festivals and events, educational activities, banqueting and hospitality space and outdoor events.

We offer free onsite parking and are open 7 days a week, 50 weeks of the year.  Our facilities are available to hire at any time during the school holidays and at weekends and evenings during term time.

With our wide range of space available, we can accommodate both small and large scale events and with either catering or as space only.
