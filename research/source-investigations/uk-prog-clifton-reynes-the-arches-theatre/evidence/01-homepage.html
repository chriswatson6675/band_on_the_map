Step out to our open air theatre Summer 2026

The Arches Theatre is Buckinghamshire's quirkiest outdoor theatre that offers exciting live theatre and music productions every summer.

Set in a unique location underneath a disused railway arch, our hidden gem in the small village of Clifton Reynes near Olney, Milton Keynes, offers an unforgettable experience for our audiences.

From family-friendly shows and Shakespeare to classic plays, whodunnits, and live music, our outdoor theatre has something for everyone.

Come and join us this summer – bring a picnic, pop open the Prosecco and prepare to be wowed.
