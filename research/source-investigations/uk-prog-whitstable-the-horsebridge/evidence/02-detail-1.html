Talks, Jazz and more

Listen to world renowned jazz musicians, delve into the world of art through our talks or join one of our events.

Studio Sessions and all our adult classes

Oils, clay, knitting and more. Express yourself artistically through our wide range of classes and course. Or learn a new skill with our yoga, dance and more active classes.

Films and Theatre

Watch new blockbusters and deep documentaries, or come and watch amazing renditions and original works live on stage.

Art Den, Family Art Club and much more

Bring your children along for an interactive weekly group, or have an indepth class on photgraphy. Classes for all ages and all types of young creatives.

Our Shop supporting local creatives

See our range of cards, prints, ceramics, glassware, homeware, bags, t-shirts, accessories and craft kits for kids. Many of our products are designed and made locally and sustainably.

The Whitstable Seaside Guide commissioned by The Horsebridge is now in stock.

Art Bank accessible art materials for all

We collect unwanted arts and crafts materials (and books) and offer these on a “pay what you can basis” along with using some of the materials in Art Bank Live! sessions where you can try something new in an informal, supportive session.

Become a FRIEND of The Horsebridge

Becoming a FRIEND not only gives you great benefits, but it really does support and benefit& us too.

With your support and donations we are able to open 6 days a week. You support us delivering youth and teen projects all year and we can provide accessible activities and community events.

For £1 a week you can be in with a chance to win £25,000 weekly!
We've teamed up with One Lottery to enables us to continue to create and provide creative projects for our local community. With your support we can generate regular funding for activities which some of our community rely on.
Our next aim is to raise £1500 to support our monthly film matinees. The money we raise will cover film licences and staff wages. The screenings are fully accessible and inclusive, offering affordable tickets and free refreshments for the Whitstable community. The money will also enable us to reach out to isolated and vulnerable groups, helping us continue spreading the positive impact and boosting social well-being in the area.
 
Please buy a ticket if you can and help more people engage in creativity.
 
Details are on the One Lottery website, please support.

Hire a room

Did you know we have rooms and spaces available for hire at incredibly reasonable rates at the centre?

Whether you are looking for a one-to-one meeting space, a creative space for workshops and demonstrations or somewhere to take the team away for the day we have spaces that can fit up to 220 people. You can also hire multiple rooms to create break out spaces at different times of the day.

Better still we are in a fantastic location right by the sea and in the heart of Whitstable. A chance for a working from home team to meet up and work together?

Prices start from £17 an hour, and you can hire as a one off or make regular bookings. We have facilities you'd expect like WiFi and our team can make sure the room furniture is setup before you arrive.
