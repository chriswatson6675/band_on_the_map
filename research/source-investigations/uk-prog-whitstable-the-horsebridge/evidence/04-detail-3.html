Talks

Art Decades

Thursday Multiple dates Art Decades series of talks look to unveil the hidden lives of rock artists outside the recording studio and finds out how they coped when they were really tested to the limit.

Art and Artists 8 with Richard Dean MA

Thursday Multiple dates A course of 8 new talks from Richard Dean MA. Combining art history, biography and critical analysis you will learn about the lives and works of some important artists and topics.

War in the Sky with Richard Dean MA

Sunday 8 November 2026 Remembrance Sunday. From 1942-45 the German Luftwaffe and the US Eighth Air Force would fight the longest continuous battle of World War 2. This is the story of that battle.
