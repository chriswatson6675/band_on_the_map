La Belle is a live music, nightclub and creative space in the heart of Edinburgh’s Cowgate area. Originally opened in the 90s and having hosted live performances by the likes of Oasis, Radiohead and the Libertines, La Belle Angele has been at the forefront of music, art, and club culture for decades. Our stage is built for bands and unforgettable club nights, where performers and audiences come together to share something that stays with you long after the lights come up.
