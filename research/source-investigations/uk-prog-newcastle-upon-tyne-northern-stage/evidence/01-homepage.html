VISIT THE CAFÉ BAR

Northern Stage Café Bar offers an open, welcoming space to work, relax, meet with friends or enjoy a pre-show tipple.

We proudly brew world-class Ouseburn Coffee co, and we offer a selection of delicious freshly baked goods and sandwiches from Pink Lane Bakery.

Our Café Bar is open Mon-Fri, from 8.30am – 3pm. 

Open 2hrs before weekend matinees for coffee and baked goods, and pre-theatre dining is available 2.5hrs before evening shows.
