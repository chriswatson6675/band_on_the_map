Buying Tickets

Box Office Hours:

The Kings Theatre Box Office will be open from 3pm on Performance Days only. On Saturdays and Sundays the Box Office will open at least 90 minutes prior to any performance. Tickets can still be purchased online 24/7, or by telephone on 02392 828282. Lines are open Monday to Friday from 10am to 5pm. Tickets can also be purchased and collected at the Theatre Royal Portsmouth Box Office for both venues. It is open Monday to Friday from 10am to 5pm.

Contact Us:

  • Phone: 023 9282 8282
  • Email: box.office@portsmouththeatres.org.uk
  • In Person: At The Kings Theatre and Theatre Royal Box Office

Note: All tickets are subject to our Ticketing Terms & Conditions which can be viewed here.

Payment Methods

We accept the following methods of payment:

  • Debit/Credit Card – Visa/Master/Maestro
  • Theatre Tokens

Tickets should be printed at home, available for checking on a phone/tablet, or collected in advance from the theatre.

Please note, all tickets are sold subject to our Ticketing Terms & Conditions which can be viewed here.

Concessions

We offer concessions on some of our shows. Details will be available on the show pages or please do speak to a member of the Box Office. The Box Office may ask for proof of entitlement to concessions. Only one concession, per person, per transaction, is applicable.

Our tickets will be charged at full price unless there is a child rate advertised. We offer a “baby on lap” ticket for parents bringing children aged 2 years or under, on selected shows only, bookable only from the Box Office.

Group Bookings

Details for group bookings will be available on the show pages.

To make a group booking of 10+, or to discuss reservations and group rates, please call 023 9285 2202 or email groupbookings@kingsportsmouth.co.uk 

Our Fees Explained

The price you see advertised is the price you pay inclusive of fees. Our fees cover all costs associated with processing your booking and also Building Improvement Fund charge (applies to shows at the theatre only) which goes towards improving our beautiful Grade II* listed building.

Booking Protect

Booking Protect is a customer-focused, technology-driven refund specialist, helping their clients increase customer satisfaction while delivering a new revenue stream.

All patrons are offered the chance to purchase Booking Protect when making a booking. 

All refunds are administered by Booking Protect. You will be provided with a refund on any unused booking if you are unable to attend the booked event due to any of the circumstances set out in Booking Protect terms and conditions here. These terms and conditions are the applicable terms and conditions of your refund protection policy.

The Kings Theatre is acting as agent for Booking Protect who are responsible for administration for any claim that you wish to make.

For more info visit their website at bookingprotect.com

Access Requirements & Companion Ticket Policy

Access requirement customers pay full price for their ticket.

You can then buy ONE companion ticket at a discounted rate.

As the access requirement customer, you must have and show one of the following letters to the Box Office Staff showing that you need to have a carer with you:

  • Disability Living Allowance (DLA)
  • Personal Independence Payment (PIP)
  • Employment and Support Allowance (ESA)
  • Nimbus Access Card+1

For more guidance, please visit the Access information page.

Theatre Tokens

Theatre Tokens are national theatre gift vouchers that can be used at more than 260 venues nationwide including the Kings Theatre. With no expiry date, they are the perfect gift for anyone. Buy online by clicking the button below (Gift Card and eGift). These can be purchased at our Box Office or on the Theatre Tokens website. To use the balance of your Theatre Token for the purchase of tickets at The Kings Theatre, please pop in to our Box Office during opening hours or give us a call on 023 9282 8282.

Information for Familes & Children

Age recommendations and restrictions are shown on each individual show page. Children aged 13 and under must be accompanied to the theatre by an adult aged 16 or over.

Children aged between 14 and 16 may attend a performance unaccompanied but must notify the Box Office upon booking tickets and on arrival. No responsibility is accepted by The Kings Theatre or any of its employees for unaccompanied children.

If available, Baby on Lap tickets are available at a heavily reduced rate. Information about baby on lap tickets can be found on the individual show page. These tickets entitle infants 24 months and under to sit on the lap of an adult. These must be booked via the phone or in person at The Box Office.

If available, discounted Family Tickets can be booked for our performances. The information will be on the individual show page and discount will automatically be applied when booking. Family tickets consist of 4 seats, of which two attendees must be under the age of 16.

There are baby change facilities available at the disabled access toilet in the rear of the stalls or our dedicated Family Room next to the ladies toilets on the Dress Circle level.

Pushchairs can be stored in our bars whilst the performance takes place.

The Safeguarding Officer for The Kings Theatre Trust Ltd is Rebecca Henderson. Rebecca.Henderson@Kingsportsmouth.co.uk.

Student Standby Tickets

£10 best available seats with Student Standby!

We strive to make theatre accessible to all, so we offer Student Standby tickets to all performances here at The Kings Theatre.

You can grab best available seats for yourself and a plus one for just £10 each by showing your student ID.

Tickets must be booked on the day of the performance either in person or by calling the Box Office on 023 9282 8282. Your valid student ID must be shown when booking/collecting tickets.

VIP Packages

Bespoke VIP experiences can be created to suit your needs. 

Packages are available for up to 24 people in our exclusive Stage Bar for those that wish for a private space when coming to see a show.

Experiences can include:

  • Private service
  • A range of selected drinks – Alcoholic and non alcoholic options available
  • Snacks, goody bags and selected merchandise

A party minimum of 15 places required. Available on Thu, Fri and Sat evenings with prices from £20pp*.

Contact our Customer Experience Lead Megan Sanger to start building your package today. Email Megan.Sanger@kingsportsmouth.co.uk or call 023 9285 2238.
