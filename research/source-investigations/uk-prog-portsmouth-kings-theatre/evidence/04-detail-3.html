Community Events

We offer a range of different community events in and around the theatre that are put in place for all to enjoy.

Find out more about our regular offerings below.

Get Involved

Theatre Tours

Please visit the What’s On section of the website to book your place on one of our theatre tours. 

History Tours

Step behind the scenes and see our iconic theatre from a new perspective. History tours are run by one of our friendly Kings Theatre Trust ltd. members.

Ghost Tours

Are you brave enough to explore our theatre once the lights go out? Hear about some of the spooky goings on over the years with a few scares along the way.

Toddler Tours

A fun, interactive tour for the little ones. Led by an industry professional, these tours are a hit!

Technical Tours

Discover the methods and magic behind running one or our in-house or touring productions.

School Tours

Experience leaning in a unique way in an environment that will inspire and engage. To arrange an educational tour, please email konnect@kingsportsmouth.co.uk.
