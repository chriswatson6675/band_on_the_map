WELCOME 👋

Macsorley’s is a family-run, multi award-winning music venue in the heart of Glasgow’s City Centre. Located only 2 minutes from Glasgow Central station.

Purveyors of live entertainment 6 nights a week ensuring a warm and musical welcoming every day of the week.

Serving Traditional Scottish fayre with a twist.

Stocking a large range of premium spirits and whiskies and on draught choices we are sure to have something to suit your needs!

Join us for good food, good music & great vibes!
