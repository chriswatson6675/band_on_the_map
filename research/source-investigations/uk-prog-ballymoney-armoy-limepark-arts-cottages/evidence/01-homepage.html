Located in the heart of

The Causeway Coast & Glens

Welcome to Limepark, a wedding venue and holiday home rental nestled in the rural countryside of Northern Ireland.

Limepark is a place to create memories with the people that matter. If you are looking for somewhere to stay as you experience the beauty of the North Coast, or searching for somewhere unique and picturesque to have your wedding day, the private estate is a piece of history you can have all to yourself.

Rent a piece of history
Unique

Home to a number of cottages that exude a traditional feel, with ample space to host your family and loved ones. Is a private garden on your wish list? Or a bath to melt away the day? Our 18th-century cottages are ready for your arrival.

Exclusive

When you book your wedding with Limepark, we want to ensure the day is entirely yours. This is why the estate is only available on an exclusive use basis, ensuring you and your guests have complete privacy for the duration of your day.

Sustainable

We’re aware of our impact. The onsite wind turbines and solar panels help our efforts to make Limepark not only beautiful, but sustainable. Cruelty free and plant based cleaning products are used throughout the premises, just one of our sustainable practices.

Family Run

Our family-run estate is overseen by Glenn and Angela Biesty and is home to cottages of all shapes and sizes that offer a traditional, comforting, and cosy environment for you and your loved ones to savour.

Rustic wedding venue in Northern Ireland

Limepark stands as one of the most enchanting rustic wedding venues in Northern Ireland, perfectly blending timeless countryside charm with exclusive privacy. Nestled in the heart of the Causeway Coast & Glens, this historic estate offers a unique setting steeped in tradition and natural beauty, ideal for couples seeking a truly authentic rustic wedding experience in Ireland. Our barn wedding venue serves as a dream wedding venue for your rustic wedding, with our cottages providing the country house wedding venue experience for those staying with us.

Our 18th-century cottages and barn, with their exposed stone walls and cosy interiors, provide a warm and inviting atmosphere that captures the essence of rustic elegance. Whether you envision an intimate ceremony surrounded by lush gardens or a lively celebration under the open skies, Limepark’s private estate ensures your wedding day is both personal and picturesque.

The barn at Limepark is truly a stunning wedding venue, and provides the the perfect wedding venue for your dream day in a beatiful elegant and rustic setting. Check out our reviews and testimonials to see what other guests have to say about their weddings at Limepark and see why we’re a truly unique wedding venue for your country house rustic wedding.

Exclusivity is at the core of our offering — when you choose Limepark, the entire estate is yours, allowing you and your guests to enjoy complete privacy and a relaxed, homely environment throughout your wedding celebrations. This makes Limepark not only one of the most sought-after rustic wedding venues in Northern Ireland but also a sanctuary where memories are crafted with care and style.

For couples dreaming of a rustic wedding venue in Ireland that combines historic charm, exclusive use, and a breathtaking rural backdrop, Limepark offers an unparalleled experience that will make your wedding day truly unforgettable.

Discover why so many couples choose Limepark to celebrate their love amidst the serene beauty of Northern Ireland’s countryside — a perfect blend of rustic charm and elegant comfort awaits you here.
