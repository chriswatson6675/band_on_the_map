About Jack Dee: Jack's Joke Show

A show by Jack about jokes.

Who doesn’t like jokes? If your answer to that question is “Me!” then steer well clear of this show.

Celebrating 40 years in standup comedy, Jack takes us on a memoir of all his favourite old stories. Jokes and funny anecdotes have intrigued Jack for as long as he can remember.

Now he has assembled the best of them to form a new show that offers a glimpse of the influences that led him into and helped shape his comedy career.

Guaranteed no new material.
