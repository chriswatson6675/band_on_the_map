Inspired by Flaxman Talk: Flaxman and the Royal Academy by Dr Richard Johns – Wed 23 Sep 2026

Date: Wednesday 23 September 2026, 12:00 pm-12:30 pm
Venue: York Art Gallery

Inspired by Flaxman Talk: Flaxman and the Royal Academy by Dr Richard Johns
Wednesday 23 September 2026
12 noon – 12.30pm
Included in general admission | Book Tickets

This talk explores John Flaxman’s lifelong creative association with the Royal Academy of Arts in London. From his early years as one of the first cohort of students of the nascent Academy in 1769, to his appointment as its inaugural Professor of Sculpture, you’ll discover more about the role of anatomical study in the training of aspiring artists.

Richard Johns is a Professor in History of Art at University of York and specialises in aspects of British art from the 17th – 21st centuries, with a focus on the long 18th century. University of York partnered with York Art Gallery to co-curate Turner, Wedgwood and Beyond: Inspired by Flaxman.

Gallery Babies – Thu 24 Sep 2026

Date: Thursday 24 September 2026, 10:00 am-12:00 pm
Venue: York Art Gallery

Gallery Babies
Thursday 24 September 2026 |10am - 12pm
£2 per person | Book Now

Drop-in sensory play sessions every Thursday during term-time, for pre-walking babies and their grown-ups, set in the atmospheric surroundings of York Art Gallery.

Using our beautiful sensory toolkit, a collection of colourful squishy sculptural objects, sessions will be gently facilitated, exploring shape, colour and texture.

We are also offering a special offer in Sketch café for anybody attending a session: Hot drink and cake £7.00 and lunch and drink £15.00.

Please note: Participants must have a pre-booked ticket to attend the session. Gallery Babies admission includes free access to the permanent gallery spaces, tickets for temporary exhibitions require a separate admissions ticket. This event takes place in the Burton Gallery.

Date: Saturday 3 October 2026, 2:00 pm-4:00 pm
Venue: York Art Gallery

Life Studies: Line, Form & Figure
Saturday 3 October 2026
2pm – 4pm
£15 per person* | Book Now
Adult only (18+)

Join us at York Art Gallery for a special life drawing class, set in our latest exhibition Turner, Wedgwood and Beyond: Inspired by Flaxman.

Led by York based artist and tutor, Rob Oldfield, you’ll draw from a live model, exploring contour, proportion, rhythm and gesture, inspired by Flaxman’s distinctive linear drawings and his fascination with classical form.

Inspired by Flaxman Talk: Flaxman and the Royal Academy by Michael Adam Smith – Wed 7 Oct 2026

Date: Wednesday 7 October 2026, 12:00 pm-12:30 pm
Venue: York Art Gallery

Inspired by Flaxman Talk: John Flaxman’s Funerary Monuments by Michael Adam Smith 
Wednesday 7 October 2026
12 noon – 12.30pm
Included in general admission | Book Tickets

The bulk of Flaxman’s sculptural output comprises over 200 extant funerary monuments. Installed in churches across Britain and overseas, they range from small memorial tablets commissioned by bereaved families to publicly funded monuments commemorating British military heroes. This talk will provide an overview of Flaxman’s work in this field, discussing his modelling process, his relationship with clients and his continual reinterpretation of favourite motifs. 

Michael Adam Smith is an academic from York University currently studying for a PhD entitled, John Flaxman: art, design and the intermedial body. His academic research has contributed to the curation of Turner, Wedgwood & Beyond: Inspired by Flaxman and has played a pivotal role in the themes explored throughout.

Inspired by Flaxman Exclusive Tour + The Penelopiad at York Theatre Royal – Mon 26 Oct 2026

Date: Monday 26 October 2026, 5:00 pm-8:00 pm
Venue: York Art Gallery

Inspired by Flaxman Exclusive Tour and Evening Performance of The Penelopiad at York Theatre Royal
Monday 26 October 2026
5pm – 8pm
£57 per person (includes tour of York Art Gallery and ticket to theatre) | Book Tickets via York Theatre Royal

Join York Art Gallery’s Senior Curator, Dr Beatrice Bertram, for an exclusive tour of the Gallery’s new exhibition, Turner, Wedgwood & Beyond: Inspired by Flaxman before heading to York Theatre Royal for an evening performance of The Penelopiad.

Celebrating the life and legacy of York-born designer and artist, John Flaxman, the exhibition explores his influence over fellow artists and design movements since the eighteenth century.

Flaxman’s illustrations of Homer’s Odyssey will be on display alongside works with strong classical themes by Flaxman, J.M.W. Turner, William Blake, Angelica Kauffman, Josiah Wedgwood and other acclaimed artists.

The tour starts at 5pm and will take approximately one hour, finishing at 6pm at York Art Gallery.

A top price ticket to see the 6.30pm performance of The Penelopiad at York Theatre Royal is included. Please email boxoffice@yorktheatreroyal.co.uk if you have any access requirements for your seat(s).

The play is written by Margaret Atwood, author of the modern feminist classic The Handmaid’s Tale and revisits Homer’s The Odyssey in a powerful, irreverent and darkly humorous retelling that unpicks one of the oldest of myths.

Tickets are £57 – which includes both the tour at York Art Gallery and the performance of The Penelopiad and can be purchased through York Theatre Royal’s website.

Please email boxoffice@yorktheatreroyal.co.uk for questions about your tickets.
