Immersive Arts Journey - Summer Edition: Revealing Unseen Layers

The Africa Centre and Blending With Arts Present :
"Immersive Arts Journey: Revealing Unseen Layers"

A New Dimension of Art Exploration

Featuring Artists: Gino, Incogni-Ko, Misoko, SoloniA, Zoum

Join us for a vibrant and family-friendly experience. Get ready for an unparalleled artistic adventure where art transcends its traditional boundaries and invites you into its very essence. This isn't just an exhibition; it's an interactive and dynamic exploration designed to ignite your senses and challenge your perceptions.

Imagine stepping into a gallery where paintings speak, characters' faces are unveiled through Augmented Reality (AR),and their untold stories whisper directly to you.

Go beyond the canvas as you literally step inside every artwork via Virtual Reality (VR), exploring its hidden worlds and discovering perspectives previously unimagined.

But the journey doesn't stop there. Slip on Mixed-Reality glasses to expose secret layers within the art, revealing details and dimensions invisible to the naked eye. And for those eager to leave their own mark, seize the unique opportunity to become an artist yourself, co-creating a digital masterpiece that becomes part of this evolving exhibition.

And don't worry if you're a low-tech or no-tech art enthusiast – we've got something special for you too! Our art experience is designed to delight everyone, ensuring a rich and engaging adventure regardless of your comfort with technology.

We look forward to celebrating with you the immersive arts in all its forms!

We’ve set up a limited number of time slots for the duration of this exhibition to keep the experience personal and special. Please choose your preferred time below. We can’t wait to welcome you!

  • Our work

    The Africa Centre is a ‘home away from home’ for Africans in the UK and a warm and welcoming space offering authentic African heritage and cultural experiences to anyone with an interest in Africa. Our mission rests on Five Pillars of activity: Culture, Entrepreneurship and Innovation, Community, and Intellectual Leadership.

  • Our Story

    Originally conceived to foster non-governmental relations between newly independent Africa and Britain, The Africa Centre's mission is to educate, connect, and advocate for Africa and its diaspora. Previously located in Covent Garden, London for over five decades, we launched our new HQ in Southwark in June 2022.
