The Exchange

Our original home. Think of it as the town’s front room, filled with decent coffee, tasty cocktails, comfy sofas, music, board games, quiz nights, comedy, parties, talks and everything in between.

Find out more

The Yard

Ingrams Yard, located a few minutes walk from the Exchange, is our new site which we are transforming into a place for making, learning and creating. It’s also home to markets and events.

Find out more

The Arena

The Ventnor Exchange Arena is our largest venue. A beautiful Italian designed Big Top that can be transported anywhere to host the very best new circus, dance, theatre, comedy and music.

Find out more

Ventnor Fringe

Created by Ventnor Exchange, Ventnor Fringe is the Isle of Wight’s largest arts festival.

Over 10 days you can discover performers from around the world. 

Find out more

Culture Pass

Ventnor Fringe Supporters

Love the Fringe? Help us secure its future.

Each year the Ventnor Fringe Guarantee Fund has to raise around 10% of the costs of the festival from supporters and sponsors. 

Becoming a Supporter or Patron of the Fringe is the best way to ensure the long term future of the Fringe 

Find out more about the Guarantee Fund here.

Donate
Ventnor Exchange is on a mission to help transform creative opportunities on the Isle of Wight.
 
We work day in and day out to help improve our community; helping to stage incredible performances, provide new facilities, supporting hundreds of young people and giving everyone opportunities to be creative and learn new skills.
 
If you believe in our work and want to support us you can make a donation here.

Brave Island

Aged 14-25?

Brave Island is the creative network for young people on the Isle of Wight, with over 600 members. 

Created by Ventnor Exchange,  we provide free opportunities every single week.

Get Involved

If you’re aged 14-25, living on the Isle of Wight and interested in the creative industries sign up for free!

You can then apply for opportunities including industry placements, mentoring, funding, trips, workshops, commissions and training.
