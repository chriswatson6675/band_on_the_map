Welcome to the Conquest Theatre!

We are a vibrant 153-seat venue in Bromyard which offers a diverse range of events.
From captivating plays and musicals with talented actors, to live music, films and event cinema screenings.
Our open-plan foyer features a coffee shop and licensed bar, providing a welcoming space for all visitors.
We also offer opportunities for people to get involved in volunteering and acting for both youth and adults to help bring the performing arts to the community.
Our box office is open every Wednesday and Saturday 10.30am – 12.30pm
Every Saturday, our cafe is open for tea and coffee morning 10.30am – 12.30pm

Latest Conquest News

Conquest Theatre shortlisted in the Hereford Times Community Awards 2025

The Conquest Theatre in Bromyard has been shortlisted in the Contribution to the Arts category at the Hereford Times Community Awards 2025. The awards celebrate the outstanding people, organisations, and projects that make Herefordshire a vibrant and inclusive community.

For more than 40 years, the Conquest Theatre has been at the heart of Bromyard’s cultural life, providing high-quality performing arts for audiences across the rural market town. The theatre is entirely volunteer-run, with teams working tirelessly behind the scenes and on stage to ensure every production is a success. From performers and backstage crews to front of house, box office, and technical teams, each volunteer contributes their time, skills, and passion to bring the arts to life.
