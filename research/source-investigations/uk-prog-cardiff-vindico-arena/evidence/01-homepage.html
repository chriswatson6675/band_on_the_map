    The Cardiff Devils

    Explore

    Vindico Arena is the official home of the Cardiff Devils Ice Hockey team who play in the professional British Elite Ice Hockey League, which is the top tier of ice hockey in the UK.

    Plan your visit

    About

    Vindico Arena, located in the International Sports Village Complex, is Wales' premier destination for ice sports. With a large ice pad and seating for 3,100 spectators, it is the home of the Cardiff Devils Hockey team.

    Getting Here

    Vindico Arena is located in the International Sports Village in Cardiff Bay, directly across the road from the former Toys R Us site, and literally next to the International Pool. Our front entrance faces the pool's front entrance.

    Accessibility

    When it comes to accessibility at Vindico Arena, we’re committed to delivering you the best customer experience. We are a member of the Hidden Disabilities Sunflower Scheme.

    Food & Drink

    Keep your energy up with a variety of food and drink options at Vindico Arena. From quick bites to refreshing beverages, there’s something for everyone.

    Facilities

    Vindico Arena in Cardiff Bay has an Olympic-sized ice rink offering public skating, skate hire, lessons, and family-friendly sessions. It also features accessible facilities, a cafe, and programs like figure skating and birthday parties.
