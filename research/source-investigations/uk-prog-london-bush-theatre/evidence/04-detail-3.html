Booking Tickets

Nights at the Bush, now for less

We’ve changed how our tickets are priced to make it simpler than ever. We’re making more £15 tickets available than ever before, and we’ve got rid of dynamic pricing so ticket prices won’t change.

  • Standard tickets range from £15 to £45 in the Holloway Theatre, and £15 to £25 in the Studio. Find £15 tickets.
  • Concessions offer £5 off standard price tickets above £20. Available for students, senior citizens, disabled and unemployed / unwaged patrons.
  • Under 30s and Locals can get up to £10 off tickets and discounts at the bar with a free Bush membership.

We never charge booking fees on any of our tickets whenever you book directly with us. We recommend only booking directly with us, or from one of our authorised agents. Our authorised agents include TodayTix and Official London Theatre.

Ticket FAQs

  •  

    How do I book tickets?

    Online: Tickets can be booked online quickly and securely 24 hours a day. Click here.

    By phone: Call our Box Office on 020 8743 5050. There is no booking fee. The Box Office line is open 4pm – 8pm Monday to Saturday.

    In person: Our Box Office is open 5pm-8pm Monday to Saturday. Please note, we are currently a cashless venue.

     

    Book online
  •  

    How much are tickets?

    Standard tickets range from £15 to £45 in the Holloway Theatre, and £15 to £25 in the Studio. Concessions offer £5 off standard tickets.

    Prices may vary between productions. Be sure to check out individual production pages for accurate pricing details.

    Special event ticket prices may vary, check the individual events for more details.

  •  

    How do I book access tickets?

    Access tickets, wheelchair spaces and free Personal Assistant / Companion tickets can be booked online, over the phone or in person.

    Find out more
  •  

    What discounts and concessions are available?

    We offer discounted Concession rate tickets for students, senior citizens, disabled and unemployed / unwaged patrons. Free tickets are also available for Personal Assistants.

    If you’re under 30 or live or work in a 2 mile radius of the Bush, you can sign up for free as a member to access exclusive discounts on tickets and at the bar.

    Find out more Memberships
  •  

    How do I book a season ticket?

    The more you book, the more you save. Add tickets for more than one production to your basket and the savings will be applied automatically.

    Ways to Save
  •  

    If the performance I want is sold out, how can I get tickets? Are there any last minute tickets?

    If the performance you want is sold out, you may still be able to get tickets…

    • Waiting List: we sometimes get tickets returned in advance. Email boxoffice@bushtheatre.co.uk a contact number, 3 preferred dates and the number of tickets you would like. Please note we cap the waiting list at 10 people per performance.
    • Returns Queue: we often get tickets returned just before the show. These tickets will be offered to a returns queue in person at the Box Office on a first-come, first-served basis. You can join the queue from 2 hours before the show.
    • Join as a supporter: become a supporter from just £30 a month to unlock benefits including the best seats for every production, even after the show has sold out. Click here to find out more and join now.
    Contact Join as a supporter
  •  

    How will I receive my tickets?

    We use contactless e-tickets on all orders. When you book, you will receive e-tickets sent to your email address. You can also access these by logging in to your account and clicking ‘E-tickets’.

    We will also send you an SMS text message on the day of your performance, with a link to your mobile tickets.

    You will need to show your tickets on your phone or mobile device when entering the auditorium, so make sure you have them ready. There is no need to print your e-tickets.

  •  

    What are the dimensions of your seats?

    In the Holloway Theatre (the Bush Theatre’s main house), seats are 53cm wide, with a gap of between 7cm and 14cm between each seat. Seats do not have arm rests.

    In the Studio, seats are 45cm wide, with a gap of 7cm between each seat. Seats do not have arms rests.
