Everyone should get to experience great art and events. With affordable ticketing options across all our own programme, we want to make sure there’s nothing stopping you from getting involved. Our affordable ticket options include:

  • Aviva £10 Tickets for those who financially need them the most, across all our own shows and events, supported by Aviva.
  • Concession tickets. Students and those on universal credit or pension credit get up to 25% discount across all our own shows. These tickets can be found in our regular booking journey.

How to claim tickets

If you think you qualify for an Aviva £10 Ticket, simply enter the following code in the promotion field before choosing your tickets.

AVIVA1026
Look for this promotion field
to enter in the code

Enter your code and select any ticket type. Don’t worry, your ticket will be discounted to £10 at the point of checkout and there won't be any additional costs.
