History

Originally opened as Chorley’s first electric cinema in 1910, Chorley Theatre has become a thriving arts venue in the centre of the town. It is owned and operated by Chorley Amateur Dramatic & Operatic Society (CADOS – established 1933) who present productions across two performance spaces throughout the year. Their programme of plays is supplemented by the best films, comedy and more.
