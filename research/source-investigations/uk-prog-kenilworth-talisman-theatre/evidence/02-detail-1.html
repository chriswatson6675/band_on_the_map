Talisman Members & Volunteers – please see link here so you can view whether the theatre spaces (auditorium, foyer and studio) are booked for a live event whether that be a show, a rehearsal or an arranged meeting.

If you are intending to come to the theatre to do some voluntary department work please do check the calendar before you pop down to the theatre so you know the space will be free. As our theatre gets busier and busier with outside lets we need to ensure that people who have pre-booked the space have full uninterrupted access at the times they have booked.

If you wish to book any of our spaces for theatre related work or rehearsals then the space needs to be booked in advance by emailing secretary@talismantheatre.net

If you would like to stay up to date with us, please enter your details below to sign up to our newsletter
