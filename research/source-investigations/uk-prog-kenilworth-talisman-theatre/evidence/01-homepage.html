CINDERELLA

19th December 2026 - 2nd January 2027

A sparkling family adventure!

The Talisman Theatre and Arts Centre has an active and talented volunteer membership who have been Putting the Arts at the Heart of our Community for over 80 years.


Our theatre in Kenilworth, Warwickshire delivers an abundance of local shows and entertainment all year round. Annually, we host nine main stage productions, ranging from the classics, comedy, musicals, thrillers, mysteries, and of course our fun-filled family pantomime.


We also have a vibrant youth theatre, regular cinema, and Fringe theatre nights (at the Kenilworth Holiday Inn) – so check out what’s on as there really is something for everyone.
