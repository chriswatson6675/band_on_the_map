The Heart of the Nix Community

More than just an arena, Ellesmere Port Sports Village (EPSV) is where our club, our fans, and our local community come together. Since moving into £15 million facility, we’ve built a matchday experience rooted in family, inclusivity, and local pride.

From welcoming grassroots youth programs onto the floor to hearing the roar of the Nix Nation on game day, EPSV is designed to bring people together through the power of basketball. Whether it’s your very first game or your twentieth season, there is a seat in the stands for everyone.

 

Venue Details:
Location: Ellesmere Port Sports Village
Address: Stanney Lane, Ellesmere Port, CH65 9LB
Atmosphere: Family-friendly, accessible, and community-focused

About Us

The Cheshire Phoenix is a professional basketball team based in Ellesmere Port. Founded in 1984, we are members of the Super League Basketball and play our home games out of Ellesmere Port Sports Village.
