Exmouth Pavilion | Theatre, Live Entertainment & Café Bar on Exmouth Seafront

Exmouth Pavilion is a seafront theatre in Devon, perfectly positioned opposite Exmouth’s sandy beach. A hub for live entertainment, it’s one of the top things to do in Exmouth, whether you’re visiting for a show, food, or a day by the sea.

Our year‑round programme brings together live music, touring theatre, comedy, ballet, tribute acts and family shows, making Exmouth Pavilion a go‑to venue for entertainment on the Devon coast.

Open every day from 9am, The Pavilion Café & Bar is a relaxed spot to eat, drink and take in the seafront setting. Drop in for breakfast, lunch, coffee or cake, or meet friends before a show. With fresh, high‑quality food, indoor seating and an outdoor patio, it’s an easy choice any time of day.

We also have two well‑stocked bars, including a dedicated theatre bar on event nights, perfect for a pre‑show drink or interval refreshment.

With easy access, nearby parking and great transport links, Exmouth Pavilion makes planning your visit simple.

Explore what’s on, book tickets online, and make a day of it on Exmouth seafront.
