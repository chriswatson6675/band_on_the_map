For the love of a good time

Inspired by bier kellers from around the globe, Wunderbar is an inclusive place to discover new things and be your own kind of weird and wunderful. With unique flavours from around the world, we aim to inspire a new way of enjoyment through our biers, live sports, cocktails, and live music.

We’re your no-frills, friendly neighbourhood spot, right in the heart of Glasgow’s City Centre and West End. Expect endless fun in our lively and playful space.
