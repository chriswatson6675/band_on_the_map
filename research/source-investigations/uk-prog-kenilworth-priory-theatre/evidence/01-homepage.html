Jack and the Beanstalk - Relaxed Performance

The Priory presents a relaxed performance of our Christmas 2025/26 pantomime!

Designed to create a welcoming environment to our customers with sensory needs and other visible and hidden disabilities, we're really excited to welcome you!

Arrangements include:
- No strobe lighting
- No pyrotechnics
- Volume turned down
- House lights remain on throughout the show
- The audience are free to move around and make noise during the show
- An introduction to the show before we begin
- Sensory tour prior to the show

Welcome to The Priory Theatre

We’re a community run theatre in Kenilworth, Warwickshire producing around 7 in-house shows a year and staging countless more productions from other groups and companies. There's something for everyone to enjoy; from drama to comedy, crime thrillers, musicals and pantomimes!

We also offer anyone the opportunity to get involved in all aspects of theatre. Our volunteers come from all walks of life, and work in our front-of-house, backstage, production and technical teams as well as perform on stage.

So whether you’re looking for tickets; a new hobby or simply a drink from the bar, we look forward to welcoming you soon.
