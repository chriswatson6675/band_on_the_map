A Passionate Woman

by Kay Mellor

It’s the morning of her son’s wedding and housewife Betty is in crisis. She withdraws to the roof-space and its store of memories. Enter the much-loved son, an irate husband, a former Polish lover called Craze; and with a steadily filling church and nosey neighbours looking on … Which way will Betty jump?
