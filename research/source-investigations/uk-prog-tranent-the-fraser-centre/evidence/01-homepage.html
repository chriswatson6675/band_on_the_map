Travelling to The Fraser Centre:

Walking: The Fraser Centre is situated in the heart of Tranent and within walking distance from many residential areas of the town.

Cycling: The Fraser Centre has cycle racks at our front gate for you to use.

Bus: Bus stops can be found on Church St, and the High St under 5 minuets walk from The Fraser Centre.

Please check with LRT and Prentice for the latest bus route information.

Taxi: The nearest taxi rank is on Tranent High St, under 5 minutes walk away. There are several excellent local Taxi firms available to assist you and pick up from The Fraser Centre.

Car: There is free 90 minute parking outside The Fraser Centre Monday – Saturday until 6.30pm.

Across the street from our main gate is another car park which unrestricted and free.

2 minutes walk from The Fraser Centre on Lindores Drive is Forresters Car park also free and unrestricted. This will be busy on football match days.

3 minutes walk from The Fraser Centre is a car park on Loch Road.

Please park considerately in residential areas without blocking access.

Train: The nearest Train stations are located in nearby towns: Prestonpans (2 miles) and Wallyford (2.5 miles).

Hire Us

The Fraser Centre has a selection of rooms and prices available for your event starting from only £11.

The building is fully accessible to wheelchair users and has town centre parking at our entrance as well as being on the network for both Edinburgh and East Lothian bus services. Bicycle racks are located at our main entrance.

In-house catering for your event is available from our dedicated in-house team.
