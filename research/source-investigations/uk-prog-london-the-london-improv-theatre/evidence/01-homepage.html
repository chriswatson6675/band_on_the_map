Corporate Training & Team Building

Improv in the Workplace

​Workshops and training, exploiting the techniques of Improvisation, to companies and business.

​Revitalising, fun Improv experiences to develop team building. 

Designed to improve collaboration,  participants awaken their imaginations and creativity to enjoy exercises, scenes and discoveries. A revitalising, fun and rewarding experience that brings a renewed energy.

'One Day'

Improv Workshops

Six-Hour Saturday Workshop

 

This fun six-hour workshop is aimed at those who would like to consolidate Improv Basics, explore the foundations of Improv technique and then enjoy the fun of game formats and scene work.  

 

Gain insights into the Improv techniques of confidence, heightened communication, and spontaneity.

All great transferable skills for work, life and social interaction.​​​​​

Improv 'Drop In' 

Workshops

Stand-alone, fun, two-hour sessions.

 

Consolidating techniques of Improvisation and offering insights into the techniques of confidence and spontaneity.

Transferable skills for work, life and social interaction. 

Never forgetting that it’s fun!

Making and accepting offers, 'yes and' - building ideas and collaboration. 

Re-awaken imagination, creativity, playfulness and laughter.

Suitable for Beginners through to the more experienced ensuring participants are challenged at the right level.​​​​​
