Fall in love with Coventry

Coventry is a thriving city of business, industry and culture. The city is renowned for its rich history and heritage, from medieval buildings to Coventry Cathedral, one of the world’s finest examples of 20th century architecture.

The city is a global leader in key sectors including automotive, engineering and advanced manufacturing and its cultural scene earned the city the prestigious title of UK City of Culture in 2021.

Getting Here

Coventry Building Society Arena is in the heart of the UK with excellent transport links to London, Birmingham and the Midlands. The venue is within two hour’s drive of 75 per cent of the country.

By rail, Coventry is 55 minutes from London and 20 minutes from Birmingham Airport, with easy access to the M6 and wider motorway network.
