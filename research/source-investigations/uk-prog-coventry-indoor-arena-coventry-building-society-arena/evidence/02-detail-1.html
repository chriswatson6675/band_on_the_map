Engineering Design Show 2026

Event: Engineering Design Show 2026 

Date: 7th-8th October 2026 

Time: 7th October 09:30-16:30, 8th October 09:30-16:00 

Location: Coventry Building Society Arena, Judds Ln, Coventry CV6 6GE

Find out more

Meet more than 4,500 design engineers actively searching for the products, technologies and expertise to solve their next challenge. Spanning Embedded, Electronic and Mechanical Design, EDS attracts engineers and decision-makers from ambitious start-ups to global manufacturers. 

Discover the latest technologies, compare leading suppliers, gain practical insights from industry experts and explore the innovations shaping the future of design engineering. 

Engineering Design Show is proudly organised by Mark Allen Group, one of the UK's leading engineering media and events businesses. Alongside EDS, our portfolio includes trusted publications such as The Engineer, Eureka, New Electronics, Manufacturing Management, FAST and Made in Ireland, as well as industry-leading events including Subcon.
