Cookies at the Mill

Essential cookies keep the site working — signing in, security, and remembering this choice. Optional ones stay off unless you say yes: the maps, video and audio some pages embed from Google, YouTube and SoundCloud.

Cookie settings

Choose what we may use. You can change your mind whenever you like — “Cookie preferences” lives in the footer of every page.

Essential

Always on

Signing in, security tokens that stop forged form submissions, the spam check on the registration page (Cloudflare Turnstile), and remembering the choices you make here. The site doesn’t work without these. Cookies: islingtonmill-session and XSRF-TOKEN (for your visit), im_cookie_consent (six months — then we ask again).

Embedded media

Some pages embed maps, video and audio from Google Maps, YouTube and SoundCloud, and those services set their own cookies when they load. Leave this off and we’ll show a button instead, so you can load each one only when you want it.
