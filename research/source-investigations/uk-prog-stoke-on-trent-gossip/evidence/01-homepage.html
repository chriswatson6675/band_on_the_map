Hanley's No.1 Party Venue!

Come and join us at Hanley's safest late night LGBT+ venue. With 2 floors indoors and heated outdoor terraces on a further two levels. We offer you the best in freshly made cocktails, craft beers, ciders and draught. Friday and Saturday sees the opening of our popular hot food bar to the rear of the garden.

The dancefloor's the place to be seen with great company and atmosphere, but we also have a range of lush sofa areas and games facilities for your enjoyment.

We aim to offer the safest venue in the area for all the LGBT community and friends. Operating on a Challenge25 basis we request that all visitors register on our Netscan ID system on entry.
