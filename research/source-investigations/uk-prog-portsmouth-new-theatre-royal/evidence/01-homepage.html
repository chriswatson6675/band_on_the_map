Honouring heritage inspiring today

Step into the Theatre Royal – a historic gem with a bold, modern edge. Rooted in a legacy of over 160 years, our stage is a bridge between timeless stories and today’s vibrant creativity.

From amazing in-house productions to intimate evenings with household names, we are a home for culture and community. Explore a space where Portsmouth’s rich past inspires its exciting present.
