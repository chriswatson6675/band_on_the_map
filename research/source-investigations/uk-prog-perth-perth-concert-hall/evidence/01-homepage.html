About Us

Based in the heart of Scotland and central to local cultural life for over 120 years, Perth Theatre and Concert Hall create life affirming, shared experiences in the performing arts that ignite the imagination and make living more meaningful. 

Address: Mill Street, Perth, PH1 5HZ

Box Office Hours: Tue-Sat 10:00-16:00, plus 1 hour before performance

Need help with bookings and enquiries? Contact our Ticketing Team on 01738 621031 or ticketing@perththeatreandconcerthall.com. You can also pop in during our core opening hours at our Perth Theatre box office.
