Move with Purpose. Dance with Power.

G-Expressions [GX] is a street dance project that practices numerous types of dance disciplines, based in the ever changing city of Newport. GX believe education is the key to self determination in making creative ideas come alive.

Through dance, theatre, and leadership activities, we give young people the confidence, tools, and skills to achieve their goals and supportive conditions in which to grow and thrive. We then encourage them to go out and have new experiences which they bring back and share with their younger peers.
