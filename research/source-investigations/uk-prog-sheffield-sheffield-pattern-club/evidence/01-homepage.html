Sheffield

Sheffield Pattern Club is a group of people who meet up to explore patterns in craft, technology, music, performing arts etc. Imposters and beginners are welcome. We also run the alpacalab studio in central Sheffield. If you have thoughts/questions to share/ask please get in contact.

Instigated by Ray, Lucy and Alex.

Radio show

We have had a month radio show on Resonance FM and before that Mondo Radio. We’re currently focussing on other things but may return to it later in 2026. You can listen to our resonance fm shows on mixcloud or older mondo ones on soundcloud.
