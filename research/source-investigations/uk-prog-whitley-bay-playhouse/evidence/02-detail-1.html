The UK’s longest-running burlesque theatre spectacular returns with a dazzling new production, packed with glamour, excitement and unforgettable entertainment.

Welcome to An Evening of Burlesque - a sensational journey into the world of cabaret, variety and modern burlesque, where elegance meets extravagance in a show unlike any other.

Prepare to be captivated by a breathtaking cast of glamorous showgirls, extraordinary speciality acts and charismatic performers. Combining stunning cabaret, laugh-out-loud comedy and seductive burlesque artistry, this spectacular production delivers world-class entertainment from the moment the curtain rises.

A timeless art form reimagined for today’s audiences, An Evening of Burlesque celebrates the glamour, sophistication and playful spirit that have made burlesque a global phenomenon. With spectacular costumes, dazzling performances and a touch of vintage charm, every moment is designed to delight.

Gather your friends, dress to impress and raise a glass as the UK's longest-running burlesque theatre spectacular transports you to a world of glamour, laughter and pure escapism.

The curtain is rising. The spotlight is waiting. Don’t miss the magic. 

 

Age restriction 18+
