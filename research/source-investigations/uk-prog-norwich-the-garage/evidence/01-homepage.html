The Garage

The Garage Trust is a Norfolk-based performing arts charity running two arts venues: The Garage in Norwich &The Workshop in King’s Lynn. We run dance, drama and music classes for all ages and abilities; present the best of small-scale theatre from around the UK; provide GCSE qualifications and an Alternative Provision (unregulated) programme for primary and secondary school pupils; run a wide range of community engagement programmes; support artist development; and co-produce great performances for children and young people. 

Our purpose is to change people’s lives through art, especially the lives of young people, and those who face challenging circumstances.

Our Registered Charity No. is 1098975
