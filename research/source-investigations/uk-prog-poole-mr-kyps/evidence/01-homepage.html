About US

Music. Culture. Good Stories.

Mr Kyps began with live entertainment and a simple idea: bring people together for a great night out.

For more than twenty years, the original Mr Kyps venue in Poole played host to live bands, tribute acts, local talent, touring performers, and audiences who shared a love of music and entertainment. The venue became part of countless stories, from first gigs and unforgettable performances to friendships built over years of returning to the same familiar room.

A lot of those stories still get told today. Sometimes it’s a conversation over a pint. Sometimes it’s an old photo that appears online and sends people down memory lane for half an hour. One person remembers the band. Someone else remembers the crowd. Another remembers getting home at two in the morning with ringing ears and absolutely no regrets.

This site grew out of that history. Some of what you’ll find here looks back at the past, while other articles focus on what’s happening now. Music remains close to the heart of Mr Kyps, though the scope has widened over the years. Entertainment, comedy, local culture, community stories, event experiences, and the people behind them all have a place here.

We’re interested in the things people actually talk about afterwards. The show nobody expected to be that good. The local act that won over a room full of strangers. The comedian who had the audience in stitches within minutes. The small moments that somehow end up becoming the biggest memories.

Whether you’re interested in live music, comedy, event culture, audience experiences, or stories from the archives, you’ll find plenty to read, revisit, and share.

Culture & Community

Every event starts with people and ends with people.

The posters, tickets, stages, and sound systems matter. The people matter more.

Behind every packed room sits a mix of organisers, performers, bar staff, volunteers, regulars, and first-time visitors. Some spend months planning an event. Others simply show up and become part of the atmosphere. Together they create something that can’t be replicated once the night is over.

You’ll find stories about local culture, entertainment, nostalgia, audience experiences, community projects, and the traditions that shape social life across the South Coast. Some articles focus on venues and events. Others focus on the people who support them year after year.

Not every memorable moment happens under a spotlight. Plenty happen at the bar, in the queue outside, or during a conversation between strangers who happen to share the same favourite song. Those small details often tell a bigger story than the headline act.

Communities aren’t built overnight. They grow through shared experiences, familiar faces, and places that people care about enough to keep coming back to. That’s something worth talking about.

Looking Forward

The site continues to grow, picking up new stories, new contributors, and new ideas along the way. Some articles will focus on music. Others will wander into comedy, entertainment, local history, community life, or whatever happens to spark a bit of conversation.

If a story feels interesting, if a memory deserves another look, or if somebody has something worth saying, there’s usually room for it here. That’s always been part of the appeal.

The names may change. The venues may change. New faces arrive while old friends reappear when you least expect it. That’s how things tend to work.

What remains constant is the interest in people, places, and experiences that leave their mark. The kind of moments that get mentioned years later when somebody says, “Do you remember that night?”
