OUR VENUE

Since 1997, Corporation has been more than just a venue; it’s been a home for music, community, and culture in Sheffield. For the past 25 years, Milton Street has been our home and has seen some of the biggest names in music grow from grassroots beginnings to global stardom, and we’ve stood proudly as one of the UK’s most iconic independent venues.
