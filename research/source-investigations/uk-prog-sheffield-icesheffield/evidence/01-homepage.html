About this Centre

Ice Sheffield is a premier ice-skating venue in the UK, featuring two Olympic-sized rinks - The Bauer Arena and the Community Ice Pad. The first of these is our elite ice rink, home to competitions and the Sheffield Steeldogs, providing exciting ice hockey matches for spectators. Secondly, The Community Ice Pad is where skaters of all ages and abilities can enjoy the ice. Enjoy leisure skating sessions, take ice-skating lessons, participate in training programs, and compete in various events.

Beyond the ice, Ice Sheffield offers convenient amenities for a complete experience. The Core Bar Restaurant provides refreshments, while Willies Ice - Inline and Figure shop caters to all your ice-skating equipment needs.

For those seeking more than just skating, Ice Sheffield offers two meeting and conference rooms available for hire, along with ample parking and modern changing facilities for your convenience.

Bauer Arena

The Bauer Arena is our Olympic size ice pad and the home of Elite Ice Sport activity at Ice Sheffield. It is the official home rink of the Sheffield Steeldogs and the Sheffield Junior Ice Hockey Academy.

Across the year, we host a wide range of major events, including multiple British Ice Skating competitions, culminating in the British Ice Skating Figure Skating Championships. The arena also stages several England Ice Hockey events such as the Steel City Showdown, Junior Finals, and the Ladies Trophy Weekend.

Each December, the Bauer Arena becomes the stage for our annual Christmas show, featuring a cast of around 300 skaters who train at the facility throughout the year.

Community Ice Pad

The Community Ice Pad offers a welcoming environment for skaters of all ages and abilities. It hosts our public skating sessions and the largest Learn to Skate programme in the UK, alongside the Learn to Play Ice Hockey programme powered by Bauer.

The pad is frequently used for ice hockey matches, training sessions, private hire, birthday parties, and a variety of smaller club events, making it a versatile and vibrant space for the whole community.

Core Bar Restaurant

We have a fully-licensed bar – the Core Bar Restaurant – on-site serving a variety of soft and alcoholic drinks to suit everyone. There’s also the Core Bar Restaurant, which serves a selection of meals and snacks at the rink side.

360° View Of The Core Bar Restaurant

Willies - Ice - Inline - Figure Shop

Willies offers a wide variety of equipment for both ice hockey and ice skating, including skates, sticks, helmets, and protective gear. They also carry a selection of clothing and accessories. Whether you are a beginner or a seasoned pro, Willies have everything you need to get out on the ice and have fun. They are committed to providing our customers with the best possible service and selection.

Meeting & Conference Rooms

We have two meeting and conference spaces available for hire – the Boardroom and the Glacier Suite. The former has space for up to 20 people, while the latter can host up to 100.
