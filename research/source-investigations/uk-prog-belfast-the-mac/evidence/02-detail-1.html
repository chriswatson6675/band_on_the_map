Details

This Christmas at the MAC, step into a magical wintery woodland for Goldilocks and the Three Bears - where the newly adopted Baby Bear arrives with a tiny suitcase and one big wish: to belong.

Waiting for him is a cosy cottage and two wonderfully overexcited grizzly bear parents, Mama Bear and Papa Bear, who can’t wait to welcome him home.

Determined to impress his new family, Baby Bear stumbles into a series of hilarious mishaps as he tries to prove he can be truly perfect.

But as Christmas Eve draws near, the fabulous (and very wicked) showgirl Goldilocks sweeps into the forest in search of her next theatrical sidekick… and Baby Bear is top of her list.

Will Baby Bear finally find where he belongs, or will Goldilocks’ wicked tricks steal him away?

Packed with catchy songs, laugh-out-loud, fun-filled moments, and a few Home Alone-style gags, this joyful Christmas musical is a heartwarming story about family, friendship and the magic of being yourself.

Whether you've made the MAC Christmas show your yearly family tradition, or you’re starting a new one this year, Goldilocks and the Three Bears is just right!

Written By Ciarán Haggerty. Directed by Patrick J O'Reilly.

Artwork by Shesaid Design.

Goldilocks and the Three Bears is a show for all ages, but kids aged 5+ will get the biggest kicks.

How Our Prices Work

How Our Prices Work

Pick the best seats for you

We offer a range of pricing bands so you can pick the seat - and the price - that works best for you. Prices vary depending on where you sit in the Main Stage, from seats up close to the stage, to good-value spots in the mezzanine, and great sight lines a little further back. Whichever you choose, you’ll still enjoy the very same show.

Booking Fee: The MAC is a charity dedicated to creativity, culture, and connection - every £1.50 we make goes directly into supporting our transformative arts and community programmes. Therefore, a booking fee of £1.50 per ticket will apply to all bookings made through our website and over the phone.

Child tickets are for 15yrs and under. Babies under 12 months go free - please contact us to add a free ‘Babes in Arms’ ticket to your booking.

*There is no booking fee charge for bookings made in person, free events, school groups, community groups, personal assistant tickets and 16-25 member tickets.

Interested in bringing a group?

School tickets are £10 but buy before 30 June and grab our early bird rate of only £9 and with every 10 tickets booked you’ll get the 11th ticket free. Fill out our school booking form here and we'll be in touch to confirm your preferred date and tickets.

For community rate (available on selected dates), corporate or large bookings please email [email protected] for more information.

Accessibility

Accessible Seats

Wheelchair Accessible Seats

We have 6 dedicated wheelchair seats in The Main Stage – two on the balcony and four on the ground floor (please see seats listed below). These seats are locked off sale on our website so that we can keep them available for wheelchair users. To book a wheelchair accessible seat please call our box office team at 028 9023 5053.

Wheelchair Accessible Seats:

Stalls - Row A1-2 and A18-19 (accessible via ground floor) & Row L1-2 and L18-19 (accessible via floor 1 via lift)

Balcony - Row P22-23 and 20-21 (accessible via floor 2 via lift)

Seats with no steps

If you have any issues with steps, we have a number of seats available with no steps to access.

Rows with no steps:

  • Row A in the Stalls (accessible via ground floor)
  • Row L in the Stalls (accessible via floor 1 via lift)
  • Row P in the Balcony (accessible via floor 2 via lift)

Hearing Loop

There is an induction loop in The Main Stage which is available during all performances. The loop amplifies the sound of the show via customer’s hearing aid or earphones alongside our infrared devices.

  • Infrared necklace – compatible with hearing aids switched to channel T.
  • Infrared headset – for customers with no hearing aid.

To book an infrared necklace/headset ahead of your visit, please contact our box office at [email protected] or via phone at 028 9023 5053.

Join our Access Register

The MAC has developed an Access Register which aims to help us do all possible to meet your needs when visiting us‚ whatever your needs may be. We welcome you to join our Access Register here.

Carer’s Ticket Policy

If you require a personal assistant or carer, you are entitled to a free carer’s ticket. Please contact our box office at [email protected] or via phone at 028 9023 5053 to book a free carer’s ticket.
