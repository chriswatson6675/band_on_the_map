Frensham Foundation Ball

The inaugural Frensham Foundation Ball will take place on Saturday 17 October 2026, bringing together alumni, parents, staff, governors and friends of Frensham Heights for a special evening of celebration and community.

​

Guests can look forward to dinner, live music, mixologist-crafted cocktails, casino entertainment and a live auction, all with a nod to the elegance and glamour of the 1920s.
