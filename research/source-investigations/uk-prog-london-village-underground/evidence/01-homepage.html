Private hire

The Village Underground warehouse is a blank canvas ready for events of all kinds to transform it. From global brand activations, unique weddings, film shoots, cool-than-your-average conferences and tons more – the limit is your imagination.  All commercial revenue contributes to our community and artistic programming.

Village Underground Rooftop

For the first time, Village Underground opens its rooftop to the public.

Set above our main venue, a renovated Victorian warehouse, the space brings together an open-air bar with access to our tube carriages – long a feature of the Shoreditch skyline but never before open to visitors.
