Robert turns Large bowls, Hollow forms and Vessels in Burr, Exotic and Native woods ~ especially Monkey Puzzle, all from sustainable forests/sources.  Robert is a professional wood turner and is on the Register of Professional Turners supported by the Worshipful Company of Turners, he has been turning for over 30 years. His work consists of a variety of shapes using different woods. Many pieces are considered to be ‘outstanding’ and are exhibited in Art Galleries, Exhibitions, Craft Events and Shows and are widely sought after to add to Private collections internationally and at home here in the UK.
