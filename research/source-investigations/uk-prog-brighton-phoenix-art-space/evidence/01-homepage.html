ACCESSIBILITY

We want all of our visitors to have an enjoyable experience.

Our ground floor is wheelchair accessible, which includes the galleries, workshop rooms, café and toilets. Unfortunately our studios are only accessible via stairs. 

If you have any suggestions on how to make your visit easier, please get in touch.
