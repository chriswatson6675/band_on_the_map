Organising An Event

Discover more

Live Entertainment

A 9,000 sqm multi-purpose arena with a 10,000 all seated capacity and a 15,000 seated/standing capacity, P&J Live makes wowing your audience easy.

Conferences & Meetings

We cater for conferences and meetings of all sizes, offering adaptable spaces, breakout suites and registration areas.

Banquets & Balls

Our flexible floor plans and exceptional spaces allow for a range of seated occasions such as intimate dining experiences, right up to banquets of 2,790.

Exhibitions

Our multi-purpose event spaces can be tailored to exhibitions of any size - from large-scale trade expos to smaller consumer shows.

About Aberdeen

Where to stay

Our 2 on-site hotels are perfect for concert-goers, organisers and event delegates seeking the most convenient place to stay.

Food & Drink
