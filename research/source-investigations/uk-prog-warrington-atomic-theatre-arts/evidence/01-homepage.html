WHERE STARS

ARE BORN

IS NOW…

Quality theatre arts training in Warrington, Cheshire

 
 

We are building on our past 27 years of developing ATDance into a successful dance and performing arts training centre and now Karen, Carla and Sam have created Atomic Theatre Arts.

With a reminder of our roots,  AT is and always will be the nucleus of AtomicTheatre Arts.

Atomic is derived from the word Atom. We liked the idea that we as a school are a small component or the foundation building bricks of something more significant in our students’ life journeys. Whether they decide to go into theatre or choose a different career path, we absolutely want to provide them with the skills they need for the future. Still, it’s also about the friends they make, along with learning discipline, gaining confidence and understanding the importance of communication, hard work, punctuality, patience, kindness, and teamwork. We aim to weave all of these into every class to ensure that we play our part in our student's success stories, whatever path they choose.

We have been part of the local community for many years and we offer classes in theatre arts for children and adults. With many performance opportunities and dance and Lamda examinations offered, see how a high-quality  theatre arts education will help your child flourish as a young person.

Karen, Carla and Sam xxx

Atomic Theatre Arts

Part of the local community for 25 years, Atomic Theatre Arts (formerly ATDance) in Stockton Heath provides dance classes for children and adults. With a wide range of genres available our students are given the opportunity to fulfil their potential, grow in confidence and develop skills that will remain with them forever.

With many performance opportunities and dance examinations offered, let’s see how a high-quality dance education will help your child flourish as a young person.
