  • Fri 9

    Blues Echoes

    October 9 @ 7:30 pm - 9:30 pm
    The Melville Theatre The Melville Centre, Pen-y-Pound, Abergavenny, Monmouthshire, United Kingdom

    Blues Echoes is a dynamic mix of personal story, myth and folktales from both sides of the Atlantic. Drawing on mythical legends of America and parallels to attitudes around the industrial revolution and machine revolution in south Wales and the treatment of the working classes. At the same time Phil and Bill explore their own personal experiences of how music, and blues music shaped not only their lives and attitudes, but also in society. The show has some hard-hitting moments, while also creating a very warm and engaging performance that will make you laugh out loud.

    Readings & Music in aid of FLOW

    November 7 @ 7:00 pm - 9:00 pm
    The Melville Theatre The Melville Centre, Pen-y-Pound, Abergavenny, Monmouthshire, United Kingdom

    Supporting people facing mental health challenges, including those affected by PTSD, bereavement, war, and other trauma. FLOW provides a safe and welcoming space where individuals can share their feelings and experiences through poetry, stories, letters, and lyrics. Members are encouraged to read and support one another’s writing, creating a community built on understanding and shared expression. Participation is free through the FLOW website, where contributors can upload their work for others to read and comment on, with some pieces even finding their way into published collections.   • Fri 13

    Thrifty

    November 13 @ 7:30 pm - November 14 @ 9:30 pm
    The Melville Theatre The Melville Centre, Pen-y-Pound, Abergavenny, Monmouthshire, United Kingdom

    Step into a world where bargain bins brim with secrets, and second-hand treasures spark unforgettable tales—Thrifty, the dazzling new comedy by Cecilia Bawler, transforms a humble charity shop into a riotous stage for laughter, love, and a few surprising revelations. With every actor shape-shifting into a cast of quirky staff and eccentric customers, this heart warming romp invites you to discover how far people will go to support a cause unlike any other. Come for the comedy, stay for the compassion—Thrifty is not just a play, it’s a feel-good frenzy of human connection, priced to make your heart sing.
