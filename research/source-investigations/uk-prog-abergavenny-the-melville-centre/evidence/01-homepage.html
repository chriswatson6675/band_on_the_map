ACCESSIBILITY:

The Melville Centre warmly welcomes people with disabilities.
Please let us know in advance of any additional access arrangement you would require.
We have a disabled parking space at the rear of the building with a step Free pathway to the entrance.
We also have fully accessible toilet facilities, plus changing facilities for babies, and all rooms and our Theatre are accessible.

A PLACE FOR THE WHOLE COMMUNITY

Abergavenny’s hidden gem – an intimate theatre, rehearsal and event rooms and a bar.

  • Welsh Courses – learn Welsh in the evening

  • Crafts – we encourage learning and growth

  • People not profits – education is a right not a privilege

  • Room hire – for individuals, groups or events

OUR VALUES

OUR AIM

Providing opportunities for all ages and abilities to participate in successful arts activities and education.

OUR RESIDENTS

On site MCA CIC  hosts  Abergavenny Film Society, Black Mountain Jazz, Improv Stars Academy, Suitcase Theatre Company, Savoy Youth Theatre and many more.  We currently host a variety of classes presented by Coleg Gwent, Community Education, Writing for Fun Group, Abergavenny Lip Reading Group, & Stitch Together to name a few.

OUR ROOMS

Melville Centre for the Arts CIC manages the main building of the Melville Centre and its classrooms, meeting rooms, coffee-bar,  & the Melville Theatre (75 seats)  all available for hire. Book your space here.
