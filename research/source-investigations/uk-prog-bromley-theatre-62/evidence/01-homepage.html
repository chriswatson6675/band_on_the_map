Welcome to Theatre 62

Theatre 62 is an established community theatre group in West Wickham, south-east London, with its own studio theatre. Thanks for a taking a look at us.

What’s On

Silver Lining opens 27th September. Contact box office for returns only

Box office open. This comedy tells the tale of a group of five extraordinary-yet-forgotten women. They are residents in the Silver retirement home in Gravesend. They come together one treacherous stormy night to create The Great Escape – senior citizen style – when a storm causes a flood in the home.
But with no rescue team in sight, the ladies realise that in order to survive they must do what they have always done – do it themselves.
Pure joy from one of Britain’s best-loved writers, Sandi Toksvig. Warning: includes adult themes, strong language and loud noises.

About Us

Theatre 62 is a busy amateur dramatic group. For over 60 years we have been producing a variety of shows – from dramas to comedies to thrillers. We produce several shows each year, running Monday to Friday evenings, with Saturday and Sunday matinees.

As well as the studio theatre we have a rehearsal room, a licensed bar, and extensive wardrobe, scenery, props, sound and lighting facilities. Find out more about what we do and how you can get involved here

The 119 bus stop is nearby. West Wickham train station is 15-20 minutes walk away. There is free parking available at the Theatre.

How to get involved

If you would like to audition for a role, or fancy trying your hand backstage or front of house, or serving refreshments to our audiences, there will be something for you. You can get in touch with us via news@theatre62.org.uk
