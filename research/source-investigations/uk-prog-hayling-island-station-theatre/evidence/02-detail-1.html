Station Sessions 2

Sun 20th Sep to Sun 22nd Nov , 10:30am to 12:30pm.
Price per sessions:
£5 for HIADS Members,
£10 for Non Members. Ages 16+

Our popular weekend workshops are back, and this season there are two strands. Performance sessions cover audition technique, pantomime skills, voice and improvisation: the skills that help you walk on stage feeling prepared and confident. Tech sessions put you hands-on with our real lighting and sound equipment, from rigging the lights to plotting cues and programming sound, so you could help run an actual show.

The strands suit different people, but you're very welcome at both. Every session is built around doing rather than listening, whether you've been at this for decades or have never tried it at all. No experience needed.

The kettle's on from 10am, so come early for a coffee and a chat.

The sessions :

Sun 26 Jul — Audition Technique
Sat 12 Sep — Lighting 1 – Rigging
Sun 20 Sep — Lighting 2 – Equipment
Sun 27 Sep — Pantomime Skills
Sun 04 Oct — Lighting 3 – Plotting & Cueing
Sun 11 Oct — Voice
Sat 07 Nov — Improvisation in Performance
Sun 08 Nov — The Sound System
Sun 22 Nov — Programming Sound

Hayling Island Amateur Dramatic Society presents

Collaboration

Fri 23rd Oct to Sat 31st Oct
Thursday, Friday and Saturday evenings at 7:30pm, £14 (Early Bird £12)

This amateur production of "Collaboration" is presented by arrangement with Concord Theatricals Ltd. on behalf of Samuel French Ltd. www.concordtheatricals.co.uk

A gripping drama by Ronald Harwood, from the Oscar-winning writer of The Pianist and The Dresser.

Germany, 1931. The world's most celebrated composer, Richard Strauss, meets the brilliant Austrian writer Stefan Zweig, and together they begin work on a glorious new opera. It is a partnership made in heaven: two great artists at the height of their powers, sparking off one another with wit, warmth and soaring ambition.

But Zweig is Jewish, and the Nazis are on the march. As the regime tightens its grip on every stage and concert hall, Strauss is forced into an impossible bargain, one that could protect his family or betray his dearest friend. How far would you go to keep creating the thing you love? And where, exactly, does survival end and collaboration begin?

By turns funny, tender and devastating, Collaboration is a true story about art, friendship and conscience in the darkest of times, and the quiet compromises ordinary people make simply to carry on.

HIADS Film Showing:

Quartet

Fri 11th Dec 2:30pm, £6 (front 3 rows £4.50).

Starring: Maggie Smith, Michael Gambon, Pauline Collins, Tom Courtenay, Billy Connolly.

Dustin Hoffman directs this comedy drama adapted by writer Ronald Harwood from his own stage play of the same name. Beecham House is a retirement home with a difference: it specifically caters for former artistes including one-time opera singers Cissy (Pauline Collins), Reginald (Tom Courtenay) and Wilf (Billy Connolly).

Once part of a quartet, the three still take part in an annual concert to celebrate the birthday of composer Giuseppe Verdi. But this year things are complicated by the arrival of the fourth member of their quartet, Jean (Maggie Smith), a die-hard diva who also happens to be Reginald's ex-wife. Theatrical temperaments and old hostilities flare as the dramatic foursome fuss, flirt and flounce their way through rehearsals, adhering emphatically to that old showbiz adage, 'the show must go on'.

Cert 12, 98 mins.

Hayling Island Amateur Dramatic Society presents

Doorway to Christmas

Sun 13th Dec
Drinks and Snacks (included) from 2pm, Show 2:30pm to 3:00pm,
£10 Adults, £8 Children.

This December, one girl discovers a secret doorway in the snow that leads to a magical land where Christmas is in chaos.

The baubles are burning, the tinsel's in a tangle, the crackers won't bang, the star has fallen from the tree, and everything is running desperately behind schedule. Only she can help save Christmas in time.

In this brand new immersive family show, your adventure starts in Grandpa's Hideaway, where festive drinks and sweet treats are included and Grandpa is waiting with stories, mince pies, Christmas cookies and a secret to share.

Then it is out through the doorway into Christmasland for a 30 minute show full of colourful, madcap characters, mischief and carol singing. And if we're very lucky, Santa himself might pop in to wish everyone a Merry Christmas.

Perfect for children aged 4 and up, and anyone who still believes in a little Christmas magic.

Hayling Island Amateur Dramatic Society presents

Mother Goose

Fri 15th Jan to Sat 23rd Jan
Fridays 7:30pm, Saturdays and Sunday 3pm,
£16 Adults £12 Under 16's.
(HIADS members £14/£10)

By arrangement with Stage Right Creative Ltd.

A magical pantomime written by Alan P Frayn.

Oh yes it is! HIADS starts off 2027 with Mother Goose, the most warm-hearted (and feather-brained) pantomime of them all.

When a poor goose farmer takes in a magical goose called Priscilla, her luck turns golden overnight. But with riches come temptation, and the wicked Demon Discontent is determined to turn Mother Goose's head and win himself "Baddie of the Year".

Will the good Fairy, kind-hearted Jill and her beau Colin, and the not-so-silly Silly Billy save the day before our Dame loses everything that really matters?

Packed with golden eggs, groan-worthy gags, dastardly bailiffs, glittering song and dance, and all the boos, cheers and "he's behind you!" moments a family could wish for, this is feel-good fun for ages three to ninety-three.

Bring the whole gang, warm up your singing voices and join us at the Station Theatre for a right royal romp in the clouds.

Need a New Hobby?

Why not join the team at HIADS and find a great way to learn new, or show off old, talents? It's not only directors and actors that we need, but also all the great folk who sell tickets, look after visitors, can help build and paint sets, or make costumes and props, or setup lighting and sound or look after the accounts, or building maintenance, or advertising, social media and websites. Whatever your skill set or whatever you'd like to learn, we're sure to be able to use you. So why not Join Us.

We may be tucked away at the bottom of Hayling Island, but our current membership come from all over the Hamsphire and West Sussex south coast area, i.e. Hayling, Havant, Portsmouth and Emsworth, Porchester, Gosport and Chichester.
