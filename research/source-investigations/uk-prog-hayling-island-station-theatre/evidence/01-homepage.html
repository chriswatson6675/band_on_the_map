Warm Welcome To The Station Theatre Website

The Station Theatre on Hayling Island, Hampshire, is a non-profit making theatre run by HIADS (Hayling Island Amateur Dramatic Society) and is a member of the Little Theatre Guild.

The theatre puts on a wide variety of productions (plays and music events, etc) performed by both professional and amateur groups. We also show blockbuster films on one Friday of every month.

Cleverly converted from a disused railway good shed, the theatre is well equipped with parking, disabled access, bar and refreshments, a 144 seat auditorium with a raked seating to provide clear views of the stage for all, hearing loop, air conditioning and the latest sound and lighting technology.
