Embracing life

A love for learning

We inspire every child to love learning and create a foundation to realise their full potential, embracing life with confidence, passion, integrity and resilience, all underpinned by a mindset that values the importance of making a positive difference in the world.

An education that
inspires

A seamless journey from age 3 to 18

Clifton College provides a continuous, well-structured education that supports children at every stage, ensuring smooth transitions and a strong sense of community from Pre-Prep to Sixth Form.

Outstanding teaching and pastoral care

Our dedicated staff nurture every child’s strengths, providing personalised support in a caring environment where pupils feel valued, confident, and ready to achieve their potential.

Enriching opportunities beyond the classroom

From competitive sports and performing arts to outdoor adventures and diverse clubs, pupils have endless opportunities to explore new passions and develop lifelong skills.

Applying from overseas?

International applications

At Clifton College, we welcome students from all over the world. Our dedicated team provides support throughout the application process, ensuring a smooth transition from applying, through to life at our school. Learn more about what it's like to study here as an international pupil and how we create a homely and welcoming environment for your child.
