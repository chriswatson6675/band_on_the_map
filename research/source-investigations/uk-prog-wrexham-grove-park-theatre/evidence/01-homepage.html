Grove Park Theatre, Wrexham

We are Wrexham’s oldest amateur theatre, based in the town centre. We aim to provide the community with good value entertainment of a consistently high standard. Our shows range from comedy, drama and suspense through to costume plays, pantomimes and musicals.

We are run entirely by volunteers and are always keen to welcome new members in both the adult and youth sections, whether you want to appear on stage or help behind the scenes.
