The Centaur

For larger events, The Centaur has the wow factor. It has played host to award ceremonies, exhibitions and live events including World Championship Snooker, Paul Weller, and Jools Holland, and can morph seamlessly from a theatre style auditorium for 2,200 people to a dinner dance for 700 or a vibrant exhibition space. Its versatility is endless and it offers the largest single event space in the local area.​

  • Versatile space that can be used as a theatre style auditorium, a dinner dance, or a vibrant exhibition space.

  • Separate kitchen, with our award-winning catering team ready to create and serve your bespoke menus.

  • Balcony area, providing either additional exhibition space or a safe viewing platform to survey the action below. ​

  • Access for vehicles to drive in and unload. 
  • 1300 m²
  • AV equipment
  • Fast, reliable Wi-Fi
  • Room draping
