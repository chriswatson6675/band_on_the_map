OUR PAINTING SESSIONS

We offer ceramic painting sessions, where you can choose from a wide variety of pre-made pottery to create an artistic masterpiece, ready to collect roughly 7 days later.


You can now book online for groups of 6 or less!

OUR MAKING SESSIONS

If you fancy getting messy, we also have clay hand building  or wheel throwing sessions on set dates throughout the month here. To book for throwing, please call 0131 229 1399.




 Please be aware prepayments or deposits apply for all larger group bookings / clay sessions and must be booked over the phone by calling 0131 229 1399. 



We have been helping people decorate and make pottery since 1999. 


You can visit us 7 days a week for your creative enjoyment. Booking is highly recommended, especially at weekends and school holidays.
