Three days of exceptional racing and non-stop entertainment!

 

Join us for three unforgettable days of thrilling racing and epic Oktoberfest celebrations. With an impressive lineup of 7 Group Races, you won't want to miss out on seeing the top equine stars in action at the iconic home of horseracing. Racing on Saturday sees the highly anticipated Cheveley Park & Middle Park Stakes, both kindly supported by Tattersalls. The bet365 Cambridgeshire Handicap is another for the racing fans and forms the first leg of the 'Autumn Double'.

 

With Oktoberfest on course entertainment giving you Oompah Beats alongside racing feats - The Cambridgeshire Meeting will be a fixture that cannot be missed this Autumn!

 

Don't miss out and book your tickets today. 

 

BUY TICKETS

 

Don't forget, U18s go free. A parent or responsible adult (21+) must accompany all under 18s for entry and on course, with a limit of three children per adult. Read More.

 

Oktoberfest comes to Cambridgeshire Meeting!

Across all three days, expect Bavarian-inspired food, themed areas and live entertainment from The Hosenbrass Trio.

Fancy dress isn’t required, but it’s encouraged. So bring your best Oktoberfest look. Then, on Saturday, the celebrations ramp up for a full Oktoberfest experience, complete with an MC, Hornbrokers, Papparazi and even more Bavarian fun.
