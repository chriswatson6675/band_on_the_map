THE WAIT IS OVER. WE ARE BACK.

 

The William Hill Showcase raises the curtain to the 2026/27 Cheltenham season, and gives an exciting glimpse of the stars and emerging talent that could go on to feature at the Cheltenham Festival later in the season.

 

Competitive racing, an electric atmosphere and the unmistakable excitement of an opening meeting - all at the Home of Jump Racing on Friday 23th and Saturday 24th October 2026.

 

FABBAGIRLS are coming to Cheltenham! Following an action-packed day of racing, keep the celebrations going with iconic hits, powerhouse vocals, dazzling costumes and feel-good energy from the world's leading ABBA tribute act on Saturday of The Showcase. For more information, please go to Saturday's event page. 

 

Junior Jumpers can enjoy an interactive activity trail around the racecourse and ride on our Race.Ride.Cheltenham VR equicisers on Saturday 24th October. For more information, please go to Saturday's event page. 

 

Under 18’s race free. Please ensure you order your free childrens' ticket from our ticketing page.

Club/Tatts Enclosure

There's no better way to enjoy the day at Cheltenham than from the Club/Tatts Enclosure. It's an experience to remember with access to all public areas and spectacular views from the main grandstand, lawn or Winning Post. In addition to paddock viewing access, you can enjoy the Hall of Fame, all the public areas of the Princess Royal Stand.

Best Mate Enclosure

Best Mate Enclosure offers an atmosphere like no other. Arguably with the best views of the racing directly opposite the main grandstand and adjacent to the finish line. Best Mate is a favourite spot for groups, offering the perfect setting for a day of unforgettable racing action and celebration.
