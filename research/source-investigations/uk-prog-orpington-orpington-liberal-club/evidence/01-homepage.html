About OLC

Orpington Liberal Club is a small and friendly volunteer run social club in Orpington town centre, facing Tesco just up from the war memorial and near to the station.

We have a strong reputation for real ale and cider, and have multiple awards from CAMRA having been local and regional Club of the Year and a National Club of the Year finalist.

OLC stages many live music events ranging from folk to jazz, blues, rock & more.
We are also the home of Orpington Bridge Club & regularly host tabletop gaming events.

We have facilities for wheelchair access to the club and hall.

Friendly dogs on leads are also welcome.

We no longer have any formal connection with any political party; however we are members of the National Union of Liberal Clubs.
