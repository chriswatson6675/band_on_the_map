Towngate Theatre

We've been entertaining Basildon for 30 years – with drama, musicals, classical concerts, comedy, panto and so much more. On Saint Martin's Square at the heart of Basildon, we're just 6 minutes walk from Basildon Station, with plenty of parking next door.

Café

Coffee and cake at the Towngate makes a fantastic break from a day's shopping in Basildon. Pre-theatre meals, cocktails and lunch break snacks mean Towngate Café is the perfect place to share the fun and drama.
