Pink by Vicky Jackson

Saturday 3 October 2026

Vicky Jackson brings her sell-out Pink show back to The Grand!

Known for her incredibly close vocal and physical resemblance, Vicky Jackson’s Tribute to Pink has been a run-away success and has consistently been her most booked act since 2008.

The show sold out way in advance when she performed here in 2024, so early booking is advised!

Vicky has performed well over a thousand shows as Pink over a seven year period, and is the most vocally accurate, aesthetically similar tribute act to Pink available in the UK & Europe. Vicky’s decade of experience as a performer makes every show a huge success and regular repeat bookings from a wide range of satisfied clients are testament to this.
