Jack & the Beanstalk – Our company is cast!

After two fantastic days of auditions – and some incredibly difficult decisions – we're delighted to have brought together the company for our 2027 pantomime, Jack & the Beanstalk!

A huge thank you to everyone who auditioned. The standard across both our Ensemble and Principal auditions was exceptional, and we're enormously grateful to everyone who gave us their time, energy and talent.

Our cast will soon be heading into rehearsals as we begin bringing the magic, mayhem and larger-than-life characters of Jack & the Beanstalk to the stage.

We'll be introducing our cast and sharing plenty more from behind the scenes over the coming months – so watch this space!

Eight in a row for Upstagers! 🏆

We were absolutely thrilled to see our 2026 pantomime Snow White named Best Pantomime at the Wharfedale Festival of Theatre Awards – making it an incredible eight consecutive Best Pantomime wins for Upstagers.

Snow White also received awards for Best Principal Girl (Amy-Pearl Watt), Best Dame (Mike Riley), Best Villain (Daisy Button), Best Choreographer (Laura Williams, joint winner), Best Director (John Clark, Andrew Hewitt & Joey Wilby) and Best Programme (Andrew Tarbett & Daisy Tarbett), alongside a fantastic collection of Wharfie Awards for the Mini Miners.

Pantomime is a huge team effort, and we're enormously proud of everyone who helped bring Snow White to the stage – our cast, crew, creatives and the many volunteers working behind the scenes.

A huge thank you, too, to the Wharfedale Festival of Theatre, its committee and assessors for everything they do to celebrate and encourage amateur theatre across our area – and congratulations to all the groups, performers and creatives recognised at this year's awards. We're incredibly lucky to be part of such a thriving local theatre community.

Upstagers Academy

Upstagers has been providing a creative outlet for youngsters in the Ilkley area since 1987. We have a great team of teachers providing fulfilling, fun and beneficial classes to our next generation of Upstagers stars. Our students get the most out of their experience by building confidence and developing communication skills, whilst also having a lot of fun and meeting new friends. We're proud that many Upstagers alumni have created successful careers in theatre and other forms of performance.

Classes are available for six-year-olds up to young adults and take place at the Upstagers Barn opposite Ilkley railway station.
