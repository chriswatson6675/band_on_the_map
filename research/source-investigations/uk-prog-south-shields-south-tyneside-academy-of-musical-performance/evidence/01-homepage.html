Joining STAMP

What age can my child start at STAMP?

Children can join STAMP from age 3. Our groups are organised by age so that sessions are suitable, enjoyable and engaging for everyone.

Explore our classes

Are STAMP classes currently accepting new members?

Availability changes throughout the year. Check our Classes or Join STAMP pages for the latest availability and trial options.

Explore Classes · Join STAMP

Does my child need previous experience?

No. STAMP welcomes complete beginners as well as young performers with previous experience. Sessions help every member develop their singing, dancing and acting skills from their own starting point.

Is an audition required to join a weekly group?

No. You do not need to audition to join our regular weekly classes. Some productions may have separate auditions for principal or named roles.

Can my child try a class before joining?

Where spaces are available, children can usually book a trial before joining. Visit the Join STAMP page to see the current options for each group.

Visit Join STAMP

Classes

What happens in a STAMP class?

Our weekly classes combine singing, dancing or movement, and acting. The balance changes depending on the age group and what members are learning or rehearsing at the time.
