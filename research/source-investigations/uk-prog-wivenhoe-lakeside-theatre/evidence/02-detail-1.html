Animal Farm

Thu 12 Nov - Sat 14 Nov

“All animals are equal, but some are more equal than others.”

When Old Major shares his dream of a Golden Future Time, the animals of Manor Farm dare to believe that another world is possible.

Change is in the air. Hooves thunder. Gates are broken. The old order falls, and the farm is theirs at last.

But promises can be rebranded, rules can be rewritten, and before long, the pigs are promising to make Animal Farm great again.

This bold and physical retelling of George Orwell’s Animal Farm is a charged, chaotic and darkly funny exploration of power, propaganda and the corruption of ideals. In an age of misinformation, political division and populist rhetoric, Orwell’s masterpiece feels less like a warning from the past and more like a commentary on the present.

After all, revolutions don’t belong to heroes. They belong to everyone.

Adapted from the novel by George Orwell
Directed by Rich Hay
Presented by East 15 BA Acting & Fight Performance graduating actors
