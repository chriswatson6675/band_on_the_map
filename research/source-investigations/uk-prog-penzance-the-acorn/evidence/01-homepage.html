We’re on the look-out for new trustees!

We’re currently looking for a couple of new people to join our Board of Trustees and help support the future of The Acorn. If you have experience in HR, finance, marketing or legal process, and a passion for the arts and your local community, we’d love to hear from you. Being a trustee is a fantastic opportunity to use your skills and experience to make a real difference, helping to shape the future of The Acorn and supporting us as we continue to bring great arts, entertainment and experiences to our community. You don’t need to have been a trustee before – we’re looking for enthusiastic, committed people who can bring their knowledge, ideas and a fresh perspective to the team.

Interested in getting involved? Send your CV and a cover letter to [email protected]. We look forward to hearing from you!
