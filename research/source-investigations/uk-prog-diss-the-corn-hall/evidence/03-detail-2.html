Aladdin
Panto
18 Dec to 3 Jan
Book Tickets

Aladdin

Production Company: Ryan Holt Ltd
Venue: The Corn Hall
Adults: £20.50
Under 16s: £18.50
Family Ticket: (2 adults and 2 under 16s OR 1 adult and 3 under 16s) £74.00
Restricted View: £17.95

After last year’s smash-hit Beauty & the Beast, SparkLive Pantomimes are back this festive season with a truly genie-us adventure  - Aladdin! ✨

Get ready for a magical journey packed with laughter, spectacle, and all your favourite panto traditions. Diss favourites return in style, with the hilarious dame Dave Short as Widow Twankey and the brilliant Jefferson Parlett bringing the laughs as Wishee Washee.

Leading the cast is the legendary Graham Cole OBE, best known as PC Tony Stamp from ITV’s The Bill, stepping into the role of the sinister sorcerer Abanazar. Expect mischief, mayhem, and plenty of boos!

Also returning to the Diss stage are Evie Forsdicke as the enchanting Spirit of the Ring and Genie of the Lamp, alongside Maisie Wild as the dazzling Princess Jasmine.

From hilarious comedy and outrageous slapstick to show-stopping musical numbers and breathtaking magical moments, Aladdin is the ultimate festive treat for all ages. Whether you're young, young-at-heart, or somewhere in between, there’s something for everyone to enjoy this festive season. 

Our much-loved pantomime blends timeless tradition with a modern sparkle, delivering unforgettable family entertainment without breaking the bank. With stunning sets, dazzling costumes, and laugh-out-loud fun, it’s no wonder this production is the jewel in South Norfolk’s festive offering.

So hop on your magic carpet and get ready to experience a whole new world this Christmas at The Corn Hall Diss! 

Estimated Running Time: 2hrs 30mins (inc interval) 

🎫 Panto Pay
Make your Christmas panto booking feel like magic with Panto Pay!
 
Panto Pay spreads the cost of a family ticket over three manageable payments. Panto Pay is available when family tickets are purchased in person at our Box Office or via telephone sales only.
 
Panto Pay is available for family tickets only. A family ticket is for either 2 adults and 2 children under 16, or 1 adult and 3 children under 16 with the total ticket price including a £1 per person booking fee. 
 
Simply pay £23.33 when you book, £23.33 between Saturday 1 August and Friday 28 August 2026, and the final £23.34 between Thursday 1 October and Friday 30 October 2026. 
 
Please note that all payments are non-refundable, tickets cannot be transferred to another panto performance or show as part of the payment plan and the final balance must be paid by Friday 30 October 2026 to keep your booking. 
 
It’s a handy way to secure your family’s seats now and look forward to a truly genie-us festive treat!
