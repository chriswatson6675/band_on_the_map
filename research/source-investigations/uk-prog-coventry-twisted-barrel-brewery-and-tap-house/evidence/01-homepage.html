The Tap House is where you can get all of Twisted Barrel Ale's beers first and freshest.

Our current opening hours are as follows:
Wednesday/Thursday - 5pm to 10pm
Friday -  5pm to 11pm
Saturday - 12pm 11pm
Sunday - 12pm to 8pm

Please note we maintain our opening hours on this website, Google and Facebook - please do not trust any times stated on any other source!

We serve food from opening to 8pm (5pm Sundays).

We regularly run a number of events, including weekly live music, quizzes, home brew clubs, tap takeovers, beer and music festivals and many more. Check out our Facebook page for full details.
