Pig and Pump - Bar and Kitchen, Chesterfield

We would like to welcome you to Chesterfields newest pub and kitchen. Formerly known as The White Swan, The Pig and Pump is a traditional pub serving a large range of real ales, traditional ciders, a craft offering and home cooked locally sourced food. Our menu will be complemented by ever-changing bar snacks, quality wine and stand out spirits. We will be showcasing live bands from around the country every Saturday night, bringing the town centre something slightly different.

Real ale is at the forefront of our concept, every effort will be made to join in the towns pedigree of excellent beer festivals, meet the brewer evenings and brewery tours, plus strong relationships with the local CAMRA groups. Our home cooked local menu will be served every day, appealing to all ages and dietary requirements, including lovely coffees and cake. Dogs will be welcome, the dog bar will be outside on the terrace.
