Attending Events at the Brighton Centre

We look forward to welcoming you to the Brighton Centre.

If you're attending an event here soon please take the time to familiarise (or remind) yourself about everything you need to know ahead of your visit.

Please arrive early. Doors usually open x1 hour before the first act goes on stage. This allows you time to get through security checks, avoid lengthy queues etc. It also means you can grab a drink and start your evening with us in a relaxed way.

SECURITY: your safety and security remains our number one priority. Please read through our security information page, noting what items are prohibited (including reusable water bottles) and remember not to bring a bag bigger than an A4 piece of paper (21cm x 30cm x 19cm).

WAYS TO PAY: we're strongly encouraging the use of contactless or card payment only. All the venue's catering outlets are card only. Please avoid using cash when you can in other parts of the venue.

TRAVEL: plan ahead, please leave early to avoid delays and rush hour/ busy times on public transport.

FAQs: we have a huge FAQs section available which should answer any questions you have but if you still haven't found what you're looking for please contact us.
