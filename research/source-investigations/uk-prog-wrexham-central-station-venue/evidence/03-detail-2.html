The Heavy North

The Heavy North is an independent, six-piece garage blues-rock outfit from Liverpool. Their sound pairs a heavy, soulful, vinyl-ready energy with contemporary indie grit—landing directly for fans of The Black Keys, Jack White, and Alabama Shakes.

Formed in late 2018, The Heavy North released their critically-acclaimed debut studio album 'Electric Soul Machine' in 2022. In recent years the band has caught the attention of BBC Radio 2, 6 Music, Radio X, BBC Introducing, Classic Rock Magazine and Tim's Twitter Listening Party.

While the band has continued to perform live across the UK during the last 12 months, including headline shows at iconic venues such as London's 100 Club and Liverpool's Cavern Club, The Heavy North has kept fans and followers eagerly awaiting new releases.
