Bournemouth’s biggest and most loved pantomime is back - now with Coronation Street star James Cartwright and BGT's Semi-Finalist Kristel Herrera!

Your wish is our command! This Christmas, step into the magical world of Aladdin at Bournemouth Pavilion Theatre, where adventure, laughter and festive sparkle come together in one unforgettable family pantomime.

Fresh from one of Coronation Street’s biggest and most talked-about storylines, James Cartwright stars as the wicked Abanazar - so get ready to bring your loudest boos! Known to millions as Theo Silverton, James is set to cast his spell over Bournemouth as one of panto’s most deliciously dastardly villains.

He joins Britain's Got Talent Finalist Kristel Herrera and Princess Jasmine, Panto newcomer Dante Nardone as Aladdin, Jamie Steen as the gloriously outrageous Widow Twankey, and Bournemouth favourite Noel Brodie, returning for his incredible 12th year as Wishee Washee.

Bigger. Bolder. More Magical.

0 Stars

Innuendos galore, non-stop banter, outrageous customs, pop culture and plenty of audience participation!

New Milton Advertiser and Lymington Times

Expect spectacular scenery, breathtaking lighting, fabulous costumes, high-energy choreography and all the sparkle of a festive theatre experience made for the whole family. From the first burst of music to the final wave goodbye, Aladdin will sweep you away on a magic carpet ride packed with laughter, surprises, songs, silliness and heart.

Magic. Mayhem. Must-see!

Cheer the heroes, boo Abanazar, shout yourself silly and laugh out loud as the Pavilion is transformed into a world of wishes, wonder and festive fun.

For returning families, this is the Christmas tradition you know and love. For first-time visitors, it is the perfect year to discover why Bournemouth Pavilion Theatre’s pantomime means so much to so many. This is one festive adventure you will not want to miss. Tickets are flying out, starting from just £18*.

Don’t leave it to a lamp, a genie or the organiser in the family saying, “we’ll sort it nearer the time.” Book now and make Aladdin the Christmas experience your family will be talking about long after the last “oh yes it is!” has echoed around the Pavilion.

This Christmas, the adventure of a lifetime awaits!

*Excluding booking fees.

Venue info

Excellent transport links

Our theatre is perfectly located with excellent transport links. Whether you're arriving by bus, train or by bike, getting here is simple and hassle-free.

Parking

Travelling by car? There’s convenient parking available at the venue.

Please note the car park is operated by BCP Council.

Shows for everyone

Enjoy a packed programme featuring West End musicals and plays, comedy, live music, dance, panto and much more.

Friendly team

Our welcoming staff are on hand to make your visit as smooth and enjoyable as possible. Got a question? Just ask, we're happy to help!

Refreshments & Bars

Relax before the show or during the interval with a choice of hot and cold drinks and light snacks from our bars.

Fantastic location

Make a day of it! Our theatre is located in the heart of Bournemouth. There is plenty to see and do in the area, especially the stretch of golden beaches.
