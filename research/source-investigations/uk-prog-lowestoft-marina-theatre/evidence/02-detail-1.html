Mirror mirror on the wall…don’t miss the fairest panto of them all!

When the Wicked Queen learns that Snow White is the fairest in the land, she’ll stop at nothing to get to number one – even if it takes a poisoned apple to get there. Snow White flees to the enchanted forest to escape her evil clutches, but trouble is afoot when there’s an unwelcome knock at the door…

This timeless tale comes to life with laugh-out-loud comedy, dazzling costumes and plenty of audience participation.

Boo the baddies, cheer the heroes, and join in the magic!

Snow White is the perfect festive treat for the whole family!

Heigh-ho, heigh-ho, it’s off to the Marina Theatre we go!

 

Starring…

X Factor Winner and I’m A Celebrity Get Me Out Of Here contestant Myles Stephenson from Rak-Su as Prince Louis

Returning for his 11th season, Terry Gleed as Muddles

International Award-Winning Comedian Patrick Monahan as Herman the Henchman

Simon Howe as Dame Dolly

Alexandra Lonsdale as Snow White

Jessica Spalis as The Wicked Queen
