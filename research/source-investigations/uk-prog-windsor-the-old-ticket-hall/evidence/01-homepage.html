Old Ticket Hall

The Old Ticket Hall is open Thursday through to Saturday, so why stay in at home when you could be out partying with us? Our great mix of artists range from acoustic acts to live bands, making each evening here unique. Whether you live in the area or are here on a visit, you mustn’t miss out on Windsor’s best live music venue. With our singers and bands playing an eclectic mix of your favourite chilled out tunes, as well as the chart toppers, it’s always a brilliant night! Alternatively, sign up to our weekly email and get continuous access to latest news on our live events.
