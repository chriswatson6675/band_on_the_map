Eat & Drink at Wilton's

Rehearsal Space

Tucked away on the second floor this light and airy space is perfect for rehearsals, auditions, workshops and readings; this is where Wilton’s rehearses our annual Christmas production.

Hourly rate (bar closed):
£60 + VAT per hour (minimum of 3 hours) for access between 10.00 – 17.00 Monday to Friday 

Daily Rate (bar closed):
£240 + VAT for access 10.00 – 17.00 Monday to Friday
£340 + VAT for access10.00 – 17.00 Saturday 

Evening Rate with access to our staffed bar:
£550 + VAT for access 17.00 – 23.00 Monday to Thursday 

Weekend Evening Rate with access to our staffed bar:
£650 + VAT for access 17.00 – 23.00 Friday and Saturday 

Weekly Rate:
£1,000 + VAT for access 10.00 – 18.00 Monday to Friday
£1,200 + VAT for access 10.00 – 18.00 Monday to Saturday

Capacity dependent on the event and seating layout: Theatre style row – 40 seated. Standing – 60 standing.

Please note there is limited lift access to the AALP Studio which is located on the second floor of the building.
