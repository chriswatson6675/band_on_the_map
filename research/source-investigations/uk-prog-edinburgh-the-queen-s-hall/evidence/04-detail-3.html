Wishbone Ash

One of rock music’s most enduring and influential forces.

 
  • facebook
  • twitter

For over five decades, Wishbone Ash has stood as one of rock music’s most enduring and influential forces. 

Their pioneering twin-lead guitar sound provided a blueprint for the generations of rock, metal, and progressive artists that followed in their footsteps. With an extensive catalogue, countless miles on the road, and a legacy woven into the fabric of rock history, they continue to inspire audiences worldwide. Don’t miss this exciting opportunity to experience a legendary band that don’t just celebrate their illustrious past, but continue to embrace the future.
