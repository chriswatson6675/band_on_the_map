ARCHIVE: VIVIEN EVERS ‘STILL LIVES’ – MEMORIAL EXHIBITION – WEEK ONLY (TUESDAY 16TH – SUNDAY 21ST JUNE)

Vivien graduated The Glasgow School of Art in 1964. Teaching art for many years, she continued her passion for creativity into her 80’s. Living in Faversham for the last 50 years, she was a member of the Challock Art Group and exhibited here at Creek Creative, and at the Pilgrims Way Art Show in Lenham. This exhibition served as a reminder of her unique talent, providing an opportunity for family, friends and visitors to enjoy some of her lovely paintings again in a memorial week special. (Elizabeth Vivien Louise Evers, 18th February 1944 – October 3rd 2025). More photos can be found in our Facebook Page Album ‘Still Lives’: Vivien Evers Memorial Exhibition).

ARCHIVE: ‘BIOPHILIC ART’ CONNECTING WITH NATURE – CAROL DALE, KIM O’NEIL & PENNY MURCHISON (TUESDAY 15TH – SUNDAY 27TH APRIL 2025)

Artists Carol Dale, Kim O’Neil & Penny Murchison presented ‘BIOPHILIC ART’ Connecting with Nature, as the Enviro-Painters art collective (Tue 15th to Sun 27th April) and it was a highly popular show which also pleasingly swelled the ranks of the collective. The Enviro-Painters use found objects sympathetically as tools, allowing nature to make marks within their process, while encompassing a broad, holistic eco-friendly approach to the generation of each artwork.
Photos of this exhibition can be found in our Facebook Page Album ‘BIOPHILIC ART’ Connecting with Nature – Dale, O’Neil & Murchison.

What we offer

    About Creek Creative

    We are a Community Interest Company supporting a wide range of artists and creatives by providing an affordable, friendly and productive environment in which to create and showcase work. As a not-for-profit organisation, any surplus income not spent on overheads goes towards improving the facilities.

    Room Hire

    Unusual Art Galleries (choice of Upper & Lower), Print Room and Teaching / Project Spaces for hire, with easy access, all conveniently close to Faversham town centre, where Abbey Street meets Court Street near the creek.

    Gallery Kitchen Café

    Our Café offers delicious light lunches, fabulous cakes and quality coffee or teas every day except Monday. All food is cooked by our chef Lorraine, with many gluten-free and dairy-free options available. Larger groups please book ahead: special occasions catered for in style.

    Art Studios

    A flexible arts facility arranged over 3 floors of a former Victorian brewery, we are home to 45 artists and designer-makers in 32 separate studios and workshops and 7 deskspaces in our co-working zone.

    Artisan Shop

    A tempting range of affordable, hand-crafted local designer-makers’ work, including a unique selection of high quality silver jewellery, ceramics, prints, paintings and unusual gifts.
