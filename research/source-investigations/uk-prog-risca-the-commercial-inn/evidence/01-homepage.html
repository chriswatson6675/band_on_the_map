About Our Pub

Rooted at the heart of the historic and vibrant community around her, The Commercial stands proud as one of the last truly great locals in Wales.

We, as a family, have been custodians of this multi-award-winning independent free-house for the past 25 years.

Steadfast in our belief that a proper pub is the best place in the world to relax and have the most memorable time, we pride ourselves on being the home of Great Drinks, Great Food, and Great Service.

Great Service

At the Commercial we prioritise your comfort, whilst retaining an atmosphere true to our proud origins. Day or night, you can choose to relax either: inside, in our open-plan lounge and snug; or outside, on our fully lit, heated patio with bi-folding windows and brand new retractable built-in canopies.

From the heartland of Welsh song, we love our music. All day, every day our state-of-the-art jukebox rotates the best tunes for you to chill out to. From 6pm till close, every Thursday through Sunday, we are a party pub, playing the greatest hits all night long.

On matchdays, we are your home of Welsh rugby and football, offering an unmatched viewing experience. From build-up anthems to post-match drinks, we show every moment of all the big games, with full surround sound across our 6 HD Screens, featuring: SKY SPORTS; TNT SPORTS; AMAZON PRIME; ESPN; and EUROSPORT.

Want to play as well? With a long history of thriving trophy-winning bar sports teams, we emphasise maintaining our top-class pool table and darts board facilities, available at your leisure.

When it comes to our Great Drinks and Great Food, make sure to ask our expertly trained, super friendly team!

Great Drinks

We are dedicated to giving you the best drinks selection in South Wales at the most affordable prices.

Retaining our historic wooden-panelled bar (among the longest of any independent pub in the UK), we serve seventeen draught beers, lagers, and ciders, from as many as fifteen different countries. Plus, many more options in bottles!

At The Commercial, we take our beers very seriously. Cask Marque accredited since 2002, we have continually featured in the CAMRA Good Beer Guide. For 2025, The Commercial has, once again, received the highest possible – 5-star rating – for beer and cellar hygiene.

As passionate proprietors of the finest craft and cask beers, we hand-select the best collection from independent brewers on our dedicated kegs and rotating pumps.

In the same vein, we source a unique selection of over 60 of the finest gins, spirits, and wines for you to enjoy.

Great Food

Our Kitchen is passionately committed to creating the finest pub meals, which can be enjoyed at the most affordable prices.

The Commercial’s Head Chef, Neil Tate, is a longtime Risca-resident who, having trained at the original Junction 28, has over two decades of experience working in and running some of the best food establishments in South Wales and beyond.

Using the best local produce, we keep our menus fresh, changing them every other week. In doing so, we seek to offer something for everyone to savour.

Our always-available Commercial Classics can be found on our chalkboards, including include the famous Commercial All-Day Breakfast, Gammon, Egg & Chips, and Mixed Grill, as well as our local ale battered Fish or Sampi and Chips. Plus, much more!

Our Sunday Lunch is not to be missed. We change our three-course menu weekly to offer locally sourced beef, chicken, pork, lamb, and fish. Another highlight is the sensational homemade desserts, which include bread and butter pudding, pineapple upside down cake, sticky toffee pudding, treacle sponge, and rhubarb crumble… to name but a few!

Due to demand, booking is advised, though some tables are always left to welcome walk-ins.

Plus, keep an eye out for our regular weekend BBQ dates!

One of our many regulars? The Commercial Loyalty card scheme allows you to come back and collect for a free meal.

Our takeaway menu is available upon request.

Devoted to giving great food to all, our meals are optionally gluten free, with many yummy vegan options.
