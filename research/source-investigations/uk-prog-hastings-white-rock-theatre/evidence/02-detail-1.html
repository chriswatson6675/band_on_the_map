Join us from Saturday 12 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 for Hastings’ favourite festive tradition.

Mirror Mirror On The Wall. Which is the fairest panto of them all?

Pantomime at the White Rock is always the highlight of the festive calendar and this year’s show promises to be bigger and better than ever as we present this year’s magical Christmas extravaganza SNOW WHITE.

Starring acclaimed actress and I’m a Celebrity…Get Me Out of Here! star Shona McGarty and X Factor winner Matt Terry.

Leading the company as Snow White is acclaimed actress and singer Shona McGarty, best known for her iconic role as Whitney Dean in BBC’s EastEnders. Away from Albert Square, Shona released her debut solo single and performed alongside Ronan Keating on BBC One’s New Year’s Eve special last year. Her powerful vocals and natural stage presence promise to bring magic, heart and sparkle to Hastings this Christmas.

Taking centre stage as Prince Henry is Matt Terry. Since winning The X Factor, Matt has enjoyed chart success as a singer and songwriter as well as impressing on the stage in Rock of Ages, Madagascar and The Grinch. He will also star in The Best of Frankie Valli and The Four Seasons at the White Rock in March 2027!

Hastings favourites Ben Watson and Tim McArthur return by popular demand to delight White Rock audiences once again. Ben is back as everyone’s favourite comic sidekick, Muddles, while Tim brings his larger-than-life presence as the villainous Herman the Henchman. Ayo Sodeinde joins the company as the enchanting Fairy Fortune to guide Snow White on her journey. Musical theatre drag sensation Tuppence A’Bag stars as the wicked Queen Morgana, with Chris Casserly completing the cast as the irrepressible Dame, Nurse Nelly.

With a fabulous cast, this enchanting fairy tale will captivate audiences of all ages and is the perfect festive treat for the whole family. With spectacular scenery, dazzling costumes, amazing special effects, hilarious jokes and plenty of audience participation SNOW WHITE promises something for everyone.

So don’t delay, book today and secure your seats now!
