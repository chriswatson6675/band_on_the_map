Penrith Playhouse is the only permanent, Member run Theatre in Penrith, and thrives on the commitment of volunteers.

There are not only opportunities to act on stage, but also to help with a variety of tasks behind the scenes; including set building & painting, costumes & make-up, sound & lighting, props & stage management. 

Penrith Players continue to go from strength to strength with an undiminished commitment to putting on excellent productions in a modern, comfortable and friendly environment. We always offer a warm welcome to all new members who want to become a part of the club and would jump at the chance of getting more younger members of the community on board. There is plenty to do both on stage and behind the scenes.

The future is here —  take a part in it…
