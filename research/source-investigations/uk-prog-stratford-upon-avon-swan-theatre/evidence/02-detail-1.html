Matilda The Musical

Matilda The Musical, the multi-award winning musical from the Royal Shakespeare Company is heading back on tour around the UK and Ireland! Inspired by Roald Dahl's beloved book, Matilda The Musical is written by Dennis Kelly with music and lyrics by Tim Minchin.
Matilda is the story of an extraordinary little girl who, armed with a vivid imagination and a sharp mind, dares to take a stand and change her own destiny. Winner of over 100 international awards, Matilda continues to delight audiences in London's West End and will be touring the UK and Ireland in 2025 - 2026

Theatre Tours

Immerse yourself in the unique history of the RSC, from the 1800s to the present day.

The hidden history of the RSC is at your fingertips as we begin the journey of the company in the most-seen areas of the building, introducing you to the people and stories that have made the theatre what it is today.

On the journey, you could get a glimpse inside the iconic Royal Shakespeare Theatre, or the beautiful Swan Theatre, or the newly transformed The Other Place Studio – as well as the chance to get up close to some of our costumes, explore our hair and make-up box, and try on some items of headwear - maybe a crown or a helmet?

No day at the RSC is the same so depending on what is happening on the day of your tour, you will get to see a part of the building that isn't usually accessible to the public.

Scenic Workshop Tours

Discover how our skilled carpenters, engineers and artists build the world of a performance.
See the life cycle of a set, from the design phase to the workshops where a team of engineers and carpenters bring the drawings to life. Watch our expert propmakers create the objects you see on stage and explore our Prop Hire store, a treasure trove of objects, small and large, where we store the furniture and props used in our shows and hire them out.

Tour suitable for adults and young people aged 12.

The tour takes place at our workshops at Timothy Bridge Road, on the outskirts of Stratford-upon-Avon. Onsite parking is only available for blue badge holders. Parking and public transport is available from Stratford-upon-Avon Parkway Station (12 minutes' walk).

Unfortunately this tour isn't accessible for mobility scooters or wheelchair users. You will need good mobility to explore the building. Wear closed toe and heel footwear, with no high heels.

Ghost, Gore & Folklore Tours

Did someone say "Macbeth"? Learn about theatre superstitions and some supernatural sightings There has been a theatre on our site since 1879 and this tour will take you through some of the stories that have arisen during that 140+ years, including ghostly exits and entrances, but also the history of theatre superstition.

You will visit the Royal Shakespeare and Swan Theatres, then move through to other parts of the buildings where visitors are normally not allowed to find out more about how we achieve some of the gory or ghostly effects in our performances. This tour is fully wheelchair accessible.
