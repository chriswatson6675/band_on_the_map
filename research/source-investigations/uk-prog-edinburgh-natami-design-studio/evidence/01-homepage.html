Christmas Ceramic Workshop 20 Dec 2025 and 10 Jan 2026 (see calender)

December 2025

We’re excited to invite you to our Christmas Ceramic Workshop on Saturday 20th of December.

Join us for a cosy afternoon of creativity — we’ll be hand building a ceramic tea/coffee cup, saucer, and spoon, just in time to add a bit of handmade warmth to the winter days.

Three weeks later, January the 10th 2026, we’ll meet again to decorate and glaze your unique creations I will have bisque fired by then.

Let’s create some original and joyful memories to bring a smile every day.

Children Art classes

Art school for children ( primary school age) runs by blocks of 3 lessons on Saturdays 4-6pm. The blocks cover different topics - drawing, painting, sculpture, mix medias and others.
