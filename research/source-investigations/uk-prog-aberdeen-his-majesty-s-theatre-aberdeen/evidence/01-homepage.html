Festivals

We curate and produce an exciting series of annual festivals helping Aberdeen be a year round festival city.

From crime writing to cracking comedy, we’ve got something going on all year round to keep you engaged and entertained. Check out our festivals and look forward to their return every year!

no-logo

Eat and Drink 

Make yourself at home in one of our café bars around our venues. We have Coda Café at the Music Hall and The Terrace at His Majesty’s Theatre to keep you company through all hours of the day.

By day pop in for a caffeine fix, a lunch break re-fuel or catch-up drinks with friends. By evening, save time and be ready for the performance with drinks or a pre-show bite to eat.

no-logo

Support us 

Aberdeen Performing Arts is the registered charity (SC033733) responsible for operating His Majesty’s Theatre, the Music Hall and the Lemon Tree.

The tickets that we sell to the 400+ performances throughout the year account for most of the income that we receive to cover the costs of keeping our doors open.

However, in order to continue to grow as the cultural hub in the North East, we rely on funding from other sources: trusts, corporate partners and the kind generosity from our audience members.

Celebrating 120 years of His Majesty’s Theatre

For 120 years, His Majesty’s Theatre has set the stage for Aberdeen’s cultural offering.

So, this special anniversary year, we’re putting His Majesty’s Theatre in the spotlight and celebrating its past, present and future.

Join us for a landmark year of unforgettable performances, special events and shared moments that will shape the next 120 years of His Majesty’s Theatre.
