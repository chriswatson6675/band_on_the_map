    Membership

    Become a Full Member

    South London Theatre is entirely run by its members. As well as for those wanting to direct or appear in shows, there are opportunities to get involved backstage, learn technical skills, help staff the bar, box office or front of house, or help out with our huge costume collection.

    Learn more
    Support Us

    Join the SLT Supporter Circle

    If you or someone you know would enjoy supporting SLT and seeing our shows, but perhaps wouldn't wish to get involved on the participation side, you can join our Supporter Circle. This is ideal for anyone who visits us to see shows twice or more in a year.

    Learn more
    Donate to us

    Make a donation

    Coming to see our shows is a great way to support us, but if you’d like to do more to help us fund our wonderful theatre and activities, donations are always gratefully received. You can either donate via Just Giving, (tick Gift Aid if you can to enable us to claim an extra 25% at no extra cost to you) or by making direct payments.

    Learn more
    Hire from us

    Costume and prop hire

    South London Theatre Wardrobe has thousands of items of clothing and costumes for hire. We also hire out props from our collection, accumulated over 50 years.

    Our collection spans centuries and cultures, from Greek tunics to medieval tabards and Elizabethan doublet and hose, from Victorian dresses to Edwardian blazers, military uniforms and late 20th century fashion.
