About The Animal Guyz: Amazing Animals


Amazing Animals bring the magic of the wild to theatre stages in 2027

An action packed, ethical, animal experience like no other. Get ready for a heartwarming, laugh out loud theatrical adventure with Amazing Animals. 

This spectacular family show is brought to life by professional animal handler Craig Crowton who has featured on This Morning, Judge Rinder, Coronation Street, The Jeremy Kyle Show, and more. 

From snuggly sloths to colossal polar bears, and a towering 12 foot giraffe, life sized creatures burst onto stage in a spectacular display of music, comedy, and special effects. And here's the best part: there are no live animals, making it a safe, ethical, and utterly magical experience. Instead, creatures are brought to life with master puppetry, cutting edge animatronics, and a variety of special effects.

Packed with fascinating insights, side splitting gags and jaw dropping costumes, this fun filled show educates as well as entertains. Crocodiles, sloths, reindeer, penguins, orangutans and a spraying skunk are just a few of the amazing animals which grace the stage.

Described as the pantomime of the animal kingdom, the show is full of surprises, silliness, and ROARS of laughter! Love animals? Then you're going to go wild for this show!
