    Support Us

    Established in 2006 by Mary Hare, Arlington Arts Centre plays a pivotal role in supporting the mission and values of the largest school for deaf children in the UK. The venue provides students from Mary Hare School with learning and performance spaces throughout the school day in addition to supporting the organisation with their programme of events generating revenue.
