The Old Bus Depot/Fisher Gate Point are delighted to announce that, as part of our rosta of artists performing in The Old Bus Depot gig space for Dot2Dot Festival 2026, we will be welcoming our good friends @zaphyria.official and @kodachromeband to join the party !! KODACHROME are a rising six-piece band from Bristol, known for their explosive live shows and fearless, genre-inclusive sound. Commanding the stage with absolute authority, they treat every performance like theatre — each song a story, each set a narrative. Blending indie and progressive rock with funk, soul and metal, Kodachrome reflect the musical range of their members. They were the band chosen as UK representatives at the Imagine Music Experience (IMX) International Final 2025 in Brussel, Belgium, where they were runners-up in the whole competition and were awarded a special prize on the night. Appearing as special guests on the night of the final, ZAPHYRIA, from Amsterdam, quickly became friends with the Kodachrome members and agreed to join them to further celebrate with all of us at FGP/The Old Bus Depot and our young members of the @Circle Of Light project, strengthening UK National city & International ties through our @YouthMusicUK & @JMInetwork connections. ZAPHYRIA are described as ‘like throwing a molotov cocktail of aggressive grunge fused with introspective rap and shoegaze noise through the front window’. The 4-piece deliver confrontational lyrics through distorted guitar riffs, slapped basslines and chaotic drum patterns. Live, the frontwoman effortlessly commands the room through her raw charisma and contrasting presence, whilst the rest throw themselves headfirst into mosh pits and stage dives.

We’re incredibly proud to share that Circle of Light has received a Highly Commended recognition in the Rocksteady Award for Progressive and Inclusive Music Education at the Music & Drama Education Awards 2026. ✨🎶 Circle of Light is a youth music frunded project for young people aged 16–25, creating space to explore thoughts, feelings and emotions through music making. This recognition celebrates the creativity, honesty, courage and talenmt of the young people who take part. A huge thank you to Youth Music, RSL Awards (Rockschool London) and the Music & Drama Education Awards for supporting and recognising inclusive music education. Most of all, thank you to the young people and the dedicated team whose voices and music make Circle of Light what it is.
