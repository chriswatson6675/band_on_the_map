ARTS FOR ALL

QPAC is one of the largest independent arts centres in the UK. Our mission statement is ‘arts for all’ – every year we help thousands of people to engage in creative activities.

From participatory weekly workshops, short courses and one-day classes; to live theatre, music, and comedy; special events, outreach projects, and free-to-view exhibitions, there’s always something to inspire you at QPAC.

Find out what’s on in our ever-expanding programme, discover a bit more about us, or find out how you can get involved in QPAC’s incredible creative community.
