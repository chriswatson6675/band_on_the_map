About UEA Ticket Bookings

East Anglia’s Central Hub for Tickets

If you want gig tickets to the biggest and best shows in Norwich and beyond, you’ve come to the right place. We are proud to be the primary ticket site for our three ueasu venues The LCR, The Waterfront and Waterfront Studio. We also support and sell tickets for a wide range of venues, events and festivals across the region.
