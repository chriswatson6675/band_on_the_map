Featured Answers

1 Do you have a cloakroom?

We do not operate a cloakroom at Leicester Square Theatre, however we have partnered with Stasher who offer affordable and secure bag storage in various hotels and shops nearby. Bag storage bookings can be made HERE.

 

You are welcome to bring small bags to the theatre, however our security team may ask to search bags on arrival.

2 Do you accept cash?

No, we no longer accept cash at Leicester Square Theatre. Card and contactless payments are accepted.

3 What time do the bars open?

There are 4 bars in the venue. The lounge bar is open approximately 1 hour before the show on most nights, the foyer bar, and the 2 bars inside the auditorium open approximately 30 mins before the start time of the show. Our lounge bar often remains open after the show however has a strictly enforced capacity of 50.

4 Do you offer a membership scheme?

LST Priority is our membership scheme. LST Priority members can take advantage of advance alerts for popular shows, waiting list priority and exclusive member-only ticket offers. Membership is currently priced at £40 per year, paid annually.

5 What time does the box office open?

Our opening times vary but it’s safe to assume that the Box Office will always be open from 90 minutes before our first performance and will remain open until 30 minutes after the start time of our last performance of the day. Call to check on 020 7734 2222.

6 Can I book a ticket by phone?

Yes, you are welcome to give us a call on 020 7734 2222. If your call goes to voicemail then please leave a message and our team will get back to you as soon as they are available.

7 I have lost / deleted / not received my confirmation email and tickets?

If you are unable to locate your order confirmation or link to download your tickets, please contact boxoffice@leicestersquaretheatre.com providing the show name, date and time of the performance and the name on the booking. The box office team are able to resend confirmation emails.
