WELCOME

Fire Station Creative is a unique art gallery venue in the heart of the city of Dunfermline.  Inside the old art deco Fire Station building, our friendly and welcoming venue hosts a wide range of activities for you to enjoy.

Open Wednesday – Sunday

Fresh food everyday

Our cafe is situated in our art gallery. We serve coffee, cakes, light bites, brunch, and lunch throughout the day, with options to suit every taste and appetite. You can also enjoy cocktails and mocktails with us at our bar every Friday and Saturday.

We serve hot food from the menu until 3pm in the daytime.

See our tasty menu

Perfect For Groups

Our art gallery is perfect for catching up with friends and family for a tasty lunch or enjoying a great night out with drinks and live music at the weekend.

We also offer venue hire for private occasions and celebrations, from Birthdays to anniversaries, or social events and group meetings.

Make a booking

Space for Artists

We are home to over 30 artists on the floors above our art gallery. There are painters, printmakers, graphic designers, jewelers, photographers, ceramicists, textile designers, upholsterers, upcyclers, illustrators, and sculptors.

The studios are normally closed to the public but we do host Open Studio weekends twice a year.
