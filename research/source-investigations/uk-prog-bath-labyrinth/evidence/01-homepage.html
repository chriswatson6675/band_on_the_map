FAQs

What days do you open?

We are open Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday & Saturday.

Doors open at 22:00 each day.

We are also open every Bank Holiday Sunday from 22:00.

Times and dates may change, please check us on socials for more info

Do you have a dress code?

We know this is a difficult rule to set, which is why we try and keep it as simple as we can.
We ask that you arrive smartly dressed and stylish. So get glammed up and leave any scruffy trainers and sportswear at home! We work hard to make sure Labyrinth looks ready for the party and ask you do the same.
Remember dress to impress! No effort, No entry!
Joggers/Sweatpants are not allowed in the venue

What time is last entry?

Last entry is 1 hour before the close of the business night. (2am weekdays & 3am weekends)
To avoid any disappointment we always advise to get down early to avoid any queues!

We reserve the right of admission and also to amend entry times.

Is there a cloakroom?

Yes, our cloakroom is open during venue hours. Ask any staff member for directions.

Cloakroom charge: £3 per item (one item per ticket).

Terms of Use:

  1. The cloakroom is used at your own risk. The Civil Code relating to “custody” does not apply to agreements between Labyrinth and cloakroom users.
  2. The fixed price is £3 per item, payable in advance. We only accept coats, jackets, small bags, helmets, and umbrellas. These or similar items must not be taken into the venue, except for handbags. Items valued at £50 or more should not be left in the cloakroom. Labyrinth assumes no liability for lost or damaged items.
  3. Labyrinth reserves the right to check item contents and may refuse very valuable, large, or unusual items, as well as those containing prohibited, dangerous, or questionable materials.
  4. A receipt will be issued for each item. Items can only be reclaimed with this receipt. If you temporarily collect an item and return it later, a new ticket must be purchased (£2 per item).
  5. If you lose your receipt, Labyrinth will attempt to locate your item after closing or at the end of the event. However, without a receipt, Labyrinth is not responsible for lost items. We recommend taking a photo of your cloakroom ticket to help us assist you if it is lost.
  6. We take care to return all items safely and promptly, but we cannot guarantee all items will be returned undamaged.
  7. Labyrinth is not liable for damages unless caused by intentional or gross negligence by staff. Liability for indirect or consequential damages is excluded.
  8. Unclaimed items will be kept for 14 days. After this period, they become the property of Labyrinth and will be disposed of accordingly. Identification documents will be returned to the relevant agencies. Management’s decision is final.
LOST AN ITEM

Please contact us. Add as many details about your item including where and when you last have the item.

Items can be collected any night we are open from 21:30 until the opening of the business to the public. Alternatively at the following time:

Monday 10am – 1pm

Please contact us to arrange a time and date prior to collection without this we may not be available to retrieve your item.
After 14 days items will be discarded or given to charity.
