What we do

The Carnegie offers a wide ranging programme of quality theatre, music, performance and culture. We’re home to our award winning Music Centre where young people and adults enjoy learning the art of musical instruments, singing and steel drums. The Carnegie is home to three talented amateur theatre groups and our many participatory classes range from learning to sing with the Carnegie Singers to learning the art of Karate with E.S.K.K Martial Arts. By day the beating heart is Cafe Carnegie where you can sit, relax and have conversation over food and drink. By night you can be sat in our beautiful theatre watching a quality show or participating in something totally unique. There is truly something for everyone at the Carnegie.
