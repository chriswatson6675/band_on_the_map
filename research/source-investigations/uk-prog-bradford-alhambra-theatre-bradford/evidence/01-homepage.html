Bradford’s cultural centre since 1914

Welcome to the Alhambra Theatre. Our historic venue has been delighting audiences for over 100 years with an eclectic mix of musicals, drama, dance and one of Yorkshire's biggest pantomimes! Regarded as the North's premier receiving venue, the iconic Alhambra Theatre attracts the very best in star names and live entertainment to the city of Bradford.

This stunning venue seats 1,400 people and over the years has staged major West End shows including The Lion King, War Horse, Billy Elliot, Wicked and Mary Poppins. A popular choice for major touring companies, the Alhambra Theatre is also a member of the Dance Consortium, a group of venues who work together to bring the best in international dance to the UK.

Getting Here

The Alhambra Theatre is located in the heart of the city centre, and can be easily reached via car or public transport.

For information about accessible travel, visit our access page.

For driving directions, use the postcode BD7 1AJ. You can find live information about congestion and road closures via the Causeway website.

There are several car parks in the city centre within close walking distance. These include The Broadway Shopping Centre; Crown Court; Kirkgate Shopping Centre (daytime only); Leisure Exchange; Ocean Parking Southgate; Sharpe Street (behind the National Science and Media Museum).

The car parks in the city centre can be very busy during the daytime – please allow a little extra time to make your journey to the venue.

Disabled parking for Blue Badge holders is available on Morley Street and Great Horton Road.

The Alhambra Theatre is a short walk from the Interchange and Forster Square train station, and various bus stops.

To find out about train times, please go to West Yorkshire Metro or Trainline.

Seating and Access

Bradford Theatres and Bradford Council are committed to ensuring all customers can participate and enjoy Bradford Theatres events equally. For customers who need someone to be with them to access the service or facility at Bradford Theatres, there is an Access Scheme in operation. Membership is free.

At Bradford Theatres we aim to make our venues fully accessible, and we offer an extensive range of access facilities to ensure an easy and enjoyable experience for all our customers.

Find out more about the type of assisted performances we offer on our access page.

There are 6 wheelchair spaces at the Alhambra Theatre. The accessible entrance is via Great Horton Road. Unfortunately, due to the layout of our wheelchair accessible spaces, we are not able to accommodate scooters and other vehicles designed for outdoor use. Please let us know your requirements at the time of booking. Wheelchair accessible toilets are available.

There are sign language interpreted performances, audio described performances and captioned performances at stated shows.

Hearing enhancement
The auditorium at the Alhambra Theatre is equipped with infra-red audio system. This enables patrons to independently control the level of volume required wherever they sit in the auditorium.

Headsets are available on request.
 

Captioned performances
We offer captioned performances for some shows. Captioning is similar to television subtitling and gives deaf, deafened or hard of hearing people access to live performance. The actors' words appear on display units at each side of the stage at the same time as they are spoken or sung. A qualified captioner operates the captions as the action happens on stage so it can be read by hard of hearing attenders at the same time as the actors are saying the words on stage.
 

Sign language interpreted performances
We offer sign language interpreted performances for some shows. A signed performance is the usual full production with the addition of a Sign Language Interpreter who stands on or near the stage interpreting the show for deaf attenders.
 

Audio described performances
We offer audio description for some shows. Audio Described performances are designed to improve the experience of visually impaired and blind attenders. As well as listening to the dialogue on stage the visually impaired customer wears a headset and listens to a live description of the action on stage. The description only happens in between dialogue and supplements the voices of the performers in explaining the emotions and action of each scene. Touch Tours are available to support audio described performances – please ask for more information when booking your tickets.

Search by access performance type on our What's On page to see a list of access assisted performances that are currently available.

Assistance dogs are welcome at our venues.

The accessible entrance is to the main foyer at the Alhambra Theatre (including kiosk, box office, toilets and cloakroom) and theatre stalls level (including the bar) from Great Horton Road. There are baby-changing facilities also on this level. A limited number of booster seats are available - please ask a member of our Front of House when you arrive.

We don't recommend that you bring children under the age of two to family shows. "Babes in Arms" tickets may be available at some shows but cannot be booked online. Please call the Box Office on 01274 432000 for further information/to book if available.

Our printed brochures are available in large print, Braille and on audio CD. Programmes for some shows can be made available in large print, Braille and on audio CD but you will need to ask us for them at least two weeks before the performance you want to come to.

Get in touch with us to request printed materials via our online enquiry form or call us on 01274 432375. 

Access Scheme

Access Scheme members are entitled to a free Essential Companion ticket, and are eligible to receive access to best position seats for our Access performances, including wheelchair spaces.

Food and Drink

The Alhambra Theatre offers a wide selection of drinks to enjoy before a performance or at the interval. On arrival at the theatre, you can pre-order your drinks for the interval and avoid having to queue.

The Pit Bar

Our largest bar at the Alhambra Theatre, located on the first floor level. You can choose from a range of hot and cold drink options, along with a range of bar snacks.

Dress Circle Café

For something quick and light before the show, join us at Circle Café, located on the Dress Circle level. You can choose from a range of hot and cold food and drink options.

Dress Circle Bar

Fancy a quick drink before the show? Join us at Dress Circle bar, located on the Dress Circle level. You can choose from a range of alcoholic and soft drink options.

Kiosk

Get all your sweet treats to enjoy, located in the foyer at ground level through the main entrance. You can choose from a range of snacks and drink options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the most commonly asked questions from our Alhambra Theatre customers.

Bradford Theatres’ gift vouchers can be spent on show tickets across all of our venues: the Alhambra Theatre, St George’s Hall, The Studio or King’s Hall at Ilkley. Bradford Theatres’ gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase or the date it is sent to the recipient as a gift.

National Theatre Tokens can also be accepted as payment over the phone or in person, or online through converting them into Bradford Theatres' gift vouchers in the purchase path.

Concessions are available on some shows for children aged 16 and under, full time students, over 60s, disabled people, Bradford Leisure Card holders, Script Yorkshire, and Equity members. Only one concession/discount per ticket subject to availability. Proof of eligibility must be provided. See the show page for more information. 

Some shows have an age guidance or recommendation applied to them. Babes in Arms tickets may be available at some shows for a cost of £2.50, for babes up to 18 months.

Please call the Box Office on 01274 432000 for further information or to book if available - these tickets cannot be booked online.

Please note that, if available, Babes in Arms cannot be seated in the front rows of our Stalls, Dress Circle, or Upper Circle areas, or Boxes, for safety reasons.

Groups and schools’ group bookings get generous discounts on tickets at most shows at the Alhambra Theatre and selected shows at our other venues. Priority notice and booking periods are given, where possible, about forthcoming shows. You can pre-order ice creams, drinks, and programmes for your group. There is also an extended reservation period on your booking before payment is required to help with your planning.

Yes, our ticket refund protection, provided by Secure My Booking, allows you to recover 100 percent of the paid ticket price (including booking fees and postage), for just £1.95 per ticket. With refund protection, you can apply for a refund in the event of accident, injury, or illness including COVID19, severe weather preventing travel to the venue, travel delays due to breakdown or public transport breakdowns and strikes, plus much more. This is available for online bookings only, at the same time as buying your tickets. You can find out more about this here: www.securemybooking.com/terms/uk.

The doors to the auditorium open 30 minutes before the show starts.

A lot of shows have merchandise available, which you can purchase at various kiosks in the foyer. House programmes are available for each show, and some shows sell souvenir programmes.

Our Sister Venues

St George's Hall

One of the UK’s oldest concert halls, the Victorian St George’s Hall hosts the best in live music and comedy, variety, children's shows and is home to one of the longest running classical music seasons in the UK.

The Studio

The Studio is a flexible small theatre and rehearsal space adjacent to the Alhambra Theatre. This intimate setting is popular with both established and up-and-coming comedians.

King's Hall & Winter Garden

Located in the heart of the picturesque town of Ilkley, this venue hosts a wide range of theatre events including family shows, music concerts and talks.
