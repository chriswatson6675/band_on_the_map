    Update From Our Panto Team 2026

    After a year away, we are hoping to return for Christmas 2026

     

    Although some health challenges and heavier workloads are still present; we are hoping to continue our re-charge while resuming our weekly meet-ups from May onwards to devise and put together our pantomime.

    ​

    As such we are looking for new members to join our team. If you are interested in joining us please send an email to Andy at info@themarttheatre.org.uk
