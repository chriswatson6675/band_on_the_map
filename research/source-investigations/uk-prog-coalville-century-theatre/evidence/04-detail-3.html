    Grab your bucket hat and get ready to party like it’s 1999! Retro Feva 90’s brings the golden era of the 1990s roaring back to life in a high-energy, feel-good celebration of the decade that gave us the best pop, R&B, hip hop and dance-floor anthems ever made.
    Produced by Bexstar Entertainment Productions and supported by Arts Council England, this dazzling cabaret-style spectacular features a cast of live singers, a host, professional dancers and a live DJ, all backed by neon lights, big visuals and non-stop nostalgia. Over two hours of pure throwback joy, you’ll sing along and dance along to the hits of Take That, Spice Girls, Robbie Williams, Steps, Britney Spears and Oasis, through to TLC, En Vogue, Will Smith, Faithless, SNAP and Fat Boy Slim – and many, many more.
    Whether you lived through the 90s or just wish you had, it’s an unmissable night of quality, high-energy entertainment for anyone who loves to let the good times roll. Expect big tunes, big moves and an even bigger feel-good factor.
    Perfect for friends, couples, works nights out, birthdays and hen or stag parties – gather your crew and get ready for the ultimate 90’s throwback.
    Group bookings of 10 or more people will recieve a 10% discount
