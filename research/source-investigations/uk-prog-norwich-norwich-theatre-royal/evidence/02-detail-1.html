Access performances at Norwich Theatre

We offer audio described, captioned , British Sign Language (BSL) and relaxed performances along with the occasional touch tours to enhance your experience across Norwich Theatre.

For any access related enquiries please contact visitor services on 01603 598 500 or visitorservices@norwichtheatre.org

Touch tours that are currently available are listed below. Please note that any details of future touch tours will be released closer to performance dates.

MyTheatre Accessibility Scheme

We are delighted to introduce MyTheatre’s Accessibility scheme, allowing audiences to book access performances online. This scheme is free to join with the aim of making your visit to the theatre and smooth and comfortable as possible. Find out more and apply here.
