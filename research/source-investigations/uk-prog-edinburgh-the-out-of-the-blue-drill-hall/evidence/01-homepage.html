About us

Our vision for Edinburgh is a place where everyone can freely express their creativity, with access to the spaces and resources they need, regardless of who they are or how they choose to create. With our Ventures across Edinburgh as a backdrop to our mission to shape Edinburgh's artistic landscape. We are Out of the Blue. This is our story.

Spaces to eat, drink, work and play
