FAQs

When are you open?

Friday: 10PM – 3AM

Saturday: 10PM – 3AM

We are also open every Bank Holiday Sunday. 

Can I hire the venue?

Yes! We have a 5 room venue with all the facilities for your next event or function. Please contact our team and we’ll be in touch.

Is there a dress code?

A Smart-Casual dress code is strictly enforced at Home & Botanic for admission into the venue. Please avoid prohibited items including tracksuits, athletic apparel and baseball caps. Please also avoid clothing that is work related or offensive. Trainers may be permitted at the manager’s discretion and would only be considered if they are clean and tidy and contain no air bubbles.

Saturday nights are super strict so please dress to the nines. Fancy dress is negotiable, please contact us in advance with your specifics. Management reserve all rights of admission please note we do not refund tickets/booths if you are turned away for dress code or behaviour.

Any questions, please contact us by using the form on the contact us page.

Can I book a table?

You can book a table for each of our events on the event page. For further enquries, please contact us through our form, or at vip@homecheltenham.com.

Accessibility

Home & Botanic is not wheelchair accessible, due to our venue being a Grade II listed building. If you have any further questions regarding accessibility, please contact our team.
