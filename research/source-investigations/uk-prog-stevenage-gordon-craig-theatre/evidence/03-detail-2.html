Snow White

Showing at The Gordon Craig Theatre…
Snow White

Mirror, mirror on the wall, the perfect panto for one and all!

Join us on a magical adventure to meet the Wicked Queen with a jealous streak, a handsome Prince who is searching for love and the fairest of all damsels. Not forgetting a magical mirror, a tempting red apple and seven magnificent Dwarfs.

Stevenage favourite AIDAN O’NEILL returns as Herbie the Huntsman along with the hilarious MARK SINEY as Dame Dolly and CARLI NORRIS as Narcissia, the wicked queen.

Get ready for another magical extravaganza, brought to you from the team behind last year’s award-nominated Dick Whittington and his Cat, with show-stopping live music, slapstick silliness and lots of audience participation!

Relaxed & BSL Signed Performance – Wednesday 16th December at 6pm
BSL Signed Performance – Tuesday 29th December at 2pm
BSL interpretation will be provided live throughout these performances. The interpreter will be positioned downstage left, which is on the right-hand side of the stage from the audience’s perspective when looking towards the stage.
