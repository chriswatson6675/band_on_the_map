    Welcome to the Britannia Panopticon

    We are a registered charity, operating in the heart of Glasgow’s City Centre. Our aim is to promote and continue the legacy of the world’s oldest surviving music hall, advance the conservation and ensure the building’s future as a viable visitor attraction, museum and working venue. The Panopticon is where Stan Laurel made his debut in 1906 and the story is one of the many tales that you can hear about when you visit us for shows and open days. We have an operating stair climber for disabled access, however please contact us beforehand so that we can prepare. There is an exhibition of ephemera, a merchandise stall with postcards, bags, posters, mugs, tee-shirts etc and of course our friendly staff and volunteers will be available to regale you with tales and the history of the world’s oldest surviving music hall. Please check our What's On page for any coming events and open days, and check out our walking tours on our Tours page.  

    The friends of the Britannia Panopticon Hall Trust (SCIO) Charity Number: SC029031

    We are open for general viewing on Fridays and Saturdays from 12pm - 4.30pm

    Cost of entry is only £2 for adults and £1 for children 

    Please also note that the music hall is an ongoing conservation project and therefore modern luxuries such as heating are currently not available, so please dress extra warmly.

    Glasgow Doors Open Festival 17th - 20th September 2026

    This year is a very special Glasgow Doors Open Festival as the theme is going out! This year the Britannia Panopticon will be open from the Thursday until the Sunday of the Festival from 12pm until 5pm with last admissions at 4.30pm. It's your chance to see the building for free! And there are a few wee extras over the weekend which we hope you will enjoy including our exhibition of ephemera, a few ad hoc acts and a dressing up box ready for your selfies.
