Private Events

Why book with us

At a glance

Whatever the vision for your event, we'll help you make it a reality. With four private function areas to choose from, we can help you host a business meeting, formal dining event, or something more casual.

On the food front, you can opt for formal dining or a delicious buffet. For drinks you'll be spoilt for choice too, with Fuller's being renowned for outstanding cask conditioned ales and a constantly changing range, you'll always be able to sample something new and interesting, whether it's an ale, craft beer, cider, spirit, artisan soft drink or one of our exclusive wines.

Function rooms

The Chatwin Room

Overview

Overview

The Chatwin Room, named after the architect of the building, is located on the first floor. Complete with it's own exclusive bar, this space is perfect for a work or family party, and is equally popular as a corporate meeting space or small conferences. Also used as our Theatre's Bar for pre/post-drinks and intervals when the space is not being used for functions.

Due to the location of this space, we are unable to offer formal dining in this room, however our buffet is always a popular choice for bookings in this room.

Hire rates and minimum spends options are available upon request.
Please contact our Events Team on 0121 200 1892, or drop us an email to oldjointstock.events@fullers.co.uk

The Mezzanine Balcony

Overview

Overview

Our stunning Mezzanine Balcony is the perfect setting for a sociable gathering. Set above our beautiful bar, this space puts you right in the heart of our bustling venue.

Perfect for dining, or a more relaxed gathering for drinks, we are able to offer formal dining or our popular buffet in this space.

Hire rates and minimum spend options are available upon request.

Please contact our Events Team on 0121 200 1892, or drop us an email to oldjointstock.events@fullers.co.uk

The Manager's Office

Overview

Overview

Formerly the Bank Manager's office in the days when the Old Joint Stock was actually the Joint Stock Exchange (See what we did there?). This intimate space is the perfect private room for small parties that want to celebrate with drinks, and either a buffet or formal dining, or even meetings with access to a screen. 

With a large fireplace and original features, this space transports you back to the classic era the venue was built in.
Located on the ground floor, this space gives easy access to the main bar whilst being able to have closed doors, to keep your function separate from the busy bar area.

Hire rates and minimum spends options are available upon request.
Please contact our Events Team on 0121 200 1892, or drop us an email to oldjointstock.events@fullers.co.uk

Old Joint Stock Theatre

Overview All Features Floorplans

Overview

Located on the second floor, our popular theatre is available for private hire on days where we do not have a performance playing. 

The entire top floor can be separated from the rest of the venue so it is perfect for keeping your function all in one place, away from the bustling main bar.
This space is well equipped with theatre lighting and sound and is great for celebrations, conferences or a meeting room with a difference, with the additional option to hire The Sett (former Theatre Bar) as a bottle bar to compliment your event.

Hire prices for the theatre spaces are based on individual requirements of each booking and subject to programming.
For a quote, please contact the Theatre Team on 0121 200 0946 or email oldjointstock.theatre@fullers.co.uk
