Outreach Programme

Blackfriars Outreach Programme. 

Over the coming month's we are going to be starting a number of Outreach events for Children and Young People here at Blackfriars Theatre. 

These events are separate to our current Theatre Academy and there is no requirement for you to be involved in that to get on board for these new activities and programmes.

For more details please contact ; Victoria.burden@blackfriarsartscentre.co.uk.
