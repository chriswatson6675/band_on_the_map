Allyson June Smith: Allyson Wonderland UK Tour

Thu, 22nd October @ 19:00 - 22:00
£12.00

Thursday 22nd October 2026

Doors 7pm – Show 8pm

 

In a world that’s imploding, an hour in the company of Allyson Smith is the only antidote you’ll ever need. 

Brilliantly witty and wonderfully warm, she’ll guide you through her own personal Wonderland, down the rabbit hole of family foibles and the travelling tales of a people pleasing, polite but vicious Canadian. So if you hate infectious joy and razor sharp jokes, definitely don’t come. Otherwise, she’ll see you there!

“Allyson is a hilarious charisma bomb!” Jen Brister

“Allyson…had crowds in every city in such fits of hysterics that I was forced to question my career choices” Katherine Ryan
