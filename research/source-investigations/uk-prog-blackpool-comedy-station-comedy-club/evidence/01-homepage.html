Welcome to Blackpool’s first ever comedy club!

Comedy Station Comedy Club Blackpool

We’ve been making the Fylde Coast laugh, since 2002, by bringing the best that the U.K. comedy circuit has to offer, to Blackpool
Find us right opposite Blackpool Tower, on Bank Hey St.(not the side where the sea is!), open all year round with shows every Friday & Saturday, plus selected Thursdays & Sundays
Over the past 22 years, we’ve hosted comedians such as Jason Manford, Eddie Izzard, Seann Walsh, Gary Delaney, Dan Nightingale, Maisie Adam, Adam Rowe and Justin Moorhouse, to name but a few, so it’s no wonder the Comedy Station Comedy Club Blackpool is the Fylde Coast’s funniest night out!
