In Brief!

We are an amateur society, run totally by volunteers, bringing live theatre to you at a reasonable price. We usually perform several plays a year along with our traditional family pantomime. Our plays are varied, offering a wide range of entertainment from comedy and thrillers through to farce. For more details about us, membership, what we do etc., please Click Here

Theatre phone number - Please do not send texts to this number or attempt to leave messages as they are not monitored, please use the 'Ticket Enquiry Line' number.

The ATM is situated about 20 mtrs along on the right, charges may be made.

TELEPHONE BOX OFFICE DETAILS

PLEASE READ THIS CAREFULLY TO AVOID ANY CONFUSION

After the success of this service during out 2025/26 season, we are continuing with the TicketSource 'Telephone Box Office' service. This should not be confused with the usual TLT General Enquiry Line enquiry number. The TicketSource 'Telephone Box Office' enables you to phone TicketSource directly to book your tickets over the phone using your credit/debit card, a service which TLT cannot offer on our General Enquiry Line number. The system is similar to booking on-line and is ideally suited to patrons who find booking via the internet difficult for whatever reason. We must stress that this TicketSource service should only be used for booking tickets as their staff will not be able to answer any general questions about the production, the theatre, facilities, parking etc., or deal with any problems. For problems or general enquiries you should still use the TLT General Enquiry Line number, email tickets@tlt.org.uk or read the Frequently Asked Questions section at the bottom of the bookings page - Click Here

TicketSource'Telephone Box Office'number is 0333 666 3366 (Bookings Only) and is only open when tickets for a partitcular production are on sale
Lines are open - Monday to Friday 9:00am to 7:00pm (excluding bank holidays) - Saturday 9:00am to 5:00pm - Closed Sunday. Lines close for bookings 2 hours before the performance start time
The TicketSource serviceuses a local rate 03 telephone number, which means that calls should be included in a bundled calls package/contract. Normal call costs range from - up to 16p per minute (landlines) or 3p to 65p per minute (mobiles) depending on your telephone service provider*
