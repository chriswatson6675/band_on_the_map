Ivandro was born on 26 July 1998 in Benguela, Angola, and moved to Portugal at the age of three, settling in Algueirão-Mem Martins, in the municipality of Sintra. He began his musical journey in 2013 by posting guitar covers online and later expanded his skills to include mixing and mastering both his own original songs and tracks by other artists.

Among his greatest hits are Essa Saia, in collaboration with rapper Bispo, Moça, Lua, Como Tu with Bárbara Bandeira, and Chakras, recorded with Julinho KSD. In 2022, he became the most-streamed Portuguese artist on Spotify in Portugal.

In 2024, after performing on some of the country’s biggest stages, he released his debut album, Trovador, featuring collaborations with renowned Portuguese artists such as António Zambujo, Marisa Liz and Slow J, as well as international artists including Brazilian singer-songwriter Vitor Kley and British rapper M Huncho.

A singer, songwriter and producer, Ivandro is a truly versatile artist, widely recognised for his ability to blend different musical styles and create a distinctive sound that resonates strongly with Portuguese audiences.

Just over a year after the release of his debut album, he returned with his second record, Amanheceu, marking a new chapter in his professional and artistic journey. The album features ten solo tracks and further showcases his artistic evolution.

2026 promises new music and even more live performances.
