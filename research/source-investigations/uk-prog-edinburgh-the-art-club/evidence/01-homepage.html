  • The Art Club

    We inspire budding artists of all ages and abilities to explore their unique abilities. From our studio in Canonmills we host adult & children's classes, paint & wine evenings, private events plus a host of regular weekly and holiday courses. Our friendly team of tutors welcome you to join us and get creative!

    Book Now

  • Classes, Activities & Events

    Adult Classes

    Mon to Fri day 10-12pm or 7-9pm

    Ten week courses for all abilities in our welcoming studio. Learn new techniques, develop existing skills and explore exciting new mediums. Enjoyable classes with lots of demonstration and guidance.

    Children's Classes

    Mon to Friday 4:30-6pm | Sat 10-12pm

    Small, friendly classes where children can experiment, gain confidence, and develop their skills with guidance that suits their age and experience. Various themes, each term covering drawing, painting and 3D projects.

    Child Holiday Weeks

    Mon to Friday 10am-4:30pm

    Full-day holiday programmes for ages 6–10 or 10-16 yrs. Projects include: graphic novels, sculpture, stop-motion animation, textile design and more. A chance to gain new skills under friendly, expert tuition and make new friends along the way.

    Adult Weeks & Workshops

    Various Dates 10am-4:30pm

    Full day weeks and workshops focussing on particular areas of art. From oil-painting to watercolour skills or botanical illustration, these workshops improve your skills, with small groups and lots of specialist guidance.

    Day, Weekend or Full Week

    Paint & Wine

    Saturdays 7-9pm

    A fun and creative evening for all to enjoy. Prosecco, popcorn and choccies served whilst you enjoy a guided experience in creating your own unique masterpiece!

    All abilities welcome.

  • The Art Club Refund Policy

    Reschedules or refunds

    Paint & Wine Events: We require a notification of cancellation of at least one week if unable to attend an event that you have booked.

    All Art Courses & Holiday Weeks: We require notification of at least one month for any cancellations.

    If you are unable to attend an art course, we make every attempt to replace your booking from our waiting list in order to refund your booking.

    The Art Club is a small private art studio. As a small business, we cannot offer refunds on late cancellations.
