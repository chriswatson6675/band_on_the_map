Event details

Sat 26 September 2026 • 12:00pm – 6:00pm

Welcome to London’s Outdoor Youth Club.

Greenwich YouthFest is a one‑day takeover made by young people, for young people.
 Music. Sports. Gaming. Street culture. Creative vibes. All in one place.

On Saturday 26 September, Woolwich turns into a full outdoor youth party! Packed with live stages, zones to explore and spaces to just hang out and be you.

Where: General Gordon Square, Woolwich Waves & Tramshed
When: Saturday 26 September
Time: 12pm–6pm
Tickets: FREE

What’s the Vibe?

Think festival meets youth club meets culture takeover.

From DJs and live performances to gaming, sports, theatre and street food, YouthFest puts young people at the centre! On stage, behind the scenes and running the show.

Pull up with your friends.
 Watch. Perform. Compete. Create. Chill.
 

Who’s It For?

  • Young people aged 11–19 in the Royal Borough of Greenwich
  • Creatives, performers, gamers, athletes, entrepreneurs
  • Anyone who wants to come and vibe, chill or try something new

This Event and all pre-event programmes are funded by the Royal Borough of Greenwich as part of Royal Greenwich Festivals

Theatre Zone @ Tramshed

Ricochet Theatre will be running two workshops over the course of the afternoon:

Introduction to Theatre Making

11 - 14-year-olds

12pm - 1:30pm

A relaxed creative space for young people to build core theatre skills including ensemble work and improv games. This is an opportunity to develop confidence and collaboratively work with a team.

Perfect for:

  • Young people looking to build confidence
  • Students interested in taking drama further within school
  • Those curious about a career in the arts

Devising Theatre

15 - 18-year-olds

2pm - 4pm

An opportunity for GCSE and A-Level students to gain vital experience creating work in response to stimuli in a professional theatre setting. This workshop will build participants’ devising toolkit and develop their confidence in creating original work within a low-stakes and supportive environment. 

Perfect for:

  • Students taking GCSE or A-Level Theatre
  • Those curious about a career in the arts
  • Those looking to pursue a career in theatre

These are free walk-in workshops

Sports Zone @ Woolwich Waves

We will be running a series of taster sports sessions throughout the afternoon. All of the sports activities are part of current programmes or memberships in Greenwich.

Full schedule and booking links coming soon!

 

Accessibility

General Gordon Square (Zone 1&2)

  • Step-free access
  • Limited parking available
  • The nearest accessible toilets are at Woolwich Tesco Extra & the Woolwich Centre Library
  • Volume: Loud music, with unexpected elements to the performances

Tramshed (Zone 3)

  • Step-free access
  • The nearest accessible toilets can be found Tramshed
  • Volume: Quieter environment 

Woolwich Waves (Zone 4)

  • Step-free access
  • The nearest accessible toilets can be found Woolwich Waves
  • Volume: Lively sports environment with potential sudden loud sounds
  • Trained professionals to lead workshops

If you require help or access information, please speak to someone in a high-vis pink vest.
