A global home for creative culture, based on the historical street, Sadler Gate, Derby.

A three-floor shop, studio, gallery, and events space.
Built for locals. Designed for the world. You don’t have to live in Derby to be part of this.
Visit in person · Join from anywhere · See what’s on 

⁠The Five Pillars - Built to Travel 

  • Shop 

    Independent artists and designers stocked in Derby. Available online for the global community. Limited editions tied to exhibitions and events. Drops announced across People of Print channels. 

  • Print Space 

    Workshops in person. Process breakdowns, guides, and behind-the-scenes content shared globally. International artists can submit files for remote exhibitions. We print, install, document, and amplify. 

  • Gallery 

    Street-facing windows in Derby. Global audience online. Exhibitions documented professionally and published to millions across the People of Print network. 

1 / of 3

  • Studio + Events 

    Talks, panels, crits, and brand activations in the building. Selected sessions livestreamed or recorded. International contributors joining remotely. The room might hold 40 people but the reach can be tens of thousands. 

  • Library + Knowledge Hub 

    Physical books, paper samples, and supplier networks in-house. Digital resources, interviews, and process insights shared online. A local resource that feeds a global platform. 

Built in Derby. Connected Everywhere. 

The address is in Derby, the audience is international. 
Whether you walk through the door or log in from the other side of the world, you’re part of the same community.
