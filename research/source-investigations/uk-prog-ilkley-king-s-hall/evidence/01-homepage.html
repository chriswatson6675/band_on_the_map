Traditional theatre and Winter Garden

Allow us to welcome you to our beautiful Edwardian theatre King’s Hall and our elegant Winter Garden, located in the heart of the picturesque town of Ilkley.

King's Hall and Winter Garden currently host a wide range of events, including a variety of professional touring productions, such as enchanting family shows, must-see music concerts, top comedy, and inspiring talks. The venue also welcomes local amateur dramatic and performing arts companies. It is also a great venue to hold weddings, parties, lifestyle fairs and community events.

King's Hall and Winter Garden is one of the key venues for the Summer Festival held in August every year and the hugely popular Ilkley Literature Festival.

Getting Here

King's Hall and Winter Garden are situated in the centre of Ilkley, adjacent to the Town Hall, Library and Visitor Information Centre on Station Road.

For information about accessible travel, visit our access page.

For driving directions put the postcode LS29 8HB into your Satellite Navigation (Sat Nav) or into a route planner on the web at RAC Route Planner or AA Route Finder.

There is a main car park and on-street parking available in Ilkley Town Centre. The nearest car park is in South Hawksworth Street. There are other pay & display car parks in central Ilkley.

On street parking is available, however restrictions apply. There are 2 disabled parking bays within 30 metres of the venue, but Bradford Theatres hold no jurisdiction over availability.

The car parks in the city centre can be very busy during the daytime – please allow a little extra time to make your journey to the venue. You can find further information on Bradford Council’s website.

King's Hall is located directly opposite Ilkley train and bus station.

To find out about train times, please go to West Yorkshire Metro or Trainline.

Belongings & Security

Your tickets will be checked when you enter the venue. We conduct bag searches before shows, for the safety and security of customers and staff. Please avoid bringing rucksacks and suitcases to the venue and please allow enough time before shows. We do not have any onsite cloakrooms.

Seating and Access

Bradford Theatres and Bradford Council are committed to ensuring all customers can participate and enjoy Bradford Theatres events equally. For customers who need someone to be with them to access the service or facility at Bradford Theatres, there is an Access Scheme in operation. Membership is free.

At Bradford Theatres we aim to make our venues fully accessible, and we offer an extensive range of access facilities to ensure an easy and enjoyable experience for all our customers.

Find out more about the type of assisted performances we offer on our access page.

There are 6 wheelchair spaces at King's Hall. Unfortunately, due to the layout of our wheelchair accessible spaces, we are not able to accommodate scooters and other vehicles designed for outdoor use. Please let us know your requirements at the time of booking. Wheelchair accessible toilets are available.

Wheelchair access is available via a ramp from the pavement outside the venue and close to the disabled parking bays. 

There is a lift from the ground floor of Winter Garden to the balcony and bar level.

Our printed brochures are available in large print, Braille and on audio CD. Programmes for some shows can be made available in large print, Braille and on audio CD but you will need to ask us for them at least two weeks before the performance you want to come to.

Get in touch with us to request printed materials via our online enquiry form or call us on 01274 432375. 

Access Scheme

Access Scheme members are entitled to a free Essential Companion ticket, and are eligible to receive access to best position seats for our Access performances, including wheelchair spaces.

Our Sister Venues

Alhambra Theatre

Often said to be the jewel in the crown of the city of Bradford, the Alhambra Theatre is regarded as the North's premier receiving venue, and has been delighting audiences for over 100 years with an exciting mix of musicals, drama and dance.

St George's Hall

One of the UK’s oldest concert halls, the Victorian St George’s Hall hosts the best in live music and comedy, variety, children's shows and is home to one of the longest running classical music seasons in the UK.

The Studio

The Studio is a flexible small theatre and rehearsal space adjacent to the Alhambra Theatre. This intimate setting is popular with both established and up-and-coming comedians.
