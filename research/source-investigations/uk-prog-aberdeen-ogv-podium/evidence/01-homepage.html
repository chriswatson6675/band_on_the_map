OGV Podium

The Podium is committed to providing engaging and meaningful entertainment that enhances the experience of our audiences. We programme a range of events that span across various genres and connect multiple communities with live entertainment. 

We have two spaces, The Podium, our main events room with a standing capacity for an audience of 450, and our second, The Taproom, our craft beer and cocktail bar, with a standing capacity for 200.
