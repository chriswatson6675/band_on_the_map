We’re open! Call in and see us!

Welcome to our Community pub! We have a great range of hand pulled ales with new beers coming every week. We always keep a brown bitter on for lovers of a traditional pint along with a brew from our friends at Big Trip. We don’t do meals but if you get the munchies we have the usual snacks and pork pies.
