Welcome to the Gallery at Hastings Arts Forum

We are proud to show the work of our members and invited artists here, in a wide ranging programme of fortnightly exhibitions.

Come and visit our Gallery on the seafront in St.Leonards on Sea.

  • Gathered Earth

Become a member

Become a member and join this community of both professional and aspiring artists, alongside art lovers, who all value this unique organisation.

We are an independent charitable organisation, and rely on the generosity of our members, volunteers and Trustees. We depend on income from membership, gallery hire and modest commissions to operate this not for profit gallery
