Welcome to Murrayfield Ice Arena

Edinburgh’s home of ice sports and family fun since 1957.

Step onto the ice and experience one of Scotland’s most iconic rinks. From public skating sessions and ice discos to figure skating, hockey, and lessons for all ages, Murrayfield Ice Arena offers something for everyone. Whether you’re here to learn, play, or just have fun, our friendly team and lively atmosphere make every visit unforgettable.
