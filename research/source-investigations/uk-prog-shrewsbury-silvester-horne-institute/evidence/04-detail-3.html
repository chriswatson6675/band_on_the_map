Silvester Horne Institute

For Live Music, Film and Drama

The SHI

The Silvester Horne Institute is the major venue in the town of Church Stretton.

In the Autumn and Winter months it hosts the Night Out at the Horne music and entertainment events. Equally frequently, Flicks In the Sticks provides the latest cinema entertainment, and Stretton Players give us live drama on stage.

The venue is also available for private hire. If you are an organiser of a public entertainment event for the community we can consider adding your event to this website.

Keep watching this space or subscribe to our newsletter for the latest information.
