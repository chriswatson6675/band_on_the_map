Eagles' Landing - Community Event Center

From luncheons and squadron parties to receptions and banquets, Eagles’ Landing – Community Event Center on RAF Lakenheath has a space to meet your event needs. Whether you’re hosting an official function or a private event, our Battle of Britain Room, East Anglia Room, and Ballroom are available for bookings Monday through Saturday.

Official or Private?

Before picking a space, you have the important task of determining what type of event you are holding. There are two types of functions: official (Tier 1) and private (Tier 2). When held during normal operating hours, Tier 1 events do not include set-up, tear-down, or clean-up, and are not subject to facility usage fees; Tier 1 events outside of normal operating hours and all Tier 2 events are subject to facility usage fees. 

Normal operating hours are Monday – Friday • 8am – 5pm (excludes federal holidays, bank holidays, and goal/family days)

All out-of-hour bookings are for a minimum of three hours.

The Battle of Britain Room

The Battle of Britain Room is 900 sq feet of beautiful event space. With historical pieces and artwork on the walls, you’ll be in the right space for putting your best foot forward for your next function.

This room’s capacity is dependent on how you need the room set up:

  • Theater | 48 persons
  • Conference | 20 persons
  • Reception | 40 persons

Fees

Before proceeding, please note that fees are applicable to Private Functions (Tier 2) and any out-of-hour Official Functions (Tier 1).

Tier 1 Events

  • Weekday booking: Monday – Friday • 8am – 5pm | No fee | No deposit
  • Out-of-hour booking | $20/hour | 50% deposit

Tier 2 Events

  • Weekday booking: Monday – Friday • 8am – 5pm | $20/hour | 50% deposit
  • Out-of-hour booking | $25/hour | 50% deposit

No minimum booking is required during normal operating hours, however, evenings and weekends have a minimum-hour booking of three hours.

Steps for submitting your inquiry

Follow these steps to reserve this space:

  1. Contact Eagles’ Landing – Community Event Center to get a Facility Usage Request Form. Call 01638.52.5113, email 48fss.events.community@us.af.mil, or click here to download.
  2. Send the completed form to 48fss.events.community@us.af.mil
  3. Our staff will contact you regarding availability for your event, notify you of any applicable fees, and inquire about additional preferences.
  4. Pay your deposit.
  5. The facility is reserved for your event!
  6. Contact lakenheathcatering@gmail.com for Liberty Club Catering, or check out the ‘FOOD’ tab above for alternate catering options.
