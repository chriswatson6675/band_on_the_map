Donate

Did you know, Z-arts is a registered charity? Donations big or small truly make our world go round! We’re really proud of the work we do and with your help, we can continue to support young people in Greater Manchester and beyond to use creativity to maximise their potential. Thank you!

Interactive play & family escape rooms

Adventures in Wonderland

An interactive play experience for ages 1-8. Fall down the rabbit hole and step into Lewis Carroll’s classic world. Families can imagine, explore, and make unforgettable memories together.

Family-friendly escape room – The Library of Enchantment

Family-friendly escape room for ages 8+. Full of seafaring adventures, time travelling tricks and a pesky old bookworm who’s always up to meddlesome mischief!

Family-friendly escape room – Mission: Mad Hatter

Family-friendly escape room for ages 9+. Part family escape room, part scavenger hunt, part digital adventure – all set within Z-arts’ magical Adventures in Wonderland space. Created in collaboration with Supermassive.
