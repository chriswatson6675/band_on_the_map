At the Heart of Leeds’ Cultural Story.

Leeds Heritage Theatres is Leeds Grand Theatre, City Varieties Music Hall and Hyde Park Picture House. Together, we welcome more than 450,000 visitors every year and play a key role in the UK’s cultural landscape.

As a Leeds-based charity, we support touring productions and create opportunities for artists, communities and audiences to connect through shared cultural experiences. We help develop local talent through learning and engagement programmes, working with more than 18,000 children and young people each year through performance, film, festivals, and creative and technical skills training.

Our work supports creativity, education, wellbeing, economic growth and civic pride, helping to strengthen Leeds’ cultural sector and benefit communities locally and beyond.
