Cinema: Hocus Pocus

29th October 2026
Y Tabernacl Auditorium

Enter a world of magical mischief and Halloween fun with Hocus Pocus.

When three mischievous witch sisters are accidentally brought back to life on Halloween night, it’s up to a group of unlikely young heroes—and a very special black cat—to stop them before they become immortal. Packed with spellbinding adventures, unforgettable characters, and plenty of laughs, this family favourite has become a Halloween tradition for generations.

Full of charm, humour, and a touch of spooky magic, Hocus Pocus is the perfect blend of fantasy and comedy, making it an enchanting big-screen experience for audiences of all ages.

Certificate: PG
Runtime: 96 minutes

Please note: To help make our cinema more accessible, this film may be screened with subtitles if requested prior to screening day.
