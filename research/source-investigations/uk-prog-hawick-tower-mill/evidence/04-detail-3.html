Heart of Hawick: Cinema, Events & Venue Hire in Hawick

Formerly a historic spinning mill featuring a 14-foot Victorian waterwheel, Heart of Hawick has been transformed into one of the leading entertainment venues in Hawick.

Films, Theatre & Live Broadcasts

Our 108-seat cinema in Hawick screens a wide range of content, including:

  • Popular new releases and family films
  • Foreign language and arthouse cinema
  • Live broadcasts from the National Theatre, Royal Shakespeare Company and Royal Opera House.

Events & What’s On in Hawick

We host a diverse programme of events in Hawick throughout the year.

Explore upcoming shows and screenings on our Heart of Hawick what’s on page.

Café, Workspaces & Venue Hire

Heart of Hawick also offers:

  • A welcoming on-site café
  • Flexible workspace rental in Hawick
  • Professional meeting rooms and conference facilities

Ticket Exchanges & Refunds

Please check the details of your booking confirmation as it is your responsibility to ensure you have booked for the right event, time and date. We are unable to change bookings made under mistaken circumstances.

No refunds or exchanges are available, except when a film, broadcast or live event is cancelled or the date is changed. Exchanges for films or broadcasts are limited to another showing of the same title.

Where the date of a live event is changed, tickets will automatically be transferred to the new date unless a refund is requested. We cannot exchange for a different event within the same purchase.

If you are unable to make the date of the film or event, we recommend you firstly try to sell the ticket(s) privately, as we can only offer a resale if the event has sold out in advance.  Resales may incur an administration charge.

For events purchased through a third party (i.e. if you purchased from a ticket agency with additional fees) where the event has cancelled, the full face-value of your ticket will be refunded, but booking fees, transaction fee and postage cost (if applicable) may be non-refundable.  You should contact point of purchase in the first instance if this happens.
