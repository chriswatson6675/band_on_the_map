Celebrating 100 years of entertainment...

This year, the Embassy Theatre, right here in Skegness celebrates the wonderful milestone of one hundred years as the area’s premier live entertainment venue. We’ll be celebrating throughout the year and on 14th February we told everyone all about our plans and of course, how you can get involved.

Join us for a coffee in our cafe, comment on social media or send us an email – we want to hear your stories, share your memories and also, we obviously want to entertain you! This is what we do.

Pop in and treat yourselves to some centenary souvenirs – only available in the venue. Click to visit our souvenirs page to learn more. You can also collect your souvenir brochure from the theatre too…

As the year progresses, you’ll be able to help us celebrate. If you’re looking to find out more, stay tuned to our website or of course, follow us on social media. This is going to be one long party…

Centenary souvenirs...

We’re celebrating our one hundredth centenary season and throughout the summer and beyond, you can buy our exclusive centenary merchandise. Our range of souvenirs are perfectly priced to suit every budget and each of them are available from every bar in the venue as well as directly from the Box Office. Just pop in when we’re open and also, buy your chosen souvenirs from our bars at any live performance! Mugs, pens or postcards – and plenty more too…

Visit our souvenirs page to browse the range of centenary season goodies!

Finding the venue...

Our postcode for satellite navigation users is PE25 2UG.

There is a large car park at the rear of the theatre – access is via Tower Esplanade. Access to the Festival Car Park, often used by theatre patrons has changed. For more information about how to get to our venue including how to access additional car parks, please click here.

At peak times, please allow plenty of time for your journey as parking will be limited when there are large capacity audiences, school holidays and bank holiday weekends. Charges may apply as these are car parks managed by East Lindsey District Council.

Your Skegness pantomime in 2026...

The fine folk of Sherwood Forest are terrorised by the mounting taxes imposed by the wicked Sheriff of Nottingham, who’ll stop at nothing to seize the throne of England and claim the fair Maid Marian as his bride. Only the outlaw Robyn Hood and a band of Merry Men can save the day. With the help of the hapless Silly Willy and his not so glamorous mother Nanny Fanny Flapjack, can Sherwood be saved from the Sheriff’s clutches or will they be left all aquiver?

Find out in this “merry” pantomime adventure! Robyn Hood – your Skegness pantomime, that always hits the target…

Venue hospitality...

Have you ever considered turning your show tickets into the full Embassy Experience?

Our venue hospitality packages now include our super seats! You can enjoy the show from the comfiest seat in the house however, if that’s not enough, you can also enhance your visit further by booking a hospitality package in the Embassy Upstairs bar before making your way to your super seat and table – situated in the auditorium!

If you’re celebrating a special occasion, they make the perfect gift and if you need anything further, let us know – we can even arrange a cake! Our Experience Team are waiting to welcome you…
