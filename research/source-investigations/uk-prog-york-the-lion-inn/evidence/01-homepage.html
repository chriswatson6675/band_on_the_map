Welcome to Blakey

The Lion Inn on remote Blakey Ridge is a 16th Century family owned, Freehouse.

Located at the highest point of the North York Moors National Park, it stands at an elevation of 1,325 feet offering breathtaking views over the valleys of Rosedale and Farndale.

Steeped in history, open fires burn in the ancient stone fireplaces. The original low beamed ceilings and stone walls add to the friendly, timeless atmosphere of the Inn.

Real Ale

The bar is well known for its good selection of quality real ales which include Black Sheep Best Bitter, Theakston’s Best Bitter, our award winning Old Peculier, Thwaites Wainwright Bitter, Timothy Taylors Landlord, Theakstons Quencher and a range of regularly changing guest ales.

Rooms at the Inn

The Inn is fully residential with 13 bedrooms. Each room has its own individual character and is equipped with TV and tea/coffee making facilities.

All prices are per room, per night and include breakfast

Dogs are allowed in some rooms on request and secure storage is available for cycles.
