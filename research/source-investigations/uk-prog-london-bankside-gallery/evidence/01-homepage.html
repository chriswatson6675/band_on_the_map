  • Discover Free Art Exhibitions in London at Bankside Gallery

     
    Welcome to Bankside Gallery – a contemporary art gallery in London dedicated to painting and printmaking. Located on the Thames Path next to the Tate Modern, we offer a vibrant programme of free art exhibitions showcasing the work of both emerging and established artists.
     
    An unmissable destination for discerning art lovers for over 45 years, Bankside Gallery represents around 300 of the finest practitioners in contemporary water-based media and original printmaking.
     

  • SMALL BUT MIGHTY 2026

     

    Small But Mighty showcases all forms of printmaking and highlights the powerful, ambitious and impressive nature of works created on a small scale.

     

    This open submission exhibition, established by the Royal Society of Printmakers (RE) aims to to create long-term opportunities for artists and promote printmaking to a wider audience. 

     

    This year's Guest Curator is John Harle OBE, saxophonist, composer and producer.

  • Gallery hire on London’s vibrant South Bank

  • Situated on the Thames Path, directly in between the Tate Modern and Blackfriars station, Bankside Gallery attracts over 55,000 visitors a year. The Gallery is cool and spacious, and the perfect space for a wide range of solo and group shows, drinks receptions, networking events and more.

     

    Venue hire at Bankside Gallery

    We offer several ways to hire the gallery, depending on the kind of event you are planning.

     

    Exhibition Hire 

    If your main focus is showing artwork, we offer dedicated gallery hire in London for solo and group exhibitions. Our central exhibition space hire in London provides a professional setting for artists, collectives and organisations.

     

    Evening event hire 

    If you are planning a reception, drinks or networking event, our gallery is also available for evening venue hire in London. Host guests in a contemporary riverside setting after hours, with exhibitions providing a distinctive backdrop.
