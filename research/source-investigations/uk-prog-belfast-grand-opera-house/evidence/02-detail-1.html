Deli Platters

Feeling peckish? Delight your taste buds before the show with a delicious deli platter for two.

Each decadent platter comes with a premium drink option (alcoholic and non-alcoholic available) to enjoy in the stunning Upper Circle Bar.

Platters feature a delectable selection of premium meats, cheeses, fruit, bread and spread; the perfect appetiser before you indulge in an evening of drama, dance or musical magic.

The Theatre opens one hour before each performance, so there’s plenty of time for you to relax and tuck into a mouth-watering deli platter while enjoying the spectacular surroundings of the Theatre.

Prices start at £45 for two people, including your platter, a drink option, and a reserved table to enjoy. Deli platters must be pre-booked in advance of your visit to the Theatre.

  • Charcuterie Platter For Two

    Your delicious charcuterie platter includes tapenade, sunblush tomato, pesto, parma ham, prosciutto, chorizo, salami, pepperoni, sourdough, pitta bread, brie, Cashel blue cheese, emmental, grapes, olives and crackers.

  • Cheese Platter For Two

    Your delicious cheese platter includes hummus, goat cheese, brie, mozzarella, Cashel blue cheese, emmental, figs, dried apricots, grapes, bread sticks and crackers.

Please note: Platters are purchased in addition to tickets for a performance.
