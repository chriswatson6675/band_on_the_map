A Grade II* listed Variety Theatre & Concert Hall
at the heart of Morecambe

Opened as the Victoria Pavillion in 1897, the building was saved from demolition by the people of Morecambe in 1985. 

We are now run by the Morecambe Winter Gardens Preservation Trust, a volunteer-led, charitable organisation.

We’re writing a new chapter and restoring this magical place to its former glory.
