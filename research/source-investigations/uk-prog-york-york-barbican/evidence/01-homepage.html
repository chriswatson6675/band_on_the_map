About York Barbican

York Barbican is managed by Legends Global. Legends Global is the premier partner to the world's greatest live events, venues and brands, delivering a fully integrated solution of premium services. The expertise and power of its network includes over 450 venues worldwide hosting 20,000 events and entertaining 165 million guests each year.

York Barbican is the largest music and arts venue in York, hosting some of the biggest touring acts in the country such as The Specials, Suede, Manic Street Preachers, Little Mix and the UK Snooker Championships.

The venue offers a diverse programme of world-class events, including comedy, a range of live bands, sports, family shows and community events.
