Another Goodbye

A performance and conversation about love, loss and saying goodbye.

“We don’t know whether this will be the last time we’ll see each other…”

When Adie Mueller’s mum decided it was time to stop her medical treatment, Adie promised herself she would travel to Germany to visit her regularly. Then the pandemic happened.

Another Goodbye is a deeply personal yet universal exploration of what it means to lose someone we cannot imagine living without. Through intimate storytelling, movement, sound and striking visual imagery, Adie reflects on love, grief, letting go and the questions we face when confronted with death.

Gentle, honest and quietly uplifting, the performance creates a space to consider our own experiences of loss and remembrance, while opening up conversations about death and dying.

The 55-minute performance is followed by a 45-60 minute conversation with staff from Tapping House Hospice, offering audiences the opportunity to continue the discussion in a welcoming and supportive environment.

Supported using public funding by the National Lottery through Arts Council England.

Age guidance: 12+

Running time: Approximately 2 hours including post-show conversation

Content: Themes of end-of-life care, death and grief.
