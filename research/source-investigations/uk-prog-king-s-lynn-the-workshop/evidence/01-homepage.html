the workshop king's lynn

The Workshop is a creative hub located in King’s Lynn, supporting the creative journeys of participants, audiences and artists.
We are a charity operating under The Garage Trust that operates The Workshop King’s Lynn and our sister site, The Garage Norwich.

Our mission is to transform people’s lives through art, especially the lives of young people, and those who face challenging circumstances.
Our Registered Charity No. is 1098975
