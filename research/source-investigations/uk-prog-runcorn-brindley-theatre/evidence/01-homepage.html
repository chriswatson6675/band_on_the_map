IMPORTANT NOTICE: The Brindley will return to normal public daytime opening times from Monday 14 September - Monday – Friday 11am – 3pm. Thank you for your patience during the current building work. THE BRINDLEY TEAM

BRINDLEY ENHANCEMENT BUILDING WORK

The Brindley Enhancement work is still in progress and access to the Brindley is via the Terrace Café. There is new signage around the building and adjoining roads to assist visitors to the building.

See Access & Timelapse videos

You can also keep up to date via the Brindley social media pages.

To see the architects video of the new look Brindley and to find out more about all the Reconnecting Runcorn projects, visit: www.reconnectingruncorn.info.

CAFÉ

The café is open weekdays 11am – 3pm and 1 hour before performances.

PARKING

FREE parking for the Brindley is on the former Crossville car park (next to Costa), opposite the Brindley, adjacent to the Bridgewater canal. Access to the car park WA7 5TW is via the A533 Bridgewater Expressway. What3Words: rocky.complains.ends

ACCESSIBLE PARKING

There are 11 Blue Badge spaces adjacent to the theatre
