Join Spotlight School of Dance as they take a ‘Trip Around the World’ to celebrate the dance and music genres of countries across the globe. Bopping to a scene of new pop & dance hits before the ‘pretty young things’ end the show with a ‘dangerous’ tribute to the King of Pop, Michael Jackson! It’s a show not to miss and is sure to have you clapping and dancing along to a mix of music for all generations!
