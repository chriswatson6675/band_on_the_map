Remembering Jeremy Edwards

It is with great sadness that we learn of the death of Jeremy Edwards on Sunday 6th September 2026
Jeremy has been involved in the Riverside Arts Centre project for many years, and only a few days ago, was still assisting with financial matters.
We extend our sympathies to Moira and family in their loss
