Catch Every Moment of Live Sport at Punchbowl York

From thrilling football fixtures to edge-of-your-seat boxing bouts, Punchbowl York is the go-to live sport pub for every major event. We show it all — Premier League, Champions League, Six Nations, Formula 1, horse racing, boxing and more.

No matter the sport, no matter the day, we’ve got the action live on our big screens. With multiple TVs and full sound during the biggest games, there's no better place for fans to come together and catch every kick, tackle, or final lap.

Great Value Drinks, Made for Matchday's

What’s live sport without a great drink in hand? At Punchbowl York, we keep the good times coming with unbeatable drink deals all week long.

Whether it's crisp pints, refreshing coolers, or low and no-alcohol options, there’s something for everyone at fantastic prices.

Our matchday offers mean you don’t have to spend big to enjoy the big games. So, grab a round, settle in, and let the sport (and savings) begin.
