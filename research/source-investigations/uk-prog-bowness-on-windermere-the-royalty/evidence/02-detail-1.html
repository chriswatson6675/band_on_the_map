The Death Of Robin Hood

THE DEATH OF ROBIN HOOD is a darker reimagining of the classic Robin Hood tale. Set of its time, the film sees the title character grappling with his past after a life of crime and murder; a battleworn loner who finds himself gravely injured after a battle he thought would be his last. In the hands of a mysterious woman, he is offered a chance at salvation.
Director: Michael Sarnoski
Cast: Hugh Jackman, Bill Skarsgård, Jodie Comer, Murray Bartlett, Noah Jupe
Running time: 2hrs 3min
