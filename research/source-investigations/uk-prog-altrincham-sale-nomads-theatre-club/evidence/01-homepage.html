About Us

We are a local amateur theatre club & charity (Registered Charity No.1088204) based in Sale and strive to produce a positive and entertaining experience for all who watch our productions.


For many, many years, the Nomads have been the team behind the ‘Sale Panto’ performed each January at the Waterside Arts Centre. Generations of families have been coming to boo, hiss and cheer at all the colourful characters.


Our Studio Theatre, situated in central Sale, presents shows ranging from youth productions to drama to comedy each season.
