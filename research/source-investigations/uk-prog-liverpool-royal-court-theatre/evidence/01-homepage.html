Did You Know? Eat at Your Seat

Dining with a difference

Our unique seating layout means Stalls audiences can dine in the very seats they’ll watch the show from. Just choose dining when you book, arrive 1 hour 45 minutes before the performance and our chefs will serve a freshly-made meal straight to your table. Plates are cleared before curtain up so you can relax and enjoy the show!

What’s Going On? More exciting ways to engage

The People’s Players

The People's Players is open to anyone over the age of 18 who wants to learn the craft of acting and theatre. What skills are we looking for? Nothing, just your enthusiasm. It is okay if you have no previous experience. Just come along and try it out....

 Come and join us! Community Choir

Liverpool’s Royal Court invites you to take part in our Community Choir. Choir sessions are FREE to attend and open to all abilities so why not come along, have a laugh, make new friends and enjoy yourself. If you are interested in coming along, just turn up to a session....

 Come and join us! Support Us

 Come and join us! New Writing
