Hull Music Service & The Albemarle Music Centre

Hull Music Service – Lead Partner for the Humber Music hub
Hull Music Service has over 40 teachers who teach over 6000 children and young people in schools and colleges across the City.

At its home – The Albemarle Music Centre (next to Hull Truck on Ferensway) the Music Service also provides a comprehensive ranges of groups, choirs, ensembles, band and orchestras.

The Albemarle also hosts a wide range of additional workshops, events and performances.

For schools these inspirational musical experiences can tailored to complement, enrich and contribute to the wider school curriculum and/or priorities in a school’s music plan.

Through an ever increasing network of partnerships, Hull Music Service is engaging with schools and the wider community through a variety of projects, from singing to song-writing, composition to urban music as well as Live Music for Schools and large showcase events in the City’s premier venues such as the Middleton Hall, Hull Minster, Connexin Live and City Hall.

Hull Music Service is committed to supporting our most vulnerable young people, including supporting Children with Special Educational Needs & Disabilities (SEND) and Hull’s Looked After Children, by forging closer links with schools, key community organisations and specialist providers. This work will ensure that access to music opportunities in Hull is both equitable and relevant to the needs of the children and young people across the city.

Hull Music Service would like to thank all of the City’s schools, families, children, young people and partner organisations (both in the City and further afield) for their ongoing commitment and passion for music-making.

Hull Music Service is also grateful to Hull City Council for its sustained and generous support that enables so many of Hull children to have access to high quality music education.

From 2022 Hull Music Service has also been the Music Hub Grant holder for North-East Lincolnshire and in September 2024, Hull Music Service became Lead Partner for the Humber Music Hub.

The Albemarle Music Centre

The £3.5 million Albemarle Music Centre was purpose-built for Hull Music Service in 2007. It provides accommodation for the ensembles, partner workshops and tuition as well as being the base for the leadership team, administration and teaching team of Hull Music Service.

The building has a 300 seat auditorium, 100 seat large performance space, 8 further rehearsal spaces and office space.

The Albemarle Music Centre is available to hire. for both musical and corporate events further details call 01482 318800 or email music.service@hullcc.gov.uk
