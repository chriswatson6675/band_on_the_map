Online Events

Our online events are usually free but in any case you still need to obtain an electronic ticket for the event via our agents. This allows us to manage the online event securely. All events are subject to our legal and privacy terms.

Get a Ticket Online

To obtain a ticket, you need to register online with our ticketing agent Eventbrite. Note that ticketing closes at least 30 minutes before the event so please book in plenty of time.

Eventbrite

Using Eventbrite, you will need to provide your details, including name, email address*, and any potential conflicts of interest. You will need to create an Eventbrite account (or login in to your existing Eventbrite account) to access your ticket.

* CAUTION: if you are using Apple’s private email address function be careful as it may register you using that private address.

The ticket will be emailed to you, and you will be sent a number of reminder emails prior to the event – typically 2 days, 2 hours and at the start. If you don’t see them, please check your spam or junk folders.

The ticket and reminder emails will contain a button to access the online joining instructions for the event. The button is usually orange and says something like [Go to online] [View the event] [Check access] or [Join the event] – it varies according to the timing of the email.

When you click on that button your web browser should open an Eventbrite page with the joining instructions. You will need to login to the same Eventbrite account through which you obtained the ticket, in order to see the joining instructions.

Note that, especially if you use multiple Eventbrite accounts on your computer, you may need to:

  • log out of any Eventbrite accounts,
  • close and reopen your web browser,
  • log back in using the Eventbrite account you used to obtain the ticket.

We have found that sometimes the button doesn’t work, so using the View and manage your order online link or the Order # link in the ticket email can be more reliable. Or use the Eventbrite dropdown menu item for Tickets.

Follow the Joining Instructions

The joining instructions contain a link you must click on to join online in Zoom. However this link will be only added close to the start of the event, so if it is not there you will need to check back later. This is a security measure. Please do not share your ticket or joining instructions with anyone else; they are only valid for you.

Joining the Event Online

The joining instructions will include a link to join online in Zoom, which we use to run our online events. Clicking on the link will attempt to connect you to the event using the Zoom application (if you have it installed, or accept its installation) or via your web browser. If asked, provide the meeting id and password from the joining instructions.

Please ensure that video is on and audio off, and that you use the name with which you registered for a ticket, otherwise you may not be admitted.

Please join the online event on time or a few minutes before. Note that at busy times there may be a delay or you may need to try a number times before you get through.

In the event of problems joining, please check your email, our socials and our website for messages – it may be that we have had to change joining instructions due to technical issues.

We will close the event to new joiners after a few minutes (10 – 15 minutes max depending on the number of tickets). If you haven’t joined (or entered the waiting room) by then you will not be able to get in.

Waiting Room

Our online events open at the start time given. If you try to join earlier, you will be held in a waiting room. When the event starts, the Facilitators will check tickets and admit valid ticket holders. This means you may remain in the waiting room for a short while. Please do not attempt to contact us or any organisers by phone/text/email at this point as we will be busy checking people in so cannot respond.

Please make sure your name (and organisation if appropriate) is displayed correctly.

As you are admitted to the event please check your camera video is on and sound off – in a large event you will be automatically admitted with video on but audio off to avoid confusion.

Listen out for information about the start of the event.

During the Event

When the event opens you will be admitted. You may be asked to identify yourself, with live camera on, and state any affiliations or conflicts of interest. We reserve the right of admission; anyone remaining anonymous or appearing to be disguised may be removed from the event.

We ask all participants to be polite and follow normal meeting etiquette. Facilitators can remove anyone from the event if they feel they are causing a nuisance or worse.

During the event you can turn your video on or off as required using the Zoom controls. However please keep your sound off (“muted”) unless and until you are invited to speak. The Facilitators can control your sound if necessary.

If you are having problems with video or sound quality, it may be due to load on your internet connection, so turning off your video may help.

You may need to arrange or change screens and settings in Zoom to get the best “view”.

The Chat function may be useful to pass messages to the Facilitators or other participants but please don’t over do it! Facilitators may be too busy to immediately respond.

At the End

At the end of the event you can click the Leave button, or just close down Zoom.

    Streatham Hill Theatre

    110 Streatham Hill
    London
    SW2 4RD

    No visitors or mail please.

    The Friends have no access to, or control of, the building, which is currently partially in use as a slot machine lounge by Merkur Casino UK and as a church by Ruach City Church who bought the freehold.

    The original headlease held by Mecca Bingo (Top Rank) has been cancelled; and Merkur’s original lease has been replaced.

    About

    The Friends of Streatham Hill Theatre is a volunteer-run charity campaigning to save this ‘sleeping beauty’ for the benefit of the community.

    We are registered in England as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO) Number CE024993 and  Registered Charity Number 1193643.

    We are members of NCVO ncvo.org.uk
