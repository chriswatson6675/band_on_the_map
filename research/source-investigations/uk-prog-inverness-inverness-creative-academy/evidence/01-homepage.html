Inverness Creative Academy

Midmills Building

Stephens Street

Inverness

IV2 3JP

The Victorian-era former Inverness Royal Academy building was redeveloped by Wasps to establish much needed provision for creatives across the region.

more info
available spaces

Inverness Creative Academy is the Highlands’ first major creative hub. It offers working accommodation for artists and makers, creative industries, social enterprises and charities.

Inverness Creative Academy also offers flexible workplace options, including fixed desks and meeting room provision. It boasts a stunning gallery space in the former Assembly Hall, as well as a sizeable event space in the Gym Hall. Located close to the city centre, it is the perfect location for this welcome mix of art, business, creativity and events.

Inverness Creative Academy benefits from the following:

  • 24/7 secure access
  • Lift access to all floors
  • Great natural light throughout
  • Fast fibre Wi-Fi included in rent, with upgrade options also available
  • Short walk from the city centre
  • Meeting rooms and gallery space available to rent
  • Staffed reception Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm
  • Café on ground floor within the Assembly Hall

Alice Prentice, the founder of Isle of Riso, has had a studio at Inverness Creative Academy since 2021.

‘Being based at Inverness Creative Academy has given my practice stability, security and a recognised home. My studio is a practical, welcoming space where I print and work day to day, and the ability to regularly hire the workshop spaces within the building has been invaluable. It’s made it possible to host workshops, social events and collaborative activity consistently, without having to look elsewhere, and has been key to sustaining Isle of Riso over time.

Alongside this, the wider ecosystem of the building adds real value. Working alongside other tenants creates a supportive environment where informal skill-sharing happens naturally, and where creative practice feels visible and part of a shared culture. Being part of Inverness Creative Academy has made it much easier to build relationships locally and to keep creative activity rooted in the Highlands, with open studios and market weekends helping people to find us, or discover us for the first time.’
