Bar & Events

352a Mare Street London E8 1HR

By Night

Shop by day and bar by night, Paper Dress transforms from vintage boutique to live music venue and events space within a matter of minutes with a fully stocked bar selling cocktails, local craft beers and Hackney Brewery on tap.

Spread over two levels (total capacity 180) with a lovely intimate live music room upstairs, seating downstairs, a spacious backyard that's open till late and a friendly house party atmosphere running throughout.

Licensed till 3am on Saturday, 2.30am on Fridays and between 11am – midnight during the week, check events tab for details.

Past guests include: Arlo Parks, Teleman, Tom Odell, Squid, Public Service Broadcasting, Sam Fender, Luvcat, Opus Kink, Man/Woman/Chainsaw, Kit Sebastian, Flamingods, Moshi Moshi Records, Dream Wife, Home Counties, Alien Chicks, Bass Clef, Transgressive Records, DEADLETTER, Fat White Family, The Wave Pictures, Parallel Lines, London In Stereo, Prima Queen, Jim E. Brown, Juanita Stein, The New Eves, Aldous Harding, Alexis Taylor, Visions Festival, The Great Escape, WOOZE, and many more...
