Your favourite vintage shop and music venue in Hackney!



Welcome to Paper Dress Vintage, where fashion and music converge in a unique blend of style and entertainment.

As a sought-after clothing shop, we pride ourselves on hand-sourcing exquisite pieces from across the UK, spanning the Victorian era to the 1990s.

Join us in the evening for a transformed atmosphere, where our vintage shop becomes a vibrant live music venue and nightclub.

Indulge in our fully stocked bar offering craft beer and cocktails, while enjoying live music events five nights a week and club nights on weekends.

Get Tickets

Our curated selection of genuine vintage items is not only a nod to the past but a testament to our commitment to quality at reasonable prices.

Recognized by Time Out and Secret Ldn as one of the top vintage clothing shops in London, we invite you to experience the charm for yourself.

Explore our collection, meticulously chosen to embody the eclectic spirit of Hackney's fashion scene.

Discover the allure of Paper Dress Vintage, your go-to vintage clothing shop and late night hot spot in the heart of Hackney.
