CHROMATIC: CORNETTA, LARA SINCLAIR, ZO3

Saturday 26 September
23:00 - 03:00

“We are back after a long summer break and we’ve put together a lineup of iconic acts from across the country to get the dance floor back in action!” say the Chromatic ladies.

“Bari born, Aberdeen based Cornetta touches down for her Chromatic debut, a serious vinyl archivist spinning everything bassy from techno, breaks, idm and more. Joining Cornetta is Chromatic faves Flipside. If you were lucky enough to catch their last set with us you’ll know you’re in for a real treat. Proper UK dance music across the 140 realm and beyond. Chromatic residents zo3 and Lara Sinclair will warm up the dance floor with their signature grooves and Records? Join Steve Austin in room 2 providing house, disco and funk flavours.”

LINE UP:

Cornetta
FLIPSIDE.
Lara Sinclair
ZO3 

RA TICKETS

Subtle Radio · Chameleon: Liminal Bleeps 003 – 08/02/2026

Chromatic is a monthly night from Cowgate staples Lara Sinclair and ZO3, which launched in Spring ’24, championing all things UKG, grime, dubstep, bass and more.

It also features residents Mumbo Jumbo upstairs, playing house, disco, funk and soul.

Past guests:  Mia Koden, Saint Ludo, Flipside, Salo, Yemz, Clean Up Crew, Buckley, Proc Fiskal (Hyperdub), Casement and MC M.A.D. (Groove Armada).

Event type:
  • Club
Tags: bass, breaks, Chromatic, Cornetta, dubstep, grime, IDM, techno, UKG

Tickets available via link above or on the door (unless sold out).
