Our Mission

BOM (Birmingham Open Media) is an immersive arts organisation, empowering communities through innovation with immersive technology and inclusive learning programmes. We create award-winning experiences using VR, AR and games with a strong social purpose, and we're on a mission to diversify the immersive sector. We're passionate about the potential of immersive technologies to build social connections, support learning, and inspire creativity.

Learning & Inclusion

Our immersive arts learning programmes engage underserved children, young people and adults through accessible, high quality formal and informal education, helping people overcome barriers to develop successful careers in the creative industries.

Children & Young People

4 – 18 Years. Our school programmes support children who face economic barriers, those with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) and young people excluded from mainstream education, at the start of their career pathways.

Adult Learning

19+ Years. We run free Immersive Arts Bootcamps for people who want to learn the skills to develop immersive experiences. Priority is given to people from underserved backgrounds, who face barriers to entering the industry.

Venue Hire

Are you looking for a venue for an event?

Birmingham Open Media’s unique home is available for hire. A converted 1960’s Post Office in the centre of England, with excellent transport links. Situated right next to New Street Station, BOM is accessible, Neurodivergent Friendly and offers unusual spaces for all sorts of functions, including conferences, parties, meetings and workshops.

BOM is fully licenced and can accommodate a bar and catering. We also offer a lunch menu, but you are welcome to bring your own catering ,if you prefer. Teas, filter coffee and soft drinks are put out at no extra cost.

We can supply a barista to make you amazing lattes and macchiatos and  OTT hot chocolates! *

We have gender neutral and disabled toilets as well as baby changing/feeding facilities. BOM is designed to be Neurodivergent Friendly, with low lighting and quiet spaces available for those who need them. There is also a ramp in the main entrance and a lift accessing all floors and wifi in all rooms.

*Barista service not included in hire price.
