WELCOME TOThe Hole In the Wall

Situated conveniently between the popular York Art Gallery, York Museum and the historic York Minister, the Hole in the Wall is an ideal spot for a casual drink and some hearty pub food after a day of exploring or when visiting loved ones in the area. We are a traditional, red-brick venue with a welcoming atmosphere and extensive pub food menu – so be sure to come with an appetite! We are extremely proud of our fantastic team who are sure to offer a friendly welcome and make your experience as relaxed and enjoyable no matter what the occasion. Whether you’re after a quick drink with friends, or fancy getting together for some quality time in the evening, or even inviting the family round for a celebration – we’ll be sure to help you enjoy that time together. We can’t wait to see you!
