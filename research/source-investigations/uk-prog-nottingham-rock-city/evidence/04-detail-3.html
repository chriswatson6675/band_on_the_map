ROCK CITY’S GIG ARCHIVE

THIS IS ALL OF THE PAST GIGS THAT HAVE BEEN HELD HERE AT ROCK CITY. WITH A RICH HISTORY OF GIGS, THERE IS PLENTY TO RELIVE!

Here at Rock City, we have a long and storied history of hosting some of the best gigs in Nottingham. From our very first official night, which was supposed to feature Iron Maiden, to David Bowie’s iconic performance in 1997!  Our club and venue has seen it all, even the challenges we faced during COVID-19!

Whether you’re a fan of rock, indie, or any other genre, you’ll find something to love in our rich history of gigs and live performances. Our Gig Archive is a testament to the incredible talent and energy that has graced our stage over the years. From legendary acts to up-and-coming artists! Our club has played host to some of the best and most exciting music in the world.

So if you’re looking for the best clubs and nightclubs in Nottingham, look no further than Rock City. With our rich history of gigs and live performances, we are the go-to destination for music lovers and gig-goers. And with our Gig Archive, you can relive some of the most memorable moments in our club’s history, and discover new artists and genres that you may have missed before.

So what are you waiting for? Visit our Gig Archive and start exploring the rich history of gigs and live performances! Whether you’re reliving past memories or discovering new music, our Gig Archive is a must-visit for anyone interested in the best gigs in the Nottingham city centre.

Club Guide

Rock City

Looking for the best nightclubs in Nottingham? Look no further than Rock City, the best Nottingham club around! We’ve been going strong since 1980, offering music fans a fantastic venue to enjoy their favourite bands and dance the night away.

As one of the best clubs in Nottingham, we pride ourselves on offering a wide range of events to suit everyone’s taste. On Thursdays, we host Tuned, the official Trent student night, while our Friday night Get Lucky event is perfect for those who want to dance to the latest pop bangers all night long. And on Saturdays, Rebel Rebel plays the greatest songs in the world…ever! With such a wide range of club nights on offer, it’s easy to see why we’re considered one of the best nightclubs in Nottingham.

But we’re not just a club! As one of the best Nottingham clubs, we also offer a rich history of gigs featuring legendary acts like David Bowie and Oasis. Whether you’re a die-hard music fan or just looking for a fun night out, our gigs are sure to impress. Plus, with ongoing gigs throughout the year (including during the summer months), there’s always something new and exciting happening at Rock City.
