Chisenhale Art Place (C.A.P.) is an arts education charity in East London. Occupying a former factory on Chisenhale Road, it is home to three distinct initiatives: two independent arts organisations; Chisenhale Dance Space and Chisenhale Gallery; and Chisenhale Studios which comprises 40 artist studios.
