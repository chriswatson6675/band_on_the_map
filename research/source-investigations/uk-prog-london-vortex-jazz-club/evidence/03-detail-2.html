Craig Taborn / Peter Evans (2 Shows)

Wed 28 October 2026, 7.30PM

Standard £25 / Vortex Members, Students & UC Beneficiaries £23 (limited availability)

Book online

Timings:

*Please note each ticket will give you access to the time slot you book for. Please arrive for the door time.

1st Show

Doors 7:30 PM, Music 8:00 PM – 1 set of music

2nd Show

Doors 9:30 PM, Music 10:00 PM – 1 set of music

Line-up:

Craig Taborn – Piano
Peter Evans – Trumpet

About:

Duo featuring two groundbreaking pioneers from the New York avant-garde.

Craig Taborn is being hailed as a ‘visionary of the current wave’ (Downbeat) because of his intriguing and concentrated style of playing in which he explores the tones and overtones of the piano in their smallest details. Over the last decade Taborn has refined and developed his approach, attaining new high ground with his latest piano solo release “Shadow Plays”.

Peter Evans is a composer, trumpet player, improvisor and bandleader based in New York City since 2003. Evans is part of a broad, diverse scene of musical experimentation, and his work cuts across a wide range of modern musical practices and traditions. Evans is committed to the simultaneously self-determining and collaborative nature of musical improvisation as a compositional tool, and works with an ever-expanding group of musicians and composers in the creation of new music.
