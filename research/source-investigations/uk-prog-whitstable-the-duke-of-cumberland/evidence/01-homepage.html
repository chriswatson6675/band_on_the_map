Careers with Shepherd Neame

Historic pub hotel in Whitstable

Situated in the heart of the popular coastal town of Whitstable, the Duke of Cumberland is a unique place to dine, drink and stay.

Seafood Tower Experience

For two guests or more

Stacked with lobster, oysters, prawns, smoked salmon and mussels, our head-turning Seafood Tower is made for seafood enthusiasts and food lovers alike. Beautifully presented and served chilled with warm home-baked sea salt bread and plenty of sauces on the side, it’s fresh, delicious and designed to be enjoyed with good company around the table.

Each guest receives a Shepherd Neame seafood apron to wear and keep after the experience.

Seafood Broil Experience

For two guests or more

Big coastal flavours and plenty to dig into. The Seafood Broil is a hot, indulgent, shareable seafood feast, bringing together shell-on prawns, mussels and clams with smoky chorizo, sweetcorn and garlic, all served with warm home-baked bread for soaking up every last bit. Choose your favourite sauce to accompany your seafood feast and enjoy a relaxed, hands-on dining experience designed for sharing.

Each guest receives a Shepherd Neame seafood apron to wear and keep after the experience.

Coastal pub with rooms

This historic pub with rooms, which dates back to 1867, boasts a prominent position at the top of the High Street. It has recently undergone an extensive transformation, retaining its unique character while introducing a stylish look and feel. 
Enjoy a seaside break with a difference in one of its eight stunning boutique bedrooms, each with its own unique style and character.

We are family-friendly and we welcome dogs too in our bar and courtyard areas only; they are not allowed in the restaurant.

Mussel bar at the Duke

Seafood fans won’t want to miss out on a delicious new addition - our very own Midweek Mussels Menu.
Offering a mouthwatering serving of fresh steamed mussels in seven tantalising sauce varieties, including: Orchard View cider, leek and shallot cream; Spitfire Ale and bacon, and Steamed Bombay-Curried Mussels with shallots, brandy and garam masala.
As with all the seafood on offer at The Duke, the mussels will be freshly caught locally and served with freshly baked sourdough bread and crisp, skin-on fries.
Executive Chef Gareth Finney, who devised the menu, said: “The Duke has built a fantastic reputation for its seafood. We’re also really excited about the seven new sauces that we have created, aimed at offering something for all tastes, from traditional flavours to bolder combinations - with a couple even including our own award-winning beer and cider.”

The menu is available from Monday to Thursday, priced £15.95 per portion. Book now or call 01227 280617

Outside dining in Whitstable

When the weather is fine, enjoy al fresco dining and drinking in the Duke of Cumberland’s spacious courtyard garden, including an outside kitchen. Freshly prepared food is served every day, using the finest seasonal local produce to create a delicious menu of contemporary dishes and light bites. As you might expect from the pub’s coastal location, freshly caught seafood is a speciality.
