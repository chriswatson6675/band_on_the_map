Home

The very best quality theatre, live music, comedy, cabaret, film and pantomime in the heart of Saddleworth.

Check out shows with five productions by the Saddleworth Players . Look out for performances by SPY Theatre, our very own young persons’ theatre company. Enjoy four quality classical and jazz concerts presented by the Saddleworth Concerts Society . Watch an exciting programme of movies brought to you by the Saddleworth Film Society including National Theatre Live films, and Saddleworth’s very own Panto: Hansel & Gretel together with other visiting performances from near and far.
