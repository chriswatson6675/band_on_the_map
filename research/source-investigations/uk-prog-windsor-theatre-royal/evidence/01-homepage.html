Theatre Royal Windsor

Nestled beside the castle, our beautiful historic theatre is in the heart of regal Windsor. We delight and entertain audiences with a wide range of quality drama, comedy, musicals, opera and family shows throughout the year.

We’re proud to launch several new productions here. Enjoy browsing our website, you’re sure to find something that catches your eye. We’re looking forward to welcoming you through our doors.
