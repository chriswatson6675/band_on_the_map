Launch Event for the New Actors Centre

We were thrilled that over 250 people turned up to support the launch of the New Actors Centre. Dame Sheila Hancock gave a key-note speech, describing her experiences as one of the founders of the original Actors Centre. It was a great morning, full of joy and a positive mood for the future.
