Welcome to Our Music School in Bromsgrove, Worcestershire

Hey and welcome to Octave Music Tuition (Bromsgrove School of Music), a family run Music school nestled in the heart of Worcestershire with a rich history of nurturing musical talent in all ages. Our tutors provide expert guidance, and we're all about showing our students the incredible benefits of music. Whether you're just starting out or already a pro, we're here to fuel your passion for music and help you grow, every step of the way.

About Us

Hi, we are Hannah, Steph and Steve. Back in 2010, we took over Bromsgrove School of Music. We wanted to create a space where there was music for everyone and as a family we take pride in providing an inclusive environment where anyone of any age can learn and thrive.

Without a doubt music is an integral part of our lives. It has the ability to stir emotions, transport us to different worlds and connect us with others. From the soundtracks of blockbuster films and the masterpieces of classical composers, to the electrifying energy of live concerts, music is an undeniable force. And now more than ever it is being used for its power to promote wellness and healing. Our goal here is to help and inspire the next generation of musicians in wherever music takes them.
