About

Chronic Insanity is led by Nat Henderson and Joe Strickland. We have been staging work in Nottingham for the past four years, often working together to deliver thought-provoking and visually stimulating live performances. Having successfully completed four years of our 12 shows in 12 months, we are on track to hit production number 100 before our 5th birthday.

We pledge to:

  • Stage high-quality and diverse live performances
  • Stage a high volume of performances
  • Create theatre sustainably in line with the guidelines from Staging Change
  • Make all our work digitally available so people located outside of Nottingham can still experience our work
  • Document the running of the company and the creative process, via blog posts, so other new companies can learn from our successes or failures
  • Create opportunities for other theatre makers and creatives to gain experience alongside us, including writing competitions and scratch nights
