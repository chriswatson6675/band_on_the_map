Welcome to The Dusty Miller

The Dusty Miller is a fantastic traditional pub located in Longwood, Huddersfield.

Ran by Milltown Brewing Co, the pub has a huge emphasis on its great real ales. The Dusty Miller will be showcasing our own range of beers along with a rotating selection of other ales from regional and national brewers.

Whilst having recently been fully refurbished, the pub has retained its traditional feel with wood burning stoves and a traditional décor.

The facilities at the Dusty Miller include free Wi-Fi, real ales, wines, spirits, soft drinks along with tea and coffee!

We welcome families and the bar and garden areas are dog friendly.

We also host weekly events: Wednesday – Quiz Night starts at 8pm and Pork pie Friday and have a monthly walking group. For more information check out our Facebook page.

Bank holiday opening hours: 2pm – 10pm

We look forward to welcoming you to Huddersfield’s leading destination ale house.
