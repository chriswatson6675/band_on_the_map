Platform Theatre

State-of-the-art performance venue for the stars of tomorrow

From interpretive dance shows to live music performances, witness the industry's future stars start their journey to become industry professionals

Welcome to Platform Theatre

Platform Theatre is City College Norwich's state-of-the-art performance venue that trains the next generation of performers and theatre technicians by allowing them to create, reinterpret and perform well known, unique and one off shows.

With a company of student performers and technicians under the guidance of industry professional staff, Platform Theatre hosts a range of theatre productions each year from the college's dance, musical theatre, acting, music and performance courses.
