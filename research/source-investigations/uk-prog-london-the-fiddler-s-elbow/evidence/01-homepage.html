PUSHKICK

Pushkick is a high-voltage response to the noise of the modern world, a sonic byproduct of colonialism forged from the diverse heritage of its four members. Drawing from 90s-00s Rock, Alternative, and Hip-hop, the band embodies the quote of Joey Diaz saying “The Church of What’s Happening Now.” While many treat instruments as mere tools, Pushkick puts musicianship first, making every gig a unique, real-time experience. Colliding hardcore grit, funk grooves, blues soul, and sharp rap flows, Pushkick delivers a genre-bending “melody of chaos” defined by technical precision and an absolute refusal to be ignored.

Follow Pushkick

US LOT

Us Lot are a four-piece, electric cello-infused rock & punk band based in the remains of Camden. They aim to evoke wry smirks and headbanging in equal measure, with chaotic punk riffs and four-on-the-floor dance grooves.

Follow Us Lot

LAST JULY

Formed in London, Last July is a rock ‘n’ roll band known for heavy riffs, catchy melodies, and high-energy live performances. The band features frontman Johnathan Stipesevic, lead guitarist Max Connolly, bassist Kyle Matthews, and drummer Andrew Greenleaves. Last July have truly honed their craft – a milestone recently highlighted by their performance at the Henley Rewind Festival 2026. Following a successful summer of growing their fanbase, Last July is heading into Sensible Studios in Kings Cross this September to record their best material. Their setlist features standout tracks like the moving “Hopeless” and the melodic “Fly On By,” establishing them as an unforgettable live act.

THE NEUTRONZ - EXCLUSIVE LONDON GIG!
The Neutronz were formed in 2009 with the idea to play the neo tunes that introduced them to Rockabilly back in the early eighties. But after releasing three cds of mostly originals (Killer on the Loose, Knucklehead, Motel Hell), the set now compromises of a good mix of covers and originals. They have played weekenders, such as Viva, Hemsby, the Shakedown, and venues all over Europe and the UK.
The band split in 2023 but recently reformed with the new lineup of Steve Clark on vocals and lead guitar, John Maddison on drums and Jez Jordan on double bass.
They've recently released their new CD " Dead Elvis" featuring 11 self penned originals and 3 covers.

WIGSVILLE SPLIFFS-RARE LONDON GIG!
In the 1980's The Wigsville Spliffs were legendary at the iconic Klub Foot a cornerstone of the UK psychobilly and neo rockabilly scenes. Their distinctive sound earned them a place on numerous compilations like “Rockin’ It Up”, “Stomping At The Klub Foot”, and “Zorch Factor” and The James Deans of The Dole queue solidifying their status as genre pioneers.
.. Comprising Ian Aitken (guitar/vocals), Danny Mowl (drums), and Mike Lister (bass), the original trio captivated audiences with their fast-paced neo-rockabilly melodies and hard-hitting bass lines. “Al Capone” and “Highclass Power” became instant fan favorites, showcasing their raw talent and infectious energy.
In 2011, after a hiatus of 25 years, The Spliffs returned with their highly anticipated album “Fat 41”, released on Drunkabilly Records (BE). With Paul Richardson now on drums.
From their humble beginnings to their appearances at the Klub Foot, The Wigsville Spliffs remain an essential part of the psychobilly and rockabilly scene, proving that their music transcends generations and continues to inspire fans worldwide.
