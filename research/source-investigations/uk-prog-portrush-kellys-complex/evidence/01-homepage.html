Welcome To Kellys Complex, Portrush


Welcome to the official website for Kellys Complex in Portrush, showcasing everything we have to offer our visitors, guests and residents.

For over 50 years Kellys has been a fixture for entertainment, from hosting local acts to internationally acclaimed acts throughout its many years, our reputation, and willingness to move with the times has always kept us one step ahead!

The Barn

Our current event space, The Barn can host events holding upwards of 450 people and in its first 2 years has welcomed some great names in live music and dance music. We have hosted weddings, birthdays and fundraisers alongside our own calendar of events. See below for upcoming ticketed events. 

The Deerstalker Bar

Kellys Complex’s public bar, The Deerstalker has just re-opened after the addition of is extension as it used to get very cramped at the weekend. Now with added seating, 100 inch LED TVs and a revised entertainment schedule, its no wonder our regulars come back again and again!

The Library Bar

Just between The White Pheasant and Kellys Kitchen sits The Library Bar, a homely and cozy space where customers can enjoy a pint, maybe catch some sports on TV, or even a takeaway by the roaring fire.

Find out More

Kon Tiki Boat Bar

If its cocktails you like, there’s no better place to be in Northern Ireland than here. Using only the finest ingredients, the Tiki Bar team have earned a reputation for crafting some of the most creative and down right tasty cocktails on the Island. Superb service, live entertainment sometimes and also available for hire for private parties, for which it is VERY popular.

Find out More

Golflinks Hotel

Why not check out our very own 23 room hotel, in a perfect location for a range of activities on the North Coast of Ireland. Whilst hugely popular with golfers, we welcome everyone with the same hospitable smile.

Find out More

The White Pheasant

Dine in The White Pheasant and take in the comfortable and stylish surroundings, whilst enjoying some of the finest local produce available. Enjoy spacious seating, free car parking and a carefully crafted menu in Portrush's newest dining spot. 

Find out More

Kellys Kitchen

A Takeaway like no other, combining traditional Fish & Chip Shop food, with freshly made Neapolitan Pizzas ordered either by app, phone or in person. The only Takeaway in the area with a sit in area offering full bar service (The Library Bar), so it doesn’t have to be eaten in the car!

Golflinks Holiday Homes

Surrounding the Kellys Complex is Golflinks Holiday Home Park, consisting of over 600 Static Mobile Homes. This brings a community to the site, and also to parts of the complex as residents will dine, drink and dance with us, with not too far to travel when closing time comes.
