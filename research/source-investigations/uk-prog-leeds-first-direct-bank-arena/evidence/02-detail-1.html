Event Details

Scottish rock favourites Deacon Blue are heading to first direct bank arena, Leeds, on Sunday 10 October 2027 as part of their Raintown 40th Anniversary Tour.

Celebrating four decades of their iconic debut album, Raintown, the band will perform tracks from the landmark record alongside some of their biggest and best-loved hits from across their career.

With classic tracks including ‘Dignity’, ‘When Will You (Make My Telephone Ring)’ and ‘Chocolate Girl’, Deacon Blue will celebrate 40 years of Raintown live in Leeds.

Parking

Standard Parking
There are over 7,500 car parking spaces within a 15-minute walk of first direct bank arena. The arena doesn't have it's own car park however there are lots of options nearby. To receive 5% off Q Park when attending a show at first direct bank arena click here.

The nearest Car Park with EV charging is located on Albion Street click here.

None of the above car parks are operated by the arena so for questions, queries or comments we ask that you contact the companies directly.

Accessible Parking
Closest on-street disabled persons parking spaces are available at; Elmwood Close (9 spaces - 136m from doors), Tower House Street (7 spaces - 180m from doors), Jacob Street (2 spaces - 160m from doors).  Off street parking is available at: Woodhouse Lane Car Park (14 blue badge spaces on levels 1 & 7), CitiPark Merrion Centre (4 blue badge spaces on each level - 12 in total).  Along with an accessible minibus parking space (subject to availability) on Brunswick Terrace with 4hr max stay.

Please check local signs for time limit information.

Accessibility

Accessibility
The first direct bank arena is designed to be accessible for all visitors and holds a Silver Attitude is Everything accreditation.

Accessible Tickets
Wheelchair and ambulant accessible tickets are available online via AXS, with a free personal assistant ticket included if required (eligibility may be requested).

Getting Around
All entrances are accessible, with lifts and escalators serving all levels. Step-free seating areas are available, and wheelchairs can be provided on arrival (subject to availability).

Facilities
Accessible toilets are available on every level, along with a Changing Places facility, quiet space, and assistance for a range of needs including hearing support via induction loops.

Additional Support
BSL interpreters, audio description, and large print information can be arranged in advance. Assistance dogs are welcome.

Need Help?
If you have specific requirements, contact the accessibility team ahead of your visit for support and arrangements.

Getting Here

By Train & Bus
Leeds City Station and Leeds Bus/Coach Station are both around a 15-minute walk away, with regular national and regional connections. Nearby bus stops include Clay Pit Lane, Wade Lane and Woodhouse Lane.

Park & Ride
Leeds Park & Ride services stop within a 15-minute walk of the arena, offering a convenient way to travel into the city centre.

By Car
Easily accessible via the A58(M), A64, M621, M62 and M1. There’s no on-site parking, but over 7,500 spaces are available within a 15-minute walk.

Taxis & Drop Off
Taxi ranks are located outside the arena and nearby streets. Due to congestion and road closures after events, plan drop-off and pick-up points in advance.

On Foot & Bike
The arena is easy to reach on foot, with walking routes across the city centre. E-bikes and cycle hire options are also available nearby.

Prohibited Items

The following items are not permitted inside the arena: megaphones, umbrellas, narcotics, helmets, cans, bottles, professional cameras, flags, sport colours, tools, balls, flares or fireworks, inflatables, explosives and weapons.

Special Effects & Strobe Lighting

Most shows have some form of flashing lights or strobes. Signage is displayed around the concourse to advise people if pyros, lasers, or strobes will be in use. We also try to notify guests on our website in advance, but can’t guarantee this information is always 100% correct.
