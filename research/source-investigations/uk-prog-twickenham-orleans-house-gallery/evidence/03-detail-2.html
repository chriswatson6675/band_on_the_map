What’s on

Children & Families

Ready, set, play! There is lots of fun to be had at Orleans House Gallery, and many of our activities are free. Pick up a family trail from the front desk or visit one of our family art workshops. We’re looking forward to welcoming you and your family.  

The gallery has step free access and changing facilities, for more information on the accessibility of the site please visit our access page: Accessibility
