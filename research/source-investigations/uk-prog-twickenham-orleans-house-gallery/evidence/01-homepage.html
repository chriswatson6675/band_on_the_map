Weddings

The Grade I Listed Octagon Room is available for civil ceremonies for up to 60 guests. Following the ceremony guests can enjoy a drinks reception in the Octagon Room or on our lawns as pictures are taken against the stunning backdrop of the River Thames and surrounding woodlands.
