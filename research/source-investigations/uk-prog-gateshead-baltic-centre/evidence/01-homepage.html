Welcome to Baltic

Step inside Baltic on Gateshead quayside with four floors of art, two viewing decks, a café, shop, library and sensory room. 

We're open Wednesday to Sunday with exhibitions, events, guided tours and activities to experience. We've welcomed 10 million visitors to date and we look forward to seeing you soon.
