NW2 Community Group Hire

Get in touch

Got a question about hiring NW2? Click the button below to get in touch with someone from our Creative Communities team.

Contact us

NW2 is available to hire on a regular, or one off basis, and we offer a discounted hire rate to local community groups.

NW2 is a modern, purpose built community space that sits directly in front of our main theatre in Theatre Square. All of the New Wolsey Theatre’s Youth Theatres take place in NW2 and we regularly use the large participation space for our Creative Communities projects.

A few groups who have hired NW2:

  • Suffolk Refugee Support sewing group
  • Unscene Suffolk Choir
  • Cohere Arts High Spirits Drama group
  • Caribbean and African Community Health Support Forum exercise class
  • Britten-Pears Group A Choir
  • Future Female Society

If you are a community group or local organisation and are looking for a creative, modern and welcoming space in the heart of Ipswich, please contact our Creative Communities Administrator Becks Laws on rlaws@wolseytheatre.co.uk. We look forward to hearing from you.

“As BME communities who often feel marginalised, we have been made to feel equal, welcome, and heard! We work with a wide range of adults with differing, diverse, and often complex needs. The Creative Communities team have listened and responded in a way which is inclusive and enabling.”

BME Community Group leader

Useful information

NW2 has a small kitchen, foyer area and accessible toilet with adult changing facilities (Changing Places toilet), which are all included in the price of the hire.

We cannot offer free parking but there a number of car parks a short walking distance away. Please see more details about parking here

We will provide a Duty Manager to welcome you to the building, be your first point of contact and responsible for First Aid.

 

NW2 is available to hire during working hours (9am-5pm), outside of these times, there would be an additional fee for the Duty Manager. All hires are subject to availability.
