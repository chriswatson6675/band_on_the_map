Live Entertainment at Golden Palm Resort

Club Tropicana is the premier entertainment venue at Golden Palm Resort in Chapel St Leonards, near Skegness. Hosting live shows, tribute acts, comedy nights, themed weekends and family entertainment throughout the season, Club Tropicana is one of the leading live entertainment venues on the Lincolnshire coast.

From spectacular evening shows and special events to family activities with the Blue Anchor Superstars, there’s entertainment for all ages to enjoy throughout the year.
