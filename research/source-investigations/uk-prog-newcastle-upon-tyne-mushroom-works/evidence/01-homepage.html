Whether a jeweller, furniture maker, illustrator or textile artist, we are home to a  diverse group of professional artists and crafts people.

Based in the creative quarter of Newcastle upon Tyne in North East England, Mushroom Works provide affordable studio space for a whole host of creative people. 

We host regular open studio events and exhibitions giving you the chance to come and see The Studios for yourself.  

Look through our latest events and get a chance to meet with artists and designers and purchase work directly from them.
