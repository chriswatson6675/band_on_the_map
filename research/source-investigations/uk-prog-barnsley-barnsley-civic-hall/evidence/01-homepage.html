Welcome!

At Barnsley Civic, the arts are for you. Whether you’re here to create, connect, or enjoy and share new experiences, this is where you belong. We tell stories, embrace creativity, and celebrate the culture that makes our community special. Whether you’re watching a show, exploring an exhibition, or joining an event, our doors are open.

Come in — it’s your space, your culture, your Barnsley Civic.
