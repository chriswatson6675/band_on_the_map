Weekly Events
There are several weekly events that take place at The Hive, find out more about what we have coming up below
The Mere Singers
Term time Mondays 7.30-9.00.

We are a friendly, open-access community choir run by local musician and Hive artist Mary Keith and welcome all voices. We sing acapella songs from around the world and newly commissioned works for specific events. All learning is done by ear. We would particularly welcome low and male voices. Reduced rate termly fees apply or weekly PAYG.

Contact Mary Keith 07875637786 or maryskeith@ aol.com

Life Drawing
Mondays 2pm - 4pm and Tuesdays 7pm - 9pm

All levels welcome to drop in on these informal sessions. Different model weekly and occasional special events.

Contact: louisediggle@gmail.com

Please note this is not a tutored class. Please bring your own materials.

Shropshire Youth Theatre

Step into the world of drama and literary magic with SYT! We focus on producing classic plays and adapting stories for a new generation. Develop your acting skills, work on exciting devised projects, and perform at The Hive and across Shrewsbury. While our Senior and Junior groups are currently full, we’re delighted to welcome new members to our Beginner Group. Email Lisa at sytlisamorris@gmail.com or visit www.shropshireyouththeatre.co.uk to learn more.

Saturdays during term time:
Beginners 6 – 9 yrs, 9.30am -10.45am
Juniors 9 – 13 yrs, 11am – 1pm
Thursdays during term time:
Seniors 13 – 25 yrs, 7pm – 9.30pm
