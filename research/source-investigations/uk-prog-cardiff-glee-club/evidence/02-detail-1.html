      MAKING A BOOKING

      DO I HAVE TO BOOK IN ADVANCE?

      We always recommend booking in advance, as many of our shows sell out nearer to the day. You can do this online, or by calling our Box Office (opening hours can be found here).

      HOW DO I PAY / RESERVE? CAN I PAY ON THE DOOR?

      We always recommend booking in advance. You can book online or over the phone by making full payment using a credit/debit card.

      For some shows, as long as they are not sold out in advance, we may still have a limited number of spaces available on the door. These are first come, first served. Please note, tickets will be more expensive on the door.

      Paying on the door to join another party that has pre-booked:
      We will do our best to accommodate you, but we can not guarantee you’ll get a seat with your pals if you’ve left it to the last minute.

      WHERE ARE MY TICKETS?

      We are a ticketless venue, it's less hassle for you, and saves a few trees. Once you have booked you will receive an email confirmation of your booking. This is for your own records, there's no need to print it, but do read it through and make a note of the booking reference number (beginning with GL). Upon arriving at the club, we just need the name you booked under, this will gain you entry. If you are the organiser of the party, please ensure all members of the group have the booking reference number and party name to give at the door.

      You can login to the MyGlee section of the website at any time, to take view or amend any current bookings you have with us.

      You can also visit MyGlee to resend your booking confirmation if you can't find it.

      HOW MANY TICKETS CAN I BOOK?

      Glee is a great night out, whether you are a solo comedy fan, a couple on the tiles or a group of 200. The capacity is the limit! If we’ve got the seats available, they’re yours for the taking. Some tour shows have restrictions on booking group size. Please contact the Box Office with any queries. 

      CAN I ADD EXTRA TICKETS TO MY BOOKING?

      As long as we still have ticket availability for the show you require, and there are no restrictions on group size, then yes. Get in touch and we’ll take you through it, or go ahead and book those online, by logging in and adding to your original booking. As with all bookings, we recommend doing this as soon as possible to avoid any disappointment.

      I NEED TO BOOK A GROUP, DO I HAVE TO PAY FOR EVERYONE?

      Not unless you're feeling generous! We take lots of group bookings, and we want you all to be able to sit together where possible, and have things sorted before you visit.

      You can book your own ticket, and then link to another booking after you've checked out, or contact the Box Office to link your bookings. This needs to be done at least two hours before the show doors open. 

      Some events have limits on the group booking size. If your group is over the size limit, please contact the Box Office to check availability.

      SOME PEOPLE IN MY PARTY AREN’T COMING NOW. CAN WE GET THEIR TICKET MONEY CONVERTED TO DRINKS OR FOOD?

      Sorry no. If you have any drop-outs, please get in touch with the box office on 0871 472 0400 / [email protected] and we’ll see what we can do for you.

      Do you offer Concession Tickets/Discounts? What ID do I need to bring?

      Currently, the only concessions we offer are for students, the disabled, the unwaged and over 65's - only available on Weekend Comedy shows. Valid ID is required on the night. The discount only covers those who qualify.

      We do not currently offer discounts/concessions for Blue Light/Emergency Services, NHS or Military Staff.

      ACCEPTED ID/PROOFS OF ELIGIBILITY

      Below are our accepted forms of ID for concession tickets. You must bring this with you when you attend the show:

      Students - a validated ID on the UNIDAYS, Student Beans or Totum app or an official student card that has a photo and expiry date.

      Disabled - PIP/DLA letter, Blue Badge, Nimbus/Access Card, Hynt Card, 

      Unwaged - Confirmation letter of JSA, DLA, Pension Credit, Universal Credit or Income Support

      Over 65's - Drivers License or passport, Over 65's Travel card

      If you can't provide one of the above forms of ID, you will be charged the difference between the concession price and the full price ticket on the door. This is not refundable, should you find your card or ID at some point later.

      Free companion/personal assistant tickets are available for those who need assistance. Head to our Accessibility page or email [email protected].

      For Lunchtime Family Comedy Shows, concessions and child tickets are available. All under-14s must attend with an Adult (over 18).

      For Tour shows, please refer to the specific event information for your event - we cannot guarantee concession tickets will be available.

      We also have group offers for some group, birthday, student and stag/hen bookings. Please contact the Box Office for further information. Alternatively, head to our Groups page and fill out the enquiry form.

      Can we pick our seats?

      For all allocated seating shows, we write a unique seating-plan each night, so we allocate your seats on the day of the show. All tickets in the same booking will be seated together. We don't know in advance exactly where you will be sat, but if you are a large group (10+), you are likely to be seated in the middle / towards the back of the auditorium.

      If you have a seating request for an allocated seating show (for instance 'Not on the front row!'), please give us a call on 0871 472 0400 or email [email protected] so we can note it down for you. We can't 100% guarantee your request will be granted, but we will do our very best for you!

      Some tour shows including Music, Cabaret & Burlesque, Spoken Word / Talks and some Comedy Tour Shows may be unreserved seating with no tables, or standing gigs. Please check the ‘More Info’ section next to all show listings to find out.

      What's your age policy - do I need ID?

      Our venue is obliged by local licensing to enforce a 'Challenge 25' policy, which means anyone who looks under 25 may be asked for ID to gain entry. If when asked you fail to produce legally acceptable ID you may be refused entry without refund.

      Please check the individual event for the age restrictions. 

      Under 16s must carry photo ID and be accompanied by an adult with a ticket to the show at all times.

      Accepted forms of ID:

      - Passport
      - Photo driving licence (provisional licences are accepted)
      - Card with official PASS hologram

      If you cannot provide valid when requested you may be refused entry. Refunds will not be issued for incorrectly booked tickets.

      Our management and security teams regularly review procedure and protocol with all staff. As with every live event, there are lots of things that go on behind the scenes, so please be patient if things take a little longer than you’re used to. All of our venues are monitored by CCTV at all times.

      Parties found bringing in food and drink without prior arrangement, or any dangerous or illegal weapons / substances onto our premises will be denied entry without refund, and the party's details passed on to the authorities where relevant.

      Speak to us
      If at any time you see or hear anything untoward, please speak to a member of our team – we’re here to help.

      Barry's had 'One too many', why can't he come in?

      Join us, be merry, have a great time, but we are a licensed venue - which means we cannot legally permit entry, or serve alcohol, to anyone who has clearly stayed in the pub too long after work.

      Customers who arrive already intoxicated will not be permitted entry and no refund offered. Any member of your party found in possession of or found using any prohibited drugs will be asked to leave. We reserve the right to conduct bag and body searches and also to pass any relevant information to police.

      Can I use my phone? Can I take photos or recordings?

      No. We want you and everyone in the room to be able to enjoy the show, undisturbed. We've all scowled at someone in the cinema who's phone goes off at a crucial moment, it's the same for a live show.

      We require all attendees to set their phones to silent.

      We can't tell if customers are recording the show unless phones are turned off and put away, it's illegal to film a show, it is piracy.

      Every time you check your phone or go to text, the phone screen lights up like a christmas tree and so does your face! This is distracting to the acts and those around and behind you.

      This only applies during the show, you have plenty of time before, during intervals, and after the show to check & use your phone. If you need to use your phone whilst the show is on, please simply leave the auditorium to do so.
