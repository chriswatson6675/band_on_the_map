Challenges and Events

An exciting way you can support us is by taking part in a sponsored challenge or by running an event to raise money for Kirkgate Arts and Heritage.

There are many ways that you can support us, such as:

  • Sponsored events (marathons, swims, sky dives and more)
  • Cake and bake sales
  • Requesting donations to us in lieu of gifts at birthdays and special occasions

We are always looking for creative, new ways to raise money with your support – do let us know if you have an idea!
