About Bedwas Workmen’s Hall & Institute

Bedwas Workmen’s Hall is a volunteer-run entertainment venue in the heart of Bedwas. A theatre upstairs and an art centre downstairs, both are available for hire for a range of activities including productions, parties and music events.

If you’re interested in learning new skills, we hold classes for a number of different subjects. For more information on what we have going on, check out the What’s On page.

Bedwas Workmen’s Hall was built in 1923 thanks to local miners who together raised the money by donating a penny a week.

Join us as a volunteer

There’s lots going on, and getting involved can be rewarding and fun. You don’t need any qualifications or specific experience, and training is provided. The time commitment is once a fortnight, or as little or as much as you can spare. Volunteering looks good on your CV and Personal Statement for uni. Earn Counts for Welsh Bacc., Millenium Volunteer, and D of E Awards.
