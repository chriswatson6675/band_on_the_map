How do I book tickets?

Please only buy tickets with authorised ticketing agents such as our official ticketing partner, Ticketmaster

You can book tickets online by clicking on the 'Buy Tickets' button on the event you wish to attend from our Event Calendar.

Accessibility tickets can also be purchased online via our ticketing partner, Ticketmaster.

How do I upgrade my experience?

Each show has a variety of upgrade options available, from early entry with a pre-show glass of champagne in one of our private bars to a fully catered exclusive suite for the evening. 

Call the hospitality team on 0161 950 1069 for more information and to book or click here:

Find out about upgrades

 

Event timings?

We always try to include the most up to date information to the event pages on the website, but timing information can change.

Please always arrive in plenty of time to allow plenty of time to pass through our ticket and security checks.
