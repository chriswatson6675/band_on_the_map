Tcha Limberger Quartet | 22nd September @ 6:30pm

Celebrated multi-instrumentalist, composer and educator Tcha Limberger brings his extraordinary musicianship to the stage.

Born into a renowned Belgian Manouche musical family and inspired by the legacy of Django Reinhardt, Limberger has built an international career spanning jazz, Flamenco and the rich folk traditions of the Carpathian Basin. His acclaimed album Fekete éjszaka, borulj a világra with the Budapest Gypsy Orchestra received a Songlines Music Award nomination in 2015.

A virtuoso performer with a deeply expressive style, Tcha continues to captivate audiences around the world through projects including I Silenti, Les Violons de Bruxelles and The Viper Club.
