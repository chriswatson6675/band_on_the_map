INDUSTRIAL COAST PRESENTS: ANCIENT METHODS

  • Saturday 26 September 2026
  • 19:00 23:00
  • Google Calendar ICS

Ancient Methods, founder of the Persephonic Sirens Label, has cemented his status as a steadfast presence within the electronic music scene for nearly two decades. His

venture embodies an unyielding and resolute passion for otherworldly, multifaceted

music, underpinned by his distinctive "pitch black techno war funk."

Firmly grounded in the conviction that innovation arises from dismantling barriers, his DJ sets, live performances, and productions are a perpetual force of nonconformist renewal, devoid of ingratiation to fleeting vogues. His performances and collaborations with Regis, Orphx, and Cindytalk have taken him to stages worldwide, from ADE to Tresor

Tickets.
