The Auxiliary is a leading artist-led organisation backed by an unstoppable creative community, based in an 14,000sq ft creative complex in the heart of Middlesbrough.

Currently undergoing a major redevelopment, we host 25 studios, an exhibition space, an experimental music venue, a community programme, two annual festivals and welcome over 100 artists annually through our artistic programme.

What We Do

The Auxiliary project space consists of two galleries, artist studios, a workshop and performance space. It is dedicated to the production and dissemination of experimental and sound art practices, through investigating local industrial heritage and the reimagining of Tees Valley as a site for invigorated creative industries.

Gallery Opening Times

The Auxiliary warehouse is currently closed due to refurbishment and will reopen on September 9th, 2026.
