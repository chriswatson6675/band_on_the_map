CAMP Theatre Company-The Farndale Avenue Housing Estate Townswomen's Dramatic Society Murder Mystery

Three decades before The Play That Goes Wrong opened on the London stage there was The Farndale Avenue Housing Estate Townswomen’s Guild Dramatic Society Murder Mystery. Mrs Reece, doyenne of the F.A.H.E.T.G. Dramatic Society sometimes thinks her shows are jinxed compared to productions she has seen at the Old Vic and Stratford-upon-Avon (“of course, they’re not as adventurous as us”). For some inexplicable reason lighting and sound cues are sometimes misplaced, lines are forgotten and there was that unfortunate time when the set collapsed; at least the Stage Manager is now out of Intensive Care. Tonight’s production, though – the self penned Murder At Checkmate Manor – promises to be a triumph. With a cast of five, some of whom have acted before, the plot is a real cracker. Will Lady Bishop survive the night? Have Colonel and Mrs King made a wrong move? What fate awaits Mrs Castle the cook? And did Pawn the Butler really do it? Throw in a delicious recipe for Lemon Souffle, a Fashion Show featuring the perennial winner of the Miss Farndale Beauty Competition plus a Murder Mystery Quiz with a prize for lucky audience member and the evening is likely to linger long in the memory. What could possibly go wrong? ABOUT THE COMPANY Camp Theatre is a Community Theatre Company that was founded in 1994 and has just performed its 75th Production. The company has toured the South West regularly since 2011 and has donated more than £75,000 to a wide range of local, national and international charities. ABOUT STERTS Originally a pig farm, Sterts is a four acre site where nature and creativity collide and is home to the Barn Theatre (400 seats), multi-purpose Sturrock Studio (50-70 seats) and Community Hub. Following a period of major redevelopment in 2024, the original amphitheatre is now covered by a solar powered barn, which is run solely off renewable energy. Named after our founders, Ewart & Anne Sturrock, the Sturrock Studio is an intimate studio space for small scale plays, music, comedy and more. The Sturrock Studio is a heated space, hosting performances throughout the year. FOOD AND DRINK Our on site bar stocks drinks from The Cornish Wine Company, snacks from Redmoor Snacks and Ice Cream from Gibsons of Looe Cornish Ice Cream. Our bar opens half an hour before Sturrock Studio performances. Sterts does not have an onsite cafe, so we recommend visiting one of our local pubs, restaurants or farm shops for pre-show food. Our bar is staffed by our wonderful volunteer team throughout the year. Audiences are welcome to bring a food to site but please do not bring alcohol to the premises. HOW TO GET HERE Sterts Arts & Environmental Centre is in Upton Cross, just six miles from Liskeard, eight miles from Callington and ten miles from Launceston. Sterts is within easy reach of the A30 and A38, a one hour drive from Truro and 45 minute drive from Plymouth. Public Transport is limited. Want to limit your carbon footprint or save money on fuel? Check out the national Liftshare network and find someone to share your journey with. PARKING We have free onsite parking, including disabled parking. From January 2026, we will have to 2 EV car charging points on our site. We recommend arriving in good time for each performance. ACCESSIBILITY We have a registered Changing Place on site. The Sturrock Studio is accessed via a 50m walk from our car park via ramps. The Sturrock Studio does not have a lift or hearing loop. Our seating is unreserved, however we will always reserve seating for audience members with access needs and are happy to provide chairs if needed. Please contact us on 01579 362382 or [email protected] and our team will be happy to help.
