Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

What's the dress code?

Wear clothing that makes you feel flamboyant and fabulous or simply whatever makes you happy.

But please do make an effort. On occasions we turn away tracksuits or certain types of sports wear. Please dress to impress to avoid disappointment at the door.

Party reservations?

We are happy to reserve seating for your guests provided bookings are received with plenty of notice.

Do you host private events?

We offer our space for hire at reasonable rates on any nights we are closed and also for Pre-Party's on nights we are open.

Can we film in your venue?

Yes... But please be respectful of others.

We are also happy to negotiate availability of our space as a location set for film and media production.

Are you wheel chair accessible?

We have no official access to toilet facilities but welcome disabled people and wheelchair users when ever we can. Drop us a message for more information or ask security for help when you arrive.

Help! I left my coat or personal belongs behind!

If we have only just closed try giving the door a general tap. Otherwise contact us via the duty phone listed under "contact us".

Please bear in mind that our staff work into the early hours of the morning, often until 6am. If you leave a message we will get back to you but only after we wake up. Repeatedly calling and banging the doors in the early hours won't help either of us resolve the issue.
