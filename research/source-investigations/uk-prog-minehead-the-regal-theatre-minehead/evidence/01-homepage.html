The Regal Theatre – where performance comes alive!

A much loved part of the local community, the Regal provides an inspirational range of diverse entertainment in its large 400-seat auditorium, which includes spaces for wheelchair users, good acoustics and a hearing loop.

The theatre bar is open during intervals and also before each performance and is an ideal place to meet friends and enjoy refreshments or to relax on the balcony overlooking the town centre.

A lift provides access to the first-floor bar and auditorium for people with limited mobility.

Support the Regal

The Regal receives no local, regional or central government support.  Box Office revenue alone does not meet the costs of putting on over 150 quality performances a year, together with the maintenance of our 90 year old building, WE also have many ambitions for the future.  

This can only be made possible through vital additional support from individuals, trusts and foundations and community group support.

You can make a difference. Discover the many ways you can you show your support for the Regal and help to ensure it has a bright and secure future.
