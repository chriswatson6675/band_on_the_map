Hospitalfield Collections

Hospitalfield’s collections continue to grow with a selection of 21st century artworks acquired through our art projects. The original collection was mainly built up by Patrick and Elizabeth Allan Fraser and has at its core paintings and sculptures commissioned by the couple in the 19th century. It also includes painting by Patrick Allan Fraser, works on paper, the library, an archive and 20th century works by artists who have studied and taught at Hospitalfield.
