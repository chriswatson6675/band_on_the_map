Not Just a Game Show

Date: 02 Dec 2026 - 23 Dec 2026

Time: 19:00

Venue: Museum of Comedy

Ever dreamt of starring in your own TV game show? This is your chance… sort of. 

From the creative team behind Sh!t-faced Shakespeare comes Not Just a Game Show, a high-octane comedy parody where the audience takes centre stage. 

We’ve mashed up the best of 80s TV, from Family Fortunes to Catchphrase, and thrown in an utterly dysfunctional production team, a studio mascot named 'Mr Jacket Potato', a cuddly toy in bondage, and a final, desperate round of darts that has to hit the bullseye. It’s fast, furious, and pure comedic gold. So round up your friends, get involved and become the contestant you always knew you could be. 

Are you game?

Running Time: 60m

Age Advisory: 16+

Ticket Levy: Ticket prices include a £1.25 venue restoration levy
