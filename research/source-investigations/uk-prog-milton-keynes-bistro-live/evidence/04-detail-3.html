🙋‍♂️ Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in your ticket?
Your night includes your entry, a three-course meal, entertainment, dancing and the option to add drinks or VIP extras.

 

Do you offer Birthday Packages?
Yes, we have a birthday package, you can add this on anytime prior to the night and includes a foil and cluster balloons, and a drink each. 

 

Can we bring our own decorations?
Absolutely! Balloons, banners and sashes are all welcome. Please no confetti or glitter cannons though.

 

Is there a dress code?
Dress to impress, dress for fun - or come in full fancy dress. We welcome it all. Leave your scruffs at home though.

 

Can we customise our booking?
Yes. Add drinks, swap package items, and chat with our team to personalise your party.

Important Message to Our Customers

After much reflection, and with very heavy hearts, we have made the incredibly difficult decision to permanently close Bistro Live after 25 unforgettable years. This decision has not come easily, but the ongoing economic challenges facing hospitality in the UK have made it impossible for us to continue. To every customer who danced on our tables, celebrated milestones with us, supported us through the years or recommended us to friends, … thank you. We are deeply proud of what we created, and grateful for every moment shared within these walls.

From all of us at Bistro Live, thank you for being part of our story.

Customer Q&A – Existing Bookings & Payments

We understand that many of our customers will have questions regarding existing bookings, deposits, and payments following the closure of Bistro Live.

We’ve answered the most common questions below and thank you in advance for your patience and understanding during this difficult time.

1. I have an upcoming booking — will it still go ahead?

Our final day of opening across all Bistro Live locations was:

Saturday 31st January 2026

As this date has now passed, all future bookings will unfortunately not be able to go ahead.

2. Are all Bistro Live venues closing?

Yes — Bistro Live has permanently closed across all locations:

• Leicester

• Nottingham

• Milton Keynes

3. I paid a deposit or made a payment in advance — will I receive a refund?

If you have made a reservation, booking, or paid a deposit, you may be able to recover this by requesting a chargeback from your bank or card provider. For any other queries, please contact FRP Advisory Trading Limited:

leicester@frpadvisory.com
+44 (0)116 303 3333

4. I have a credit note — can I still use it or request a refund?

Credit notes are issued as a gesture of goodwill to customers who did not attend on the evening.

Unfortunately, as such, credit notes cannot be honoured or refunded following the permanent closure of the business.

5. Will Bistro Live reopen in the future?

Bistro Live has now permanently closed, and there are no plans for reopening.

We want to thank every guest who supported us over the years — it has meant the world.

A Final Thank You

To everyone who visited Bistro Live in Leicester, Nottingham, or Milton Keynes — thank you for your support, your celebrations, and the memories shared with us over the years.
