🔥Energetic Entertainment Every Weekend🔥

Every weekend, Bistro Live transforms into the ultimate party destination. Our carefully curated events feature live bands, resident DJs, and themed nights designed to get you on your feet and singing along. Expect a fun-packed atmosphere, brilliant music, and our award-winning team making sure your night runs smoothly from start to finish.

From Club Classic Nights and the Ultimate DJ Party to 80s-themed throwbacks, Cheesy Nights, and Absolute Anthems, there’s always something happening to suit every music taste and mood. Love a bit of interaction? Join us for Crowd Karaoke or Music Bingo and get the whole room singing and laughing together.

 

🎶Types of Events You’ll Find🎶

Live Music & Tribute Nights: Sing along to iconic anthems performed by professional live bands and tribute acts, covering everything from pop legends to rock classics.

DJ & Party Nights: High-energy DJ sets packed with floor-fillers, throwbacks, and chart hits, perfect for dancing all night with friends.

Themed & Interactive Events: From Music Bingo to seasonal specials, our themed nights add an interactive twist to your evening.

Seasonal & Special Events: Christmas parties, New Year’s Eve, summer specials, and one-off event nights run throughout the year.

 

🥳Celebrate with Us🥳

At Bistro Live, we don’t just host parties - we create unforgettable celebrations. Whether you're planning a Birthday Night Out, a Hen Party, or organising your office Christmas Party, we’ll help you pull off the ultimate night to remember. Our team delivers a full party experience complete with a freshly prepared three-course meal, brilliant entertainment, and full table service all night long.

 

For the biggest celebration of the year, our New Year’s Eve party is not to be missed. With live music, great food, and a lively countdown to midnight, there’s no better place to welcome the new year.

 

📍Three Incredible Venues – One Epic Experience📍

No matter which location you choose, every Bistro Live venue offers the same incredible atmosphere, food, and entertainment. Our Leicester, Nottingham, and Milton Keynes venues are easy to reach, perfectly located for your next night out, and each brings its own unique energy to your party experience.

Each venue regularly hosts a mix of live bands, DJ-led parties, themed nights, and special events, all designed to create the ultimate night out. Check out what’s on at your nearest Bistro Live venue and secure your tickets early - our party nights are hugely popular and sell out fast!

Important Message to Our Customers

After much reflection, and with very heavy hearts, we have made the incredibly difficult decision to permanently close Bistro Live after 25 unforgettable years. This decision has not come easily, but the ongoing economic challenges facing hospitality in the UK have made it impossible for us to continue. To every customer who danced on our tables, celebrated milestones with us, supported us through the years or recommended us to friends, … thank you. We are deeply proud of what we created, and grateful for every moment shared within these walls.

From all of us at Bistro Live, thank you for being part of our story.

Customer Q&A – Existing Bookings & Payments

We understand that many of our customers will have questions regarding existing bookings, deposits, and payments following the closure of Bistro Live.

We’ve answered the most common questions below and thank you in advance for your patience and understanding during this difficult time.

1. I have an upcoming booking — will it still go ahead?

Our final day of opening across all Bistro Live locations was:

Saturday 31st January 2026

As this date has now passed, all future bookings will unfortunately not be able to go ahead.

2. Are all Bistro Live venues closing?

Yes — Bistro Live has permanently closed across all locations:

• Leicester

• Nottingham

• Milton Keynes

3. I paid a deposit or made a payment in advance — will I receive a refund?

If you have made a reservation, booking, or paid a deposit, you may be able to recover this by requesting a chargeback from your bank or card provider. For any other queries, please contact FRP Advisory Trading Limited:

leicester@frpadvisory.com
+44 (0)116 303 3333

4. I have a credit note — can I still use it or request a refund?

Credit notes are issued as a gesture of goodwill to customers who did not attend on the evening.

Unfortunately, as such, credit notes cannot be honoured or refunded following the permanent closure of the business.

5. Will Bistro Live reopen in the future?

Bistro Live has now permanently closed, and there are no plans for reopening.

We want to thank every guest who supported us over the years — it has meant the world.

A Final Thank You

To everyone who visited Bistro Live in Leicester, Nottingham, or Milton Keynes — thank you for your support, your celebrations, and the memories shared with us over the years.
