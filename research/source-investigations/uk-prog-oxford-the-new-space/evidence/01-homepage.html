What's On

Hire the New Space
https://www.new.ox.ac.uk/hire-new-space

The New Space is an intimate venue situated in the heart of Oxford specialising in chamber and instrumental music, early music and song. The repertoire of the hall extends from contemporary jazz through to music of the renaissance. 

Music has been central to the foundation of New College in 1379, and the choir and choir school persist to this day. New College’s musical culture today is livelier than ever, and the College is host to the New College Music Society, New Chamber Opera and a wide array of professional and student music. Now with the addition of the New Space the College can continue to deliver music making of an exceptional quality.
