ABOUT US

Who we are

For over four decades, The Citadel has been a place that helps children and young people in Leith on their journey called life. Youth clubs, trips and life lessons – there’s a little bit of something for everyone. We work with families, young mums, volunteers and older people, even offering one-to-one support for kids who need a little extra help to make their way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about The Citadel? Here you’ll find quick answers about who we are, what we do, and how you can get involved. If your question isn’t listed, just ask — we’d love to hear from you.

No, we do not charge for your child to attend our clubs.

We run clubs for children and young people from 6 yrs old up to 21.

Go to our Clubs & Activities page to find out what runs when. Many of our clubs and group require you to register first before attending as some, particularly our under 12s group have waiting lists to join. To find out more about our clubs and groups please contact the office and ask for the relevant team;

  • Under 12s Team
  • 13 and Over Team
  • Employability Team
  • Families Team (Family support is available to the parents of young people attending the Citadel)
