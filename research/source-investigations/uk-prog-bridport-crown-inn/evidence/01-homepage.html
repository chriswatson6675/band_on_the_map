Crown Inn

Situated next to the A35 Crown Roundabout between Bridport and West Bay, the Crown Inn is a local landmark and a favorite gathering spot for both locals and visitors. Known for its welcoming atmosphere and lively entertainment, the pub offers everything you need for a great day or night out in Dorset. Whether you’re stopping by for a quick bite, a refreshing drink, or some live entertainment, the Crown Inn promises a memorable experience.

Dorset Hospitality At Its Best

Serving a wide selection of delicious food lunchtime and evening, Thursday to Monday, the pub’s menu is filled with classic pub dishes made from fresh, locally sourced ingredients. From hearty meals to lighter fare, there’s something for everyone. Enjoy your meal in the spacious beer garden during warmer months, where outside dining offers the perfect setting to relax and take in the fresh air. The Crown Inn also caters to sports fans, with TNT Sports showing live games, and a friendly darts scene, adding to the lively pub culture.

Every weekend, the Crown Inn comes alive with live music, making it the go-to spot for those seeking entertainment and a vibrant atmosphere. Regular quiz nights bring the community together for some friendly competition, and of course, the pub serves Palmers’ fine ales, along with a comprehensive selection of wines and spirits to complement your visit.

With a large car park and easy access to both Bridport and West Bay, the Crown Inn is convenient for travellers and locals alike. The pub is dog-friendly, making it a great stop for walkers, and offers disabled facilities, ensuring everyone can enjoy the experience. Whether you’re visiting for the entertainment, the food, or the relaxed atmosphere, the Crown Inn is the perfect place to unwind and enjoy Dorset hospitality at its best.

A story of family, heritage, quality… and beer.

Palmers ales are brewed in one of Britain’s oldest and prettiest breweries and have been since 1794. The only thatched brewery in the UK, Palmers sits adjacent to the river Brit just a mile from Dorset’s Jurassic Coast. All our fine ales are brewed using water from our own naturally rising spring.

Our Head Brewer uses only the finest Maris Otter malt and carefully selected whole leaf hops to produce ales in a way they have been made for generations. Palmers historic brewhouse has a traditional Mash Tun, an open top Copper, along with top fermentation, this is the way ale should be brewed.
