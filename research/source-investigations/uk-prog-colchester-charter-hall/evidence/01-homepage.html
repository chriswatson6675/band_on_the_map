Charter Hall

Charter Hall Colchester is a multipurpose events venue in the heart of Colchester and is fast becoming a ‘go to destination’ for the best in live music, comedy, sports and special events. A packed programme of events brings great value entertainment with Jack Whitehall, Sarah Millican and Martin Kemp among the many class acts we’ve welcomed to the Charter Hall stage. This year will be no exception with stand-up from the UK’s funniest comics including Jimmy Carr, Paul Smith, Ed Gamble and Frankie Boyle; live concerts from Fleetwood Mac Legacy, Shalamar and a number of amazing tribute acts, sporting prowess from the SOS WRESTLING and the very popular Gin To My Tonic Craft Spirit Festival

Colchester Events supports the Council's declaration of a climate emergency and net zero emissions target by 2030, and the need for us all to reduce the environmental impact of our lifestyles. One way we can do this is by travelling by walking, cycling or using public transport, rather than cars where possible. Please see the information about travelling to our event locations by walking, cycling and using public transport. Information is also listed about parking where this is necessary.

Charter Hall is a short walk from the town centre through Castle Park, with great local bus links and within walking distance of Colchester North and Colchester Town Station.

By Bike

There are plenty of bike stands outside the main Leisure World entrance. You can download the Colchester Cycle Map from the Cycle Colchester website, or use one of our suggested routes on this map

By Bus

There are bus stops nearby to Charter Hall on Cowdrey Avenue which are served by several different bus routes.

You can use Travel Essex to plan your route by bus online or by downloading the app.

Parking Information

There are 400 parking spaces, including accessible parking spaces, available at Charter Hall.

If parking on site, please arrive at the ‘Doors’ time to secure a parking space, we cannot guarantee a parking space as this car park is a shared location with other businesses.

Leisure World Car Park uses MiPermit, Location number 737941. You can pay for parking in advance using: secure.mipermit.com/northessex/Account/PayAndStayAdd.aspx

The MiPermit webpage below includes a user guide on how to download and use the MiPermit app: north.parkingpartnership.org/mipermit/

Alternatively:

Send an SMS to 61600 with the word PARK and your vehicle registration number. For example: “PARK T123EST” The automated system will call you back and ask for the car park location number (737941) and your payment card details. Existing SMS Customers Send an SMS to 61600 with the word PARK, location number (737941) and duration in hours or minutes. For example: “PARK 737941 3hrs”

Or you can call 0345 505 1155 (Automated telephone service). Calls will cost the standard national rate set by your mobile network provider. You will be asked for the following: • Car park location number: 737941 • Stay duration in hours or minutes • Your vehicle registration number
