Imperial Classical Ballet® Presents The Nutcracker | The Victoria Theatre Halifax | Saturday 17 October 2026

Featuring a Large Live Orchestra

Following last year’s phenomenal success, Imperial Classical Ballet® is delighted to return to the Victoria Theatre Halifax with their breathtaking production of The Nutcracker.

A Festive Tradition

Christmas simply wouldn’t be complete without a trip to the theatre to experience the magic of the world’s most beloved ballet – The Nutcracker. With snow flurries, sweets, princes, magic, and love, this enchanting production captures the essence of the holiday season.

Set to Tchaikovsky’s timeless score, including the iconic Waltz of the Flowers and the Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy, The Nutcracker continues to captivate audiences of all ages. It is the perfect introduction to the beauty of ballet while remaining a cherished classic for seasoned enthusiasts.

Join Us This Autumn

Immerse yourself in the magic of The Nutcracker, a seasonal favourite that has delighted audiences across the globe. Don’t miss this spellbinding production brought to life by the artistry of Imperial Classical Ballet® and the rich sounds of a live orchestra.

 

Imperial Classical Ballet will also be performing Swan Lake on Saturday 17 October 2026 at 7:30pm
