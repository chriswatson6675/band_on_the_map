WHATS ON AT THE CAROUSEL

Welcome to The Carousel.

Whether you're looking for a collaborative workspace, a private studio, or specialist digital and printmaking facilities, we provide a supportive and inspiring environment to create and connect.

Our versatile event space is available to hire, fully equipped to host workshops, performances, exhibitions, and more. Join our vibrant community and make The Carousel your home for work, creativity, and culture.

Community Building

In 2019 we set out to build a friendly, supportive and inspiring art space in Nottingham City Centre. The foundation of our building supports people's ideas and develops their creativity. Through art projects, exhibitions, workshops and events we have collaborated with hundreds of individuals. From our foundations we are now building a social enterprise that brings people together, provides space and builds community. 

Welcome to The Carousel…
a community building.

Our Memberships

We created The Carousel to bring diverse talents and ideas together. Our inclusive community now consists of small businesses, freelancers, remote workers, designers and a diverse group of artists and creative practitioners. We offer flexible and affordable memberships to suit your work requirements. All memberships include access to facilities that will develop productivity, encourage collaboration and help you find inspiration.

Event Space

Hire The Carousel for your event

Beyond the workday, The Carousel transforms into a fully licensed, well equipped and exciting event space. Our space is a blank canvas for imaginative expression, cultural gatherings and private parties. We have hosted hundreds of events and welcomed thousands of people through our doors.
