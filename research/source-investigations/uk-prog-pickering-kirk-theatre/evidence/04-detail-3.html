STAPE SILVER BAND – THEATRICAL BRASS

Returning to the Kirk Theatre the brilliant Stape Silver Band with ‘Theatrical Brass’

Step into the world of stage and screen with Theatrical Brass, an uplifting and entertaining concert performed by Stape Silver Brass Band and accompanied by members of Pickering Musical Society.

This special programme celebrates the power of brass music through some of the most loved and recognisable themes from theatre, musicals, and cinematic classics. From dramatic overtures to memorable melodies, the band brings storytelling to life with rich harmonies, dynamic performances, and a true sense of occasion.

Renowned for their musical excellence and engaging performances, Stape Silver Brass Band combines precision, warmth, and flair to create a concert that appeals to seasoned brass enthusiasts and newcomers alike.
