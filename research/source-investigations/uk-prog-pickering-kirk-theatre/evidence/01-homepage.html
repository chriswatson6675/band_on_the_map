Welcome

Welcome from our Theatre Manager

Our Summer programme opens with The Invisibe Man followed by Let’s Do It! – The Cole Porter Songbook, celebrating timeless music and classic songwriting. Music continues to take centre stage with The Rod Stewart Songbook, The Shadow of Cliff, DC Blues, and The Rat Pack – Las Vegas Live, alongside uplifting evenings such as Shout Out to the 60’s.

Audiences can also enjoy special appearances including The Yorkshire Auctioneer Angus Ashworth, as well as a varied
programme of theatre and live performances, from a Noël Coward Double-Bill to The Three Musketeers.

As the year progresses, highlights include Stape Silver Band – Theatrical Brass, The Jake Leg Jug Band, and wartime-themed favourites such as V for Victory – Johnny Victory and Tom Carradine’s Wartime Songbook. We’re also delighted to welcome broadcaster Jonathan Agnew for an evening of stories and insight.

Looking ahead, we’re excited to return in January 2027 with our annual pantomime, Sleeping Beauty — a magical, fun-filled production for all the family.

With such a diverse programme of music, theatre, and special events, there is something for everyone to enjoy. We look forward to welcoming you to the Kirk Theatre for a fantastic season of live entertainment.
