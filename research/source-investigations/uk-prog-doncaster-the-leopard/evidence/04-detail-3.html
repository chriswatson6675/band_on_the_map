“My favourite song of the last decade… because it’s human, it’s real and it has soul.” — Iggy Pop
Wildly original, gloriously unpredictable, and impossible to pigeonhole, Mik Artistik’s Ego Trip are one of the UK’s most beloved underground live acts. For many years they were a Glastonbury tradition, drawing crowds who wouldn’t dream of starting their festival without witnessing Mik’s riotous sets at the Croissant Neuf Bandstand or the Rocket Lounge—performances that have become part of Glastonbury folklore.
At the heart of it all is Mik himself—part punk-poet, part surreal philosopher, part stand-up comic—fixing the audience with his compelling stare before launching into improvised tales, spoken-word detours, spontaneous tangents, and moments of both hilarity and tenderness. Every show feels handcrafted for the people in the room, full of unpredictable interaction, surreal humour, and the occasional burst of unexpected interpretive dancing that leave audiences roaring with laughter.
This unique performance energy is powered by the razor-sharp musicianship of Jonny Flockton and a rotating cast of superb bass players, weaving together rock, soul, funk, cosmic country, and DIY punk spirit into a sound entirely their own.
Their fan base spans generations—anyone who loves clever lyrics, fearless live performance, and gigs that feel alive. Champions at BBC 6Music, they’ve earned high praise from Tom Robinson, Chris Hawkins and Gideon Coe, who called them “the best live band I’ve seen all summer.”
“What would life be for me without Mik Artistik? Well smaller and not as much fun.” — Iggy Pop

Event Details

Definitely Oasis are internationally recognised as the most authentic and in-demand Oasis tribute band on the planet. Known for their unrivalled sound, look, and attitude, the band delivers the full Oasis experience across the UK and around the world. 

Since forming, Definitely Oasis have toured extensively, playing sold-out shows in the UK, Europe, the Middle East, and Australia. In August 2025, they hit a major milestone, performing four huge shows at Edinburgh’s Ice Arena, just hours before the real Oasis took to the stage next door at Murrayfield Stadium. With only a thin fence between the venues, the timing and energy made headlines — and there’s no doubt Oasis heard them. 

Their reputation for detail and authenticity has seen them headline some of the UK’s most iconic venues — including a sold-out show at Glasgow’s Barrowland Ballroom, famously used by Oasis for their 10 Years of Noise and Confusion gig. 

They’ve also followed in the Gallaghers’ early footsteps, playing historic venues like King Tut’s Wah Wah Hut in Glasgow, The Joiners in Southampton, The Tivoli in Buckley, and La Belle Angele in Edinburgh — the same stages Oasis cut their teeth on before becoming legends. 

Whether it’s a 100-capacity club or a major festival stage, Definitely Oasis bring the swagger, sound, and spirit of Oasis to a new generation of fans — while staying true to the legacy that defined a generation.

ANT & SEB are a duo from Wales who auditioned for the fifth series of The X Factor. They are known for their entertaining rendition of “Mysterious Girl,” which quickly became one of the show’s most iconic and widely shared performances. During the audition, Ant led with smooth vocals while Seb delivered unexpected Jamaican-inspired, robotic rap adlibs and comedic flair. Although the judges criticised Seb’s performance, audiences loved it, and the audition went viral, inspiring memes such as “When your mum says take your brother with you,” and generating widespread media attention across the UK for their unique style.

The duo will perform their crowd-favourite version of “Mysterious Girl,” alongside fun, feel-good tracks and their viral Christmas charity single, the comedy song Sprinkle It, which reached No.1 on the Amazon Music Hot New Releases Chart, ranking ahead of major international artists including Drake and Post Malone at the time of release.

Despite failing to impress Simon Cowell and advance as a novelty act, they have taken social media by storm. After sharing a brief clip on TikTok revealing their return, the response was phenomenal — within just 24 hours the clip amassed more than 480,000 views, with countless comments pouring in. Supporters have been eager to mark the occasion, with reactions including, “This is the best day of my life.

Event Details

A multi‑band showcase celebrating some of the most exciting new rock talent emerging across Yorkshire.

This Halloween, The Leopard hosts a powerful night of live music featuring rising acts from Bradford, Leeds, Barnsley and Sheffield. Expect high‑energy performances, standout musicianship and a diverse mix of modern rock styles – from alt‑rock and shoegaze to blues‑driven classic rock and raw indie.

Lineup

  • Echoviolet — Edgy, contemporary alt‑rock packed with catchy riffs, big choruses, and a modern twist on the 2000s post‑grunge sound. FFO: Jack White, QOTSA, Soundgarden, Foo Fighters.
  • Belcarra — Loud noisy four piece who blur the sounds of shoegaze and metal. FFO: Deftones, Thrice and Alexisonfire.
  • The High Sonic Council — Four man rock band from Leeds reinvigorating the blues rock sound for a new generation. FFO: Frank Zappa, Blue Oyster Cult, The Who.
  • Venice Beach Boyfriend — Exciting new female fronted indie‑emo rock band from Leeds.
  • The Seam — Raw alternative‑indie rock band from Barnsley.

Event Overview

Halloween Music Fest brings together five emerging bands for a night built around discovery, atmosphere and live intensity. Whether you follow the local scene or simply want to experience fresh new talent, this event offers a curated snapshot of the region’s most compelling new rock acts.

Secure your tickets and spend Halloween immersed in loud guitars, new music and the unmistakable character of The Leopard.

We are thrilled to welcome British Rhythm ‘n’ Blues legends Dr. Feelgood.
Formed on Canvey Island in Essex in the early 1970’s Dr. Feelgood have become one of the most popular live rhythm and blues acts in the world.
Dr. Feelgood have enjoyed a string of hit singles in many countries including Milk & Alcohol, Down at the Doctors, Roxette, She’s a Wind Up and See You Later Alligator.
The current line-up of the band is led by their drummer Kevin Morris, and features Gordon Russell on lead guitar, Phil Mitchell on bass and charismatic vocalist, Robert Kane.
