 Settle Victoria Hall

Est. 1853: The world’s oldest Music Hall

Settle Victoria Hall is a Grade II listed working Victorian Music Hall in the market town of Settle, on the edge of the iconic Yorkshire Dales. Our wide programme of live music, cinema, theatre and entertainment is supplemented by community events, cafes, classes, markets and more.

Still going strong since 1853!

Opening Night 1853

Victoria Hall was opened on 11 October 1853, and has been at the heart of community and culture in Settle ever since. We operate as an award-winning and successful social business, bringing arts, culture, and community space to our beautiful corner of North Yorkshire. Settle Victoria Hall Ltd. is a registered independent charity run by a small management team, and supported by trustees and volunteers.

Partnerships

Settle Victoria Hall is grateful to Keyfund, Power to Change, and NIF for help with the financing of our cinema project. With their help, and the generosity of local individuals and organisations, we have been able to install projection and sound equipment, allowing us to bring Cinema back to Settle after 40 years. To view our film programme, click here. 

Settle Victoria Hall is proud to be part of the Music Venues Alliance, representing Grassroots music venues across the UK, and the Theatres Trust.
