Private Hire

Elevate your conference, corporate meeting, wedding, or private celebration at Albert Hall in Manchester, one of the city’s most unique and sought-after event venues.

Our space offers a memorable backdrop for weddings, corporate events, product launches, team away days, and special occasions of every kind. With flexible layouts, state-of-the-art facilities, and a prime city-centre location, Albert Hall provides everything you need to create an unforgettable event that leaves a lasting impression.
