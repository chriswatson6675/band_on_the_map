CURRENT EXHIBITION

Annual Fund raising Auction

Saturday 12th September to Saturday October 3rd

Hildegard Wildebeest's Collector Picks from the Auction

Go to the online auction

There are 90+ lots from Local, National and International artist.  Pop into the gallery, each lot has a QR code that can be scanned to take you to the Jumblebee Auction where you can place your bid.  Oriel Q receives no regular public funding and relies on volunteers, artist support, sales and fundraising.  Many artists have entered works at special starting prices specifically to support the gallery. Starting bids should therefore not be read as normal market valuations.

Oriel Q has been an important part of the Narberth art scene for around 20 years and we are determined that it should continue.

We are now volunteer led with a great group of Professionals and Artists whose job it is to make the Gallery sustainable for the future and retain this independant Art Gallery for the whole community in South West Wales, a far reaching vision for the Arts and opportunities for All kinds of Artists to utilise this fabulous Art Space and Gallery.
