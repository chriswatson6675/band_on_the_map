Hadestown

About Hadestown

Hadestown: Teen Edition is a spellbinding musical that blends ancient Greek myth with the sound of American folk and jazz. Set in a Depression-era world, it tells two powerful love stories: young dreamers Orpheus and Eurydice, and the immortal rulers of the Underworld, Hades and Persephone. 

Based on the Tony Award-winning West End and Broadway musical, this moving, hopeful story about love, resilience, and the power of belief is brought to life by the talented young performers of Lichfield Musical Youth Theatre.
