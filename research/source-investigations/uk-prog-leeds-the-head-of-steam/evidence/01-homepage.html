Leeds City Centre

Est. 04.12.14

Drinks
Situated on Mill Hill in the heart of Leeds City Centre, Head of Steam Mill Hill is a great spot for quality drinks, good atmosphere and everything from after-work pints to weekend meet-ups.
The bar offers a large selection of draught, cask and keg beers, with a strong focus on UK craft beer alongside Belgian favourites. We also have a carefully selected wine menu, with a range of wines available by the glass or bottle. Our craft can fridge features some of the best breweries from the UK and around the world, including beers from across Europe, Scandinavia, the USA and Canada.
For something a little more traditional, we also have a selection of Belgian and German bottled beers, alongside a range of sharing bottles featuring some of the best stouts and sours from around the world. With our drinks selection constantly changing, there’s always something new to discover.
Alongside our extensive drinks selection, we also host live entertainment throughout the week, including live jazz and our open mic. Come along, grab a drink and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere in the heart of Leeds City Centre.
