Ye Olde Rose and Crown is an award winning theatre pub located on Hoe Street in the heart of Walthamstow. Whatever it is you love - be it beer,community, friendly people or music and theatre - you’ll find it here...

Our current opening hours are:

  • Monday-Thursday 3pm-11pm  
  • Friday 2pm-1am
  • Saturday 12noon-1am
  • Sunday 12noon-11pm

The Beer

As an award winning  pub, we aim to offer an extensive range of beers. Local and further afield as there are just too many good beer makers to say no. 

We have a mixture of fixed and rotating taps ensuring that you as a customer can never get bored but you still have your firm favourites.

The Food

OUR KITCHEN IS OPEN MONDAY-SATURDAY

All food served at Ye Olde Rose and Crown is home cooked, from Hans On Kitchen Sunday Lunch to the Pop Up's. 

We aim to work with local suppliers and food makers as much as possible.
