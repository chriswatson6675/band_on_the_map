Who is at The Fort Theatre?

The Antonine Theatre Group was established in 1960 and have been at their home, The Fort Theatre, since 1969.

We usually produce a spring, an autumn play as well as our annual pantomime in December. The ATG currently host he SCDA Glasgow District One Act Festival in March. As well as drama productions, we also host and produce musical events throughout the year. 

Like to join our group? Get in touch via our contact form to find out how you could be involved. No experience necessary, just your enthusiasm!
