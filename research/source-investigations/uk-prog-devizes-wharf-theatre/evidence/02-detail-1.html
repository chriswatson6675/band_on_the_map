Scenes from an Execution
From: September 21, 2026, till September 26, 2026,

Written by Howard Barker, this play explores what happens when art clashes with politics and religion.
In 16th-century Venice, renowned female artist Galactia is commissioned by the Catholic Church and the Venetian State to paint the Battle of Lepanto, celebrating the defeat of the pagan Turk and glorifying Venice. Instead, Galactia paints the cruel, senseless slaughter of one of the bloodiest battles in human history.
Will Galactia be strong enough to withstand the machinations of Church and State, winning her own personal battle to get her truth heard?
More relevant today than ever before, with facts being twisted by politicians, voices silenced by those with the most power, and the arts one of the few mediums left through which people may find their voice. Galactia’s personal battle will empower and inspire audiences to keep demanding the truth.

Director | Jax Brady

Devizes Film Club | Fremont
From: October 1, 2026,

Donya (Anaita Wali Zada) is a young Afghan refugee living in Fremont, California. Previously a translator for the American army, she now spends her days working at a fortune cookie factory. Lonely in her new environment, she has trouble sleeping. She’s trying to rebuild her life but is tormented by survivor’s guilt for leaving her family and friends behind in Kabul. One day, in an attempt to connect with the wider world, she sends a personal message through a fortune cookie. Tinged with wry, deadpan humour, Fremont is a delightful and visually captivating film. Winner of 7 international film awards.

Playhouse Creatures
From: October 8, 2026, till October 10, 2026,

This tragi-comic play by April De Angelis, set in 1660s London, focuses on the first English actresses allowed on stage after the Restoration, and explores the lives, struggles and camaraderie of five women navigating a male-dominated world, dealing with sexual exploitation, ageing, and the fight for artistic freedom.
Bawdily funny yet profound, Playhouse Creatures explores the pitilessness of ageism and the need for economic independence. The play highlights the harsh realities of the early theatre scene, where actresses were often seen as ‘animals’ or ‘creatures’, rather than respected professionals.

Produced & Directed by Debby Wilkinson

Contains strong language & adult themes | Suggested age 14+

Shakespeare’s Green-Eyed Monster
From: October 18, 2026,

O beware of Jealousy! It is the green-eyed monster.

Jealousy and envy, powerful and destructive emotions that sow doubt, suspicion, disgust, anger and even murder. Shakespeare’s Green-Eyed Monster is a full-length play created by director Gill Morrell for Shakespeare Live from extracts & quotes, some serious and even tragic, but others light-hearted and full of laughter.
Including scenes from Othello, The Winter’s Tale, Twelfth Night, A Midsummer Night’s Dream, The Merchant of Venice, Cymbeline, The Merry Wives of Windsor, The Two Noble Kinsmen and many more, this is an entertainment for anyone who enjoys great theatre. An ensemble cast will transport you into these differing worlds united by the theme of green-eyed jealousy.

The Phantom Raspberry Blower of Old London Town
From: October 23, 2026,

Something is rotten in Old London Town…
In Victorian England, a rotten, raspberry blower is claiming the lives of the upper-classes. The dark alleyways and moonlit graveyards of London are alive with the repetitive dins of the Phantom. Inspector Corner of the Yard and his faithful sidekick, Sergeant Bowles, are fed up with these din-dins. The case is afoot. Will the Phantom be uncloaked? All will be revealed… particularly if the Phantom uncloaks himself. Immortalised by The Two Ronnies, this Jack the Ripper parody is raspberry-jam-packed with Spike Milligan’s and Ronnie Barker’s love of surreal absurdism, clever puns, near-the-knuckle ribaldry, quick-fire jokes and famous wordplay.

Peter Pan
From: December 3, 2026, till December 12, 2026,

Wendy Darling lives in Edwardian Devizes with her parents and two younger brothers. One December evening they find themselves transported to Neverland with the enigmatic Peter Pan, who has been secretly visiting their home in Le Marchant Barracks to listen to their bedtime stories. Once on the magical island, they meet the Lost Boys and the Flower People, but also find themselves drawn into a long-standing feud with the villainous Captain Hook.
With scenes of stage fighting, themes of youth, bravery and the bittersweet reality of growing up, along with songs that everyone will know, this year’s Christmas show promises to combine all the fun of a magical world with the gradual realisation of the importance of family life.

Adapted & directed by Jessica Bone & Karen Ellis

Please note: Saturday 5 December Matinee will be a relaxed performance.

The Valentine Letters
From: January 25, 2027, till January 30, 2027,

A poignant play by Steve Darlow exploring the real-life Second World War story of RAF airman and prisoner of war, John Valentine and his wife Ursula. Through their letters, the play delves into themes of love, resilience, and hope amidst the challenges of war. A moving testament to the human spirit.
The play is based upon the book Geprüft written by their daughter, Frances Zagni.

Director | Abigail Newton
