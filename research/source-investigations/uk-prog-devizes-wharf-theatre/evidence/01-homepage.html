THE WHARF

AW26_brochure_fullDownload

Today we are proud to offer high quality theatre and diverse entertainment with in-house produced musicals, plays, pantomimes, youth shows and live music along with guest theatre companies and speakers. The Wharf is run by a community of volunteers and we look forward to welcoming you to one of our shows very soon.
