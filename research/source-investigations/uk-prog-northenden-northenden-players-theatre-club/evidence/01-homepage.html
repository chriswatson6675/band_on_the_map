Alan Ayckbourn’s comic murder mystery is set in a crumbling country house, when a manipulative patriarch announces he’ll leave his fortune to a long-lost student, he is killed and every relative has a motive.


The play humorously spoofs classic whodunits while exposing greed and family resentment.
