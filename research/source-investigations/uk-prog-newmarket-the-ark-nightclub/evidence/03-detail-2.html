    ABOUT THE VENUE

    The ARK is situated on Newmarket High Street opposite the King Edward VII Memorial Hall. Originally the Kingsway Cinema, this impressive building has had many names - The Dance Hall, The White House, Club M, Innocence. Since re-opening in 2019 it has now been fully refurbished, transformed into the ARK!
