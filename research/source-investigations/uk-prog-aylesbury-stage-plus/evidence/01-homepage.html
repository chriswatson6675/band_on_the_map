MEET GEMMA

Gemma has a real passion for teaching dance, to make everyone feel a part of something special, to be inclusive and believe in all of her students and help them believe in themselves. She loves when past students return to pass down their knowledge and enthusiasm proving that you never really leave the Stage Plus Family.

Gemma started dancing from a very young age and spent the majority of her childhood with The Sabatini Dancers fundraising for charities and performing anywhere and everywhere. She trained from 18 at The North London Dance Studio where she recieved a Diploma in Performing Arts and her ISTD teaching qualifications.
‍
After College Gemma performed around the country and travelled abroad performing, managing and choreographing. On a break from this she covered maternity at Stage Plus and fell in love with teaching and when offered to become Principal in 2016 she jumped at the chance!
‍
Gemma has continued over the years to advance and add to her qualifications with Linda Sweetzer Teacher Training, RAD, ID Dance, Zumba, Rosina Andrew’s Method and more.
‍
She is a qualified Level 3 Early Years Practitioner, is First Aid trained, DBS checked and has her First Aid Certificate.

Our Classes
01

Dance

Dance is one of the most difficult, disciplined, and beautiful art forms! Dance is where we started and is our strong base. Not only is it fun and rewarding, it is a wonderful way to keep fit and it establishes good posture for life. Dance classes are offered to all ages from beginners to advanced for fun or to exam level, or for the more serious to professional performance level.  

We can also point you in the right direction for your teaching qualifications.

02

Ballet

Ballet is truly the foundation of all dance.
It teaches self discipline, correct body placement, strength, and gracefulness.
It is a popular class that provides the necessary base for any training.

Classes start for boys and girls at preschool level (2.5yrs) and continue on to Majors

03

Modern

Modern Jazz has become one of the most popular dance styles in recent years, mainly due to its popularity on television shows, movies, music videos, London Shows and commercials.People enjoy watching jazz dancers, as the dancing is fun and energetic. It has a strong
technical base and high performance levels.

Classes start at Primary level (5yrs) and continue to Majors.

04

Tap

Tap is an exciting form of dance in which dancers wear special shoes equipped with metal taps. Tap dancers use their feet like drums to create rhythmic patterns, a percussive sound and timely beats and as such the dancers are considered to be, somewhat, musicians. The term “tap dancing” is derived from the tapping sound produced when the small metal plates on the dancer’s shoes touch a hard floor or surface.The goal in tap dancing is to produce clear, clean sounds, with various levels of tone. Body weight should be held slightly forward, allowing most of the dancing to be done on the balls of the feet. The knees and ankles should be relaxed at all times.Ever popular with boys and girls from Primary (5yrs) to Majors.

05

Arco Limbering

Acro dance is a style that combines classical dance technique with acrobatic elements. It
is defined by its athletic character, its unique choreography, which seamlessly blends dance and acrobatics, and its use of acrobatics in a dance context. It is a popular dance style in amateur as well as professional dance theatre, such as Cirque De Soleil.Acro is an especially challenging dance style for dancers as it requires them to be trained in both dance and acrobatic skills. Acro dancers must be in excellent physical condition as well, because acro is a physically demanding activity.A class focusing on strengthening, flexibility and limbering. The students also get to practice and perfect gymnastic style tricks with support to hand. Suitable for boys and girls of all ages and abilities.

06

Modern Jazz & Lyrical

The Inter and Senior Classes mix the technique and strength or Jazz with the quality and emotion of Lyrical dance to build their abilities and broaden their knowledgeLyrical dance is a dance style that blends ballet and jazz dance. Lyrical is generally smoother and a bit faster than ballet, but not quite as fast as jazz. A lyrical dancer uses movement to express strong emotions such as love, joy and anger. Movements in lyrical dance are characterized by fluidity and grace, with the dancer flowing seamlessly from one move to another, holding finishing steps as long as possible. Leaps are exceptionally high and soaring, and turns are fluid and continuous.

07

Contemporary

There are many methods to training in contemporary dance, Cunningham, Graham and Limon
being just a few.  The main components of Contemporary are Release, fall and recovery, direction changes, isolations, core strengthening, connecting with the floor and improvisation.As the UK’s most up coming dance style used by most choreographers, contemporary dance training is fast becoming the style that all dancers should attain for their future.

08

Adult Classes

Our classes allow adults to develop and broaden their dance skills through classes that progress from week to week with the opportunity to participate in our bi-annual shows. Whether you are a beginner or have danced before, we offer classes in different genres for fitness and fun.
