The Hull Ice Arena, home to the regions only Olympic size ice rink, offers a comprehensive ice-skating programme for the whole public. It plays host to a number of semi-professional ice hockey clubs. It is a renowned entertainment venue for concerts and other attractions.

The Hull Ice Arena runs a variety of -

  • ice skating
  • figure skating
  • Learn to Skate
  • ice hockey
  • sessions which are open to the public.

In addition to this, the Hull Ice Arena is home to a number of ice hockey clubs which attract members at all levels. Customers and spectators can also enjoy a bite to eat or a drink in our café, which offers a selection of hot and cold foods.

How to find the Hull Ice Arena

The Hull Ice Arena is centrally located just off the A63 Clive Sullivan Way. It is near Kingston Retail Park. It is a short walk from the Paragon Interchange, which is a bus and train station, and many hotels.
