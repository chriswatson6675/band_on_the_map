                                                    About Us

                                                    The Pound arts centre offers a year round programme of performances, workshops, a youth theatre and exhibitions. Our mission is to connect people through creativity.

                                                    Your Visit

                                                    Cafe

                                                    A pre-show social, time to relax, work, meet up with friends, or grab a change of scenery with the little ones… All great ways to spend time in our café. We serve a delicious range of cakes and scones as well as Bristol Twenty Coffee and a selection of specialist teas. We also have plenty of plant based and very low gluten options. Friends get 10% off in the cafe bar! The cafe is usually open from 9.30am - 4pm during the week, and alongside most events.

                                                    Spaces

                                                    The Pound offers a fully equipped auditorium seating 100 people. The seating can also be put away to create a large hall space. We have full AV capabilities and with a green room and technical booth it's the ideal place for performances, talks, conferences and classes. To find out more about any of our spaces, or to request a room booking for, contact the Box Office on box.office@poundarts.org.uk or call 01249 701628.
