    About the event

    Join George, a puppy trainer, to hear about the life of a Guide Dog. From training programmes, through to starting life as a working dog, George will share the joys and challenges of preparing puppies to take on this important responsibility. There’ll be time for questions, to meet the puppies, and to hear how you can get involved and support the Guide Dogs. Suitable for all ages.
