Our new HQ is a former shop front on Middlewood Road in Hillsborough. The space is our office and meeting space, and it’s also available to hire for creative, cultural and/or community activities.

Travel Information

By foot

You'll find us on Middlewood Road, on the row of shops opposite Hillsborough Bookshop, near the edge of the park.

By bike

There are 'plantlock' planters outside the front of the shop, which you're welcome to lock your bike to. There are other places to lock your bike nearby on the high street.

By tram

The nearest tram stop is Hillsborough Park, on the Yellow Route. It's less than 2 minutes walk from this stop.

By bus

The nearest bus stops are Middlewood Road/Dykes Hall Road, and Middlewood Road/Minto Road. Both are approximately 2 minutes walk from our space.

By taxi

There is an area taxis can pull into right outside, but please note it's a loading bay with double yellow lines so they cannot park there.

By car

For drop-offs, cars can pull up in the loading bay right outside, but please note it has double yellow lines so you cannot park there. The nearest pay and display parking is right next door, between us and the park: Hawksley Avenue Car Park.

Toilets

There are two all gender toilets. One toilet is accessible (exact spec coming soon with access statement). Each toilets has a sink and hand soap, a waste bin, a sanitary bin, and free sanitary products inside the toilet. The nearest Changing Places toilet is in Hillsborough Park, beside Hillsborough Library. Baby changing facilities are coming soon.

Dogs and service animals
  • The space is dog-friendly, and sometimes a member of staff brings a dog to the office.
  • Dogs must be well behaved and on leads. Please note there may be other dogs and/or small children in the space, and bear this in mind when deciding whether or not to bring your dog.
  • Certain activities may not be suitable to bring a dog, to keep the dog safe (e.g. workshops using potentially dangerous tools or materials).
  • Service animals are always welcome in the shop and we can give them water.
