The Phoenix Arts Club is known in the showbiz world as 'that Soho bar', and it is unlike any other.

Truly independent, created by entertainers for entertainers, it now welcomes 100,000 cabaret lovers a year to its nightly multi-line-up cabaret shows, featuring the best in cabaret & variety from drag to musical theatre, burlesque to aerial, comedy and so much more.

You never know who might take to the stage!

Non-members can purchase tickets to a show and receive temporary membership for the night.
We believe the arts should be accessible, and so prices start from just £15.

Yearly membership starts at £150, and all monies raised are reinvested back into the Club's fabric and frame.

'Post Show', the venue transforms into a late night cocktail bar and hangout, welcoming the West End's theatre workers, actors, writers, painters, film-makers and more through its art deco doors.

Why not pay us a visit and see what we're all about! Advanced booking is essential.
