Wallace

The William Wallace story like you've never heard it before! Get ready for a fast-paced, cleverly crafted musical featuring a sensational soundtrack of rap, folk, pop and rock, all performed in a distinctly Scottish voice. The whip-smart cast go head-to-head in electrifying and witty rap battles, telling the story of a national icon for a new generation, and anyone who has ever shouted 'Freedom!' after watching Braveheart. With guest appearances from historical icons such as Robert The Bruce and King Edward I, Wallace is a thrilling, high-energy show that will have you cheering in your seats, marvelling at the lyrics, and talking about it all the way home, asking: could this become Scotland's Hamilton? A Raw Material production. Music & Lyrics by Dave Hook Book by Rob Drummond Directed by Orla O’Loughlin Set Designer - Neil Haynes Lighting Designer - Derek Anderson Costume Designer - Victoria Brown Sound Designer - Garry Boyle Choreographer - Kloe Dean Associate Director - Steve Lauder-Russell Dramaturg - Jackie Crichton Executive Producers - Gillian Garrity & Margaret-Anne O’Donnell CAST: Madeline Grieve - wummin’ Dave Hook - MC / Wallace Kimberley Mandindo - wummin’ Ross Mann - Scotsman Patricia Panther - wummin’ Manasa Tagica - Sassenach Pictures from a first iteration of Wallace, which was presented as a Play, A Pie and A Pint & Raw Material Co-Production at Oran Mor, Glasgow in 2025.

Rollers Forever

Back by Huge Public Demand! Rollers Forever is the smash-hit tartan-clad musical celebrating the music, memories and passionate fandom of the Bay City Rollers. This warm and funny story follows two lifelong friends who reconnect on a Saturday night and are pulled back into the excitement, laughter and teenage obsession that shaped their Roller-mania. Filled with comedy, nostalgia and the spirit of a generation, the show captures what it meant and still means to be part of the Roller world. Written by award-winning playwright Danny McCahon and directed by acclaimed theatre-maker Liz Carruthers, Rollers Forever brings the band's iconic soundtrack to the stage through a story shaped by the fans themselves. With beloved hits, heartfelt moments and a celebration of friendship at its core, the production pays tribute to the people who kept the tartan flame burning. A joyful musical experience, it promises to leave audiences smiling, singing and remembering why the Rollers meant so much.

Billy & Tim - The Widescreen Edition

Scotland’s award-winning comedy — like you’ve never seen it before. What happens when one of Scotland’s best-loved theatre comedies collides with the unpredictability of live football commentary? Welcome to The Widescreen Edition of Singin’ I’m No a Billy, He’s a Tim. Two football fans. Two sides of Glasgow. One prison cell. On the day of an Old Firm clash, Rangers supporter Billy and Celtic fan Tim find themselves locked up together — and forced to confront the rivalry, prejudice, humour and contradictions that have divided generations. Funny, fast, provocative and packed with the unmistakable Glasgow humour that has made Singin’ I’m No a Billy, He’s a Tim a Scottish theatre phenomenon, The Widescreen Edition takes the original award-winning comedy and turns it into a completely different live experience. The play unfolds on stage while some of Scottish football’s most recognisable voices, former players and personalities watch from the sidelines — providing live, unscripted commentary, analysis, predictions, stories and reactions to the action. No two performances are quite the same. And with Rangers and Celtic legends sitting side by side, sometimes the action off the pitch is every bit as entertaining as the action on it. The widescreen team Rob MacLean — broadcaster and commentator  David Tanner — broadcaster and presenter  Charlie Miller — former Rangers midfielder  Derek Ferguson — former Rangers midfielder  Peter Grant — former Celtic midfielder  Simon Donnelly — former Celtic forward  Jackie McNamara — former Celtic captain  David Robertson — former Rangers defender Together with the full cast of Singin’ I’m No a Billy, He’s a Tim, they bring the atmosphere, arguments and humour of Scottish football into the theatre — with absolutely no guarantee that everyone will agree with the referee. Beneath the laughs remains the reason Billy & Tim has endured. This is a comedy about football, friendship, family, identity and sectarianism — asking why the colours we wear can mean so much, where rivalry ends and hatred begins, and whether two people raised on opposite sides can discover they have considerably more in common than they thought. Part theatre. Part football show. Part live commentary. Completely unpredictable. Whether you’re Rangers, Celtic, neither — or couldn’t tell a penalty from a corner kick — Singin’ I’m No a Billy, He’s a Tim: The Widescreen Edition is first and foremost a great night of Scottish comedy. 90 minutes. Two teams. One stage. And absolutely no VAR.

Hen Night Horror

Hen Night Horror is back! The riotous original musical comedy-horror that’s had audiences screaming with laughter returns this Autumn. This witty production stars Dani Heron, best known for playing Lydia in the Emmy award winning drama Crime, and Scotland's West End legend Matthew Mckenna - fresh from the smash hit One Day at Edinburgh's Lyceum. They will be joined by Pavilion panto star and favourite Jennifer Neil as well as Hannah Howie and Alan Orr. Amanda’s dream wedding is just days away, but her hen night is already a disaster. Her bridesmaids are at war, secrets are bubbling over, and tensions are ready to explode. Then the girls realise something far worse than their friendship issues is lurking in the shadows… They’re not alone. What starts as a boozy getaway quickly descends into pure comedy chaos, packed with hilarious lyrics, belting tunes, sharp Scottish wit and some genuinely spine‑tingling surprises. Get your tickets now for this original musical comedy-horror that will put the willies right up you! Age guidance: 16+   Contains adult humour, strong language and moments of comic horror.
